# Origin Life Service Station Image-2 Redesign

## Purpose

Iterate `submissions/EthanHuangEbor/jingzhang-everyday-loop` around one reviewable city product: the **Origin Life Service Station / 原点生活服务站**. The submission keeps its original “one spine, three loops, nine stations” framework, but the proposal is now demonstrated through a reversible 1:1 public-service prototype, scenario contracts, synthetic failure rehearsal, named decision interfaces, and an evidence-led first 100 days.

PR #2247 is used only as a methodological reference: it shows the value of a focused prototype, explicit failure paths, responsibility interfaces, and a runnable evidence package. No text, visual composition, brand mark, image, map, or code from that submission may be copied or used as an Image-2 reference input.

## Prototype Definition

The prototype is a reversible public ground-floor and forecourt interface in the AI Origin Community. Its location is conceptual and does not assert a property agreement, official redline, heritage approval, construction approval, or operating appointment.

The five spatial moments are fixed:

1. **Ordinary Passage / 普通通行** — a continuous route that works without participation, a phone, or AI.
2. **Human Service Commons / 人工服务厅** — staffed assistance, paper information, care referral, and an appeal channel.
3. **Voluntary AI Alcove / 自愿 AI 小间** — an opt-in explanation or navigation service with a visible purpose, data ceiling, and human handoff.
4. **Public Receipt Gallery / 公开回执廊** — anonymous status, responsible role, next review, failure record, and disposition.
5. **Quiet Restoration Garden / 安静恢复园** — a screen-free heritage and blue-green edge that remains useful after equipment is removed.

The same basic task must remain possible through a staffed or non-digital route. Any loss of that route pauses the corresponding AI path.

Each scenario receives six synthetic cases: one qualified contract and five negative variants.

1. accountable role missing;
2. same-task human route unavailable;
3. declared data ceiling exceeded;
4. stop authority unavailable;

The total is fixed at `12 × 6 = 72` synthetic checks. Qualified cases may be released for tabletop review only; all negative cases must be blocked. These results do not claim field performance, safety, compliance, approval, or public acceptance.

## Decision Gates and First 100 Days

The evidence gates are original to this proposal and use fixed meanings:

- **G0 — Evidence and rights:** source status, concept location, affected groups, rights, and responsible interfaces are declared.
- **G1 — Same-task equivalence:** the staffed/non-digital baseline and data ceiling are reviewable.
- **G2 — Controlled service:** a reversible setup, human signoff, incident log, appeal route, and stop authority are available.
- **G3 — Stop and restore:** equipment, access, service, data, and public records can be restored or closed.
- **G4 — Independent continuation:** evidence supports continue, revise, or remove; absence of evidence never defaults to continuation.

The first 100 days contain eight packages: survey protocol; same-task baseline; reversible physical mock-up; synthetic and shadow rehearsal; authorized small opening; stop-and-restore drill; independent and affected-user review; continue/revise/remove decision. A calendar date cannot override a failed gate.

## Image-2-Only Visual Production

The Image-2-only requirement applies to all visible page content: spatial scenes, people, physical models, axonometrics, sections, diagrams, typography, labels, legends, numbers, textures, and decoration.

Prohibited visual construction methods:

- no PIL, SVG, HTML/CSS, Canvas, slide software, or PDF drawing commands to add or repair visible content;
- no deterministic text overlay after generation;
- no programmatically drawn maps, arrows, tables, or borders in final pages;
- no third-party competition board, photograph, brand asset, or map screenshot used as a generation input without an explicit cleared right.

Permitted non-visual operations:

- copy Image-2 outputs into the workspace;
- encode or compress an unchanged page image for repository limits;
- place unchanged page images into PDFs;
- inspect decoding, dimensions, clipping, page counts, OCR candidates, hashes, and file size;
- produce structured evidence, deterministic rehearsal receipts, manifests, and self-check records.

Each page uses three Image-2 stages:

1. **Chinese master generation:** complete architectural board with the approved physical-model language and controlled blank/label zones.
2. **Targeted Image-2 repair:** edit only inaccurate words, counts, spatial moments, warnings, or visual regressions; preserve accepted page composition.
3. **English Image-2 localization:** use the accepted Chinese page as the edit target, preserve composition and counts, and replace only language content. Any localized regression returns to targeted Image-2 repair.

Use `gpt-image-2` only. Do not downgrade to another image model. Prefer the highest available landscape resolution that remains within repository limits.

## Visual Language

The complete set uses:

- ivory physical-model backgrounds;
- graphite linework and section cuts;
- warm brick-red service cores;
- muted brass thresholds;
- blue-green accessible and landscape elements;
- timber, perforated brick, stone, weathered metal, tactile paving, and planted shade;
- inclusive but non-staged public life: older people, wheelchair users, caregivers, children, commuters, visitors, and staffed service workers;
- clear separation between ordinary passage, staffed service, voluntary AI, public evidence, and quiet recovery.

Avoid cinematic spectacle that implies a built project, invented historic replicas, steam-train nostalgia presented as current reality, Great Wall imagery, government emblems, corporate logos, measurements without evidence, tiny pseudo-text, and generic futuristic screens.

## Text and Count Contract

Image-2 page text must remain sparse:

- one title;
- one short subtitle;
- five to eight short labels;
- one concise concept/non-approval warning;
- no paragraph-sized microtext and no decorative pseudo-writing.

Fixed counts and terms:

- one public spine, three loops, nine stations;
- three key areas and three stations in each area;
- five prototype moments numbered `01` through `05` exactly once;
- twelve scenarios numbered `01` through `12` exactly once on the scenario page;
- three pilots;
- five gates `G0` through `G4` exactly once;
- eight first-100-days work packages;
- 72 synthetic checks = 12 qualified + 60 blocked negative cases;
- concept/provisional/non-approval warning on every relevant page;
- `COMMUNITY + CARE`, never `COMMUNITY + CART`.

No page may add a FAR, height, width, distance, investment, service radius, approval date, confirmed operator, or official boundary claim.

## Acceptance and Stop-Ship Rules

Every page must pass original-resolution human inspection. OCR may identify suspect regions but never substitutes for inspection.

Stop-ship conditions:

- pseudo-Chinese, tofu, broken characters, misspelled English, invented text, or illegible microtext;
- missing, duplicated, or contradictory fixed counts;
- a Chinese/English pair that differs in facts, numbers, warnings, or scenario meaning;
- invented map precision, current-condition claim, government approval, committed operator, or implementation promise;
- inaccessible public route, AI-required basic service, missing human route, missing stop authority, or missing restoration state in the prototype representation;
- copied visual identity or unlicensed reference input;
- PDF clipping, blank pages, image corruption, inconsistent page order, or package size above repository limits.

Any failed page is repaired only through a targeted Image-2 edit, followed by reinspection of that page and its language pair. Any change to the prototype or shared count requires regeneration and review of every dependent A0/A3 page.
