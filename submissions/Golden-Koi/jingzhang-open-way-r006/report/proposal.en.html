Evidence basis and data boundary

The package uses the source records retained in sources.json: official text may support named scopes, approximate areas and task context; OpenStreetMap-derived records support desk-study context only; provisional geometry supports composition and repeatable QA; internal design records explain design intent only. No verifiable official CAD/GIS, road boundaries, parcels, ownership, field survey or engineering base is supplied in this package.

Evidence states are explicit:

  • E: fact or record supported by an available source;
  • I: design inference or provisional concept;
  • U: unknown, unavailable or unverified;
  • BLOCKED: missing evidence that cannot be promoted to an implementation conclusion.

The text anchors of approximately 43.6 km², 11.4 km² and 192.1/104.3/72.0 ha are not survey results. Closed polygons are rough and non-official. All nine geometry files, figures, metrics and reports must be rebuilt from authoritative inputs if X1 is opened.

Three zones and two wings

The concept treats the corridor as one civic service spine:

  • North / Zhongguancun AI Innovation Acceleration Area: an Open Testbed for bounded, reversible safety and exit testing.
  • Middle / Beijing AI Origin Community: an Open Translation Commons connecting research with quiet, shared daily service.
  • South / Dazhongsi AI Industry Cluster: an Open Urban Gateway that protects existing businesses and daily continuity before station integration.
  • Knowledge Collaboration Wing: a desk-study relationship among universities, research and enterprise.
  • Civic Life Wing: a desk-study relationship among communities, public services and culture.

These roles express a public-value chain, not official land use or institutional commitment. Detailed brand rules, the two-wing logic and status boundaries are in deliverables/agent-deliverables.en.md.

Regional ecosystem and future-city research

Six case types frame questions rather than best-practice claims: innovation districts, regulatory sandboxes, living labs, station-area renewal, public digital infrastructure and open-source civic communities. Six ecosystem role classes are proposed: project party; public service; universities/research; firms/developers; communities/small businesses; independent professional/accessibility reviewers. No named organisation has committed.

The governing rule is evidence before investment: every relationship must state the exchange, final accountability, data boundary and exit. Low-tech service, human takeover, AI-off, data minimisation, appeal, vendor exit and publication of negative results remain mandatory comparisons.

AI innovation ecosystem, personas and AI-enabled scenarios

AI is treated as a service and governance hypothesis, not a deployed system. The ten scenario cards and five personas keep a low-tech baseline, human final responsibility, appeal, AI-off and exit. Personas are research prompts only; they cannot substitute for compensated participation or real-user burden evidence.

Overall design and land-use structure

The approximately 11.4 km² discussion layer organises corridors, nodes, public-space interfaces and focus zones without representing a statutory plan. Candidate land-use polygons are agent_generated_design with official_boundary=false. Existing conditions, ownership, heritage, municipal capacity, roads, heights, setbacks and development intensity remain unknown or unverified.

Retain/renovate/remove/do-nothing comparison is a procedure, not a parcel conclusion. Official base data, field evidence and qualified review must precede any site-level recommendation.

Focus zones and reversible scenarios

Each focus zone retains A, B and N pathways. Options are compared by public value, burden, reversibility, exit and evidence gate; none is a selected implementation scheme. A named route, date, distance, time, gradient, obstacle record, photographs and independent repeat walk are absent, so journey claims remain a desk-study protocol.

Ten scenario cards cover accessible arrival, public-service booking, small-business continuity, visitor access, night-time help, older-person service, public-space events, blue-green maintenance, developer testing and incident response. Three industry tests cover civic-AI reliability, accessible journeys and small-business digital continuity. Five personas remain hypotheses, not user evidence.

Public space, mobility, culture and components

The public-space network combines a continuous civic spine, pocket commons and blue-green buffers. Three concept landmarks are proposed: the Open Test Gate, Translation Commons and Centennial JingZhang Living Room. A public-value mark recognises verifiable accessibility, open interfaces, negative-result publication, AI-off drills, maintenance and business continuity; it cannot be bought or assigned by one algorithm.

The component library covers wayfinding posts, help points, open-data plaques, reversible test cells, seating/shade and maintenance interfaces. Every component needs offline use, a named maintainer, an acceptance method and an exit condition.

The cultural narrative uses three layers—railway memory, contemporary daily life and an open future—with independently proofed English, physical alternatives and source-controlled names/dates. QR or AR is never the only access path.

Building scale, retain/renovate/remove and implementation boundary

No parcel-level building inventory, structural assessment, ownership check, statutory height, floor-area ratio, setback, municipal capacity or cost basis is available. Retain, renovate, remove and do nothing therefore remain a comparison method. The phasing graphic indicates research order only; it does not mean funding, acquisition, procurement, construction or operating approval.

Annual programme and developer community

The annual rhythm is: Q1 public questions/open data; Q2 accessibility/daily journeys; Q3 open civic-service challenge; Q4 public-value audit/exhibition. Scene access progresses from L0 documentation to L1 synthetic sandbox, L2 controlled field observation and only after X4/X5/X6, L3 authorised small pilot.

Open problems may attract teams, but conversion is not automatic procurement. Rights/eligibility checks, a synthetic sandbox, low-tech comparison, human takeover, public-burden review and exit tests lead only to a project-party GO / REVISE / STOP dossier. Failure and stopping are valid public evidence.

Approximately 100-day evidence-acquisition plan

Five stages are defined in deliverables/agent-deliverables.en.md: authority/baseline; field/real use; co-design/comparison; cost/procurement/exit; independent review/decision. Each has a role owner, dependency, ROM cost band, acceptance and stop condition. Role owners are currently unnamed. The CNY 1.2m–2.5m total is a concept-stage ROM with possible -50%/+100% error, not a quote or approved budget, and must be replaced by X5 evidence.

Metrics and compliance boundary

Only three values are repeatably computed: provisional site area, green ratio and public-space ratio. They are low-confidence QA results from non-official geometry, not approved indicators or measured performance. Unknown inputs are never represented as zero.

Compliance and design-depth matrices index responses and gaps. addressed means a traceable participant response exists; it does not mean compliance. concept_response_complete means the requested concept topic is present; it does not mean field, engineering, statutory or independent validation.
