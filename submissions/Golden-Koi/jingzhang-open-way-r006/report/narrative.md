### Three industry tests

1. `IT-01` Civic AI reliability sandbox: synthetic or de-identified data only;
   test logs, human takeover, AI-off and vendor exit.
2. `IT-02` Accessible journey co-test: obtain real route and obstacle evidence
   before comparing low-tech, human and AI-assisted options.
3. `IT-03` Small-business digital continuity: no forced adoption, rent
   pressure or lock-in; require exportability and exit.
