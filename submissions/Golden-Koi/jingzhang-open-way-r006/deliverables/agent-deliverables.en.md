# Six agent deliverables

This register answers the requested `agent.1` to `agent.6` outputs. It is a reviewable concept-research package, not an official plan, field study, engineering design or implementation permission. All spatial boundaries are provisional and evidence gates X1-X6 remain `BLOCKED`.

## agent.1 | Brand system and the “three zones, two wings” master diagram

### Brand frame

- Chinese name: 京张开放之路
- English name: JingZhang Open Way
- Promise: 一路向新，始终为人 / A New Way Forward. Always for People.
- Mark logic: one continuous gold line represents the historic JingZhang corridor; three open nodes represent Zhongguancun, the AI Origin Community and Dazhongsi; two lateral strokes represent the Knowledge Collaboration Wing and the Civic Life Wing.
- Palette: rail gold `#D6A64A`, night blue `#10243E`, civic green `#2D7D68`, risk red `#B84A4A`. Red is reserved for blockers and risk.
- Boundary: this is a concept mark and usage rule, not a registered trade mark or claim to official identity or third-party font/graphic rights.

### Three zones, two wings

| Unit | Concept role | Public value | Current boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| North: Zhongguancun AI Innovation Acceleration Area | Open Testbed | Validate safety and exit in bounded cells first | X1/X4/X5/X6 BLOCKED |
| Middle: Beijing AI Origin Community | Open Translation Commons | Translate research into quiet, shared, usable daily service | X1/X2/X3/X4/X6 BLOCKED |
| South: Dazhongsi AI Industry Cluster | Open Urban Gateway | Protect existing businesses and continuity before station integration | X1/X2/X3/X5/X6 BLOCKED |
| West: Knowledge Collaboration Wing | University-research-industry links | Open questions, test protocols and talent exchange | Desk-study hypothesis only |
| East: Civic Life Wing | Community-service-culture links | Accessibility, care, small-business and public-space continuity | Desk-study hypothesis only |

The diagram expresses relationships only; it is not statutory land use, a road boundary, development control or project approval.

## agent.2 | Case and ecosystem map

### Five named reference cases

| Reference case | Mechanism to learn from | JingZhang transfer boundary |
|---|---|---|
| [Helsinki Smart Kalasatama](https://forumvirium.fi/en/publication/smartkalasatama-final-report/) | A 2013–2021 programme using a real district, cross-sector collaboration and 25 agile pilots | Helsinki's governance, data and participation conditions cannot be copied |
| [Helsinki Innovation Districts](https://forumvirium.fi/en/helsinki-innovation-districts-turned-suburbs-into-living-labs/) | Moving living-lab methods from new districts into existing-neighbourhood regeneration, with open learning | Local networks, compensated participation and field burden must be tested |
| [JTC LaunchPad @ one-north](https://www.jtc.gov.sg/about-jtc/news-and-stories/feature-stories/launchpad-5-things-to-know-about-launchpad) | Modular space, incubator/investor networks, prototyping and test-bedding | Singapore's industrial, tenancy and institutional arrangements do not prove local feasibility |
| [Punggol Digital District](https://www.jtc.gov.sg/punggoldigitaldistrict) | Industry-university-government collaboration and district interfaces through an Open Digital Platform | Live data, sensors and operating platforms require X4/X5/X6 first |
| [Waterfront Toronto / Sidewalk Labs termination](https://www.waterfrontoronto.ca/sites/default/files/documents/plan-development-agreement-amendments-and-sidewalk-labs--termination-notice---termination-effective-date---may-17-2020.pdf) | Independent digital advice, anti-lock-in, privacy/data governance and a formal termination mechanism | A governance lesson in how to stop; not a model for scale, contract or technology |

These cases frame comparison questions; none is endorsed as a transferable “best practice” until original material, dates, rights, failures and transfer conditions are verified.

### Six comparison questions

| Case type | Question to learn from | Why it cannot be copied directly |
|---|---|---|
| Innovation district | How universities, firms and public services share interfaces | Different governance and land systems |
| Regulatory sandbox | How scope, time, accountability and exit are bounded | Requires local legal authority |
| Living lab | How real participants are compensated and dissent retained | Participation is not support |
| Station-area renewal | How rail, walking, commerce and heritage connect | No local entrance or flow measurements yet |
| Public digital infrastructure | How human takeover, low-tech access and data minimisation remain | Operating, cyber and procurement evidence required |
| Open-source civic community | How contribution, version governance and public audit work | Maintenance budget and accountable operator required |

The ecosystem has six role classes: project party; local government/public service; universities/research; firms/developers; communities/small businesses; independent professional/accessibility reviewers. Every relationship must state the exchange, final accountability, data boundary and exit. No named institution has committed.

## agent.3 | Scenario cards, industry test scenes and personas

### Ten scenario cards

| ID | Scenario | Low-tech baseline | Possible AI augmentation | Hard stop |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| S01 | Accessible arrival | Legible signs and staffed help | Personalised route prompt | Wrong guidance or no human alternative |
| S02 | Public-service booking | Phone, counter and paper | Multilingual process assistance | No explanation, appeal or withdrawal |
| S03 | Small-business continuity | Stable lease and human service | Time-banded footfall analysis | Exclusion, rent pressure or opaque scoring |
| S04 | District visitor access | Reception and paper pass | Data-minimal appointment support | Excess collection or no deletion |
| S05 | Night-time help | Lighting, phone and patrol | Risk prompt and human escalation | Automated enforcement or discriminatory recognition |
| S06 | Older-person service | Offline counter and volunteer help | Voice and large-text assistance | Forced digitisation or no takeover |
| S07 | Public-space event | Noticeboard and staff registration | Capacity and accessibility prompts | Commercial priority displaces public use |
| S08 | Blue-green maintenance | Scheduled inspection and public work order | Assisted anomaly detection | Automated action without field check |
| S09 | Developer test | Isolated sandbox and synthetic data | Simulated APIs and audit logs | Real personal data or production control |
| S10 | Urban incident response | Existing emergency procedure | Multi-source information organisation | Automated final decision or obstruction of authority |

### Three industry test scenes

1. `IT-01 Civic AI reliability sandbox`: synthetic/de-identified data only; test logs, human takeover, AI-off and vendor exit.
2. `IT-02 Accessible journey co-test`: complete real route and obstacle evidence before comparing low-tech, human and AI-assisted options.
3. `IT-03 Small-business digital continuity`: compare operating burden with no forced adoption, no rent pressure, exportability and exit.

### Five personas (research hypotheses, not real-user evidence)

| Persona | Core job | Possible burden | Must be tested |
|---|---|---|---|
| Daily commuter | Complete station-to-work journey on time | Detour, queue, inconsistent information | Segment time and repeat walk |
| Wheelchair/mobility-limited person | Step-free access to service and public space | Slope, width and route breaks | Named accessibility review and accompanied walk |
| Older resident | Complete daily tasks without a smartphone | Digital exclusion and cognitive load | Offline service availability |
| Small-business operator | Maintain rent, footfall and supply continuity | Retrofit cost and platform lock-in | Compensation, exit and non-adoption reasons |
| Front-line maintainer | Maintain assets and handle exceptions safely | Alert overload and unclear liability | Workload, SLA and final human accountability |

## agent.4 | Public space, three landmarks, recognition system and component library

### Public-space network

- Continuous civic spine: walking, cycling, step-free access, shade and rest continuity first.
- Pocket commons: small, non-commercial places to pause and seek help at station, community and innovation interfaces; operable offline.
- Blue-green buffer: assess drainage, shade, habitat and movement together; green area does not prove ecological performance.

### Three concept landmarks

1. North “Open Test Gate”: publishes test scope, accountable role, expiry and stop mechanism.
2. Middle “Translation Commons”: shows how research passes through resident, public-service and accessibility testing before daily use.
3. South “Centennial JingZhang Living Room”: connects railway memory, existing commerce and future service without demolition-led placemaking.

### Recognition system

The “JingZhang Public Value Mark” recognises verifiable behaviours only: open interfaces, accessibility improvement, publication of negative results, AI-off drills, maintenance response and small-business continuity. It cannot be bought or assigned by a single algorithm; appeal and withdrawal require an independent role.

### Component library

| Component | Required states | Minimum acceptance |
|---|---|---|
| Wayfinding post | Chinese, English, accessible symbols, offline legibility | Sight distance, contrast, maintainer |
| Help point | Human call and reachable controls | Response time and failure bypass |
| Open-data plaque | Purpose, source, update, deletion/appeal | No personal data displayed |
| Reversible test cell | Boundary, expiry, stop and restoration | Removal/restoration cost registered |
| Seat and shade | Multiple postures, wheelchair companion, non-commercial use | Field ergonomics and heat test |
| Maintenance interface | Asset ID, owner, work order and state | Publicly queryable without exposing security-sensitive data |

## agent.5 | Cultural wayfinding and international communication

The narrative has three layers: railway memory, contemporary daily life and an open future. Every location has a short physical text, optional deep reading and a non-digital alternative; AR or QR can never be the only access path. English is an independently proofed information layer, not a decorative subtitle. Names, dates and quotations enter a terminology and source register.

The international pack consists of a 90-second concept explainer, three-minute boundary explainer, bilingual one-pager, open-data/rights note and reusable-component list. It must not show concepts as built work, portray participation as endorsement, or omit `CONCEPT RESEARCH CANDIDATE` and X1-X6 status.

## agent.6 | Annual programme, developer community, scene opening and attraction-to-conversion

### Annual rhythm

| Quarter | Activity | Verifiable output |
|---|---|---|
| Q1 | Public Questions and Open Data Week | Question backlog, data dictionary, rights/gap register |
| Q2 | Accessibility and Daily Journey Month | Compensated participation, route evidence, burden/dissent register |
| Q3 | Open Civic Service Challenge | Isolated sandbox, test log, AI-off and exit drill |
| Q4 | Public Value Audit and Exhibition | Pass/fail/stop list, maintenance and cost review |

### Developer community and scene opening

- Contribution entry: open problems, data dictionary, synthetic samples, API contracts, tests and coordinated security disclosure.
- Access levels: L0 documentation; L1 synthetic sandbox; L2 controlled field observation; L3 authorised small pilot. X4/X5/X6 must pass before L3.
- Maintenance contract: version, owner, response time, deprecation, export format and vendor exit are registered before opening.

### Attract-validate-convert

Open problems attract teams; rights and eligibility checks precede a synthetic sandbox; only options that pass low-tech comparison, human takeover, public-burden and exit tests reach controlled validation. Conversion is not automatic procurement: it creates a project-party `GO / REVISE / STOP` dossier. Failure and stopping are public evidence too.
