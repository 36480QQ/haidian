Three-Level Scope

The proposal progresses from Coordinated Research Area through Overall Design Area to Key Detailed Design Areas, linking industrial strategy, urban renewal, and key-area design to spatial layers and metrics.

project_scope_summary.csv

Coordinated Research Area

For the 43.6-km² area: AI industrial ecology, three-zone / two-wing synergy, future urban form, and cultural narrative.

  • AI innovation chain & talent chain
  • Global case transfer mechanism
  • Brand naming & communication

Overall Design Area

For the ~11.4-km² area: urban renewal, land-use structure, transport and municipal infrastructure, and the Jing-Zhang Heritage Park vitality belt.

  • Regulatory-plan-level urban design
  • Renewal project list
  • Blue-green & slow-traffic system

Key Detailed Design Areas

For the three key areas: detailed design covering functional programs, building renewal, public space, and transport organization.

  • Zhongzhiyuan AI Autonomous Innovation Acceleration Zone
  • Beijing AI Origin Community
  • Dazhongsi AI Industry Cluster

Key Areas

Each key area must specify industrial positioning, spatial strategy, building and public space actions, transport interfaces, and implementation risks.

key_areas.geojson

Zhongzhiyuan AI Autonomous Innovation Acceleration Zone

A garden-style autonomous innovation district. Focuses on national platforms, industrial showcase, Qinghe River culture, external transport links, and low-carbon exchange spaces.

Beijing AI Origin Community

A university-proximate achievement transfer district. Focuses on university-originated innovation, open-source community, talent services, retain-renovate-demolish strategy, and station-area integration.

Dazhongsi AI Industry Cluster

An urban-style intelligent economy district. Focuses on leading enterprises, AI agent new business formats, commercial services, green space mixed-use, and four-quadrant pedestrian connectivity at the intersection.
