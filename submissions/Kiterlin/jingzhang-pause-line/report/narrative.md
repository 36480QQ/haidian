# Formal Narrative / 方案摘要

**京张停靠 / THE PAUSE LINE** 把“停留权”作为 AI 创新带的公共底座：任何人都应能休息、饮水、如厕、获得遮阴和无障碍支持，看见智能系统状态，选择非 AI 路径，并在自动化失败时得到真实人员交接。

方案以“一条停靠线、三座停靠站、两翼反馈、十二场景节点、四级停留时长”组织三层范围。校准站负责安全评测、维修和暂停；共创站负责开源、人才、无障碍共测和照护；交接站负责消费者权益、夜间抵达和人工接管。所有 AI 场景均有最小数据、人工复核、非 AI 等价路径和停止/退出规则。

The proposal treats the right to pause as the public foundation of an AI innovation belt. One line, three stop typologies, two feedback wings, twelve scenario courts, and four dwell durations connect ecosystem strategy to spatial design and accountable operation. Every AI scenario specifies minimum data, human review, a non-AI equivalent, and a stop/exit rule.

All geometry and dependent metrics are conceptual. The package retains canonical provisional boundaries, explicitly discloses the alignment gaps documented in repository Issues #846 and #1029, and keeps FAR and total floor area unknown. No statement constitutes an official redline, regulatory approval, project commitment, land right, or engineering conclusion.
