三层范围 Three scope levels

Coordinated research area ~43.6 km² (strategy) → overall design area ~11.4 km² (regulatory-depth concept design) → key areas 368.4 ha (detailed concept design). All boundaries are currently provisional polygons.

重点区域 Key areas

Zhongzhiyuan AI Acceleration Area (192.1 ha announced) / Beijing AI Origin Community (104.3 ha) / Dazhongsi AI Industry Cluster (72.0 ha), chained along the heritage axis.

用地分区 Land use

Research 230.6 ha · Education 210.0 ha · Residential 161.4 ha · Commercial 126.9 ha · Cultural 119.9 ha · Park green 141.2 ha · Plaza 13.5 ha · Roads 43.5 ha · Reserved 18.1 ha. Parcels tile the site without gaps or overlaps; recomputable in EPSG:4548.

交通慢行 Mobility & slow traffic

A 9.2 km heritage greenway spine, a parallel cycle commuterway, seven east-west links and three transit connections (Dazhongsi, Wudaokou, Qinghe direction) — all conceptual alignments, not road redlines.

蓝绿公共空间 Blue-green public space

The Jingzhang heritage park activity belt and the Xiaoyue riverside band form the blue-green framework; green coverage ~12.4%, with six AI public living-room plazas.

建筑 Buildings

607 proposed building footprints (~94.9 ha) express supply magnitude only; retain-and-renovate first; FAR and heights pending official data.

更新项目 Renewal projects

10 conceptual renewal projects: greenway connection, six plazas, station plaza TOD, Origin Avenue, open-source courtyards, pilot test field, riverside restoration, street furniture, digital-twin sandbox, talent apartments. Phasing: 4.52 / 4.94 / 1.95 km².

AI 场景 AI scenarios

10 AI scenario cards (cultural guide, slow-traffic navigation, enterprise copilot, delivery-robot test, health navigation, public-safety assist, hackathon cloud, retail concierge, heritage digital twin, accessibility assistant) + 3 industry test scenarios, all with privacy boundaries and human review.
