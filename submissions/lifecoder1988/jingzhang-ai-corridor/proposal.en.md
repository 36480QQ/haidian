# Jingzhang AI Rail Corridor: Urban Design Proposal for the Centennial Jing-Zhang AI Innovation Belt

## Design Basis and Source List

This proposal responds to the "Centennial Jing-Zhang AI Innovation Belt Open Call for Urban Design" (international solicitation). Its primary controlling basis is the pre-qualification announcement issued by the Haidian Branch of the Beijing Municipal Commission of Planning and Natural Resources; the announcement's three-level scope, three key areas, design tasks, and depth requirements define the task boundary of this proposal [source:DATA-SRC-OFFICIAL-ANNOUNCEMENT-20260509]. The agent-oriented open-call taskbook provides the three positionings, five functions, Three Zones and Two Wings structure, and six agent tasks, and is the main basis for the concept-level and operations-level design of this proposal [source:DATA-SRC-AGENT-TASKBOOK-20260518].

The use of materials strictly follows the classification conclusions of the repository's public source registry: the announcement, the taskbook, the Urban Design Management Measures, the Measures for the Preparation and Approval of Regulatory Detailed Planning of Cities and Towns, and the Guide to Land and Sea Use Classification for Territorial Space Survey, Planning and Use Control serve as formal bases; the Beijing Municipal Science and Technology Commission's "Three Zones and Two Wings" report and Haidian District's "1+X+1" industrial layout serve as background material only, used solely for industrial narrative and never upgraded into statutory controls or investment-attraction commitments [source:SRC-2026-BJ-KW-THREE-AREAS-WINGS]. Because the official precise boundaries have not been published, all spatial layers in this proposal are generated from the provisional rough boundaries provided by the maintainer, whose presumed basis and error margins are registered in the repository [source:DATA-SRC-PROVISIONAL-BOUNDARIES-20260605].

The design judgment is this: given that official planning boundaries, regulatory-plan conditions, and baseline data on existing buildings are all missing, the proposal's authoritative data layer (GeoJSON and metrics.json) expresses spatial structure only at the granularity of a "conceptual recommendation"; all areas can be recalculated under EPSG:4548; every missing condition is explicitly marked as "pending official data"; and no indicator is fabricated. The complete indexes of sources, assumptions, metrics, and matrices are in `sources.json`, `assumptions.json`, `metrics.json`, and the three matrix files; the main text retains only the citations directly relevant to each judgment.

## Three-Level Scope Framework

This proposal organizes its work according to the announcement's three-level scope [source:DATA-SRC-OFFICIAL-ANNOUNCEMENT-20260509]. The first level is the Coordinated Research Area, approximately 43.6 square kilometres per the announcement — bounded by the North Fifth Ring Road to the north, the Beijing–Tibet Expressway to the east, Xizhimenwai Street to the south, and Wanquanhe Road to the west — used for industry and future-city research without assigning specific land parcels. The second level is the Overall Design Area, approximately 11.4 square kilometres per the announcement, taking the urban districts within 1–2 kilometres of the Jing-Zhang Railway Heritage Park as the corridor; it carries this proposal's conceptual land-use layout, transport and walking-and-cycling systems, and blue-green systems, with a recalculated area of 11,412,825 sqm [metric:site_area_sqm]. The third level is the Key-Area Detailed Design Area, totalling 368.4 hectares per the announcement: from north to south, the Zhongzhiyuan AI Independent Innovation Acceleration Area, the Beijing AI Origin Community, and the Dazhongsi AI Industry Cluster, which carry concept schemes at detailed-design depth.

It must be stated that all three levels currently have only provisional rough polygons [data:geometry/site_boundary.geojson#PROV-SITE-001]. These boundaries were verified by the maintainer under EPSG:4548 against the announcement's textual extents and approximate areas; they constitute a provisional constraint and cannot serve as an official planning boundary, an approval basis, or a basis for precise area figures. Every derived layer in this proposal is locked inside this provisional boundary; once the official polygon is published, all layers — land use, green space, public space, building footprints, phasing — must be re-partitioned within the new boundary and the associated metrics recalculated. The scripted generation pipeline of this proposal supports such replacement.

In terms of depth, the Coordinated Research Area corresponds to strategic-research depth; the Overall Design Area corresponds to a conceptual expression of urban design at the regulatory detailed planning stage; and the key areas correspond to a conceptual expression at the Integrated Planning Implementation Plan depth [depth:three_level_scope_framework]. Across the three levels, the "heritage green corridor" serves as the common spatial thread that progressively focuses the work.

## Coordinated Research Area: Industry and Future City Research

At the Coordinated Research Area scale of 43.6 square kilometres, this proposal answers three questions: what the belt should be named, how the industrial system should be organized, and how urban form should adapt to AI-driven new-quality productive forces [standard:PROJECT-AGENT-OPEN-CALL-TASKBOOK].

**Naming and visual identity.** The proposed primary name is "京张·智轨长廊" (Jingzhang AI Rail Corridor). The naming logic: the Jing-Zhang railway was the first trunk railway independently designed and built by the Chinese; "智轨" (smart rail) is a homophone of "铁轨" (iron rail) and points to the dual meaning of intelligent rail and intelligent technology; "长廊" (corridor) accurately describes a 9 km-scale linear urban form. The suggested logo direction takes an isomorphic "sleeper–neuron" figure as the core symbol: the equidistant rhythm of railway sleepers is translated into connected neural-network nodes, supported by a dual palette of rail cyan and signal green. An open-source, commercially usable sans-serif typeface is recommended, avoiding any unauthorized fonts, trademarks, or corporate logos. This naming and identity is a conceptual recommendation only; the final brand must be refined and determined by the call organizers and professional teams.

**Three Zones and Two Wings coordination loop.** Zhongzhiyuan carries full-stack independent AI innovation and governance-discourse functions; the AI Origin Community carries world-class innovation-ecosystem and talent-living functions; Dazhongsi carries AI-native new-business-format and consumer-experience functions; the Zhongguancun Technology Services Wing injects capital, intellectual property, and professional services; and the Xiaoyue River Scenario Enablement Wing provides real urban test scenarios [source:SRC-2026-BJ-KW-THREE-AREAS-WINGS]. The coordination loop is: the services wing delivers factors to the three zones → the three zones complete validation and demonstration within the services wing and the Xiaoyue River wing → scenario data and reputation flow back, forming a closed "R&D–testing–showcase–consumption" loop.

**Global case references (background experience, not local facts).** This proposal studies six global AI and innovation-district cases: Station F in Paris demonstrates that large-span existing buildings can be converted at low cost into very-large-scale incubators; Kendall Square in Boston demonstrates that the "university–enterprise–public space" triangle is the foundation of innovation density; Singapore's one-north demonstrates the role of work–live–play mixed use in talent retention; London's Knowledge Quarter demonstrates that open institutional boundaries and public programmes amplify innovation spillovers; Montreal's Mila community demonstrates the anchor effect of basic-research institutions; and the termination of Toronto's Quayside warns that a deficit in data governance and public trust can veto any technical scheme. These lessons are converted into this proposal's mixed-use ratios, public-space network, scenario governance, and human-centred boundary design; their spatial placement is shown in [data:geometry/land_use.geojson#LU-1401-001].

**Future urban-form judgment.** What AI new-quality productive forces demand of the city is not more office towers but a "testable city": streets, parks, stations, and commerce can all become controlled real-world validation environments while preserving people's right to choose and human-review channels. This judgment directly determines the scenario-card design and the reserved-white-space land strategy that follow [depth:overall_spatial_structure].

## Overall Design Area: Urban Renewal and Regulatory-Plan-Level Urban Design

Within the 11.4-square-kilometre Overall Design Area, this proposal puts forward a conceptual spatial structure of "one axis, two belts, three zones, and multiple nodes", and organizes the mutual support among land use, transport, municipal infrastructure, and urban character in line with the requirements of regulatory-plan-level urban design [standard:MOHURD-CONTROL-DETAILED-PLANNING].

**One axis**: the Jing-Zhang heritage green corridor, a north–south park axis following the historic railway alignment, with a recalculated length of about 9.2 km [metric:heritage_axis_length_m], serving as the shared carrier of cultural narrative, walking-and-cycling commuting, and AI public space. **Two belts**: the mixed-function renewal belts flanking the heritage green corridor, and the Xiaoyue River waterfront blue-green belt. **Three zones**: the three key areas. **Multiple nodes**: six "AI public living room" plazas and three rail-interchange nodes [data:geometry/public_space.geojson#PS-001].

**Functional layout and land-use ratios.** In the conceptual land-use zoning, research land is about 230.6 hectares, education land about 210.0 hectares, commercial and business-services land about 126.9 hectares, residential land about 161.4 hectares, cultural land about 119.9 hectares, and park green space about 141.2 hectares; a further approximately 18.1 hectares of reserved white-space land provides flexible capacity for unforeseeable future functions [metric:land_use_0802_area_sqm]. Land classification adopts the national territorial-space land-and-sea use classification codes to facilitate alignment with formal planning [standard:MNR-LAND-USE-CLASSIFICATION-GUIDE].

**Urban renewal framework.** The renewal targets are inefficient existing spaces along the corridor, under a strategy of "retention first, renovation preferred, point-like new construction": structurally sound existing buildings are retained and converted into R&D and community functions; small-scale new construction is inserted at axis nodes; large-scale demolition and reconstruction is avoided. Development-intensity indicators such as total building volume, floor area ratio, and building height are all marked as pending official data because regulatory-plan conditions are missing; this proposal draws no intensity conclusions [depth:development_intensity_controls].

**Municipal infrastructure and carrying capacity.** Public baseline data are lacking for traditional municipal capacities — water supply, drainage, electricity, gas, heating — as well as for the layout of new infrastructure such as distributed energy and edge computing. This proposal offers only the conceptual direction of "reserving municipal corridors and integrating utility tunnels"; detailed calculations are to be completed by professional teams once official data are available.

## Detailed Design of Key Areas

The three key areas are developed into concept sub-schemes at the Integrated Planning Implementation Plan depth. Because all three polygons are provisional rough extents, the conclusions below are directional designs offered for refinement by professional teams [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-SCOPE-001].

**Zhongzhiyuan AI Independent Innovation Acceleration Area (192.1 hectares per the announcement; provisionally recalculated at about 192.9 hectares).** Positioning: the core carrier of the full-stack independent AI innovation system and the source of AI governance discourse. The spatial structure is "one core, two parks": an open research core along the heritage green corridor, flanked east and west by a pilot-scale testing park and a reserved flexible park; research land predominates, with one reserved white-space parcel to accommodate the rapidly evolving spatial needs of large models and embodied intelligence [metric:key_area_zhongzhiyuan_sqm]. Building renewal is primarily retention and renovation, with openable ground-floor laboratory fronts suggested along the corridor interface. For transport, walking-and-cycling access is organized via the rail-connection passage to the north. AI scenarios focus on pilot-scale validation and governance sandboxes. Risk: the provisional boundary may deviate from the real district extent, so functional placement must be calibrated once the official polygon is available.

**Beijing AI Origin Community (104.3 hectares per the announcement; provisionally recalculated at about 104.3 hectares).** Positioning: the living and social foundation of a world-class AI innovation ecosystem, serving the Wudaokou university population and the open-source community. The spatial structure is "one street, one courtyard, one showground": Origin Street links the cultural node of the former Qinghuayuan Station site; the open-source courtyard hosts the developer community; and the AI Origin Showground hosts launches and performances [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-002]. Functions mix education, research, residential, community services, and cultural land, with talent apartments and shared laboratories recommended. Transport relies on the Wudaokou Station connection passage and strengthens cycling commuting. Risk: university and community property rights are complex, so all demolish–renovate–retain conclusions must wait for property-rights baseline data.

**Dazhongsi AI Industry Cluster (72.0 hectares per the announcement; provisionally recalculated at about 72.0 hectares).** Positioning: the showcase-and-consumption gateway for AI-native new business formats and a transit-integrated station-city node. The spatial structure is "one gate, one port": the Smart Rail Gate gateway plaza and the AI Consumer Experience Port, mixing commercial/business-services and cultural land with premieres, immersive experiences, and business services layered together [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-003]. Transport relies on Dazhongsi Station to form an integrated rail–commerce–park arrival experience. Risk: the property rights and renovation willingness of the existing large commercial facilities are unknown; the scheme serves only as conceptual guidance.

## AI Innovation Ecosystem, Personas, and AI+ Scenarios

**Talent and user personas (5 categories).** ① AI researchers and algorithm engineers: need a low-cost, close-knit environment for experimentation, exchange, and housing; ② start-up founders and independent developers: need flexible space, computing-power access, and exposure opportunities; ③ university students and young scholars: need open courses, competitions, and internship channels; ④ surrounding community residents (including the elderly): need everyday services that do not exclude them from technology, plus staffed service counters; ⑤ international developers and visitors: need recognizable landmarks, bilingual wayfinding, and short-stay amenities. The personas are distilled from the taskbook's requirements for a city that attracts talent [standard:PROJECT-AGENT-OPEN-CALL-TASKBOOK].

**AI scenario cards (10 cards, all conceptual recommendations).** Each card notes its spatial placement, target users, data boundaries, and envisaged operating entity:

1. **AI cultural guide** (full length of the heritage green corridor): multimodal narration of Jing-Zhang railway history using only public historical materials, collecting no personal data, with content reviewed by the park operator.
2. **Walking-and-cycling-friendly navigation** (greenways and cycle lanes): gap alerts and crowding alerts for pedestrians and cyclists, using anonymized counts with manual spot checks.
3. **Enterprise-service copilot** (Zhongzhiyuan and the Zhongguancun Technology Services Wing): policy matching, site selection, and administrative guidance, citing public policy texts with staffed service windows as fallback.
4. **Low-speed unmanned delivery testing** (designated blocks of the Origin Community): tests within registered time slots and road segments, with pedestrian safety first and remote human takeover.
5. **AI health-service navigation** (community service facilities): guidance to medical and eldercare facilities plus age-friendly assistance; no diagnostic conclusions are processed and staffed services remain.
6. **Public-safety operations assistance** (corridor and plazas): alerts for abnormal crowding and facility damage — event-level alerts only, no facial recognition, with human review before action.
7. **Open-source hackathon cloud platform** (Origin Showground): event registration, work attribution, and exhibition, with data owned by participants.
8. **AI consumer-experience concierge** (Dazhongsi Experience Port): product and exhibit recommendations with the algorithmic-recommendation nature disclosed and one-click opt-out.
9. **Heritage digital-twin sandbox** (former Qinghuayuan Station node): visualization of heritage evolution and collection of public feedback, with feedback reviewed before public display.
10. **Accessible-travel assistant** (rail-interchange nodes): routing and arrival assistance for people with visual, hearing, or mobility impairments, aligned with the direction of barrier-free environment development, with on-site staffed service retained [standard:BARRIER-FREE-ENVIRONMENT-LAW].

**Industrial testing and validation scenarios (3).** ① Zhongzhiyuan pilot-scale validation field: a controlled test zone for embodied intelligence and edge devices, with test plans reviewed case by case and boundaries publicly posted; ② green-corridor low-speed mobile-robot corridor: limited speed, limited time slots, limited segments; ③ Dazhongsi AI-native commerce test field: short-term pop-up validation of new consumption formats. All three are test concepts and do not constitute approved operations; every link involving personal information or public safety has human review and an exit mechanism [standard:GENERATIVE-AI-INTERIM-MEASURES].

**Scenario–space–operations mapping.** Scenario node placement is documented in the layer and metrics files; the correspondence between scenario cards and public spaces is recorded in structured form under the agent.3 entry of `compliance_matrix.json` [data:geometry/public_space.geojson#PS-003].

## Land Use, Building Scale, and Retain-Renovate-Demolish Strategy

The conceptual land-use layout takes the heritage green corridor as the public skeleton, organizing functions on both sides as "research in the north, mixed in the middle, consumption in the south": the northern Zhongzhiyuan segment is dominated by research land (about 230.6 hectares of research land belt-wide in total); the central Origin Community segment mixes education, residential, and research uses (education about 210.0 hectares, residential about 161.4 hectares); the southern Dazhongsi segment concentrates commerce and culture (commercial about 126.9 hectares, cultural about 119.9 hectares); road land is about 43.5 hectares; and reserved white-space land is about 18.1 hectares [metric:land_use_0802_area_sqm]. Adjacent parcel boundaries share coordinates at generation time — seamless and non-overlapping — and can be fully recalculated under EPSG:4548 [depth:land_use_layout].

Regarding building scale, this proposal provides only conceptual building-footprint suggestions: 607 suggested building footprints are generated within buildable parcels according to setback and spacing rules, totalling about 94.9 hectares, to express the order of magnitude of spatial supply and the direction of urban fabric [metric:building_footprint_area_sqm]. It must be emphasized that there are no public baseline data on the outlines, floor counts, ownership, or construction dates of existing buildings; therefore the retain/renovate/demolish classification can only be expressed through the principled logic of "structurally sound buildings presumed retained and renovated; point-like new construction at axis-and-belt nodes". Any parcel-specific demolish–renovate–retain conclusion must await existing-condition surveys and property-rights data [depth:retain_renovate_demolish].

Statutory control conditions — floor area ratio, building height, building coverage ratio, green ratio, setbacks — are all missing, and this proposal explicitly registers them as unknown in `metrics.json`, giving no numerical suggestions [metric:floor_area_ratio]. The spatial-supply strategy of "existing stock first, white space reserved" addresses industrial uncertainty: reserved white-space land and convertible existing stock together form a flexible supply pool for the next 10–20 years.

## Transport, Rail, Municipal Infrastructure, and Public Services

The conceptual structure of transport organization is "three east–west spines plus multiple north–south links, walking and cycling first": given that the existing arterial and secondary road pattern cannot be altered, the proposal adds conceptual alignments for seven east–west secondary connections and two north–south local streets, stitching together the blocks divided by the corridor [data:geometry/roads.geojson#RD-EW-01]. It must be stated that all road alignments are conceptual recommendations; they do not represent road planning red lines, let alone engineering schemes.

For rail interchange, the proposal identifies three connection nodes — Dazhongsi Station, Wudaokou Station, and the Qinghe direction to the north — and proposes conceptual designs integrating connection passages with station-front public space [metric:heritage_axis_length_m]. The walking and cycling network takes the 9.2 km heritage greenway as its main spine, overlaid with a parallel cycling commuter route, connecting the three key areas and the six plaza nodes; the list of network gaps must be verified after field surveys and traffic data become available.

Regarding municipal and new infrastructure: there are no public data on traditional municipal pipelines, capacities, or flood-control and drainage indicators, so this proposal only suggests the conceptual direction of reserving a comprehensive utility-tunnel corridor in conjunction with the green corridor; for new infrastructure it proposes a component-based concept of "edge-computing micro-stations + distributed photovoltaics + shared sensor poles", all marked as pending professional calculation [depth:municipal_new_infrastructure]. Public service facilities are checked for education, medical, eldercare, and cultural coverage against the 15-minute living-circle concept; facility baseline data await official public release.

## Blue-Green Network, Public Space, and Urban Character

The blue-green system takes the Jing-Zhang Railway Heritage Park vitality belt as its main spine and the Xiaoyue River waterfront green belt as its eastern wing, with conceptual green space totalling about 141.2 hectares, about 12.4% of the Overall Design Area [metric:green_ratio]. The six "AI public living room" plazas total about 13.5 hectares and, overlaid with the greenway and interchange nodes, form a continuous public realm [metric:public_space_ratio]. The activation strategy for the Jing-Zhang Railway Heritage Park is "a readable railway": retaining track imagery and historic elevations while inserting exhibitions, sports, and safe night lighting; specific heritage-protection measures must comply with cultural-relics protection requirements, and the protection scopes and control zones of the former Qinghuayuan Station and other protected sites will be verified once official GIS layers are available [depth:blue_green_public_space].

**AI pilgrimage landmarks and honour-display system (conceptual recommendations, no fewer than 3).** ① "Origin Field": an AI-origin memorial plaza anchored on the former Qinghuayuan Station site, telling the narrative of technological self-reliance from the Jing-Zhang railway to Chinese AI through ground inscriptions and a chronological timeline; ② "Smart Rail Gate": a gateway structure at Dazhongsi forming a recognizable arrival experience through the sleeper–neuron motif; ③ "Developer Walk of Fame": a series of honour walls along the Wudaokou–Origin Community segment, engraved with projects and individuals who have made genuine contributions to open source and the AI community (with authorization from the individuals or rights holders); ④ "Time Tower": a lookout and performance tower at the northern end of Zhongzhiyuan, serving as a ceremonial venue for annual events. All four landmarks are conceptual intentions only and do not constitute construction approval; all imagery, typefaces, and logos must be rights-cleared, and internet-celebrity-style or over-entertaining expression must be avoided [standard:MOHURD-URBAN-DESIGN-MEASURES].

**Public-space component library.** The proposal suggests a combinable family of "smart street furniture" components: shared sensor poles, modular service stations, movable exhibition pavilions, and bilingual wayfinding signs, facilitating phased implementation and low-cost iteration. For urban character, the base palette is "rail cyan + signal green + warm grey"; building interfaces emphasize open ground floors and the displayability of research facilities; height and massing controls will be determined once regulatory-plan conditions are available [depth:height_massing_character].

**Cultural narrative.** The narrative mainline is "from building railways independently to innovating independently": the technological self-reliance of the 1909 Jing-Zhang railway, Zhongguancun's Electronics Street of the 1980s, and original AI innovation since the 2010s are layered along the same corridor into a spatial narrative of "three waves of innovation", expressed through the wayfinding system, timeline paving, and the digital-twin sandbox — avoiding treating culture as mere decoration [depth:three_key_area_detailed_design].

## Renewal Projects, Implementation Policy, and Phasing

Conceptual renewal project list (all are suggested projects, not implementation commitments): ① heritage green corridor connection works (greenway + service stations + lighting); ② six AI public living room plazas; ③ integrated renovation of the Dazhongsi station front; ④ renewal of Wudaokou Origin Street; ⑤ conversion of the open-source courtyard and shared laboratories; ⑥ Zhongzhiyuan pilot-scale validation field; ⑦ restoration of the Xiaoyue River waterfront green belt; ⑧ deployment of the smart street-furniture components; ⑨ heritage digital-twin sandbox; ⑩ talent apartments and community-service gap-filling. Project locations correspond to the phasing layer and the land-use layer [data:geometry/phasing.geojson#PHASE-1].

Phasing suggestion: the near term (about 452.4 hectares) focuses on the Dazhongsi–Xizhimen segment and the southern section of the heritage park, prioritizing public-space projects that deliver quick results and have relatively simple ownership [metric:phase-1_area_sqm]; the medium term (about 494.3 hectares) advances the Origin Community segment and the Wudaokou node; the long term (about 194.6 hectares) completes the Zhongzhiyuan segment and activates the reserved white-space land. Implementation-policy suggestions include public-participation mechanisms, community consultation procedures, diversified operating entities, and a data-governance convention — all conceptual recommendations for study by government departments and professional teams.

**Global AI innovation event system and long-term operations (responding to task agent.6).** The suggested annual event system is "one festival, one competition, one forum, one standing programme": an annual "Jingzhang AI Festival", a global developer hackathon, an AI governance forum, and regular scenario open days; event branding follows the "Jingzhang AI Rail Corridor" visual system. Developer-community operations rely on the open-source courtyard, establishing a mechanism linking contribution records with the honour walls; scenario access operations follow an "apply–evaluate–publish–review" process; and the attraction-and-conversion path is "event participation → scenario testing → space occupancy → ecosystem co-building". All event and policy arrangements are ideas only and do not constitute confirmed government arrangements or funding commitments [standard:PROJECT-AGENT-OPEN-CALL-TASKBOOK].
