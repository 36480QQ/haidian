01 / Core Metaphor

织的隐喻

The Weaving Metaphor

总览地图

Site Overview

城市不是机器，而是一匹活的织物。京张铁路是贯穿历史的经线，创新要素是穿梭交织的纬线，而AI则是当代的提花机——可编程、可复核、可逆转的织造逻辑。

The city is not a machine, but a living fabric. The Jing-Zhang railway is the warp thread running through history, innovation elements are the interweaving weft, and AI is the contemporary Jacquard loom — programmable, reviewable, reversible weaving logic.

▭

经线The Warp

Jing / Longitudinal

京张遗址公园作为历史与公共空间主轴，纵贯统筹研究、总体设计与重点区域三个层次，是城市织物不可抽离的骨架。

The Jing-Zhang railway heritage park serves as the historical and public space main axis, spanning across research, overall design, and key areas — the indispensable backbone of the urban fabric.

▯

纬线The Weft

Wei / Transverse

跨路连接、慢行廊道、蓝绿系统与产业横向协作，将不同功能区、社区与站点编织在一起，形成可渗透的城市肌理。

Cross-road connections, slow mobility corridors, blue-green systems, and horizontal industry collaboration weave different functional zones, communities, and stations into a permeable urban fabric.

⚙

提花机The Jacquard

Tihua Ji / Programmable Loom

AI如同提花机的穿孔卡片：每一张场景卡决定一次纬线穿越的方式——可编程、可审计、可逆转，让城市织造具备可解释性。

AI is like the Jacquard loom's punch cards: each scenario card determines how a weft thread crosses — programmable, auditable, reversible, making urban weaving interpretable.

02 / Spatial Structure

一经三纬 · 密疏有致

One Warp, Three Wefts — Variable Weave Density

三处重点片区对应三种织造密度：疏织开放、密织紧凑、结构织有序，形成张弛有度的城市肌理。

Three key areas correspond to three weaving densities: open weave, dense weave, and structured weave, forming a rhythmic urban fabric.

众智园

Zhongzhiyuan

Open Weave Innovation Mesh
疏织Open Weave

花园型全栈自主创新街区。宽阔的绿色空间、低碳创新交往环境与开放测试场地，如同疏织般透气、灵活，允许未来生长。

A garden-type full-stack innovation district. Wide green spaces, low-carbon collaboration environments, and open test sites — breathable and flexible like open weave, allowing future growth.

AI原点社区

AI Origin Community

Dense Weave Talent Hub
密织Dense Weave

近校型成果转化与人才社区。校区、园区、街区高度缝合，开源协作空间、孵化器和人才服务密集交织，形成高活力创新密度。

A campus-adjacent talent community for technology transfer. Campus, park, and neighborhood seamlessly stitched together with open-source collaboration spaces, incubators, and talent services densely interwoven.

大钟寺

Dazhongsi

Structured Weave Enterprise Cluster
结构织Structured Weave

城市型智能经济与国际交往街区。以领军企业为经、智能体与内容消费为纬，在轨道站点周边形成有序而高效的产业聚落。

An urban intelligent economy and international exchange district. Leading enterprises as warp, AI agents and content consumption as weft, forming organized and efficient industry clusters around transit stations.

10 / Governance

提花治理协议

Jacquard Governance Protocol

如同提花机用穿孔卡片控制织造图案，我们用场景卡控制AI部署——每一张卡片可审查、可逆转、可替换。

Just as the Jacquard loom uses punch cards to control weaving patterns, we use scenario cards to control AI deployment — each card is reviewable, reversible, and replaceable.

从穿孔卡片到场景卡

From Punch Cards to Scenario Cards

1801年，Joseph Marie Jacquard 用穿孔卡片控制了织布的图案——每一张卡片决定一次纬线的穿越方式。这是可编程计算的起源。

In 1801, Joseph Marie Jacquard used punch cards to control weaving patterns — each card determined how a weft thread crossed. This was the origin of programmable computation.

↓

今天，我们用场景卡取代穿孔卡片：每张卡片定义一个AI场景的服务对象、空间位置、数据来源、隐私边界、人工复核机制和运营主体。

Today, we use scenario cards to replace punch cards: each card defines the target users, spatial location, data sources, privacy boundaries, human review mechanisms, and operating entity of an AI scenario.

↓

关键区别：提花机的卡片不可逆，但场景卡可以撤回。这是城市织物与工业织物的根本不同——城市是活的。

The key difference: Jacquard's cards were irreversible, but scenario cards can be withdrawn. This is the fundamental difference between urban fabric and industrial fabric — the city is alive.

↻ 可逆纬线 — 90天试验周期

↻ Reversible Weft — 90-Day Trial Cycle

每个AI场景部署后进入90天试验周期。期间持续监测公共价值实现情况与风险指标。若评估未通过，场景可撤回——如同抽回一根纬线，织物仍在，只是图案调整。这是"活的织物"治理的核心：城市可以拆织重来，而不必推倒重建。

After each AI scenario is deployed, it enters a 90-day trial cycle. During this period, public value delivery and risk indicators are continuously monitored. If the assessment fails, the scenario can be withdrawn — like pulling out a weft thread; the fabric remains, only the pattern adjusts. This is the core of "living fabric" governance: the city can unweave and reweave, without needing to demolish and rebuild.
