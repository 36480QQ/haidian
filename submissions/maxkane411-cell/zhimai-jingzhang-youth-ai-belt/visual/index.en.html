Multidisciplinary AI Innovation: AI×X Discipline Matrix

AI×X Discipline Matrix

AI innovation is not the innovation of computer science alone — each discipline empowers AI in two ways: X→AI supports faster, better AI development, and AI→X constitutes major directions and scenarios of AI application. Global benchmarks: MIT Media Lab (antidisciplinary), Stanford HAI (human-centered), AlphaFold (AI for Science), Ars Electronica (AI art co-creation).

Math & Physics: algorithm theory / new computing paradigms → physics simulation / new materialsNorth
Engineering: computing hardware / sensor networks → intelligent manufacturing / roboticsNorth
Life Sciences: brain science / cognitive science → AI drug discovery / BCI / synthetic biologySouth
Humanities: value alignment / AI ethics / linguistics → digital humanities / AI-restored Jingzhang archivesCenter
Art & Design: HCI / embodied intelligence → generative art / co-creation / digital curationCenter

Three nodes form three "discipline hearts": North = Heart of Science (MIT Media Lab-style collisions layered on Eindhoven R&D visibility); Center = Heart of Humanities (Stanford HAI + Ars Electronica, AI-restored archives in dialogue with the Young Engineers Memorial Corner); South = Heart of Life (Kendall Square biotech×AI block experience). Materialized as FM-09 installations, scenario cards SC-11 to SC-13, and AI×X events.
