02 · Design Criteria

No spatial action is built unless it answers three questions

Along the Jing-Zhang corridor, the city is no longer built once — it renews through continuous exchange.

Connect

Which specific rupture does it stitch — across the railway, inside and outside walls, between old and new communities?

Exchange

What resources does it make genuinely flow between which actors — space, equipment, materials, knowledge, data?

Grow

What interfaces does it leave behind, so the next phase can extend, adjust, or withdraw?

08 · Building System

Four building prototypes — no one-off sculptural gestures

A · Adaptive innovation hall

Adaptive reuse of existing factory halls; a central shared hall links labs, roadshows, exhibitions, and making; independent MEP modules adapt as teams change.

B · Courtyard R&D unit

Small-scale R&D buildings around shared courtyards; shared ground floors, divisible or joinable upper floors; bridges connect campuses, parks, and the spine.

C · Permeable public block

Multiple ground-floor entrances, shallow floorplates, high visibility; internal walking routes continue into surrounding communities — no closed enclaves.

D · Lightweight public station

Demountable structures, standardized interfaces, minimal foundations; deployed as pilots first, with location and function adjusted from usage data.

Clear structural grids · linear platforms and cross-track structures · legible old-and-new in brick, steel, timber, and reused components · identity comes from public activity and structural order, not one-off sculptural skins.

12 · Early Verification

Three falsifiable protocols — with continue, stop, and restore written in

P1 · Zhongzhiyuan

Ecological baseline & restoration plots

At least one full growing season of soil, habitat, and microclimate baselines; intervention and control plots side by side on the same site.

FALSIFIER If the intervention plot shows no meaningful improvement after a full cycle — publicly admit failure; the strategy leaves the toolkit.

P2 · Dazhongsi

AI community services & accessible public stations

Human and digital services run in parallel long-term; data minimization, human fallback, opt-out.

FALSIFIER If vulnerable-group satisfaction falls below the human-only baseline — admit the AI intervention fails and revert to human service.

P3 · Zhongzhiyuan workshop

Circular materials & demountable components

Reused and new components installed side by side and logged together; fire-safety and durability testing as a gate.

FALSIFIER If maintenance costs exceed the new-build control with no improvement path — scale back to display use; no rollout.

Threshold numbers stay blank until baseline studies are complete, then are confirmed and published by the review panel — no false precision. Auntie Wang doesn't need to know what a large model is; her waiting time decides whether this system continues to exist.

14 · Operations Loop

AI inside the baseline–service–operations–evaluation loop

Environmental & usage baseline→ Problem identification→ Small-scale spatial intervention→ Real-scenario operation→ Public feedback & data evaluation→ continue / adjust / exit

AI's role is limited to assisting identification, prediction, matching, and evaluation; major public decisions remain explainable and human-accountable.

Data boundaries: collection tied to explicit public purposes; anonymized aggregate data preferred; algorithmic scores never replace eligibility judgments for public services.

Before a scenario goes live: responsible party, retention period, appeal and exit mechanisms are defined; the observatory publishes indicator definitions, data timestamps, and uncertainty.

17 · Boundary Statement

What we commit not to do

Never substitute robot counts, screen counts, or algorithm names for public value;

No claims of ecological improvement without baselines; no claims of pollution remediation without testing;

New bio-based materials are tested first and enter only tested non-structural scenarios;

All scopes, metrics, and phasing are labeled conceptual — none masquerade as statutory conclusions.
