Jing-Zhang People's AI City

AI Advances, People First. People are the owners of the city. AI is a tool people use and can control.

One Centennial Line, Three Waves of Collective Creation

Centennial Jing-Zhang—people create history; Zhongguancun—people create technology; the AI era—technology returns to the people. The Jing-Zhang Railway Heritage Park is first a civic public spine and only then an AI demonstration and testing space.

Civic AI Charter

Five rights: notice, choice, refusal, human review, appeal and correction.

One duty: public authorities, operators and technology providers jointly carry explanation, maintenance, correction, shutdown and exit responsibility.

One bottom line: AI must remain under meaningful, actionable human control.

Two Red Anchors, Three Landmarks

The Civic Engineering Hall and People's AI Service Station are the two red civic anchor types. The Civic Engineering Hall, Open-Source Origin Square and Civic Intelligence Arcade are the three AI pilgrimage landmarks. Red architecture grows from railway-industry, public-building and documented red/industrial/community culture rather than saturated lighting or giant slogans.

Twelve Scenarios, Nine Acceptance Groups

All 12 AI scenarios retain a human or non-AI equivalent. Older people, disabled users, service workers, children and caregivers, and people without smartphones/accounts or who actively decline AI are included in acceptance testing. The proposal target for final automated consequential public decisions is zero.
