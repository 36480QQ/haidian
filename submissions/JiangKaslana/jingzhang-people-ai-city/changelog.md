## v0.1 — 2026-08-12

- Established the core proposition **“AI向前，人民在先 / AI Advances, People First.”**
- Defined the proposal as a **people-governed civic AI framework**, not an AI-themed technology district.
- Added a three-part historical narrative: Jing-Zhang railway engineering → Zhongguancun innovation → AI returning to everyday public life.
- Added the **Five Civic AI Rights**: notice, choice, refusal, human review, and appeal/correction.
- Added the design bottom line that public AI must remain under meaningful human control, translated into human handoff, stop, fallback, responsibility and appeal mechanisms.
- Added two red public anchors: **Civic Engineering Hall** and **People's AI Service Station**, with red/industrial material language rooted in railway, labor and community culture rather than decorative slogan architecture.
- Added 12 preliminary AI scenarios, 3 flagship validation directions and 9 acceptance user groups, including service workers and people without smartphones/accounts or who decline AI.
- Added the **ordinary-service-first** pilot sequence: BASELINE → AI PILOT → HUMAN REVIEW → PUBLIC RECEIPT → SCALE / REVISE / STOP.
- Registered initial official/public sources and claim-boundary assumptions.
