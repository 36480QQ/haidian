GROUND STATE JINGZHANG: Two public interfaces and evidence-gated transverse stitches repair east–west fragmentation, forming a civic interface network hosted by three distinct urban rooms, subject to human veto and physical return to a permanent public base.

Ground State Jingzhang connects a century of railway memory, Zhongguancun's tradition of bringing knowledge into public life, and an auditable civic record for the AI era. Verified railway facts become permanent markers, open knowledge becomes courses and joint inquiry, and AI recommendations and human decisions become a traceable contemporary record. Spatially, an Evidence-Gated Civic Stitch Ladder pairs the Jing-Zhang public-space sequence with the Xiaoyue blue-green system, while confirmed system relations, candidate audit stitches and explicit gaps organise east-west access. Distributed urban rooms host knowledge services, public testing and arrival. Within each room, fixed routes, seating, shade, wayfinding, historical interpretation and staffed service form a Permanent Public Base. Three Reversible Interfaces—EDGE-HOUSE, TERRACED FORECOURT and LINEAR SIDE-BAY—open only in an approved state. AI cannot authorise deployment. People retain authority to approve, veto, freeze, hear appeals and retire the system. If AI is unavailable, the public base and manual procedures continue.

In quarter three, local, community and maintenance roles observe Dazhongsi services, extreme-weather response and AI-off drills. In quarter four, independent public review weighs burden, maintenance, failure and public benefit, deciding replication, revision or stop.Spatial data Spatial data

The talent path is open class—mentor—real problem—public result. The enterprise path is eligibility—small test—evidence—neutral referral. The resident path is problem—joint test—appeal—revision.
