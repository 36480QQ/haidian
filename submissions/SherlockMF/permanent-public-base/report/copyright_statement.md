# Rights and Generated-Content Statement

All proposal text, diagrams, geometry formalisation, and offline HTML in this package are participant-authored with Codex assistance, or are derived from the authorised frozen design corpus. Required figures and drawings are exported from that frozen corpus and remain conceptual design diagrams.

Generated or diagrammatic assets are not presented as site photographs, resident evidence, official planning drawings, measured geometry, or construction documents. One third-party photograph appears on A3 p.16: *QINGHUAYUAN STATION OLD HOUSE 202310* by 李汉宗（Lhzss8）, retrieved from Wikimedia Commons and reused under CC BY-SA 4.0. Its on-page attribution, local licence note and `F1-SRC-019-QINGHUAYUAN-2023-CC` record preserve the licence and limit it to a dated 2023 visual record; it is not presented as 2026 conditions, controls, tenure or approval. No peer-proposal media is included. Public official sources and provisional boundary material are listed in `sources.json`; their limits remain visible in `assumptions.json` and the proposal.

The bilingual `grounded-hero-visuals` figures retain their original 2000 × 1125 px geometry and participant-authored content. For package delivery they are deterministically encoded as 256-colour adaptive-palette PNGs with Pillow median-cut quantisation, Floyd–Steinberg dithering and maximum PNG compression; this storage transformation does not change their authorship, evidentiary status or conceptual / not-existing / not-approved boundary.

The package embeds an offline glyph subset of **Noto Sans SC** (Copyright Google LLC, SIL Open Font License 1.1) in `visual/assets/cjk-font.css`. The subset is generated from the official Google Fonts source and covers the visible character set of both report HTML files and both visual HTML files; all four pages load it locally, with no remote font request or reviewer-machine font dependency. It has no remote scripts, map tiles, analytics, tracking, or external APIs. Participant-authored work is limited to community display under the proposal license; that term does not override the font's OFL 1.1 terms or the photograph's CC BY-SA 4.0 licence. Statutory, engineering, heritage, data-governance, operator, and construction decisions require the stated official and professional review.
