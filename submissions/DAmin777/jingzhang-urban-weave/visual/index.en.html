Centennial Jing-Zhang AI Innovation Belt

Jing-Zhang Urban Weave

An offline English companion to the proposal. The concept turns the railway memory spine into a public innovation network that stitches campuses, parks, industry, communities, and everyday services through three stations and two wings.

Provisional intake package
Current geometry is provisional and is for concept development, display, discussion, and self-check only. It is not an official redline, approval basis, heritage boundary, regulatory plan, or engineering conclusion.

How to read the package

Structured GeoJSON, metrics, matrices, and self-check are authoritative for this submission package; images and PDFs are display layers.

Boundary status: official_boundary=false. Replace the site and three key-area polygons when official geometry, coordinate reference, version, publication date, and responsible organization are available, then recalculate all linked layers and metrics.

Five evidence figures

The figures use the same provisional geometry and disclose sources, scale, north, residuals, and the limits of interpretation.

Three stations and two wings

The spatial structure is a connector, not a new statutory boundary.

Zhongzhiyuan Autonomous Innovation Station

North: research, standards, safety, and a garden-like innovation interface connected to Qinghe.

AI Origin Co-creation Station

Center: campus, community, and talent co-creation through learning, prototypes, public display, and feedback.

Dazhongsi Application-Transfer Station

South: agents, intelligent terminals, professional services, and public observation of urban applications.

Zhongguancun technology-service wing

Research, entrepreneurship, legal, compute, and professional-service interfaces.

Xiaoyuehe everyday-scenario wing

Everyday life, education, health, exercise, and community services along the blue-green edge.

AI scenarios and public value

Ten scenario cards connect space, users, operations, public data, human review, observable outputs, and exit conditions.

Culture and public service tests

Evidence and governance

Public or cleared sources only; AI remains subordinate to human review.

Every scenario must disclose its data boundary, source, version, operator, human reviewer, public explanation, complaint route, pause condition, and exit mode. The proposal excludes facial recognition, sensitive-attribute inference, automated enforcement, and substitution for medical, legal, planning, heritage, transport, or engineering judgment.
