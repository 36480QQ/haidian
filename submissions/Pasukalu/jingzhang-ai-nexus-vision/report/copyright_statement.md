## AI-generated concept images

The following raster images were generated specifically for this submission with OpenAI image generation on 2026-08-23:

- assets/media/zhongzhiyuan-concept.webp — a human-scale garden research district beside a reused rail green spine, with inclusive users and visible low-carbon/AI research activity.
- assets/media/ai-origin-concept.webp — adaptive reuse of a brick railway hall as an open-source community commons, with family, older, and disabled users on a step-free route.
- assets/media/dazhongsi-concept.webp — a transit-oriented mixed-use public realm with inclusive crossings, native commerce, intelligent-terminal activity, and no dominant car traffic.

The generation prompts requested contemporary Beijing urban-design realism, diverse everyday users, accessibility, no text, no logo, no watermark, and no science-fiction spectacle. The images do not depict identifiable real people and do not reuse third-party photographs, brands, or logos. They are labelled throughout as conceptual renderings and must not be read as existing conditions, survey evidence, a final design, or a built-result promise.

assets/media/cover.webp is a project-local derivative triptych composed from those three generated images and original brand graphics.
