S01CIVIC SCENARIO

Open-Source Release Hall

Space: BLDG-006 + PUBLIC-002; divisible demo hall, quiet reproduction room, staffed help desk.

Acceptance: ≥80% independently reproduced; 100% caption/transcript and step-free seating; zero serious safety or copyright events.

Human, fallback, and stop

Release owner signs off; the host corrects errors; staffed registration remains.

Printed schedule, staffed registration, offline materials, and no-registration seating.

Pause on unauthorised data/code, two consecutive reproduction failures, or unresolved safety issues.

S02INDUSTRY TEST

Model-Safety Governance Sandbox

Space: BLDG-004; isolated test network, public observation gallery, incident-review room.

Acceptance: 100% six-gate records; 100% closure of high-risk defects; 100% publication of findings and limits.

Human, fallback, and stop

Two-person release, civic observers, and independent expert sign-off.

A failed model remains offline and never enters a public service.

Stop when provenance is unclear, high-risk defects remain, or testing crosses isolation boundaries.

S03INDUSTRY TEST

Low-Carbon Edge Compute Bench

Space: BLDG-003 + GREEN-001; replaceable racks, heat-recovery exhibit, public aggregate-energy board.

Acceptance: ≥10% lower energy per task at equal quality/throughput; aggregate-only display; 100% physical-disconnect drills.

Human, fallback, and stop

Energy engineers confirm boundaries; operations staff can physically disconnect.

Return to static scheduling and local device control.

Stop on excess temperature, repeated forecast error, or unauthorised exfiltration.

S04CIVIC SCENARIO

Space: ROAD-001/005/006 + GREEN-001; tactile guides, rest points, staffed information.

Acceptance: Blockage verified within a four-hour operating target; 100% physical-wayfinding sync; ≥90% completion in diverse user tests.

Human, fallback, and stop

Maintenance staff verify blockages; representative users conduct monthly walks.

High-contrast maps, tactile maps, staffed help, and printable accessible routes.

Stop after two inaccessible recommendations or an unclosed major hazard.

Delivery + Operations

S/M/L are conceptual resource bands, not budgets; all six projects have leads, dependencies, gates, pilots, baselines, acceptance, and fallback.

R01BAND L

Public Track Pilot

Conceptual lead: public-space delivery and operations platform

At least 16 weeks, including an extreme-weather window or extension. · Phase opening; fence hazards; use human guidance; never replace site safety with navigation.
R02BAND M

Zhongzhiyuan Validation Garden

Conceptual lead: park operations platform

16–24 weeks for model and compute tests. · Remain offline with no public connection; equipment can be physically disconnected.
R03BAND M

AI Origin Release + Civic Station

Conceptual lead: joint civic-service and innovation-space operator

12 weeks, limited to three low-risk services and one weekly release. · Stop the assistant while retaining staffed service and ordinary activity space.
R04BAND L

Dazhongsi Four-Quadrant Street

Conceptual lead: station-area renewal coordination platform

16 weeks of temporary wayfinding and street operation; works require separate approval. · Start with reversible signs, temporary street rights, and shopfront micro-renewal.
R05BAND S

Scenario Passport + Data Desk

Conceptual lead: independent scenario-governance secretariat

Runs with the first three scenarios and quarterly review. · No pass means no opening; return to staffed offline service.
R06BAND M

Three Landmarks + Global Trail

Conceptual lead: cultural and international operations alliance

Operate detachable prototypes for one full event quarter. · Use a low-tech physical timeline and human interpretation.
