Following a working dad's daily life, this offline page presents the Living-Experience Belt design. All figures are local static assets.

Overview

Site overview based on the provisional Jing-Zhang boundary, linking the Qinghe waterfront and three key areas.

Three-level scope

Coordinated research area, overall design area, and detailed key-area design.

Key areas

Zhongzhiyuan AI Acceleration Park, Beijing AI Origin Community, Dazhongsi AI Cluster.

Land use

Residential, commercial, green and public-service led; six vertical functional bands fully cover the site.

Mobility

Waterfront greenway and community slow networks connect Qinghe Station and Shangdi (Line 13).

Blue-green

Proposed green space ~2.656M m² (ratio 23.3%); public space ~0.854M m² (ratio 7.5%).

Buildings

Low-density, walkable AI R&D and community-service building footprints.

Renewal

Phased renewal list delivered in three stages with verifiable public-space milestones.

AI scenes

AI+ scenarios: cultural guide, health navigation, mobility, enterprise copilot, robot delivery.
