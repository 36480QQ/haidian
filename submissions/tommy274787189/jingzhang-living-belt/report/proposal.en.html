Coordinated Research Area: Industry and Future City Research

Within the coordinated research area, we shift the question from "how many AI towers to build" to "how AI serves working families" [source:SRC-BEIJING-UA-PLAN]. The Jing-Zhang railway was the first railway independently designed by Chinese engineers; its spirit of self-reliance resonates across a century with today's quest for AI self-reliance [source:SRC-JINGZHANG-HERITAGE]. We propose positioning the innovation belt's industry on "affordable AI life infrastructure": community-level AI services, inclusive compute, and AI-literacy spaces for ordinary families, rather than another cluster of aloof headquarters. Future-city research therefore takes the "people–algorithm–daily-life" relationship as its axis, not GDP.

Overall Design Area: Urban Renewal and Regulatory-Plan-Depth Urban Design

Within the overall design area, we adopt regulatory-plan-depth land-use layout and intensity zoning, dividing the roughly 11.4 km² into six north–south functional bands: residence, mixed services, R&D, blue-green, public services, and industry from west to east [depth:overall_spatial_structure][data:geometry/land_use.geojson]. Renewal targets inefficient stock land and areas around rail stations, avoiding large-scale demolition. Intensity control raises FAR modestly within 800 m of rail while preserving a relaxed community grain outside, ensuring the basic living density that "kids can play safely downstairs and dads can walk to the metro" [depth:development_intensity_controls].

Each area includes a "Dad Station" — a community living room for charging, childcare drop-in, and temporary work — turning commute gaps into life time.

AI Innovation Ecosystem, Personas, and AI+ Scenarios

We profile the belt's core talent: "working dads/moms" aged 30–45, tight on commute, busy on parenting, dependent yet wary of AI [depth:existing_conditions_diagnosis]. From this we design four AI+ scenarios: an AI cultural guide threading the Jing-Zhang heritage; an AI traffic-and-walkability assistant easing school-run anxiety; an enterprise-service copilot lowering the startup barrier; and a public-safety operations review safeguarding courtyards [source:SRC-AGENT-OPEN-CALL-TASKBOOK]. AI is not a tech showpiece but a "life assistant" that frees ordinary people from repetitive labor.

Land Use, Building Scale, and Retain-Renovate-Demolish Strategy

The land-use scheme uses six functional bands as its skeleton, prioritizing residence and public services, with R&D and industry arranged along rail [depth:land_use_layout]. The retain-renovate-demolish strategy is predominantly "retain": keep existing residential communities and rail facilities, convert inefficient factories into AI community centers, and rebuild only modestly around stations [depth:retain_renovate_demolish]. In building scale, the proposal generates about 854,000 m² of design-proposal building footprint within the provisional boundary as a starting value for later regulatory verification [metric:building_footprint_area_sqm][metric:site_area_sqm].

Transport, Rail, Municipal Infrastructure, and Public Services

The core of the transport strategy is "let e-bikes and kids both move easily." We lay a continuous slow-mobility and AI-service corridor along the Qinghe–Shangdi line, turning the dad's school-run e-bike route from contested lanes into dedicated ones [depth:traffic_rail_slow_parking][data:geometry/roads.geojson]. For rail, we optimize connections at Qinghe Station and the Line 13 Shangdi Station to shorten the "last kilometer" [source:SRC-PUBLIC-TRANSIT]. Municipally, we reserve community-level compute and low-speed delivery nodes, and fill childcare, sports, and community services within a 5–10 minute living circle.
