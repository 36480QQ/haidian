# THE EMERGENT BELT — Jing-Zhang Hyper Line

## Design Basis and Source List

The name of this proposal is its methodology: **THE EMERGENT BELT** comes from the emergence principle of urban science — bottom-up, self-organizing, fractal; **Hyper** comes from the scientific fact of superlinear scaling (β>1) agglomeration returns — AI innovation is one of the few inherently superlinear variables in cities. The design basis has three layers: the task layer cites the official prequalification announcement and the agent-facing task book [source:DATA-SRC-OFFICIAL-ANNOUNCEMENT-20260509] [source:DATA-SRC-AGENT-TASKBOOK-20260518]; the fact layer cites the Haidian open-call release of March 2026 and the July intelligent-economy press conference (the AI Origin Community of about 3 km², over 1,000 AI scientists, over 13,000 developers, Zhizhi FlagOS adapted to 32 chips from 18 vendors, etc.) [source:DATA-SRC-HAIDIAN-GOV-20260330] [source:DATA-SRC-HAIDIAN-PRESS-20260729]; the data layer is the locally fetched Amap POI set (12 categories, 25,476 points) and OSM existing roads, rail, green, water and stations [source:DATA-SRC-AMAP-POI-20260815] [source:DATA-SRC-OSM-20260815]. Boundary data currently only exists as repository provisional polygons; the whole package will be recalculated once official boundaries are released — every spatial conclusion is worded as a conceptual recommendation, and statutory control indicators are marked "pending official data" [data:geometry/site_boundary.geojson#SITE-001].

## Three-Level Scope Framework

The coordinated research area is about 43.6 km², the overall design area about 11.4 km², and the key areas about 368.4 ha (Zhongzhiyuan 192.1, Beijing AI Origin Community 104.3, Dazhongsi 72.0 ha); tasks cascade top-down through the three levels while evidence flows bottom-up [metric:site_area_sqm]. The proposal works within the overall design area, taking the heritage-park spine as the datum from the North 5th Ring Road to Xizhimenwai Street; the three key areas take on the five functions as the Stack, Origin and Front segments. The provisional repository boundary recalculates to 11,412,800 m² in EPSG:4548 (0.11% deviation from the announced ≈11,400,000 m²) and is used for generation, display and self-check only, never as an official redline or precise-area basis; once official polygons are released, the site boundary, key areas, land-use partition, all metrics and figures will be recalculated as a whole [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#KEY-001] [depth:three_level_scope_framework].

## Coordinated Research Area: Industry and Future City Research

**Core proposition: physical adjacency does not equal functional connectivity — the belt is currently a broken chain.** We calibrated the three-areas-two-wings coupling λ with the Wilson spatial interaction model (λ_ij=exp(-d_ij/2500m), the 2,500 m of the official "ten-minute innovation circle"), against the phase-transition critical value λc≈0.42: only 2 of the 5 core nodes are in the supercritical component — Beijing AI Origin Community × Xiaoyuehe Scenario Wing crosses the threshold (λ=0.455), Zhongzhiyuan × Origin λ=0.205, Origin × Zhongguancun Wing λ=0.374 (close but below), Zhongzhiyuan × Dazhongsi λ=0.015 (deeply subcritical); the Zhongguancun Wing, Zhongzhiyuan and Dazhongsi are each isolated [metric:wilson_supercritical_net_pct] [metric:core5_giant_span_ratio]. This repeats the proposition of our earlier Yangtze-Delta four-city and Dianshan Lake studies: the binding constraint is not physical but institutional and interfacial — here it is the seven arterial severances of the spine plus enclosed university and ownership interfaces. Industrially, the belt should follow the superlinear logic toward agglomeration and acceleration rather than spreading thin: Zhongzhiyuan hosts the full-stack autonomy (FlagOS ecosystem), the Origin Community hosts origination and transfer (nearly 180 OPC one-person companies, about 60 landed Tsinghua-desk enterprises), Dazhongsi hosts AI-native consumption (the 1733 project and the Lanjinglijia parcel renewal), and the two wings supply capital and scenarios [source:DATA-SRC-HAIDIAN-PRESS-20260729] [source:DATA-SRC-BAIDU-NEWS-DAZHONGSI]. On the future city form: an AI city is not an old city with sensors attached, but a computable city running dual clocks — POI/signaling are the second-to-day high-frequency pulse, boundaries and regulatory plans the year-to-decade low-frequency skeleton; planning creates the conditions for emergence instead of designing its outcome [standard:PROJECT-AGENT-OPEN-CALL-TASKBOOK] [depth:industry_future_city_research].

The naming system and logo direction (agent.1) are finalized: the belt is "THE EMERGENT BELT", the main line is the "JINGZHANG HYPER LINE", the three segments are "HYPER STACK / HYPER ORIGIN / HYPER FRONT", the two wings are the "CAPITAL WING" and the "SCENARIO WING", and the seven stitched crossings are named "HYPER NODES". The logo direction is "the superlinear line": a straight railway line bending upward at its end (a β>1 superlinear curve), with a seven-node percolation cluster floating above it — the century-old line begins to rise. All naming is a conceptual proposal subject to trademark and font clearance before formal use (assumption A-NAMING-001).

## Overall Design Area: Urban Renewal and Regulatory-Plan-Level Urban Design

The overall structure is "**one line, three folds, two wings, seven nodes**": the line is the Jing-Zhang Hyper Line spine (the heritage-park corridor), the three folds are the three key areas, the two wings are the Zhongguancun capital wing and the Xiaoyuehe scenario wing, and the seven nodes are the seven stitched crossings. The land-use layout is generated bottom-up from a 400 m grid partition plus Amap POI existing-character recognition: **blocks without intervention keep their POI-dominant existing character, while station catchments and corridor edges receive renewal intervention** — the practical form of "network first, room for self-organization" [depth:overall_spatial_structure] [data:geometry/land_use.geojson#LU-001]. The partition yields: research land ≈31.5%, commercial ≈25.6%, education/research ≈18.2%, residential ≈16.5%, green ≈8.2% (plus a POI-identified medical cell) — the employment-dominant character matches the Amap POI work-life ratio, and the **jobs-housing imbalance (only 16.5% residential) is an explicit renewal target**; renewal projects will first add jobs-housing balance and talent apartments within 900 m of stations [metric:land_use_area_0701_sqm] [metric:land_use_area_0802_sqm]. On development intensity: statutory FAR, height, density and setback conditions are unpublished, so this package writes "pending official data" for all of them and gives only conceptual massing (782 blocks, 17.4% footprint density, low confidence) for spatial discussion, never as statutory conclusions [metric:building_density] [depth:development_intensity_controls].

## Detailed Design of Key Areas

**Zhongzhiyuan · HYPER STACK (≈192.1 ha)** — "the full-stack autonomy district, from chips to models". Spatial strategy: a "full-stack test band" along the Qinghe–Xuezhiyuan interface (a district-scale open test environment for FlagOS multi-chip adaptation, conceptual), a garden district retaining the POI-observed enterprise character while filling the weakest tourism/education/finance shares (Zhongzhiyuan POI check-up: scenic 0.4%, school 0.7%, finance 1.3%, the lowest of the three areas) [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#KEY-001] [depth:three_key_area_detailed_design].

**Beijing AI Origin Community · HYPER ORIGIN (≈104.3 ha)** — "the global AI origination and transfer interface". Anchored on the Yuandian Tower and the official ten-minute innovation circle; the POI check-up shows medical density of only 2.88 per km² (the lowest of the three), so the proposal adds medical, community service and talent apartments along the Wudaokou–Xueyuanlu interface, and places the "Origin Plaza + Developer Name Wall" honor node around the Tsinghuayuan Station heritage site. Note: the provisional rectangle of this segment is offset from the real Wudaokou core (west of the rectangle); all conclusions will be recalculated when official polygons are released [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#KEY-002].

**Dazhongsi · HYPER FRONT (≈72.0 ha)** — "the AI-native consumption and content interface". The POI check-up shows Dazhongsi is the most balanced of the three (work-life ratio 0.923, diversity 0.922, medical density 31.92 per km²); the strategy is "light intervention, strong interface": four-quadrant pedestrian stitching at Dazhongsi Station, AI-native retail guidance for the 1733 and Lanjinglijia renewal parcels, and a cultural pairing between the Ancient Bell Museum and an "Emergence Bell" — all conceptual [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#KEY-003].

## AI Innovation Ecosystem, Personas, and AI+ Scenarios

**Global cases (7, with transferable mechanisms)**: London King's Cross (railway-heritage renewal + Google DeepMind agglomeration — the most isomorphic global precedent), Boston Kendall Square (MIT near-campus ecosystem), Palo Alto Stanford Research Park (university origination), Shenzhen Yuehai Street (high-density startup blocks), Tokyo Shibuya (content-consumption interface), the Cambridge Cluster (university-town industrial belt), and Tel Aviv Rothschild Boulevard (startup boulevard). The shared lesson: railway, university and old-street constraints are causes of innovation agglomeration rather than obstacles — the methodological stance of the Emergent Belt [source:DATA-SRC-CASA-KB-KUN] [depth:global_ecosystem_cases].

**Five personas**: ①OPC one-person-company founders (age 25–30, needing affordable desks and order-taking scenarios); ②AI scientists and engineers (needing full-stack test environments and interaction density); ③university AI students (over 100,000, needing third places and night schools); ④long-time residents and retired seniors along the line (needing human-service channels and accessibility — following the Article-39 listed scenarios of the barrier-free law and the parallel-channel approach of the elderly policy); ⑤global AI pilgrims (needing an experienceable route, an honor wall and an event calendar) [standard:BARRIER-FREE-ENVIRONMENT-LAW] [standard:ELDERLY-SMART-TECH-PLAN-2020-45].

## Land Use, Building Scale, and Retain-Renovate-Demolish Strategy

The land-use structure is the POI-driven plus renewal-intervention conceptual partition described above. On buildings: 782 conceptual blocks express the spatial supply logic (dense R&D bases in the Stack segment, mixed commercial in the Front segment, talent apartments at station catchments), footprint density 17.4%, all labeled "conceptual massing, not an architectural scheme". Because existing-building inventory, ownership and heritage ranges are unavailable, the retain-renovate-demolish classification is **uniformly pending confirmation**; only three directional principles are given — heritage-protected and built park segments stay (retain), inefficient space on both sides of Jing-Zhang prioritizes functional replacement (renovate), and station catchments plus stitch nodes get limited increment (new-build, conceptual); parcel-level conclusions await official surveys and ownership data for professional teams to deepen [depth:retain_renovate_demolish] [metric:building_footprint_area_sqm]. The task-book boundary clause is fully respected: no statutory FAR, height, intensity or parcel-level demolition conclusions are given.

## Transport, Rail, Municipal Infrastructure, and Public Services

The transport strategy comes directly from computation: the seven gaps (Qinghuadonglu, Chengfulu/Wudaokou, North 4th Ring West Road, Zhichunlu, North 3rd Ring, Xueyuannanlu, Gaoliangqiao Xiejie) sever the spine's slow-mobility system; the proposal organizes footpaths, cycleways and grade-separated/at-grade concepts around "seven-node stitching" — the North 4th Ring and North 3rd Ring nodes are exactly the "overpass the ring roads" areas named in the announcement [metric:corridor_crossing_count] [data:geometry/roads.geojson#ROAD-001]. Space-syntax verification: after the spine is connected, anchor integration rises sharply (Beijing North +132%, Dazhongsi +102%, Wudaokou +65%, Zhichunlu +61%) — stitching is not decoration but a phase-transition move that turns a broken chain into a connected network [depth:traffic_rail_slow_parking]. Rail integration: station-area concepts around the seven stations (Wudaokou, Zhichunlu, Tsinghua East Road West, Xueyuanqiao, Xuezhiyuan, Dazhongsi, Beijing North); static traffic gets bicycle parking at the seven nodes. Municipal and new infrastructure: distributed energy and edge-computing cabinets fused with the three traditional utilities are system-level suggestions only (pipeline, capacity and fire conditions await official data); public services fill the POI gap lists (Zhongzhiyuan: culture/education/finance; Origin: medical; Dazhongsi: research/education) — facility placement is driven by data gaps, not slogans [depth:municipal_new_infrastructure].

## Blue-Green Network, Public Space, and Urban Character

Blue-green check-up: the existing blue-green network's giant component covers 83.9% of patch area, already above the 2D percolation threshold of 59.27% — macro connectivity is healthy; the disease is the severed spine, not missing green. After seven-node stitching it rises to 86.5% [metric:green_space_area_sqm]. Public space is organized as "one line, seven nodes, three plazas": a 60 m conceptual green band on the spine, seven Hyper Node plazas, and the three pilgrimage plazas (about 202,000 m² of conceptual public-space polygons, 1.8% of the site — conceptual nodes, not engineered schemes) [metric:public_space_ratio]. The three pilgrimage landmarks: **Tsinghuayuan Station · ORIGIN** (the 1909 origin station site → AI origination landmark + Developer Name Wall), **Wudaokou · Phase-Transition Plaza** (a public instrument displaying the belt's live λ coupling — making "is the city connected?" a visible civic gauge), and **Dazhongsi · Interface Plaza** (Emergence Bell + AI-native consumption interface). Character control adopts the dual skyline-governance paradigms: a protective overlay for the heritage corridor and Tsinghuayuan Station (view-corridor and height-control concepts), and generative growth rules for the three key areas (height gradients stepping down to the park, low front / high back) — all conceptual guidance, with height values awaiting heritage and regulatory data [depth:bluegreen_public_space_character] [standard:MOHURD-URBAN-DESIGN-MEASURES].

## Renewal Projects, Implementation Policy, and Phasing

Renewal projects are organized in three groups — "seven-node stitching + three-area renewal + jobs-housing rebalancing" (a conceptual list, not ownership or timing conclusions): ①the seven-node spine stitching works (footpath/cycleway/grade-separation concepts); ②medical and talent-apartment infill in the Origin Community, culture/education/finance infill in Zhongzhiyuan, research/education infill in Dazhongsi (matching the POI gaps); ③jobs-housing rebalancing renewal at station catchments. Concept phasing is three periods: near term (1–3 years) spine stitching and the Dazhongsi interface, mid term (3–5 years) Origin Community renewal, long term (5–10 years) the Zhongzhiyuan garden district [data:geometry/phasing.geojson#PHASE-1] [depth:renewal_project_list]. Operations (agent.6): an annual "Jing-Zhang Hyper Line Developer Festival"; turning the C1 λ calibration into an annual public release ceremony ("Phase-Transition Forum" — connectivity is a vital sign of the city and should be published yearly like a health check); seven-node open testing seasons; an OPC order-taking contest; annual honor-wall inscription. All activities, investment, policy and funding statements are conceptual suggestions, not confirmed government arrangements [depth:annual_event_system_operation].
