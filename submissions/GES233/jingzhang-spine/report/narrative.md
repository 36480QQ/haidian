# 方案摘要 / Executive Summary

京张智脊（JingZhang AI Spine）以京张铁路百年廊道为脊，用一条 9.5 公里步骑复合绿道串联众智园、北京 AI 原点社区、大钟寺三处重点区，形成「一线串三区、两翼齐赋能」的空间骨架。方案在临时边界内完成拓扑安全的用地剖分（科研 37.1%、居住 24.3%、商业 12.4%），复算绿地率 14.2%、公共空间 2.1%，提出 27 栋概念新建体量（约 149 万 m²）、智脊九驿公共空间系统、三处 AI 朝圣地标、10 张 AI 场景卡（3 张测试验证）与南中北三期概念分期。全部空间建议为概念建议，边界为 provisional 约束，待官方数据补齐后整体重算。

JingZhang AI Spine runs a 9.5 km walking-cycling greenway along the century-old rail corridor, stitching Zhongzhiyuan, the Beijing AI Origin Community, and Dazhongsi into one spine linking three zones. Inside the provisional boundary it delivers a topology-safe land-use partition (research 37.1%, residential 24.3%, commercial 12.4%), a recomputed 14.2% green ratio and 2.1% public-space ratio, 27 concept buildings (~1.49 M sqm), nine public-space nodes, three AI pilgrimage landmarks, ten scenario cards (three testbeds), and a three-phase concept program. All spatial proposals are concept suggestions on a provisional boundary, to be recomputed when official data arrives.
