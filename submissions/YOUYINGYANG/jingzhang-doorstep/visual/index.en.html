The Jingzhang Doorstep — People's AI Belt

No grand narrative. AI Scenario Cards — 13 One-Line Summaries

Each card follows one format: problem → AI answer → spatial anchor → data & privacy boundary → human review → operator → effect metric → exit condition. All scenarios are conceptual; test-type scenarios require dual approval by the competent authority and the community council before piloting.

People's AI for the people
S01 Chronic-disease management (AI+health)
Community health records and pre-consultation triage; data stays at the community edge node; doctors sign prescriptions; anchored at PS-10.
S02 In-home guardianship for seniors (AI+eldercare)
Utility-anomaly and camera-free fall detection; raw data never leaves the home; one-tap opt-out; in residential parcels.
S03 Safe school routes (AI+education/safety)
Route-risk heatmaps and guardian-post scheduling; flow counting only, no facial recognition; anchored at PS-09.
S04 Accessible feeder service (AI+transport)
Dynamic accessible routing and low-speed shuttle booking; location data anonymised instantly; corridor-wide.
S05 Community AI public classes (AI+education)
University teachers and open-source courses enter communities; no personal data collected; teachers always present; PS-04.
S06 Legal-aid smart triage (AI+legal)
Structured case triage and document templates; licensed lawyers review every output; not a substitute for legal advice; PS-03.
S07 Old-compound repair response (AI+living services)
Smart work-order dispatch with public progress; minimal data; property managers approve dispatch; residential parcels.
S08 Community canteen smart catering (AI+food)
Demand forecasting and meal optimisation; order volumes only, never linked to health data; nutritionists review menus; PS-02.
S09 Flexible-employment skill matching (AI+jobs)
Skill graphs and job matching; anonymised CVs, user-confirmed applications, account deletion anytime; PS-08 and online.
S10 Smart complaint dispatch (AI+governance)
Semantic dispatch with public progress; case-owner accountability unchanged; errors fall back to manual handling; PS-07.
S11 Last-100-metre delivery robots (industry test 1)
Low-speed delivery robots in compounds; no household data; remote operator plus on-site safety officer; overspeed triggers remote stop.
S12 Home-service robot field test (industry test 2)
Supervised carrying and inspection services; written consent for home entry; stop-on-incident with liability insurance first.
S13 Mobile health-screening station (industry test 3)
Vehicle-mounted screening with AI pre-diagnosis tours; licensed physicians sign reports; health-authority approval first; PS-10.
Privacy principles: collect nothing avoidable, keep data in the home where possible, anonymise by default, and give every scenario human review and an exit switch.
