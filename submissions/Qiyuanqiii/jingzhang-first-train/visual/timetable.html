一张城市时刻表，解释“首班车”如何运行

An urban timetable for how “First Train” operates

这不是工程时刻表，而是方案的运营叙事：AI 从研究、测试、公开体验到规模化协作，每一“站”都要经过可解释、可退出、可人工复核的门槛。

This is not an engineering timetable. It is the operating narrative of the proposal: AI moves from research and testing to public experience and wider collaboration only through explainable, reversible and human-reviewable gates.

每张“车票”是什么？

What is each “ticket”?

场景目的、使用者、数据最小化、人工接管、失败状态、停止条件与反馈渠道的统一公开卡。

A public card covering purpose, users, data minimisation, human override, failure states, stop conditions and feedback.

为什么叫“首班车”？

Why “First Train”?

不是把 AI 贴到铁路符号上，而是借铁路的“线路—车站—时刻—调度”建立可运营、可复核的城市创新秩序。

The railway metaphor is operational: line, stations, timetable and dispatch become a reviewable urban innovation protocol rather than decoration.

什么时候可以“开出去”？

When can it “leave the station”?

只有在专业团队完成法定规划、工程、安全、隐私与运营审查后；本开源方案本身不授予任何审批或实施资格。

Only after statutory planning, engineering, safety, privacy and operational review by responsible professionals; this open proposal grants no approval.
