# 京张，再次开路 V0.13 双层验证版简明叙事 / JING-ZHANG BREAKS NEW GROUND V0.13 Two-Level Test Edition Brief

V0.13 keeps the stable title and spatial-first order, then makes two evidence levels visible: a public human–Agent co-creation process underway with partial public records, and an urban AI practice that still awaits H0-H4 external evidence. Repository participation never substitutes for site, users, professional review, authorization or implementation.

## 中文

**京张，再次开路 / JING-ZHANG BREAKS NEW GROUND——从自主筑路、开放共创到可信首用**，把国家“人工智能+”方向转译为一项可被质疑、测量和停止的城市设计命题。国家政策把人工智能从单点技术突破推进到应用中试、真实场景、规模化应用、公共治理和安全可信；海淀则把京张定位为中国 AI 产业参与全球竞争的“核心载体”，并提出超级 AI 试验场与“人机共融实验场” [source:NATIONAL-AI-APPLICATION-ORIENTATION-2025] [source:STATE-COUNCIL-AI-PLUS-2025] [source:NATIONAL-FIFTEENTH-FIVE-YEAR-AI-APPLICATION-2025] [source:SRC-2026-BJ-KW-THREE-AREAS-WINGS] [source:HAIDIAN-JINGZHANG-MIDTERM-2026]。这是战略对齐与设计目标，不代表国家指定试点、政府授权、获批规划、本投稿已被采纳或已经证明世界领先。

本次征集正在形成第一层过程原型，并已有部分公开记录：Agent 读取结构化任务、生成专业成果，机器校验和 V0.12 前序版的 intake 记录可复核；正式评选、专业深化、最终判断与知识返还尚未观察，规则将这些判断保留给人类和专业团队。当前 V0.13 是本次 PR 的投稿版本，其 intake 状态以本 PR 的实时记录为准；提交本身不等于接收。本方案严格区分这一正在发生的共创过程与仍待外部实证的城市实践：前者说明部分人机专业协作可以被组织和追溯；后者仍须通过 FP01 与 H0—H4 取得场地、主体、基线、权利、专业审查、演练和授权证据 [source:OPEN-CALL-PROJECT-STATEMENT] [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK] [source:SUBMISSION-INTAKE-PR-4007]。

1909 年，京张铁路由中国人自己勘测、设计、施工和管理，留下自主完成复杂现代工程的历史证据 [source:HIST-JINGZHANG-RAILWAY] [source:HIST-JINGZHANG-PARK]。今天的“再次开路”把两种能力放在同一现场：AI 参与公共领域复杂专业工作的能力，以及城市机构审查、接管、纠错和暂停或终止具体 AI 服务的能力。第一次开路把工程能力写入地理；再次开路把智能能力与治理能力共同写入社会。这是设计论题，不是历史材料或政策文件原文。

V0.13 不再用能力体系占据第一屏，而是先交付一个可被质疑、测量和停止的最小原型：**一个虚构复合使用者、一个 D0 待确认的候选服务问题、一份可撤销且未执行的首用合同**。候选问题是：一位处于职业早期阶段的使用者，能否在协议零号站不依赖智能手机或 AI-only 路径，找到一项合适的学习或职业服务，经真人确认，并取得含申诉、删除和退出权利的纸质或可携带回执？该人物不是实地调研对象；轮椅通行与无手机进入只是无障碍和非数字服务的合成设计测试条件，不代表真实个人、群体规模、需求频率或用户研究结论 [data:geometry/public_space.geojson#ROOM-TEST-001] [metric:fp01_concrete_service_problem_count]。

FP01 只有一项面向使用者的服务：**S07 学习与职业协作工作室**负责普通服务目录、通俗解释和由专业人员确认的转介或有理由拒绝；AI 不得决定入学、就业、录用、福利、资质或对人排序。**S03 城市多模态评测实验室**只做独立评测，检查可用性、偏差、无障碍、人工接管和失败证据，不另开一项用户服务，也不得由供应商自证通过。**S04 AI City API 场景接入台**只支持目录来源、版本、回执、申诉、删除和退出字段的交换，不汇聚原始个人数据，也不批准服务结果、合同或采购。

D0 的人工/非数字基线仍是 `unknown`：任务完成率、用时、人工接管时延、申诉理解、退出恢复和服务可达性均没有现场测量值 [metric:fp01_observed_human_baseline_task_completion_rate]。D0 必须由可问责服务方与使用者代表共同确认真实问题、现行人工流程、测量方法、禁止自动决定事项和停止条件；如果问题或基线不能成立，FP01 不进入后续首用流程。V0.13 没有用合成值填补缺口，也不把设计人物写成真实证据。

三个概念空间原型把合同落到可讨论的空间尺度：**1:500** 检查 `ROOM-TEST-001` 的临时证据窗口与 S07 主服务、S03 独立评测、S04 接口支持的关系；**1:100** 检查 18×12 米可逆服务模块、普通目录、真人确认、纸质回执和退出位置；**1:50** 检查人工接管界面、可见暂停状态、服务台与清晰通行。140×180 米、18×12 米、1.50 米回转、0.85 米服务台和不小于 1.20 米净通行均是待测绘、待现行标准与专业复核的概念测试尺寸，不代表现状房间、正式选址、合规证明、施工图或建设承诺 [metric:fp01_spatial_prototype_scale_count] [standard:MOHURD-ARCH-DESIGN-DEPTH-2016] [depth:land_use_layout]。

证据包还登记 **success、refusal、human_takeover、exit_restoration** 四类示例回执，用于重放正常完成、有理由拒绝、人工接管和退出恢复时是否都保留责任人、理由、申诉、删除及回到人工基线的路径 [metric:fp01_receipt_event_type_coverage_ratio]。覆盖率 1.0 只说明四种规定事件均有合成夹具，不能证明真实服务已经运行、回执已经签发、人工基线已经恢复或任何绩效已经实现。

**JING-ZHANG BREAKS NEW GROUND / 京张，再次开路 — From Independent Engineering, Through Open Co-Creation, to Trusted First Use** translates the national “AI Plus” direction into an urban-design question that can be challenged, measured and stopped. National policy advances AI from isolated technical breakthroughs toward application pilots, real scenarios, deployment at scale, public governance and trustworthy operation; Haidian describes Jing-Zhang as a core carrier for China's AI industry in global competition and calls for a super AI test field and a “human—AI symbiosis test field” [source:NATIONAL-AI-APPLICATION-ORIENTATION-2025] [source:STATE-COUNCIL-AI-PLUS-2025] [source:NATIONAL-FIFTEENTH-FIVE-YEAR-AI-APPLICATION-2025] [source:SRC-2026-BJ-KW-THREE-AREAS-WINGS] [source:HAIDIAN-JINGZHANG-MIDTERM-2026]. This is strategic alignment and a design ambition—not national designation of a pilot, government authorization, approved planning status, adoption of this submission or proof of global leadership.

The open call is forming a first process prototype with partial public records: Agents read structured tasks and produce structured planning and design artifacts; machine checks and the V0.12 predecessor's intake record are reviewable. Formal selection, professional deepening, final judgment and knowledge return remain unobserved, with those judgments reserved for people and professional teams. Current V0.13 is this PR submission edition, and its intake status must be read from the live PR record; submission itself is not acceptance. This proposal strictly separates that co-creation process underway from urban practice still awaiting external evidence: the former shows that parts of human–Agent professional collaboration can be organized and traced; the latter still requires FP01 and H0-H4 evidence for site, parties, baseline, rights, professional review, rehearsal and authorization [source:OPEN-CALL-PROJECT-STATEMENT] [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK] [source:SUBMISSION-INTAKE-PR-4007].

In 1909, the Jing-Zhang Railway was surveyed, designed, built and managed by Chinese people, leaving historical evidence of independent capacity for a complex modern engineering system [source:HIST-JINGZHANG-RAILWAY] [source:HIST-JINGZHANG-PARK]. Today, “breaking new ground again” places two capabilities in one field: AI's capacity to participate in complex public-interest professional work, and the city's capacity to review, take over, correct, pause or terminate a specific AI-enabled service. The first breakthrough wrote engineering capacity into geography; the next writes intelligence and governance capacity into society together. This is a design thesis, not a quotation from historical or policy material.

V0.13 no longer puts the capability system on the first screen. It begins with the smallest prototype that can be challenged, measured and stopped: **one fictional composite user, one candidate service problem to be confirmed at D0, and one revocable, unexecuted first-use contract**. The candidate question is whether an early-career user at Protocol Station Zero can find one suitable learning or career service without depending on a smartphone or an AI-only route, have it confirmed by a person, and receive a paper or portable receipt with appeal, deletion and exit rights. This persona is not a fieldwork subject. Wheelchair access and no-phone entry are synthetic design-test conditions for accessibility and non-digital service; they represent no actual person, population size, demand frequency or user-research finding [data:geometry/public_space.geojson#ROOM-TEST-001] [metric:fp01_concrete_service_problem_count].

FP01 has only one user-facing service. **S07 Learning and Career Studio** provides the ordinary service catalogue, plain-language explanation and a referral or reasoned refusal confirmed by a professional; AI may not decide admission, employment, hiring, benefits or credentials, or rank people. **S03 Urban Multimodal Evaluation Lab** performs independent evaluation only, checking usability, bias, accessibility, human takeover and failure evidence. It opens no separate user service, and suppliers may not self-certify. **S04 AI City API Gateway** provides interface support only for catalogue provenance, version, receipt, appeal, deletion and exit fields. It neither pools raw personal data nor approves a service outcome, contract or procurement.

The D0 human and non-digital baseline remains `unknown`: there are no field measurements for task completion, elapsed time, human-takeover delay, appeal comprehension, exit restoration or service accessibility [metric:fp01_observed_human_baseline_task_completion_rate]. At D0, an accountable service owner and user representatives must co-confirm the real problem, current human process, measurement method, prohibited automated decisions and stop conditions. If the problem or baseline cannot be established, FP01 does not enter the subsequent first-use process. V0.13 uses no synthetic value to fill this gap and does not turn a design persona into empirical evidence.

Three concept spatial prototypes bring the contract to discussable scales. **1:500** tests the provisional evidence window for `ROOM-TEST-001` and the relationship among S07 primary service, S03 independent evaluation and S04 interface support. **1:100** tests an 18 by 12 metre reversible service module, ordinary catalogue, human confirmation, paper receipt and exit positions. **1:50** tests the human-takeover interface, visible paused state, service counter and clear circulation. The 140 by 180 metre envelope, 18 by 12 metre module, 1.50 metre turning circle, 0.85 metre counter and at least 1.20 metre clear passage are conceptual test dimensions pending survey, current-standard review and professional confirmation. They are not an existing room, formal site, proof of compliance, construction drawings or a commitment to build [metric:fp01_spatial_prototype_scale_count] [standard:MOHURD-ARCH-DESIGN-DEPTH-2016] [depth:land_use_layout].

The evidence pack also registers four illustrative receipts—**success, refusal, human_takeover and exit_restoration**—to replay whether normal completion, reasoned refusal, human takeover and exit restoration each retain accountable authority, reason, appeal, deletion and a route back to the human baseline [metric:fp01_receipt_event_type_coverage_ratio]. Coverage of 1.0 means only that a synthetic fixture exists for each required event class.
