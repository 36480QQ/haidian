# 京张，再次开路 V0.9 简明叙事 / JING-ZHANG BREAKS NEW GROUND V0.9 Brief Narrative

## 中文

**京张，再次开路 / JING-ZHANG BREAKS NEW GROUND——从自主筑路到自主用智**，把百年京张提出为面向国家“人工智能+”战略的城市级先行验证。国家政策把人工智能从单点技术突破推进到应用中试、真实场景、规模化应用、公共治理和安全可信；海淀则把京张定位为中国 AI 产业参与全球竞争的“核心载体”，并提出超级 AI 试验场与“人机共融实验场” [source:NATIONAL-AI-APPLICATION-ORIENTATION-2025] [source:STATE-COUNCIL-AI-PLUS-2025] [source:NATIONAL-FIFTEENTH-FIVE-YEAR-AI-APPLICATION-2025] [source:SRC-2026-BJ-KW-THREE-AREAS-WINGS] [source:HAIDIAN-JINGZHANG-MIDTERM-2026]。这是本方案的战略对齐与设计目标，不代表国家级试点认定、政府授权、获批规划、本投稿已被采纳或已经证明世界领先。

1909 年，京张铁路由中国人自己勘测、设计、施工和管理，留下了自主完成复杂现代工程的历史证据 [source:HIST-JINGZHANG-RAILWAY] [source:HIST-JINGZHANG-PARK]。第一次开路解决现代工程如何穿越地理；第二次开路要先行回答快速迭代的智能如何进入生产、公共服务和日常生活，并同时守住人民可感、人工可接管、系统可退出和成果可共享的城市底线。这是一条可实施、可度量、可复盘的设计路径，不是历史材料或政策文件原文。

V0.9 把 AI 应用能力拆成“六项转化能力 + 一层可信运行”：SOURCE 造得出，STACK 接得上，PROVE 验得真，LIVE & MARKET 用得上，ENABLE 推得开，COMMONS 留得下；可信运行层保障运行、纠错和安全退出。六阶段和映射覆盖率只证明提案结构完整，不证明采购、部署、外部采用、标准产出、公共效益或全球领先 [metric:application_capability_stage_count] [metric:application_capability_mapping_coverage_ratio]。六节点覆盖官方“三区两翼”而不新增法定分区：AI 原点社区承接 SOURCE、ENABLE 与 PROVE，众智园承接 STACK 与 PROVE，大钟寺承接 LIVE & MARKET，小月河与两翼提供实景验证和专业服务，京张遗址公园把开放方法、公共空间、服务基线、失败证据和城市记忆沉淀为 COMMONS。

12 个既有场景被组合为五个旗舰试点：**FP01 AI 原点首用站**（S03、S04、S07；SOURCE + ENABLE）、**FP02 众智全栈开放试验庭**（S01、S10；STACK）、**FP03 小月河场景共测网**（S02、S05、S06、S08；PROVE）、**FP04 大钟寺 AI 日常市场**（S11、S12；LIVE & MARKET）、**FP05 京张公共证据公园**（S09；COMMONS，并作为前四项的公开观察与反馈层）。五项共用“技术准备—有界试验—首用采购—扩散—标准/共同能力输出”五门，以及 D30 定责定界、D60 有界共测、D90 证据决策三道 90 日门槛；未通过只能修改或停止。运营联合体、一年指标、资金、空间和数据边界均是待确认的概念建议 [depth:phasing_implementation]。

FP01 是能力链的最小可执行原型。`FP01-CONTRACT-001` 从 D0 人工/非数字基线，经 D30 定责定界、D60 有界共测、D90 独立 go/revise/stop，到 D100 公开交接；十二条款覆盖职责分离、版本来源、场地/资金/数据边界、互操作、无账号与无障碍服务、人工接管、事件申诉、停止恢复、独立证据和双向退出。机器记录只确认 1 份模板、五门与十二条款完整 [metric:fp01_first_use_delivery_contract_template_count] [metric:fp01_100_day_gate_coverage_ratio] [metric:fp01_contract_clause_coverage_ratio]；模板始终是 `concept_template_unexecuted`，实际首用采购仍为 `unknown` [metric:flagship_first_use_procurement_count]。

AI City API、**京张 AI 可信应用公约 / Jing-Zhang Trusted AI Compact**、七类最低接口、人工接管、非数字替代、申诉补救和能力返还构成全网的可信运行层。V0.9 沿用 V0.6 登记的 3 个嵌套街室、7 个接口实体、3 条人物旅程和 3 个能力返还节点；引用覆盖与解析覆盖为 1.0，只证明机器引用闭合，不证明服务已交付 [metric:protocol_street_room_count] [metric:protocol_interface_entity_count] [metric:human_journey_count] [metric:capacity_return_node_count] [metric:return_ticket_reference_coverage_ratio] [metric:protocol_interface_entity_coverage_ratio]。

## English

**JING-ZHANG BREAKS NEW GROUND / 京张，再次开路 — From Independent Railway Engineering to Trusted AI at City Scale** proposes the Centennial Jing-Zhang corridor as an urban-scale demonstrator aligned with China’s “AI Plus” strategy. National policy advances AI from isolated technical breakthroughs toward application pilots, real scenarios, deployment at scale, public governance and trustworthy operation; Haidian describes Jing-Zhang as a core carrier for China’s AI industry in global competition and calls for a super AI test field and a “human—AI symbiosis test field” [source:NATIONAL-AI-APPLICATION-ORIENTATION-2025] [source:STATE-COUNCIL-AI-PLUS-2025] [source:NATIONAL-FIFTEENTH-FIVE-YEAR-AI-APPLICATION-2025] [source:SRC-2026-BJ-KW-THREE-AREAS-WINGS] [source:HAIDIAN-JINGZHANG-MIDTERM-2026]. This is strategic alignment and a design ambition—not national-pilot designation, government authorization, approved planning status, adoption of this submission or proven global leadership.

In 1909, the Jing-Zhang Railway was surveyed, designed, built and managed by Chinese people, leaving historical evidence of independent capacity for a complex modern engineering system [source:HIST-JINGZHANG-RAILWAY] [source:HIST-JINGZHANG-PARK]. The first breakthrough organized modern engineering across geography; the second must test how rapidly changing intelligence can enter production, public services and everyday life while remaining perceptible to people, subject to human takeover, safely reversible and capable of leaving shared value. This is an implementable, measurable and reviewable design pathway, not a quotation from historical or policy material.

V0.9 defines AI application capacity as **six conversion capabilities plus one trusted-operations layer**: SOURCE can originate, STACK can integrate, PROVE can validate in reality, LIVE & MARKET can deploy, ENABLE can scale, and COMMONS can retain methods and public value. Stage count and mapping coverage prove proposal-structure completeness only; they do not prove procurement, deployment, external adoption, standards output, public benefit or global leadership [metric:application_capability_stage_count] [metric:application_capability_mapping_coverage_ratio]. The network overlays the Three Zones and Two Wings without creating statutory zones: AI Origin leads SOURCE, ENABLE and PROVE; Zhongzhiyuan leads STACK and PROVE; Dazhongsi leads LIVE & MARKET; the wings provide real-world validation and professional support; and Jing-Zhang Heritage Park retains open methods, public space, service baselines, failure evidence and urban memory as COMMONS.

The twelve existing scenarios become five flagship pilots: **FP01 AI Origin First-Use Station** (S03, S04, S07; SOURCE + ENABLE), **FP02 Zhongzhiyuan Full-Stack Open Test Yard** (S01, S10; STACK), **FP03 Xiaoyuehe Scenario Co-Test Network** (S02, S05, S06, S08; PROVE), **FP04 Dazhongsi AI Everyday Market** (S11, S12; LIVE & MARKET), and **FP05 Jing-Zhang Public Evidence Park** (S09; COMMONS and the observation/feedback layer for the first four). All use five gates—technology readiness, bounded trial, first-use procurement, diffusion, and standards/shared-capability output—and three 90-day gates: D30 accountability/boundaries, D60 bounded co-test and D90 evidence decision. A failed gate means modify or stop. Consortia, one-year indicators and funding/space/data boundaries remain unconfirmed concept recommendations [depth:phasing_implementation].

FP01 is the smallest executable prototype of the capability chain. `FP01-CONTRACT-001` runs from a D0 human/non-digital baseline through D30 accountability and boundaries, D60 bounded co-test, D90 independent go/revise/stop and D100 public handover. Twelve clauses cover role separation, version/provenance, site/funding/data boundaries, interoperability, account-free and accessible service, human takeover, incident/appeal handling, stop/restoration, independent evidence and two-way exit. Machine records confirm only one template and complete five-gate/twelve-clause structure [metric:fp01_first_use_delivery_contract_template_count] [metric:fp01_100_day_gate_coverage_ratio] [metric:fp01_contract_clause_coverage_ratio]. The template remains `concept_template_unexecuted`, and actual first-use procurement remains `unknown` [metric:flagship_first_use_procurement_count].

The AI City API, **Jing-Zhang Trusted AI Compact / 京张 AI 可信应用公约**, seven minimum interfaces, human takeover, non-digital fallback, appeal/redress and capability return form the network’s trusted-operations layer. V0.9 retains the three nested street rooms, seven interface entities, three human journeys and three capacity-return nodes registered in V0.6. Coverage of their references remains 1.0; this proves machine-reference closure, not delivered service [metric:protocol_street_room_count] [metric:protocol_interface_entity_count] [metric:human_journey_count] [metric:capacity_return_node_count] [metric:return_ticket_reference_coverage_ratio] [metric:protocol_interface_entity_coverage_ratio].

V0.9 preserves the SITE/KEY shapes, BUILD/TEST/LIVE × four-band 3×4 topology and area denominator. Nested rooms add no unioned public-space area, which remains 432,600 m² [metric:public_space_area_sqm]. Pilot status, sites, consortia, procurement, budgets and one-year targets are unconfirmed; statutory intensity, roads, ownership, utilities, energy, fire and heritage conditions await official inputs. This is not a national pilot, official redline, planning approval, government permit, procurement decision, signed contract, engineering commitment or deployed system [data:geometry/site_boundary.geojson#SITE-001] [standard:MOHURD-ARCH-DESIGN-DEPTH-2016] [depth:land_use_layout] [depth:risk_missing_data].
