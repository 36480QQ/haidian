Three-Level Scope

The proposal uses a three-level scope framework:

  • Coordinated research area ≈ 43.6 km² · industry and future-city research
  • Overall design area ≈ 11.4 km² · renewal and regulatory-plan-level urban design
  • Key area A2 ≈ 104.3 ha · detailed urban design (focus of this submission)

Key Areas

Three detailed design units (PROV-KEY-001/002/003) address rail-heritage activation, R&D origination and living symbiosis.

Land Use

Land use follows national land-use codes, forming a compact research + housing + service mix.

Mobility, Blue-Green & Public Space

A 5-minute walking network anchored on rail stations; a continuous linear open-space spine along the heritage corridor.

Buildings & Renewal

Buildings are graded into retain / renovate / demolish. Heritage and sound stock are retained and converted to shared R&D and community uses.
