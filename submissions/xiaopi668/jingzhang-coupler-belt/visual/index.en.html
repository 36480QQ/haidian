THE JING-ZHANG COUPLER BELT: STANDARD INTERFACES, COUPLE AND UNCOUPLE, ONE URBAN CONSIST

Taking the century-old automatic-coupler wisdom of the Jing-Zhang Railway — standard interfaces, reliable coupling, buffered degradation, ready uncoupling — this proposal introduces the urban coupling protocol: every AI scenario is one car that may join the public main line only after named human confirmation. Spatial structure: one consist, three cars, two wings, six couplers. Offline display; authoritative data in GeoJSON, metrics.json and matrices.
