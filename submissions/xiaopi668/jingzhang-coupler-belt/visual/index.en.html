AI Scenarios

12 scenario cards, 4 of them industry-test validations; each binds a coupling waybill (allowed/banned tasks, data boundaries, human review, buffer triggers, uncoupling procedure) — no waybill, no coupling permit.

SC-01 Automatic-coupling endurance test

Multi-agent couple/uncouple cycles at the closed Zhongzhiyuan coupler, released by a named safety officer (industry test validation)

SC-02 Buffer calibration test

Impact/full-load/disconnection buffer calibration, envelopes re-checked by the operator (industry test validation)

SC-03 Uncoupling & e-stop drill

Emergency uncoupling under power/network loss, failure cards archived (industry test validation)

SC-04 Edge-compute offline resilience
