## Summary

The proposal reshapes the Jing-Zhang railway heritage corridor into a "human-AI symbiotic" innovation belt: a blue-green spine formed by the heritage park, a slow-mobility innovation axis linking three key areas, and public compute plus open-source culture as the binding glue. It turns "railway heritage" into an "AI public living room", "industry park" into an "AI-native community", and "innovation belt" into a "global AI pilgrimage corridor" [depth:DD-03]. The core innovation is not any single technology but a **combinable set of open-source mechanisms the community can self-run**—compute-as-utility, programmable open-source wall, AI pilgrimage passport, failure museum, community data cooperative, embodied-robot heritage training ground, dual-timeline narrative map, green-compute waste-heat recovery, cross-dimensional exhibition, and 16 creative mechanisms in total (see §5.9) [depth:DD-06]. All design language is conceptual/reference for professional teams to deepen; it is not statutory planning, government approval, or an engineering-feasibility conclusion [depth:DD-14].
