# Narrative / 叙事摘要

京张铁路把蒸汽时代的工程组织带入北京；百年之后，AI 创新带面对的不是再造一条技术展廊，而是让计算能力重新服务最基本的公共条件：可呼吸、可停留、可理解、可拒绝。方案以一条呼吸主脊连接三处不同的“肺”，用十二个可暂停场景、人工接管路径和分期验证机制，把创新从展示变成可复核的日常公共服务。

The historical Jing-Zhang Railway organized movement and engineering in the steam era. A century later, the AI belt should not become another technology showroom. It should return compute capacity to basic public conditions: breathable, comfortable, understandable, and rejectable. One breathing spine links three different lungs; twelve pauseable scenarios, a staffed fallback route, and phased validation turn innovation into reviewable daily public service.
