# 京张人字线 / Jingzhang Works

“京张人字线”不是一条新道路，也不是把规划总图画成“人”字。它把京张铁路面对困难时折返求解、最终向前的工程精神，转译为一套面向真实结果的 AI 城市服务体系：问题必须由具名责任方认领，先在影子模式中验证，进入现场后保留人工确认和降级方案，再由独立证据决定采用、整改或退出。

The proposal is not a new road and does not force the masterplan into a renzi shape. It translates the Jing-Zhang Railway's engineering metaphor - reversing direction to keep moving forward - into an outcome-led AI urban service system. A named party must accept each task; shadow mode comes first; live operation preserves human authority and fallback; independent evidence then determines adoption, repair, or exit.

三个重点区各有一个公众可感知的旗舰闭环：众智园验证 AI 产品是否被真实采用，北京 AI 原点社区解决街道问题的响应与结果两级闭环，大钟寺验证规则优先、AI 只处理异常的共享路缘。三区共享开放接口和证据格式，但可以分别降级和退出，避免一个中央平台故障拖垮整条走廊。

The three key areas carry distinct public-facing loops: real AI-product adoption in Zhongzhiyuan; response and outcome closure for recurring street issues in the Beijing AI Origin Community; and a rules-first shared curb in Dazhongsi, where AI assists only with exceptions. They share open interfaces and evidence formats, but can degrade or exit independently.

所有空间指标均来自包内临时 GeoJSON，可复算但不是官方控规。真实绩效基线、主责运营方、独立评测、合法数据目录、道路与建筑条件仍待现场和正式资料确认。A local validation PASS proves reviewability only; it does not prove government approval, procurement, construction feasibility, or field performance.
