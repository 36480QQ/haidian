# Implementation Closure | Scene, Space, Role, Indicator, Stop Condition

This is a conceptual implementation review table for S01, S06 and S07. It states what must be prepared before a real pilot; it does not claim field rehearsal, deployment or measured performance.

| Scene | Space | Accountable role (TBC) | Indicator / evidence | Stop condition | Maintenance and review record |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| S01 Rail–park first metre | Station gate, continuous wayfinding, paper route, human help point | Station service + accessibility reviewer | Human-path coverage; wait time remains unknown; entry/breakpoint photos and sign-off | Broken route, unverified night use or failed human entry → pause | Log version, service hours, reviewer and fallback reason for each route change |
| S06 First AI product use | Reversible experience table, capability-limit board, booking/human demo point | Product explainer + public-experience guide | Four disclosures complete; anonymous feedback; version/data-purpose register | Unclear capability/data boundary, no exit or no human equivalent → do not open | Version board, disclosure text, withdrawal log and human review date |
| S07 Slow-robot public observation | Observation line, detour line, emergency stop, incident log point | Safety observer + device owner | Stop, detour, disconnect and incident records; no face/continuous-track capture | Conflict, disconnect, failed stop or unclear privacy boundary → stop | Batch, incident ID, handler, exit asset and recovery sign-off |

## Common closure

`Public/human-verified input → AI advice → named human review → spatial sync → anonymous receipt → correction / pause / exit`.

Performance indicators remain `unknown` until field baseline, authority and accountable-role sign-off. “24/24” means synthetic rule consistency only, not field rehearsal or user performance.
