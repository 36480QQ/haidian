Overview map

Three-tier scopes

Official announcement caliber: coordinated research area about 43.6 km2 / overall design area about 11.4 km2 / key areas about 368.4 ha. This package's participation track is a concept sub-scope inside the overall design area (about 11.4 km2): three nodes plus a continuous slow participation axis, all boundaries provisional. The sub-scope is consistent with the announcement caliber (self-computed per the announcement, not officially issued).

Key areas

Three differentiated participation landmarks: Issue Collaboration Hall (knowledge: issue and Issue-workflow hub), Evidence Verification Station (verification: external data registration and cross-check), Sharing and Publicity Gallery (communication: publication and social sharing). Node typologies, itineraries and interfaces (rail stations, heritage park, universities, neighborhoods, barrier-free) are shown in the key-areas figure; running mechanisms include a participation covenant, deliberation rules, tiered publication, venue rotation, booking, honor-and-status display (submitted/reviewed/selected/implemented) and an annual honor board.

Transport and slow mobility

A continuous slow axis links the three nodes with walking and cycling first; rail-station connection routes are continuous and legible; barrier-free and age-friendly assistive routes and 15-minute slow-travel catchments (concept, not official boundaries) are shown in the mobility-bluegreen figure. Municipal services follow intensive sharing; lighting, barrier-free facilities and participation screens are integrated (concept).

Blue-green and public space

Green belt as the axis, node parks as pearls: the heritage park green belt links pocket green spaces; participation facilities blend in with low intervention and reversibility, never blocking heritage views. Age-friendly configuration: age-friendly services, child-friendly design, barrier-free routes and assistance for persons with disabilities and other vulnerable groups; multilingual, large-type and diagram information. Brand identity direction (concept): PAR-JZ logo, visual grammar, component family and cultural symbols (spikes, station signs, timetables), with international multilingual copy (see the logo-parjz figure and the narrative).

Buildings

Keep-renovate-demolish balance with retention first: heritage park fabric and railway relics are preserved; existing buildings are functionally replaced with participation-oriented upgrade interfaces; participation facilities come from the reversible public-space component library (info screens, bulletin boards, movable seats, modular pavilions, demountable signage) - light, reversible, relocatable; no large new buildings; no FAR or height conclusions.

Renewal projects

Four concept project lists (issue reception, evidence registration, sharing and publication, annual renewal). Phasing: near term 1-3 years (base procedures and pilot demonstration nodes), mid term 3-5 years (three nodes and slow-axis completion), long term 5-10 years (full-area mechanism with annual renewal). Pilot gating ranks station connection, existing-space suitability, barrier-free conditions and current facilities; stop conditions and the exit mechanism (removal, archiving, publication) are supported by the reversible component library.
