## Outline
- Public Participation & Continuous Collaboration Loop (PAR.JZ): a concept for a public participation and continuous collaboration mechanism along the Jingzhang AI innovation belt, following the taskbook's continuous-participation clause (issue collaboration with evidence checklists, external-data registration and validation, social sharing with status distinction, and the recommended collaboration loop).
- Issue Commons as the issue/PR collaboration hub: public issue collection, evidence checklists and collaborative responses, anonymized aggregation only (concept-level mechanism suggestion, not statutory procedures).
- Evidence Station as the external-data registration and cross-validation station: provenance records, cross-checks and unverified marking (concept-level).
- Share Gallery as the results-sharing and public-display gallery: reproducible links and status-distinguished sharing (concept-level).
- Node 1 - Issue Commons: participation agenda hub. Node 2 - Evidence Station: evidence registration and cross-validation. Node 3 - Share Gallery: results sharing and public display.
- 5 AI+ scenarios (issue clustering AI, evidence ledger AI, reproduction-check AI, share-copy AI, feedback-handling AI); anonymized aggregation only, key decisions human-reviewed, no over-surveillance (no individual-identifying tracking).
- Slow-traffic-first: a continuous slow-traffic axis along the green belt links the three nodes; blue-green public spaces host low-intervention, reversible participation facilities; annual events: public participation open day, issue collaboration briefing, results share festival.
- Three formal core metrics (site_area_sqm, green_ratio, public_space_ratio) are recomputed from this package's geometry; all figures are concept suggestions on a provisional boundary; recompute when official data is published.
