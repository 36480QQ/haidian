# BELLPIVOT AI PRECINCT: Dazhongsi AI Industry Cluster (Concept)

**English convenience summary** (full binding narrative is the Chinese proposal.md; this summary may lag the zh text).

以轨道枢纽与站城融合为锚组织「钟枢智谷」(BellPivot AI Precinct)智能原生新业态集聚概念：三节点——鸣钟智舰（智能原生旗舰综合体：旗舰企业+展示体验+算力窗口）、枢站环台（站城TOD复合节点：轨道上盖+立体复合开发+枢纽客厅）、织新里（创业加速街区：孵化加速+共享验证+小规模中试），五类AI+场景（智能零售、AI会展路演、物流分拣与无人机配送点、无障碍伴随、客流感知）嵌入站城日常，数据匿名聚合+人工复核、禁止个体追踪，TOD密度梯度+存量楼宇产业置换，设施模块化可逆。全部为概念建议、参考方案，基于 provisional 边界，官方数据发布后复算。

## Outline
- BellPivot AI Precinct (Dazhongsi AI Industry Cluster, ~72 ha per official text): a station-city integration district for native-AI new business forms - "bells ring, the pivot turns".
- Node 1 - Mingzhong AI Flagship: native-AI flagship complex (flagship enterprises, showroom experience, compute window).
- Node 2 - Pivot Station Ring Terrace: TOD composite node with station air-rights development and a day-night active hub living room.
- Node 3 - Zhixin Lane: start-up acceleration blocks (incubation, shared verification, small-scale pilot lines).
- 5 AI+ scenarios (smart retail, AI expo-roadshow, logistics sorting & drone delivery points, barrier-free accompaniment, crowd-flow sensing); all data anonymized and human-reviewed, no individual tracking.
- Renewal in 3 phases (1-3 / 3-5 / 5-10 years, concept) around a transit hub density gradient.
- All figures are concept suggestions on a provisional boundary; recompute when official data is published.
