Transport and slow traffic

A "rail + slow traffic + micro-circulation" trip chain is organised around the rail hub with shuttle loops and P+R facilities; drone delivery uses dedicated time slots, routes and isolated pads; second-floor corridors and underpasses link the hub, the flagship complex and the acceleration blocks; crowd sensing serves space management and emergency evacuation only.

Blue-green and public space

The Jingzhang Railway Heritage Park works as a culture-green corridor feeding pocket parks and street greening; ecological dry streams and rain gardens along the Xiaoyue River link a waterfront slow loop. Public space "Five Ones": Bell Ritual Axis, hub plaza group, Smart Living Street, roof-garden group, community farm; facilities are modular and reversible, forming a reusable public-space component library.

Buildings

Stock-first with limited infill: hub-periphery mixed development and stock-building industry conversion dominate; air-rights and scattered infill strictly limit net new volume and height; retain-renovate-demolish follows "retain and renovate first, demolish as exception, no large net new volume", with professional appraisal and statutory procedure for any demolition.

Renewal projects

Three-phase project list: near term (1-3 years) anchor launch (hub-periphery renewal planning, flagship siting, first unmanned-retail and barrier-free pilots); mid term (3-5 years) cluster formation (stock conversion, accelerator, routine roadshow operations); long term (5-10 years) ecosystem maturity (whole-precinct scenario connection and station-city operation). Every item states lead/collaborate roles under RACI with stop and exit conditions; costs are qualitative low/medium/high tiers only.
