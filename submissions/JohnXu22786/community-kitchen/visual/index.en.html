Warm Stove Loop KIT·JZ - Community Kitchen & Meal-Service Network (Concept)

One workshop, one garden, one stove: Shared Kitchen / Food-Education Workshop / Warm Stove Station | ten AI scenario cards | three test-and-validation protocols | five mechanism elements

Everything here is a concept proposal for professional teams to deepen; it does not constitute planning approval, engineering implementation or investment conclusions. Bilingual formal delivery.

Boundary notice: this page is based on the provisional boundary (temporary concept boundary, not an official red line) for display, discussion and revision only, not as a formal professional scoring drawing; full recalculation follows official data release.

The overall design overview (provisional geometry) is in assets/figures/site-overview.en.png (and the Chinese counterpart): overall design scope, three key areas (provisional), conceptual green and public space, slow-traffic and delivery routes, and three AI landmark sites (Shared Kitchen, Food-Education Workshop, Warm Stove Station). Three-Level Scopes

Official three-tier scopes per the announcement text: coordinated research area approx 43.6 km2, overall design area approx 11.4 km2, and key detailed-design areas approx 368.4 ha total. This package is the community-kitchen and meal-service sub-scope inside the overall design area; it does not claim full professional coverage of the whole range.

Key Areas

Three key areas host the three conceptual nodes (no specific parcels are presupposed): the Shared Kitchen (Zhongzhiyuan AI Acceleration Area side, approx 193 ha coarse-range clue), the Food-Education Workshop (Beijing AI Origin Community side, approx 104 ha) and the Warm Stove Station (Dazhongsi AI Industry Cluster side, approx 72 ha). Key-area geometry is provisional and must not be treated as an official red line.

Land Use

Single land-use caliber (six classes in land_use.geojson, recomputed in EPSG:4548): Parks & green 26.7%, R&D 22.6%, Residential 17.3%, Commercial 13.1%, Business & finance 12.7%, Education & research 7.7%, totalling approx 100%. Caliber note: the green ratio (green_ratio, approx 11.6%) uses the green_space design geometry as numerator and the overall boundary as denominator - a different caliber from Parks & green (26.7%) in this table; the two are not interchangeable.

Mobility

Slow meal-delivery route concept: the Warm Stove Station is the delivery-transfer origin; slow delivery branches extend to surrounding residential areas and connect to nearby rail stations as a rail + slow-traffic + insulated-box last-mile concept. No road alignment or engineering conclusions are given. Clean and dirty flows are separated: insulated boxes are handed over at a separate loading bay; food intake and kitchen-waste removal use independent rear entrances.

Blue-Green & Public Space

The garden plot ('one garden') is the food-education workshop's front plot and a community co-built vegetable garden concept - the blue-green anchor of the three nodes - connected by movable planters, shaded seating and continuous barrier-free paving. The reversible component library has seven types (movable planters, shaded seats, modular kiosks, insulated storage boxes, wayfinding signs, lamp-post baskets, fencing); any component can be stopped without affecting basic node functions.

Buildings

The three nodes are insert-type community service concepts embedded in existing buildings or community support space without changing statutory land use. Building scale is given as tiers only, not numbers: Shared Kitchen largest, Food-Education Workshop mid, Warm Stove Station smallest. Retain-renovate-demolish follows the verify-retain-retrofit-relocate sequence; no demolition area is proposed without a survey; FAR and heights remain pending.

Renewal Projects

Three conceptual project categories (canteen dining, shared kitchen, meal-assistance station), each with content, lead type, acceptance criteria and stop conditions. Phasing is a conceptual rhythm: Phase 1 (1-3 yrs) Warm Stove Station pilot and Food-Education Workshop reservations; Phase 2 (3-5 yrs) Shared Kitchen dining matures and AI scenario cards roll out by validation results; Phase 3 (5-10 yrs) the three nodes form a network. Operation RACI, annual programs (Community Kitchen Week, Anti-Waste Day, Elderly Meal-Assistance Open Day), developer community and the incubation-pilot-rollout conversion ladder are all concept mechanisms.

AI Scenarios

Ten AI scenario cards (meal scheduling, surplus ingredients, nutrition planning, food-safety alerts, ordering assistant, off-peak meal recommendations, elderly voice ordering, food-education content, anti-waste points, volunteer scheduling) and three test-and-validation protocols (model evaluation, data-quality evaluation, error stratification and runtime monitoring) handle anonymised aggregated data only; key decisions are human-reviewed; excessive monitoring is prohibited (no individual-identifying tracking); AI produces drafts and candidates only and can be removed at any time.
