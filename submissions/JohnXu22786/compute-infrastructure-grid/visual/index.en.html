Mobility & slow travel

A slow-mobility loop links the three nodes (concept) while maintenance/freight routes are separated from public walking; no road red lines, rail alignments or bridge/tunnel conclusions. Key-route sketch is consistent with the package's provisional road geometry; updated with official road data.

Blue-green & public space

Two concept green corridors along the Jingzhang heritage green corridor and the Xiaoyuehe water system connect the three nodes; a heat-science node at the Synergy Park. Green ratio ~12.2%, public-space ratio ~0.4% (provisional rounded, recomputed after official data).

Buildings

Retain-first with reversible light renovation: heritage park fabric retained, existing stock functionally replaced with reversible compute interfaces; demolition only for individually justified cases; nodes are modular, reversible, light-touch; no large volumes, heights or capacities assumed.

Renewal projects

Three concept categories: compute foundation (pool expansion & scheduling platform, edge embedding, energy-interface demo), coordination mechanisms (vouchers, green-power access, heat-interface standard, sandbox rules, annual carbon disclosure), service ecosystem (matching platform, developer community, science communication, talent program). 1–3y pilots, 3–5y loop connection, 5–10y full-loop operation.
