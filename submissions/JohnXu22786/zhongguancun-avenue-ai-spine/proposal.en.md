# JINGZHI AVENUE: Zhongguancun Avenue AI Innovation Spine (Concept)

**English convenience summary** (full binding narrative is the Chinese proposal.md; this summary may lag the zh text).

以中关村大街为轴组织「京智长街」(JINGZHI Avenue)AI创新主轴概念：一轴三节点——京智之门（大街门户AI展示节点）、智行长街示范段（慢行优先+AI场景集成）、知微坊（知春路—学院路交会腹地创新微单元群），五类AI+场景（智能灯杆、AI导览、无障碍伴随、无人零售、客流感知）嵌入大街日常，数据匿名聚合+人工复核、禁止个体追踪，街廓渐进更新、增量做精，设施模块化可逆。全部为概念建议、参考方案，基于 provisional 边界，官方数据发布后复算。

## Outline
- JINGZHI Avenue (Zhongguancun Avenue AI Innovation Spine): one-axis, three-node concept under the Zhongguancun Science & Technology Service Wing ("global factor allocation; Zhongguancun IP & capital empowerment").
- Node 1 - JINGZHI Gateway: avenue-portal AI exhibition node (public AI plaza, digital-art facade, arrival square echoing the railway-heritage "origin" imagery).
- Node 2 - ZHI-WALK Demonstration Block: slow-traffic-first street sections integrating AI+ scenarios and street-renewal samples.
- Node 3 - ZHIWEI Micro-Cells: courtyard-scale innovation micro-units in the Zhichun Road-Xueyuan Road hinterland, with community AI service booths.
- 5 AI+ scenarios embedded in avenue daily life (smart light poles, AI wayfinding, barrier-free accompaniment, unmanned retail, crowd-flow sensing); all sensor data anonymized at street level, aggregate-only, no facial recognition or individual tracking, human review at every AI decision.
- Incremental street-block renewal in 3 phases (1-3 / 3-5 / 5-10 years, concept).
- All figures are concept suggestions on a provisional boundary; recompute when official data is published.
