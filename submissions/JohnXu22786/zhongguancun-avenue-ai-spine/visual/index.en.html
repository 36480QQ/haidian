AI scenarios and task coverage

10 scenario cards (location/operator/data boundary/human review/offline fallback/KPI/exit): "Ask the Street" tour, "Walk With Me" accessibility companion, "Smart Cabinet" retail, "Micro-Sense" crowd sensing, "Smart Light" poles, "Easy Handling" civic station, "Container Lab" pop-up, "Joy Realm" art wall, "Good Neighbor" community AI station, "Safe Crossing" prompts; a scenario-space-operation matrix covers transport/industry/space/public service/culture/governance; TRL estimates and 3 industry test scenarios (pilot-evaluate-expand-or-exit). All sensing data is anonymized and aggregated at street level with human review; no facial recognition or individual tracking.

The compliance matrix covers 23 tasks (announcement 1.3-1.5 and agent open-call 1-6); brand VI, honor display system, wayfinding, cross-region coordination, developer community and phased operation responsibility matrices are in proposal.md; standard and design-depth matrices include new items.

Cross-region coordination

Conceptual coordination network under the three-zones-two-wings framework: Beiwei Community (resident services/age-friendly), Future Science City (compute & test scenarios), Huairou Science City (talent & open data), E-Town (smart-manufacturing & supply chain), Jing-Jin-Ji (expo/capital/talent). Factor exchange keeps data anonymized, results verifiable, no cross-region targets; mechanism diagram in cooperation-mechanism.pdf.

Operation mechanisms

Developer community (submit-review-publish-archive; open-source licenses + data sandbox); open scenario operation (publish-apply-joint review-pilot ~6 months-evaluate-expand-or-exit; no perpetual operation rights); attraction/conversion (POC + block pilots + pop-up space; no policy incentives promised); the phased implementation matrix and long-term operation responsibility matrix state lead party, cost level, approval dependence, service level, human fallback, evaluation thresholds, expansion conditions and exit/rollback per project.

Three-level scope

Coordinated research area: Zhongguancun Avenue and its hinterland (directional industry/future-city prediction; official text value 43.6 sq km); overall design area: ~11.4 sq km (official text value; avenue-axis block sequence); key areas: JINGZHI Gateway, ZHI-WALK Demonstration Block and ZHIWEI Micro-Cells detailed design (official text total 368.4 ha).

Land use zones

Conceptual land-use structure (provisional; counted by zone units of land_use.geojson; recalculation after official release): research, business/finance, commercial, residential, park green (Jingzhang heritage belt concept) and other - about 21 conceptual zone units; text summarizes only, details are in the geometry.

Buildings

Stock-first, increment second: functional replacement and facade renewal of inefficient buildings along the street form the main body; only justified point infill with strict control of increment and height, shaping a profile descending from the avenue to the hinterland in a "prosperous street front, quiet hinterland" pattern.

Renewal projects

Three-phase project list: near term (1-3 yrs) JINGZHI Gateway grounds, first demonstration-block section, AI station pilots and barrier-free catch-up; mid term (3-5 yrs) full-line section network, ZHIWEI activation, inefficient building replacement; long term (5-10 yrs) full-axis upgrade and blue-green network completion; 5 annual brand programs (JINGZHI Avenue Season, AI Open-Source Carnival, Street Pop-Up Lab, Public-Space AI Art Exhibition, AI Barrier-Free Companion Open Day).
