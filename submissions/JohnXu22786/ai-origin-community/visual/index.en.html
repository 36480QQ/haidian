Traffic and slow mobility

Rail + bus + shared mobility + slow mobility interchange forms the backbone: the slow-mobility spine follows the concept corridor of the Jing-Zhang Heritage Park; east-west stitching links and north-south connections reconnect pedestrian gaps cut by railways and expressways (concept lines, not road red lines); the smart commute assistant provides dynamic travel information at interchange points; the rail station is a conceptual position pending traffic modelling and official drawings.

Blue-green and public space

The four-level park-square-street-courtyard system with pocket parks, vertical greening and tree-lined streets weaves the blue-green network; concept indicators: green share about 20.6%, public-space share about 0.7% (machine values 0.206151 / 0.006526; provisional, pending recalculation); the public component library (smart light poles / scenario devices / wayfinding / accessibility facilities / data nodes) ensures reuse and unified operation.

Buildings

Renewal projects

AI scenarios

Ten AI scenarios (concept): startup pitch, hackathon, talent station, prototype test field, corner AI gallery, smart commute assistant, community AI deliberation, digital-twin government consultation, accessible AI guided tour, Origin White Paper co-creation, with 10 scenario cards (human review points / technology configuration / experience metrics); 3 industry test protocols (data sandbox / AI Alley device rotation / first-store first-release) govern release and exit; only anonymous aggregated data is processed, key decisions always include human review, no individual-identifying tracking, no mass behaviour profiling; mechanisms are advisory concepts and do not replace statutory duties.
