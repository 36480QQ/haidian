# AI ORIGIN COMMUNITY: World-Class AI Innovation Ecosystem Neighbourhood (Concept)

**English convenience summary** (full binding narrative is the Chinese proposal.md; this summary may lag the zh text).

以北京AI原点社区为对象，以「三节点+十场景」把世界级AI创新生态植入高密度街坊日常：原点广场（AI发布与会客厅）、街坊AI学院（孵化+实训+开放实验）、智巷生活环（场景化AI日常街区）。强调人才与创业混合、原型试错、街道即展厅、数据边界与人工复核，全部为概念建议，基于 provisional 边界。

## Outline
- Three-Layer Scope: coordinated research (AI innovation ecosystem & future-city), overall design (~1.1 sq km concept), key-area detailed design (3 nodes).
- Node 1 - Origin Launch Square: AI release stage + talent living room + street-corner exhibition cases.
- Node 2 - Block AI Academy: incubation + training + open lab (AI maker and testing ground).
- Node 3 - AI Alley: scenario-testing daily street (living lab for everyday AI life).
- 10 scenario cards (startup pitch, hackathon, talent porter, prototype field trial, street-corner AI gallery, smart commute assistant, community AI deliberation, digital-twin civic consultation, accessible AI guide, origin white-paper co-creation) + 3 test & validation scenarios.
- 14 renewal projects in 3 phases (0-2 / 2-4 / 4-6 years, concept).
- All figures are concept suggestions on a provisional boundary; recompute when official data is published.
