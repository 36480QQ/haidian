Three-level scope

Coordinated research area (announcement text): about 43.6 sq km, north to North 5th Ring Road, east to Jingzang Expressway, south to Xizhimen Outer Street, west to Wanquanhe Road. Overall design area: about 11.4 sq km, north to North 5th Ring Road, east to Xueyuan Road and Xitucheng Road, south to Xizhimen Outer Street, west to Dazhongsi East Road and Qinghe Road. Key detailed-design areas: about 368.4 ha, north to south: Zhongzhiyuan AI Independent-Innovation Acceleration Zone (about 192.1 ha), Beijing AI Origin Community (about 104.3 ha), Dazhongsi AI Industry Cluster (about 72.0 ha). This package is a last-mile logistics special study located within the overall design area and the key areas; all geometry is provisional.

Land use

Conceptual land-use structure (single caliber, provisional, recomputed on official release): green and open space about 22%; science and industry about 29%; commercial and business services about 30%; residential and supporting about 9%; roads and municipal about 9% (rounded shares total about 99%; full values in land_use.geojson). The three nodes overlay existing land as service function elements, changing no statutory land-use classification.

Transport and slow traffic

Core concept: low-speed autonomous-delivery route rights coordinated with non-motorized lanes - a speed-limited, time-shared route-rights intent (no new road redlines), yielding to pedestrians, slowing at crossings, pausing at intersections. Terminal routes integrate with the slow-traffic system and the Jingzhang Heritage Park green corridor, with slow-traffic interchange links toward Dazhongsi and Wudaokou stations; pilot segments require filing and traffic-impact assessment, and statutory approval precedes any formal step.

Blue-green network and public space

The blue-green network takes the Jingzhang Heritage Park green corridor as its spine; all three nodes sit at intersections of the corridor and the slow-traffic network; facilities do not encroach on green, blue or heritage control lines. The public-space component library (benches, sun-rain shelters, water and info counters, large-type wayfinding, manual help buttons, movable planters, temporary barriers, bicycle racks, accessible ramp modules) consists of relocatable, retrievable lightweight components.

Buildings

Retain-renovate-demolish follows the concept flow: verify first, retain, then light-renovate, and relocate or remove on conflict. Stock buildings that can safely satisfy functions are retained first; light renovation only for accessibility, fire and function adaptation; no demolition area or site-level list is proposed. The hub is a reuse direction for existing storage; the micro-station and parcel station are modular, movable, reversible light facilities (concept caliber, no building-scale assumptions).

Renewal projects

Three project categories (concept): 1) co-delivery facilities - the consolidated hub, time-shifted loading, multi-brand co-delivery access; 2) micro-station network - the delivery micro-station, the low-speed route-rights pilot segment, parking-charging-interchange facilities; 3) station services - the smart station, pickup-storage-return services, manned convenience counter. Phasing is a concept rhythm: near term (1-3 years) pilot micro-station and station (around Dazhongsi station and the Origin-Community slow segment), mid term (3-5 years) hub takes shape, long term (5-10 years) three-node network; five-stage gates with stop and reopen conditions in the body; any failure of safety, operation or public acceptance stops the item with public reasons.

AI scenarios

Ten AI+ scenario cards (S01-S10): Dispatch AI, Route-rights AI, Access AI, Capacity-matching AI, Safety-monitoring AI, Energy-ops AI, Misplacement and complaint AI, Accessibility AI, Community co-delivery AI, Annual evaluation and disclosure AI. Each card carries a spatial anchor, anonymized-aggregated data fields, model boundaries, responsible entity and human review, audit and false-alarm handling, and acceptance indicators. AI governance in three sentences: anonymized aggregation only; human review of key decisions; no over-monitoring (no individual-identifying tracking, no always-on recognition devices, no large-scale behavior profiling). Ten scenario nodes (3 AI landmarks + 7 scenario nodes), consistent with metrics.scenario_node_count.
