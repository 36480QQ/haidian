# ZHICHUN ROAD SMART-CORE: Transit-Hub Station-City Integration & AI Mobility Services (Concept)

**English convenience summary** (full binding narrative is the Chinese proposal.md; this summary may lag the zh text).

以知春路轨道枢纽（10号线/13号线换乘）为锚，组织500米半径「站城融合智核」：智核集散广场（站前集散+城市客厅）、AI出行服务大厅（通勤助手、无障碍导乘、实时客流可视化、人工兜底）、智慧换乘走廊（风雨连廊+多方式接驳）。以慢行为先的放射状接驳网络实现5-10分钟可达全覆盖，AI场景嵌入通勤日常，数据匿名聚合+人工复核，设施模块化可逆。全部为概念建议、参考方案，基于 provisional 边界，官方数据发布后复算。

## Outline
- Three-Layer Scope: coordinated research (industry & future-city; "transit hub + innovation core + liveable community") / overall design (500 m station-city integration around Zhichun Road station, concept) / key-area detailed design (3 nodes).
- Node 1 - Smart-Core Plaza: station-front distribution + urban living room (movable seating, water features, art, festival-stage flexibility).
- Node 2 - AI Mobility Service Hall: commute assistant, multimodal barrier-free guidance, real-time crowd visualization, human-service fallback counter.
- Node 3 - Smart Transit Link: weather-protected corridor linking rail, bus stops, bike-sharing and building frontages.
- 6 AI+ scenarios embedded in daily commuting (commute assistant, crowd prediction, accessible AI guidance, smart bike-sharing dispatch, smart light poles, AI community service booths), all with anonymous aggregation + human review.
- Renewal projects in 3 phases (1-3 / 3-5 / 5-10 years, concept).
- All figures are concept suggestions on a provisional boundary; recompute when official data is published.
