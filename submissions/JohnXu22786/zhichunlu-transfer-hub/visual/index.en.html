Transport & slow traffic

Slow-traffic-first radial transfer network: rail, bus, bike-sharing and walking fully covered within 5-10 minutes; a north-south through-connected spine (along Zhichun Road and the Jing-Zhang heritage belt) and three east-west stitch links (RD-CROSS-00/01/02) reconnect blocks on both sides; bus bays, bike geofences and P+R guidance. (Concept; figures carry scale bar, north arrow, legend, PROVISIONAL stamp.)

Blue-green public space

"Green chain of beads" weaves station-domain green space: tree-lined corridors link pocket parks, connecting toward the Yuan Dynasty City Wall Relics Park greenway. The Jing-Zhang heritage park vitality belt is organized in three segments (heritage display / slow-traffic vitality / community sharing); a four-tier public-space system: distribution plaza — smart corridor — street living room — pocket park. (Concept)

Buildings

Stock first, precise increments: total station-domain building scale is mostly renewal; concept study of over-track and underground integration; core ring strengthens transit and public-service functions with a "mountain-shaped" descending profile from station to city; a facility component library (corridor segments / info columns / seating / light poles / guidance desks / art bases) with unified interfaces, modular and reversible. (Concept)

Renewal projects

Three-phase list: near (1-3y) plaza micro-renewal, corridor phase I, AI hall pilot, transfer optimization, accessibility completion; mid (3-5y) corridor network, old-community public renovation, street-front opening, concept underground works; long (5-10y) concept over-track study and greenway completion. Pilot responsibility matrix (actor / dependency / data / takeover / complaints / accessibility co-creation / period / thresholds / exit) is in proposal.md. (Concept)
