# TRANSIT-CITY INTEGRATION COORDINATION SYSTEM (Concept)

**English convenience summary** (full binding narrative is the Chinese proposal.md; this summary may lag the zh text).

以「站城融环（RAIL·JZ · 轨道站城一体化统筹体系）」构建沿京张AI创新带的轨道站城一体化统筹概念：以「站城芯」为站城一体化统筹核心节点（轨道站点与城市功能联动统筹，客流与功能数据匿名聚合），「接驳环」为轨道接驳与慢行换乘环（P+R、骑行与步行接驳一体化，与轨道站点慢行接驳），「共构廊」为站城融合公共空间廊道（上盖与桥下空间共享客厅，匿名聚合展示与人工复核），五类AI+场景（客流接驳调度AI、站城联动运营AI、慢行接驳规划AI、上盖物业运维AI、公众站城反馈AI）仅处理匿名聚合数据、关键决策人工复核、禁止过度监控，交通慢行优先、轨道站点与慢行网络一体化接驳、连续慢行轴贯通三节点，绿色出行（轨交＋步行骑行）优先，分期实施+站城统筹年度计划与公众参与渠道。全部为概念建议、参考方案，不给出容积率、建筑高度、具体拆改留或工程实施结论，不编造客流与运营数据，基于 provisional 边界，官方数据发布后复算。

## Outline
- Transit-City Integration (RAIL.JZ): a transit-city integration coordination concept along the Jingzhang AI innovation belt, using the heritage green belt as the backbone.
- Transit-City Nexus as the transit-city integration central node: coordination of station functions and city functions, anonymized aggregation of passenger-flow and functional data.
- Mobility Loop as the rail connection and slow-traffic interchange ring: P+R, cycling and walking interchange organized as one network (concept-level).
- Co-Built Gallery as the transit-city public space gallery: shared living room above the podium and under the viaduct, linking slow traffic and blue-green spaces (no engineering figures preset).
- Node 1 - Transit-City Nexus: transit-city integration central node. Node 2 - Mobility Loop: rail connection and slow-traffic interchange ring. Node 3 - Co-Built Gallery: transit-city shared gallery.
- 5 AI+ scenarios (passenger-flow dispatch AI, transit-city joint operation AI, slow-traffic connection planning AI, podium property operation AI, public transit-city feedback AI); anonymized aggregation only, key decisions human-reviewed, no over-surveillance.
- Slow-traffic-first: a continuous slow-traffic axis along the green belt links the three nodes; rail stations and the slow-traffic network connect as one; green mobility (rail plus walking and cycling) comes first to reduce car dependence; annual events: rail open day, transit-city workshop, slow-traffic connection season.
- All figures are concept suggestions on a provisional boundary; recompute when official data is published.
