Overview map

Concept skeleton: the Jingzhang suburban rail corridor (transport spine) plus the Jingzhang Railway Heritage Park greenway (ecological backbone); the three nodes Nexus-Loop-Gallery are threaded by a continuous slow-mode axis; all concept points, recalculated after official boundaries are released.

Three-tier scopes

Coordinated research area: ~43.6 km² (official announcement); overall design area: ~11.4 km² (official announcement; this package uses a provisional substitute boundary) for concept-level regulatory-depth urban design; key areas: ~368.4 ha (official announcement), matched here by the Zhongzhiyuan AI Self-Innovation Acceleration Area (~192.1 ha - Nexus), Beijing AI Origin Community (~104.3 ha - Loop) and Dazhongsi AI Industry Cluster (~72.0 ha - Gallery).

Key areas

Site-level design: plans - vertical interchange hall and deck podium, ring slow-mode path and P+R roof garden, under-bridge living room and pocket parks; sections - rail / deck / greenway / street in four bands; user journey - exit, transfer, shop-or-work, greenway corridor, arrive home, with accessible and age-friendly design throughout.

Land use

Concept land-use structure (provisional; recalculated under a unified calibre after official release): R&D, commercial, office/finance and park/green dominate; 27 land-use zones and 12 concept building units; shares aggregated from this package's polygons and rounded for display.

Transport & slow modes

Rail-bus-slow-mode tiered feeder system: a primary slow-mode spine along the heritage greenway threads the three nodes; secondary stitch streets run east-west; P+R integrates with cycling; the ~70%-80% green-mode share target is a conceptual range, not a ridership forecast, recalculated when ridership ledgers arrive.

Blue-green public space

The heritage greenway is the backbone linking small green patches and open space; deck and under-bridge spaces stay light, permeable and reversible. Public space is all-ages-friendly, covering seniors, children, people with disabilities, low-digital-capability users, night users and existing small-shop keepers, with accessibility and age-friendly design (concept); supported by a Rail Honour Wall, an eight-component reversible library and three-tier bilingual wayfinding.

Buildings

Retain-renovate-demolish with retention first: heritage park fabric and relics retained; stock buildings converted with reserved station-city directions; demolition limited to case-by-case hazardous scattered buildings. The 12 concept building units are reversible lightweight indications; no building scale or height values are preset.

Renewal projects

Renewal list (concept): Nexus pilot, Loop completion, Gallery stitching, old-neighbourhood micro-renewal and industry-building conversion in a five-type library; phasing: 1-3 years pilots and greenway completion, 3-5 years three-node network and area renewal, 5-10 years corridor-wide optimisation; lead/collaborate division with stop and exit conditions; investment and costs are qualitative tiers (low/medium/high) only.

AI scenarios

Five AI+ scenarios (dispatch, joint operations, slow-mode planning, deck O&M, public feedback) process anonymised aggregates only, with human review of key decisions and no over-monitoring or individual-identifying tracking; 12 scenario cards, 3 industry test protocols and a scenario-space-operation matrix (decision authority and KPIs) are in the proposal; model evaluation, data quality, error stratification and runtime monitoring form the concept technical protocols.
