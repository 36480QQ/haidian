# DIGITAL TWIN OPERATIONS SYSTEM (Concept)

**English convenience summary** (full binding narrative is the Chinese proposal.md; this summary may lag the zh text).

以「数衍智环（DIGI·JZ · 数字孪生运营体系）」构建沿京张AI创新带的数字孪生运营概念：以「孪生枢」为数字孪生运营中枢节点（CIM公共服务底座与共智运营调度），「智检廊」为基础设施数字体检与健康档案节点（轨道桥梁、绿廊与管网运行状态聚合体检），「共营环」为虚实融合公共空间与共智运营体验环（匿名聚合展示与人工复核），五类AI+场景（运行态势推演AI、设施健康诊断AI、客流活动预测AI、能耗碳排监测AI、公众共智反馈AI）仅处理匿名聚合数据、关键决策人工复核、禁止过度监控，交通慢行优先、连续慢行轴贯通三节点，与轨道站点接驳以步行骑行为主，分期实施+共智运营年度计划与公众参与渠道。全部为概念建议、参考方案，不给出容积率、建筑高度、具体拆改留或工程实施结论，不编造运行监测数据，基于 provisional 边界，官方数据发布后复算。

## Outline
- Digital Twin Operations (DIGI.JZ): a city digital-twin operations concept along the Jingzhang AI innovation belt, using the heritage green belt as the backbone.
- Twin Hub as the digital-twin operations central node: CIM public data base and co-ops operation coordination (concept-level public data service suggestion, not official statistics).
- Sense Gallery as the infrastructure digital health-check node: aggregated condition review for rail structures, green corridors and utility networks (concept-level).
- Co-Ops Ring as the physical-virtual public space and co-ops experience ring linking slow traffic and blue-green spaces (no engineering figures preset).
- Node 1 - Twin Hub: digital-twin operations central node. Node 2 - Sense Gallery: infrastructure digital health-check node. Node 3 - Co-Ops Ring: physical-virtual co-ops ring.
- 5 AI+ scenarios (operations simulation AI, facility health diagnosis AI, pedestrian-flow forecasting AI, energy-carbon monitoring AI, public co-ops feedback AI); anonymized aggregation only, key decisions human-reviewed, no over-surveillance.
- Slow-traffic-first: a continuous slow-traffic axis along the green belt links the three nodes; green mobility (rail plus walking and cycling) comes first to reduce car dependence; annual events: digital-twin open day, co-ops workshop, physical-virtual experience season.
- All figures are concept suggestions on a provisional boundary; recompute when official data is published.
