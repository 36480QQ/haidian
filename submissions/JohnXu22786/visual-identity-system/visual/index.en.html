Mobility

Slow mobility first: continuous slow axis linking the three nodes with integrated wayfinding; rail station handoff by walking+cycling; accessible, multilingual, aging-friendly layers; no individual-identification monitoring (concept).

Blue-Green Space

Blue-green system framed by the heritage park belt; light-touch reversible identity facilities that never block heritage views; restrained unified character (concept).

Buildings

Retain-renovate-demolish combined, retention first; light, reversible, low-footprint identity facilities; no numeric conclusions (concept).

Renewal Projects

Four concept project families; phases 1-3 / 3-5 / 5-10 years; pilot = Signage Gallery section; concept gates, stop/exit and withdrawal conditions; RACI roles (concept).
