# FoodChain JZ narrative note

FoodChain JZ uses food as the everyday interface between the Centennial Jing-Zhang cultural story and an AI innovation ecosystem. The spatial proposal is three districts, three kitchens, two service wings, two markets and one food-learning walking loop. Industry mechanisms are deliberately small and reversible: FoodChain Evidence, One Table One Plan, Three Reviews Three Exits and the Grain Pact.

The package is a concept proposal. It uses anonymous aggregation only, human review for key decisions and no excessive surveillance. Residents retain paper, attended and accessible alternatives; merchants can withdraw; unverified prompts are hidden; every annual notice reports errors, appeals, accessibility improvements and exits. Areas, ratios, locations and dates are provisional and must be recalculated after official data release.
