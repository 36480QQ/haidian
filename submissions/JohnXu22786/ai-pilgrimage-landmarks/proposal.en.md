# JING-ZHANG AI PILGRIMAGE: Four Original Landmarks and One Interactive Rail Corridor (Concept)

**Primary name (conceptual suggestion):** 「智脉京张 — Centennial Jing-Zhang AI Innovation Belt」; English primary name: **"Jingzhang Pulse · AI Innovation Belt"**, abbreviated **JZ-PULSE**. "Zhi-mai (智脉)" carries the double image of "the pulse of urban compute" and "the railway artery"; "Pulse" echoes impulse, heartbeat and the continuous cadence of open-source communities — a city corridor that beats with code. Tagline (concept): **"From Steel Rails to Compute Trails"**, supplemented by "Kilometre Zero, Infinite Compute".

**Naming system (concept):** one primary name, three areas, two wings, one network of many stations. Working aliases for the three key areas: Zhongzhi Park → 「众智台 (Crowd-Wisdom Terrace)」, Beijing AI Origin Community → 「原点场 (Origin Field)」, Dazhongsi AI Industry Cluster → 「钟声里 (Inside the Bell Sound)」; the two wings are the 「智服翼 (Smart Services Wing)」 and 「场景翼 (Scenario Wing)」; stations along the corridor form the "Rail Station (轨道驿站)" system with names such as "Kilometre-Zero Station", "Algorithm Station", "Echo Station", "Dawn Bell Station" and "Ark Station", numbered J-01 to J-n (conceptual numbering, subject to detailed design). Event brands (concept): "Kilometre Zero AI Festival", "Open Rails Open-Source Conference", "Crowd-Wisdom Origin Day", "Time Capsule Sealing Ceremony". International residency brand (concept): "Rail Residency".

**Logo direction (textual concept description):** merge a rail cross-section with the mathematical symbol "0" — a dot embedded between two parallel rails, simultaneously evoking a rail profile, the digit zero and the upper arc of the infinity sign; steel cyan as the primary colour with signal red and signal yellow accents (rooted in railway safety colours); must support monochrome reversal, small-size recognition and a Braille-touch version; the Chinese/English wordmark interleaves the rhythmic element "0·1909·∞". Graphic implementation to be deepened by a professional design team.

**Overall concept (conceptual):** give the belt three identities at once — a world-class AI pilgrimage landmark route, an everyday urban park, and a global open-source collaboration site. All spatial expressions are presented as "conceptual suggestions / reference proposals" for professional teams to deepen; they do not constitute statutory planning conclusions.

## Coordinated Study Scope: Industry and Future-City Research

(43.6 km²) suggested conceptual structure: **"one belt, two wings, multiple cores"**.

- **One belt:** the Jing-Zhang Heritage Park AI public-space main belt, carrying culture, ecology and vitality;
- **Two wings:** the Zhongguancun technology-services wing (global allocation of factors, Zhongguancun IP and capital empowerment) and the Xiaoyuehe scenario-empowerment wing (AI scenario enablement and an intelligent AI-vitality city);
- **Multiple cores:** real city nodes — Qinghe, Shangdi/Zhongguancun Software Park, Zhichun Road, Zhongguancun Avenue, Xueyuan Road — as narrative background anchors and functional traffic sources (narrative context, not new statutory zones).

Future-city research topics (conceptual): (1) "work-create-live-play" mixed corridor trends; (2) public-service forms for AI practitioners and global talent (bilingual services, flexible offices, international neighbourhoods as conceptual directions only); (3) the necessity of a municipal special study on compute energy, urban heat and power-distribution coordination (no engineering conclusions); (4) block-scale research on urban-operation AI simulation and governance scenarios. These are research topics for professional teams to deepen.

## Overall Design Scope: Urban Renewal and Regulatory-Plan-Depth Urban Design

(11.4 km²) this layer is positioned as a **research method and principle framework**; it gives no statutory conclusions on FAR, building height, road redlines or retain/renovate/demolish.

Four principle suggestions (conceptual): (1) **rail-first** — use the heritage park as the stitching spine organising functions on both sides; (2) **stock-first** — "assess first, retain by default" for existing buildings and land, with all retain/renovate/demolish conclusions formed through statutory procedures; (3) **scenario-first** — each renewal unit binds 1-2 AI scenario sites so renewal has experienceable content; (4) **resilience-first** — green and open space also serve stormwater retention, heat-island mitigation and other resilience functions.

"Regulatory-plan depth" working method: a four-step chain of "existing-condition assessment — scenario insertion — form iteration — compliance check"; divide research units by the organiser's final boundary and compile a "unit scenario card" and "unit compliance self-check table" as inputs to subsequent statutory planning and urban design.

## Key-Area Detailed Design

### Zhongzhi Park AI Independent-Innovation Acceleration Area

(approx. 192 ha, official textual scope) carries the AI full-stack independent innovation system and global AI-governance voice (conceptual positioning).

Spatial concept 「three-level terrace」: the heritage-park green belt as the low-level public layer, with R&D pilot and public-service functions organised progressively on both sides (a formal intention, not a height conclusion). Governance-voice space: an "AI Governance Simulation Hall" concept — public rule-sandbox exercises, issue-hearing drills and an evaluation-report release hall; the space stresses "auditable, reviewable" expression: transparent hearing rooms, public agenda screens, minutes bulletin boards (all content human-reviewed). It is paired with the 「Algorithm Dawn Bell」 landmark and the 「Crowd-Wisdom Contribution Wall」 honour installation (see public-space chapter).

### Beijing AI Origin Community

(approx. 104 ha, official textual scope) positioned as the "origin" narrative district of a world-class AI innovation ecosystem.

Ecosystem elements: a "Release Stage" launch platform, mentor duty corner, developer honour wall and a ceremonial "Contributor Walk" (paving inscribed with contributor signature motifs selected by the community with the individual's consent — conceptual).

The 「Crowd-Source Ark」 landmark is suggested for this area (see public-space chapter). Adjacent to the university/research cluster belt, the concept stresses "campus-street" seamless slow-traffic links (without changing statutory land-use nature or management boundaries).

### Dazhongsi AI Industry Cluster

(approx. 72 ha, official textual scope) positioned as an intelligent-native new-business cluster (conceptual).

Cultural anchor: the Dazhongsi Ancient Bell Museum and the Yongle Great Bell cultural tradition (per information published by heritage authorities); working theme 「Inside the Bell Sound」 — a dialogue between the bell's cultural rhythm and algorithmic rhythm.

Intelligent-native consumption scenario concepts: a "Rail Market" night market (AI-curated music, smart-product pop-up experience stalls), "Sense Window" shopfronts (curation based on public aggregated footfall data — aggregation only, no identification), "Smart Newsstand" community service points (smart-product trials with human attendants), and a consumer-AI compliance evaluation concept store (making the "evaluation-publication" process a visitable public programme). Business scenario concepts: new-product release gallery, vertical-application showrooms and a fast-service window for small and medium developers (mechanism suggestions, not policy commitments).

## Land Use, Building Scale, and Retain/Renovate/Demolish

This proposal gives **no** land-use reclassification, building-scale figures or retain/renovate/demolish conclusions; the following are working-method suggestions (conceptual):

**"Three assessments, one list" method:** (1) existing-building assessment (quality, structure, use intensity); (2) heritage-value assessment (railway relics, old station buildings, industrial structures, per the heritage authorities' published list); (3) ecological-value assessment (mature trees, existing woodland, waterfront, bird corridors). Results feed a rolling retain/renovate/demolish list concluded by professional teams through statutory procedures.

**Scale-derivation method suggestion:** scenario approach — "conservative / balanced / aggressive" scale scenarios for discussion between professional teams and authorities; this proposal presets no values; all scenarios hold the landscape-ecological bottom lines (green lines, blue lines, mature-tree protection) and rail protection zones as hard constraints. **Untouchable bottom lines:** heritage assets and their protection zones, planned green space and blue lines, existing mature trees, rail protection zones (for the Jing-Zhang high-speed railway and urban rail, per statutory requirements).

## Blue-Green Space, Public Space and Urban Character

**I. Blue-green system (conceptual):** the Jing-Zhang Heritage Park green belt as the "spine", the Xiaoyuehe and Qinghe waterfront green belts as the "wings", and rail-station pocket parks as "knots" — a "one spine, two wings, many knots" network; blue-line, green-line and heritage constraints are respected throughout; mature trees are protected; waterfronts are near-natural.

**II. East-west stitching and north-south continuity (conceptual):** visual and pedestrian links across the rail corridor via "over-rail passages" — pedestrian-priority level crossings and "over-rail gates" at key nodes (conceptual intentions; the specific form — overpass or underpass — is for professional teams to study through statutory procedures, no engineering conclusions). North-south continuity relies on the park's longitudinal movement line, divided into "memory segment — vitality segment — future segment", all sharing one station-numbering system, ensuring a continuous experience of the kilometre-scale linear space (length per official wording).

**III. Interactive rail trail (conceptual):** preserved old rails and sleepers as the trail motif, with three low-intensity interactions: **Rail Piano** (stepping on sleepers triggers musical notes; sound material co-created by local artists and developers), **Switch Plaza** (a movable switch model changes the trail-lighting colour theme), **Sleeper Rhythm** (walking rhythm drives local lighting; only aggregate step-frequency values are sensed). All interaction data is processed anonymously on-device, can be switched off with one button, and a **sensor-free quiet zone** is provided — areas completely free of active sensing devices for citizens who prefer "digital silence".

**IV. AI public-space component library (conceptual):**

- **Smart benches:** seat heating/cooling (climate-triggered), reservation reminders, on-demand human service calls; no biometrics; call-records retention disclosed;
- **Interactive light poles:** light breathing with pedestrian rhythm, anonymous counting only, no identification; self-diagnostics in parallel with manual inspection;
- **Honour-display installations:** QR/contactless-card readable; displayed content is **voluntarily submitted and personally authorised**; Braille, voice and sign-language video versions; withdrawal at any time;
- **Barrier-free and age-friendly:** step-free as a goal, age-friendly handrails and seat heights, large-print and voice guides, resident "rail guide" volunteers; digital interfaces must pass age-friendly evaluation before launch; all auto-generated content (guides, lists, minutes) **must be human-reviewed by the operator before publication**.

**V. Privacy and human-review boundary (overall principle):** on-site sensing performs aggregate counting only — no facial recognition, licence plates or individual trajectory capture; data stays local and de-identified statistics are periodically published; any "scoring, profiling, tracking" description is prohibited; honour displays, auto guides and review conclusions all follow "machine drafts, human reviews, personal authorisation, withdrawal anytime". This proposal rejects any over-surveillance or non-human-reviewable scenario design.

**VI. AI pilgrimage landmarks (4 original concepts; spatial positions are conceptual suggestions)**

**1. 「Jing-Zhang Zero-Kilometre Coordinate Monument」** — suggested at the junction of the south section of the heritage park and the Dazhongsi area (conceptual location). Historical rigour: the actual origin of the Jing-Zhang Railway was at Liucun (per authoritative sources); this monument is a thematic "cultural coordinate", not a claim of the official origin. **Form concept:** a steel-rail ring embedded in the ground with concentric scales marking "1909—today—future", a zero-glyph stele at the centre, in weathering steel and transparent resin, prefabricated and reversible. **Experience:** walking around it triggers an annular "centennial scale" illumination; the base carries Braille and multilingual inscriptions. **Honour display:** the annual "Kilometre Zero Tribute" list as replaceable nameplates around the base, with individual consent and human review.

**2. 「Compute Hill · Algorithm Dawn Bell」** — suggested at the north end of Zhongzhi Park on the edge of open green space facing the Qinghe (conceptual location), using minimal terrain treatment without encroaching on the green-line core. **Form concept:** a low landscape hill topped by a bell-shaped acoustic structure in prefabricated timber-steel. **Experience:** at dawn and dusk the bell sequence is driven by the hash of community-submitted code from the previous day; timbres are preset by an art team and human-released; the rhythm's "code source" is queryable at nearby stations — fully traceable. **Honour display:** the weekly list of selected "Dawn Bell code" contributors and code summaries on the hill-foot wall — public submissions with voluntary attribution.

**3. 「Crowd-Source Ark」** — suggested at the central plaza of the Beijing AI Origin Community (conceptual location). **Form concept:** a boat-shaped open gallery and deliberation ground; a double-pitched "deck" roof collects rainwater and provides shade, symbolising the "ark of open-source wisdom". **Experience:** deck walkways, in-hull open-source proposal boards, the bow launch stage (Release Stage). **Honour display:** a global open-source contribution honour board and biographies of "Crowd-Wisdom Medal" recipients, displayed only with personal authorisation.

**4. 「AI Origin Sundial」** — suggested at the junction of the Zhichun Road node and the heritage park (conceptual location). **Form concept:** a symbolic steel rail as the gnomon, ground time-lines corresponding to an "AI milestone chronology" (entries vetted by an academic committee), with a giant scale inlaid in the plaza pavement. **Experience:** noon light-calibration ceremonies and daily step-on time reading. **Honour display:** annual "Origin Record" individuals/teams engraved as annual nameplates; vetting and display are human-verified with personal consent.

All four landmarks share: no encroachment on heritage assets or protection zones; no reduction of green space; prefabricated reversible installation; subdued night lighting to protect birds (light-pollution control). Specific sites and structures are for professional teams to deepen against heritage, landscape and rail-safety requirements.

**VII. Urban character guidance (conceptual):** materials of steel, stone, timber and recycled concrete continuing the railway industrial vocabulary; colour system of steel-cyan-grey base with signal red/yellow accents; skyline follows the basic order of "low along the park, rising towards both sides" (formal intention, not a height conclusion); nightscape of "rail light-belt + landmark point-lights" with strict light-pollution control.

**VIII. Cultural narrative, signage and symbol system (agent.5):** three narratives — (1) the century-long relay from the first state-built trunk railway to open-source AI; (2) "a global AI living room inside a park"; (3) "measuring innovation with the rail as the ruler". The signage system uses the "rail symbol" as motif: continuous rail lines as wayfinding lines, station numbers J-01… and distance scales ("XX to Kilometre Zero") as the positional language; the symbol system includes the "0∞" primary mark and the "three parallel tracks" motif (rail / data flow / people flow). International communication emphasises real scenarios, real evaluation, real honour — all outward-facing material is labelled "conceptual stage" and subject to review.
