Three-Level Scope

Official announcement calibers: coordinated research area approx 43.6 km2; overall design area approx 11.4 km2; key areas totalling approx 368.4 ha. This package sits inside the overall design scope as the barrier-free age-friendly concept subset; all boundaries are provisional and recomputed when official geometry is published.

Key Areas

Three concept nodes: Wu'ai Post (barrier-free slow-traffic and transit service), Yilao Block (age-friendly community renewal demonstration), Zhiban Pavilion (AI-assisted age-friendly public information). The nodes are linked by a continuous accessible slow-traffic spine; the service interface covers continuous accessible route, crossing, ramp, rest, shade, information and human service; all locations are conceptual.

Land-Use Zones

Conceptual land-use structure (qualitative tiers, not verified percentages): park green along the heritage park band is structurally dominant; R&D, business-finance and urban residential are supporting; commercial and education-R&D are supplementary; culture and transport stations are minor. Classification follows the MNR land/sea use guide; recompute when official data is published; no unverified percentages are stated.

Slow Traffic

Slow traffic first: a continuous accessible slow-traffic axis along the green belt links the three nodes; step-free rail station link routes, entrance ramps, age-friendly crossings (curb ramps, accessible signals) and time-responsive street profiles are concept directions; green mobility first, lower car dependence.

Blue-Green Network

"Green belt as axis, node parks as pearls": the heritage park belt links pocket parks and community green spaces; the age-friendly park concept includes gentle paths, anti-slip paving, rest seats and a shade network; cultural wayfinding serves age-friendly information (large type, multilingual, voice).

Buildings

Retain-renovate-demolish with retention first: heritage park fabric and railway relics are kept; existing buildings undergo functional conversion and accessibility retrofits (ramps, lift-feasibility review, wayfinding upgrades); demolition is limited to case-by-case buildings unblocking slow-traffic links; no scale figures are preset.

Renewal Projects

Four concept categories: accessibility check-up and digital ledger; slow-traffic gap treatment and crossing completion; three-node demonstration; age-friendly public services and community operation network. Phasing: near 1-3 years, mid 3-5 years, long 5-10 years. Annual programme brands: Accessibility Experience Day, Age-Friendly Living Bazaar, Intergenerational Cohesion Season.

AI Scenarios

Ten scenario cards (Wu'ai Route, Yilao Block, Zhiban Desk, Check-up Sheet, Companion, Safe Crossing, Warm Bench, Pocket Classroom, Intergenerational Stage, Guard List) and three industry test scenarios (route-planning model test, facility ledger recognition test, voice response service test); all process anonymized aggregates only, with human review of key decisions and no over-surveillance; all are concept suggestions, not approved services.
