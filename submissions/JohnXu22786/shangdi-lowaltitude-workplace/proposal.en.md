# SHANGDI SKYLINK LIVING CIRCLE: Low-Altitude Economy Nodes in Everyday Workplace Public Space

**English convenience summary** (full binding narrative is the Chinese proposal.md; this summary may lag the zh text).

以上地—中关村软件园为核心，以「三节点+十场景」将低空经济（配送、通勤接驳、应急、科普）嵌入职场人日常公共空间：中关村软件园配送枢纽与低空起降第一坪、京张铁路遗址公园上地段低空科普走廊、上地环岛通勤接驳与应急联动十字枢纽；强调平急两用、存量更新、可逆化设施与净空安全，全部为概念建议，基于 provisional 边界。

## Outline
- Three-Layer Scope: coordinated research (industry & future-city), overall design (~2.1 sq km concept), key-area detailed design (3 nodes).
- Node 1 - Zhongguancun Software Park: delivery hub + vertiport demo ("First Sky-Landing Pad"), building-station-route 3-level delivery.
- Node 2 - Jing-Zhang Railway Heritage Park (Shangdi section): low-altitude science corridor, old-platform memory stop.
- Node 3 - Shangdi Roundabout: commuter sky-shuttle + emergency linkage, cross-hub sky viewing deck.
- 10 scenario cards (shuttle, delivery, sightseeing, patrol, emergency drop, coffee delivery, smart-pole pads, airspace data wall, science education, test-flight training) + 3 test & validation scenarios.
- 18 renewal projects in 3 phases (0-2 / 2-4 / 4-6 years, concept).
- All figures are concept suggestions on a provisional boundary; recompute when official data is published.
