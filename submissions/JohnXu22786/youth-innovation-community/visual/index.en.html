Mobility and slow traffic

Slow-traffic-first concept: the north-south spine along the Jing-Zhang heritage park greenway and three east-west conceptual stitches; integrated night-return lighting and safe-path design (continuous graded lighting, open sightlines, app-free help points, readable-after-dark wayfinding, noise zoning). Transport and municipal conclusions remain unknown; no road alignments, rail alignments or quantities are given.

Blue-green public space

Blue-green network: 'three courts, one garden, one belt' - maker green court, community garden, tree-lined plaza plus greenway strips and pocket nodes; a 'youth 15-minute public life circle' (concept name only - NOT a verified time isochrone; this proposal does not equate the 2 km example radius with a 15-minute walk; network isochrones await official road-network data and must be recomputed with actual routes and walking speed) with durable-repairable, reversible-replaceable components and login-free human-service equivalent paths and accessible routes.

Buildings

Construction and retain-renovate-demolish are premised on surveys of current conditions, ownership, heritage, structure, fire safety and accessibility (concept): stock-first, reversible light-fit with reversible components; no FAR, height, footprint or total-scale figures; no demolition areas are presupposed.

Renewal projects

Three concept project categories (co-creation space/living support/vitality operation) plus conceptual phasing: near term (1-3y) pilots, mid term (3-5y) network completion, long term (5-10y) loop connection and brand assets; every phase is premised on 'staffable, maintainable, reversible, publicly vetoable'; the pilot execution table (lead/collaborators/preliminary survey/cost grade/maintenance/data minimization/human review/accessibility alternative/KPI/complaints/stop conditions) is in proposal.md.

AI scenarios

Ten scenario cards (C-01 to C-10: job coach, rent-commute, meetup, startup mentor, feedback, night return, desk share, childcare, accessible wayfinding, community events) plus three industry test-verification scenario cards (T-01 to T-03); all follow the four elements 'human-first service, AI drafts only, named human review, instant removal', with anonymized aggregation only and no individual-identifying tracking.
