GREEN.JZ: Zero-Carbon Demo Campus & Green Energy Synergy System (Concept)

This is the English translation of the Chinese proposal.md (binding narrative). GREEN.JZ (Zero-Carbon Demo Campus & Green Energy Synergy System) is a zero-carbon corridor concept along the Jingzhang AI Innovation Belt (public taskbook dimension: low-carbon, green innovation environment; national dual-carbon policy direction): one campus (Zero-Carbon Demo Campus, Zhongzhiyuan side), one corridor (Green-Energy Corridor, AI Origin Community side) and one station (Energy Service Station, Dazhongsi side); five AI+ energy scenarios plus seven supplementary scenario cards (12 cards in total) process only anonymised aggregated data with human review of key decisions and no over-monitoring; six mechanism concepts (green-power purchase and certificates, carbon-benefit points, virtual power plant aggregation, zero-carbon compact, annual carbon-efficiency disclosure, developer open interfaces); six benchmark cases summarised from public channels only and registered per item in sources.json; six land-use ratios and three formal core indicators recomputed from this package's geometry under a single calibre; regional coordination with Beiwei Community, Future Science City, Huairou Science City, E-Town (Beijing Economic-Technological Development Area) and the Jing-Jin-Ji region; the brand identity is an original concept treated as an internal working codename. All content is concept-level suggestion; no FAR, building height, demolition, generation capacity, energy-consumption, investment or engineering conclusions are given; no official data are fabricated; nothing is presented as a confirmed decision; based on provisional boundaries, recompute when official data are published.

Coordinated Research Area: Industry and Future City Research

Industry direction and future-city judgement

"Low-carbon green innovation environment" is read as a long-term operable industry line: five AI+ scenario families (energy optimisation, green-power dispatch, carbon-efficiency tracking, storage safety, carbon benefit) are both public services and test-and-validation scenarios open to green-tech enterprises. The future-city research direction is "energy as an urban interface": PV, storage and carbon-efficiency data move from back-office equipment into walkways, corridors and stations as public experience, letting residents and visitors see energy flow rather than only equipment.

Naming, identity and ecosystem-map direction

The Chinese name GREEN JZ ("绿能智环") stresses the integration of green energy and smart synergy; the English name GREEN.JZ anchors locality through the Jing-Zhang abbreviation. The original identity direction: a ring trajectory overlaid with vein and grid symbols, expressing energy circulation, monitoring and disclosure on the loop; it is not modelled on any existing city, park or enterprise mark (full VI rules in the "Brand Identity" chapter; usage boundary before trademark clearance in the risk chapter).

Green-energy industry ecosystem map (concept): organised around a "deliverable energy-carbon service loop", six actor types (park operators, green-tech enterprises, developers, universities and research institutes, communities and residents, public bodies) and eight element types (land, space, industry, capital, talent, computing, data, scenarios) form a traceable map: any element enters the next loop only with a deliverable (scenario cards, test protocols, acceptance indicators); every node is registered in compliance_matrix.json and the developer-calibre ledger, and the map is refreshed with recalculation when official data are published.

Mapping of the three positioning goals and five functions

Per the public taskbook, GREEN.JZ maps the three positioning goals: as the green narrative anchor of the century-old Jingzhang culture belt, extending the railway industrial-power memory into a public zero-carbon-energy experience; as the Haidian daily interface of the urban AI living-experience belt, making energy stations and carbon enquiries an everyday AI urban service for residents and visitors; as the energy-synergy node of the AI-integrated innovation belt, plugging the green-energy system into the test-and-validation loop of the AI innovation ecosystem. The five functions each have an anchoring: the AI full-stack independent innovation system maps to the demo-campus validation-scenario opening; the world-class AI innovation ecosystem maps to the zero-carbon-compact co-creation ecosystem and the ecosystem map; the AI+ scenario empowerment paradigm maps to the five AI+ energy scenario cards and the industry test protocols; the intelligent AI vibrant city maps to the corridor and station public-experience interface; the global AI-governance voice maps to the governance calibre of anonymised aggregation, human review and tiered disclosure. This mapping is the proposal's own consistency statement, not an official judgement on the belt's overall layout.

Overall Design Area: Urban Renewal and Regulatory-Plan-Level Urban Design

The overall structure is the "GREEN.JZ loop": the existing green belt and slow-mobility system form the skeleton linking three nodes - Zero-Carbon Demo Campus (Zhongzhiyuan side), Green-Energy Corridor (AI Origin Community side) and Energy Service Station (Dazhongsi side) - into a conceptual ring-like green-energy synergy system. The loop is not a new road redline but a concept structure that can be re-positioned as a whole once official boundaries arrive; it corresponds to the three-zones/two-wings synergy loop: the campus anchors Zhongzhiyuan's "full-stack validation", the corridor anchors the AI Origin Community's "public experience of the world-class innovation ecosystem", and the station anchors Dazhongsi's "community service end of native intelligent new business forms".

The renewal orientation is retention-first and green-belt stitching: prioritise existing roofs, green-belt edges and public spaces for energy-facility intentions, translating "east-west stitching, north-south connection" into green-belt gap repair and slow-mobility continuity. At regulatory-plan depth, land-use structure intentions and energy-siting principles (roof, green belt, community edge - three priority interfaces) are proposed; FAR, height and intensity judgements remain for the statutory process; this proposal gives no conclusion.

Detailed Design of Key Areas

All three nodes follow retention-first, reversible light-adaptation and pending-review principles; no building heights, scales or engineering conclusions are given; every key-area map carries geographic context, legend, north arrow, scale bar and a provisional stamp.

Zero-Carbon Demo Campus (Zhongzhiyuan side)

Function: a conceptual demonstration venue for the campus-level zero-carbon practice cluster, concentrating distributed PV, storage, green-power trading and carbon-benefit intentions, linked to the AI full-stack innovation system's "test and validate" role - an energy practice site and an opened validation-scenario venue for green-tech enterprises.

Space: an energy practice cluster built on existing open public space, roofs and built stock, without relying on new large structures; specific siting awaits ownership, structural and safety review.

Public experience interface: a carbon-efficiency visual wall and demonstration route; visitors see anonymised aggregated displays of PV, storage and carbon data along a publicised route; developers learn about openable validation scenarios through public interfaces; all data are anonymised-aggregated with human review.

Green-Energy Corridor (AI Origin Community side)

Function: an energy display and slow-mobility corridor along the green belt, carrying the conceptual intentions of a PV walkway and energy science nodes, turning commuting and resting routes into a zero-carbon education interface.

Space: a sectional composite of the linear green belt - continuous walking and cycling, shaded seating and science nodes along the corridor; energy facilities are embedded as reversible light components, not conflicting with green-belt, heritage or safety constraints.

Public experience interface: science nodes explain "understandable" energy knowledge - how PV generates, how storage works, how carbon efficiency is calculated - through objects, graphics and touchable devices, without forcing apps or screen interaction.

Energy Service Station (Dazhongsi side)

Function: a campus-and-community energy service node carrying charging, storage and carbon-enquiry service intentions - the daily-life service outlet of the GREEN.JZ loop.

Space: a composite service intention on existing community-edge stock, co-locating charging with rest waiting and neighbourhood services near existing rail transfers and pedestrian flows; specific siting awaits ownership and fire-safety review.

Public experience interface: carbon-enquiry terminals and neighbourhood charging; residents can query anonymised aggregated carbon-efficiency information of the park and blocks and join carbon-benefit point intentions; the enquiry process does not collect individually identifiable information. Suggestion boxes and a carbon-disclosure feedback channel sit beside the service interface; residents may comment on disclosure calibre, data scope and service experience; opinions are aggregated and answered publicly on a quarterly basis at the station and online (concept). Charging bays may adopt conceptual reservation and rotation rules, to be decided with the operator and community.

AI Innovation Ecosystem, Personas, and AI+ Scenarios

Ecosystem positioning and developer community

The "zero-carbon compact" is the conceptual bond organising park operators, green-tech enterprises, developers, residents and public bodies into a co-creation ecosystem: the compact sets data-opening boundaries, disclosure frequency and human-review responsibilities. Land, space, industry, capital, talent, computing, data and scenarios run around a closed deliverable energy-carbon service loop; no element enters the next loop without a deliverable.

Developer community and conversion pathway (concept): open interfaces (conceptual API) and a sandbox environment host green-tech enterprise and developer energy-carbon app development; the community runs in four steps - "open data dictionary - sandbox testing - scenario listing - pilot conversion": scenario listings use the industry test protocols as admission criteria; pilot conversion is bounded by acceptance indicators and exit conditions; the conversion ledger is disclosed annually (anonymised aggregation). The developer community shares the same data bottom line and human-review rules as the industry test scenarios.

The energy system unfolds six domains: transport - slow-mobility first and charging services; industry - validation and testing scenarios for green-tech enterprises; space - energy placement on the blue-green skeleton and public interface; public services - station-level charging, enquiry and rest; culture - Jingzhang industrial memory and low-carbon science narratives; governance - anonymised aggregation, human review, tiered disclosure and feedback loops. All six domains share one data bottom line and human-review rule; any domain must pass an anonymisation audit before going live.

Land Use, Building Scale, and Retain-Renovate-Demolish Strategy

Land-use expression uses a conceptual intention over the provisional overall extent with no unlabelled gaps; statutory land-use nature is untouched; after official geometry is published, the same algorithm re-slices and recomputes. This draft gives no FAR, building-height or building-footprint numbers - such indicators depend on unpublished official control conditions, remain unknown with stated reasons, and no concept value fabricates precision.

Single land-use recalibration calibre: names and codes follow the public subset of the MNR Land-Use Classification Guide (2023); six classes: Residential (0701), Research/Science (0802), Culture (0803), Commercial (0901), Business/Finance (0902), Park and Green Space (1401). Aggregation rule: merge all same-code polygons of this package's geometry/land_use.geojson per class, project to EPSG:4548, union area divided by this package's site_area_sqm. Results (shares ca.: residential 9.7%, research 30.4%, culture 3.1%, commercial 12.8%, business/finance 16.3%, park and green 27.8%, summing to ca. 100%) are identical in metrics.json, the land-use figure, HTML pages and drawings; confidence low (provisional design-model values); recompute trigger: any publication of official boundary, land-use or statutory control data triggers a full re-slice with the same algorithm. This calibre is not a judgement of statutory land-use nature.

The retain-renovate-demolish path has four steps: verify status and rights first; keep when safety, heritage and function are satisfied; lightly renovate when accessibility, fire safety, energy saving or service interfaces need it; relocate or remove concept components when unsafe or conflicting. Without investigation, no demolition area is proposed and no plot-level conclusion is given. Building character follows a low-carbon technology aesthetic - PV and storage facilities are designable public-interface elements, low-glare, durable and repairable, not "AI-shaped" objects.

Blue-Green Network, Public Space, and Urban Character

The blue-green network uses the existing green belt as the backbone, forming a conceptual network linking the three nodes, connecting to existing green resources such as the Jingzhang Heritage Park (public information only); the concept green ratio green_ratio is recomputed from provisional geometry and keeps a low-confidence marker. Public space prioritises continuous shade, shaded seating, science nodes and slow-path gap repair - shade, rest and wayfinding remain fully usable without network or AI.

Public-space and facility components follow the all-age-friendly principle: elderly-friendly seating and large-print instructions for seniors; touchable science interaction for children and families; full barrier-free access for people with disabilities plus tactile wayfinding and audio announcements as alternative channels; low-digital-literacy users receive equivalent service through human windows and paper instructions; residents, visitors, youth and practitioners share one route. Inclusive design is the default attribute of GREEN.JZ, not an add-on. Participation effectiveness is tracked with concept indicators (quarterly opinion-response rate, disclosure-review coverage, barrier-free spot checks) in the indicator chapter.

Reversible public-space component library (concept): components are managed in three states - installable, suspendable, removable; the first batch includes modular PV canopies (shade plus generation), storage cabinets (demonstration), carbon-enquiry terminals, charging posts, science devices, shaded seating, wayfinding boards and Braille/voice announcers; each component registers an asset ID, size module, maintenance owner and stop condition; no single-supplier dependency; the library is reviewed and recomputed when official control data are published.

Urban-character keywords are "transparent, readable, durable": energy facilities are neither hidden nor decorated but displayed as public interfaces with their principles and status; cultural wayfinding integrates with science communication (Jingzhang railway industrial memory + energy science + barrier-free tactile wayfinding in one system), avoiding viral or entertainment-style expression. The future feeling comes from visible, explainable energy flow, not neon skins. The formulas, sources and recompute triggers of public_space_ratio and green_ratio are in the indicator chapter.

Brand Identity, Honour Display, and International Communication (concept)

Brand identity and VI application rules

The "GREEN JZ" identity is an original concept: a ring trajectory overlaid with vein and grid symbols expressing circulation, monitoring and disclosure of energy on the loop; standard colours are Energy Blue, Ecology Green and Service Amber; application rules include pairing Chinese and English wordmarks, clear space no smaller than one ring diameter, no stretching/recolouring, and no usage resembling any government or park mark. Honour display system (concept)

International communication vehicles (concept)

Metrics, Area Recalculation, and Compliance Matrix

The concept indicator system has three groups: spatial indicators (site_area_sqm, green_ratio, public_space_ratio and the six land-use ratios, all recomputed from this package's geometry under the single calibre), mechanism indicators (compact participation count, scenario-card count, case count, persona count, annual-programme count - concept counts only, no fabricated current values), governance indicators (annual disclosure frequency, human-review coverage, opinion-response rate - intention-level wording).

The three formal core indicators are recomputed from provisional geometry: site_area_sqm is the projected area of the submitted site_boundary geometry in EPSG:4548; green_ratio = green-space geometry area / site area; public_space_ratio = public-space geometry area / site area. All three are low-confidence design-model values with provisional markers, sources and formulas; upon publication of official boundary, green-space and public-space geometry they are recomputed immediately, and the data-value numbers in visual/index.html are updated in step so indicators and geometry stay consistent. Indicators depending on unpublished official control conditions (FAR, building height) remain unknown with values null and reasons stated.

The compliance matrix covers: all three positioning goals and five functions mapped to the proposal chapters; the three-zones/two-wings synergy loop anchored by the campus-corridor-station; agent.1-6 tasks each anchored in chapters (naming and VI, innovation ecosystem and ecosystem map, scenario cards and test protocols, public space with honour display and component library, cultural narrative with international communication and wayfinding, long-term operation with the developer community and stage gates); the "concept suggestion" wording runs through the whole text per the boundary clauses; the forbidden-statement checklist passes item by item (no regulatory conclusions, demolition, engineering estimates, investment or policy commitments).

Risk, Copyright, and Compliance

Policy risk: green-power purchase, certificates and VPP aggregation touch electricity-market policy and market access; this proposal is concept-level only; dedicated policy and compliance study is required before formal implementation; nothing is stated as a confirmed arrangement.

Data risk: site geometry is a provisional rough constraint that could be misread as an official redline; the whole text uses "concept suggestion" and "reference proposal" wording; on official data publication the established formulas recompute and update; no false precision is created (ratios and areas shown as approximations or one-decimal values; formulas, sources and confidence registered with metrics.json).

Technical risk: the five AI+ scenarios are concept-validation intentions providing suggestions, visualisations and alerts only - never directly controlling grid or equipment - and are not stated as fully deployable; any scenario must pass the four protocol set (model evaluation, data quality, error stratification, runtime monitoring) before launch and is removed under the stop conditions on failure.

Privacy and data bottom line: data are anonymised-aggregated with human review; no over-monitoring (no individual-identifying tracking); by default no facial recognition, personal profiling, forced login or forced app; complaint, appeal and stop-use mechanisms precede pilot implementation; a privacy audit with a published summary (concept) is required before pilot launch.

Brand prior rights and usage boundary: no trademark search has been completed at the concept stage; "GREEN JZ" and its mark direction are internal working codenames for display within this proposal only; external use, registration or commercial promotion requires prior-rights search and clearance first; this proposal claims no unverified trademark rights and creates no confusing use with any existing city, park or enterprise mark.
