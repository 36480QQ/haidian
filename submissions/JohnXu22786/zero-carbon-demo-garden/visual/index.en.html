Overview map

One campus, one corridor, one station: Zero-Carbon Demo Campus (Zhongzhiyuan side), Green-Energy Corridor (AI Origin Community side) and Energy Service Station (Dazhongsi side) along the Jingzhang green belt. Official three-level scope (announcement): coordinated research ~43.6 km2 / overall design ~11.4 km2 / key areas ~368.4 ha; this package is the overall-design-level GREEN.JZ sub-scope (provisional boundary).

Three-level scope

Coordinated research ~43.6 km2: green-industry and future-city opportunities; overall design ~11.4 km2: campus-corridor-station plus green-belt slow-mobility skeleton (this package's provisional boundary, areas recomputed from package geometry); key areas ~368.4 ha: three key-area node prototypes. Slow mobility

Slow-mobility first: the green-energy corridor is a combined walking and cycling route that repairs green-belt gaps, continuous shade and night lighting; rail links remain a station-integration concept; vehicle movement is not AI-controlled.

Blue-green public space

Blue-green network intention built on the existing green belt: continuous shade, shaded seating, science nodes and slow-path gap repair; shade, rest and wayfinding remain fully usable without network or AI.

Buildings

Retain-renovate-demolish with retention first: adaptive reuse of existing buildings with light, reversible zero-carbon facility interfaces; modular PV, storage and rest components managed by a component library (asset ID, maintenance duty, stop conditions); no building scale assumed.

Renewal projects

Three concept project lists: energy facilities (distributed PV, storage, charging, science nodes), synergy mechanisms (green-power purchase and certificates, VPP aggregation, zero-carbon compact), carbon governance (carbon-benefit points, annual carbon disclosure, anonymised aggregation); three phases 1-3/3-5/5-10 years; pilot stage gates G0-G4 and exit conditions in proposal.md.

AI scenarios

12 scenario cards (five AI+ energy scenarios plus offline-usable scenarios; detail in proposal.md): energy-optimisation AI, green-power dispatch AI, carbon-efficiency tracking AI, storage-safety AI and carbon-benefit AI process only anonymised aggregated data with human review of key decisions, no over-monitoring (no individual-identifying tracking); 3 industry test protocols (green-power dispatch simulation testbed, carbon-accounting reconciliation test, storage-safety alert test); model evaluation, data quality, error stratification and runtime monitoring protocols (concept).
