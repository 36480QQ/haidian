# KNOWRING: Xueyuan Road Campus Innovation Belt (Concept)

**English convenience summary** (full binding narrative is the Chinese proposal.md; this summary may lag the zh text).

以学院路环高校地带为载体组织「环知里」(KnowRing)校城融合知识环概念：一环三节点——知环门（知识环廊门户：知识步道+校城共享广场+AI科普展亭）、青创里（青年共创活力街区：共享实验室+创业孵化+青年公寓）、智训场（校城共享AI教育实训节点），五类AI+场景（AI教育科普、实验室开放日、青年创业孵化、无障碍伴随、绿色共享出行）嵌入街区日常，数据匿名聚合+人工复核、禁止个体追踪，高校边界开放共享、存量楼宇青年化改造，设施模块化可逆。全部为概念建议、参考方案，基于 provisional 边界，官方数据发布后复算。

## Outline
- KnowRing (Xueyuan Road Campus Innovation Belt): one knowledge loop, three concept nodes under the "campus-city integration" theme - knowledge as a ring, youth at the core.
- Node 1 - Knowledge Ring Gate (Zhi Huan Men): loop portal with a knowledge walk, shared campus-city plaza and AI-science kiosks.
- Node 2 - Youth Innovation Lane (Qing Chuang Li): co-creation blocks with shared labs, start-up incubation and youth housing.
- Node 3 - AI Training Ground (Zhi Xun Chang): campus-city shared AI education & training node.
- 5 AI+ scenarios embedded in neighbourhood daily life (AI education, open labs, youth incubation, barrier-free accompaniment, green shared mobility); all data anonymized and human-reviewed, no individual tracking.
- Campus-city integration renewal in 3 phases (1-3 / 3-5 / 5-10 years, concept).
- All figures are concept suggestions on a provisional boundary; recompute when official data is published.
