Overview map

Three concept nodes (Knowledge Ring Gate, Youth Innovation Lane, AI Training Ground) and the conceptual knowledge loop; schematic base from provisional geometry with public site references (Xueyuan Road, Zhichun Road, Jingzhang Heritage Park).

Three-level scope

Coordinated research area approx. 43.6 km2 (official text caliber); overall design area approx. 11.4 km2 (official text caliber); key detailed-design areas approx. 368.4 ha in total (official text caliber). This package's sub-scope is the campus-innovation belt inside the overall design area (concept); all geometry is provisional.

Key areas

Three areas and two wings: Zhongzhiyuan AI acceleration area, Beijing AI Origin Community, Dazhongsi AI industry cluster + Zhongguancun tech-service wing, Xiaoyue River scenario wing; coordination nodes include Beiwei Community, Future Science City, Huairou Science City, E-Town and Jing-Jin-Ji (concept assumptions; see the regional coordination figure).

Land use

Single caliber (each land-use code area divided by the site-boundary area); 24 zone units; official baseline not yet published, so this section is a scenario assumption recomputed with the same formula when official data arrives.

Slow traffic

Walking- and cycling-first knowledge loop; conceptual rail connections via real nodes such as Qinghe and Zhichun Road; MaaS integrating shared bikes, feeder buses and accessible ride-hailing; see the mobility-bluegreen figure for the street-section concept.

Blue-green network

One spine, one loop, many points: Jingzhang Heritage Park green spine (public-report caliber) + campus knowledge loop + pocket parks and open campus green spaces; facilities are modular and reversible.

Buildings

Stock-first, increment-second: open campus interfaces and youth-oriented retrofit of existing buildings; only punctual new construction with strictly limited height; 12 retention-first concept volumes in buildings.geojson.

Renewal projects

8 concept projects plus a transformable project table (lead/collaborator roles, preconditions, permit/ownership interfaces, cost tiers, stage gates, KPIs, acceptance evidence, human fallback, fail-exit); phasing 2026-2028 / 2029-2031 / 2032-2035; two consecutive missed KPIs stop investment and trigger withdrawal.

AI scenarios

10 scenario cards plus 3 industry test scenarios; 8 scenario points (3 AI landmarks + 5 scenario nodes); all scenario data anonymized and human-reviewed, no individual tracking.

Task coverage

The compliance matrix covers 23 tasks (announcement 1.3-1.5 plus agent open-call 1-6); agent.1-6 deliveries (Logo/VI and regional coordination map, 7 global cases, 10 scenario cards + 3 industry tests, honor system and component library, international communication copy, developer community and investment funnel) are in the corresponding proposal sections; standard and design-depth matrices are in their JSON files.

Self-check status

See self_check.json: the package records the four valroot gates (deterministic/spatial/visual/professional) and known minor provisional-boundary notices truthfully.

Sources

sources.json lists the organizer announcement and taskbook, national standards and regulations, the Jingzhang Heritage Park government report, 7 official institutional pages of global cases, and package asset entries (Logo, figures, font, maps, components) each with author, generation method, source, license and limits.

Assumptions

Six key assumptions in assumptions.json: provisional boundary, unknown statutory controls, missing baselines, concept design nature, privacy boundary, programme and cost caliber; each is re-checked and recomputed when official data is published.

Boundary notice: this page uses provisional boundaries and rounded indicators for intake display and discussion only; it is not a formal scoring drawing or an approval basis.
