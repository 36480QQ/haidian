# Formal Narrative

PAR·JZ Participation Loop is the sole primary brand for a public prototyping commons along the Centennial Jing-Zhang AI Innovation Belt. The package binds three provisional scope levels, three positioning statements, five functions, the taskbook's internal three areas/two wings, three public prototype anchors, ten scenario cards and three shadow-test protocols into one learn–prototype–review–return lifecycle.

The evidence chain is deliberately bounded: geometry is participant provisional data; control-dependent values are unknown/to_verify; AI produces advice, classification or drafts only; consequential outputs require a named human gate; public participants have paper, telephone, appeal, withdrawal and exit routes. `metrics.json`, matrices, bilingual proposals, visual HTML, figures and drawings are the structured and human-facing counterparts of this narrative.
