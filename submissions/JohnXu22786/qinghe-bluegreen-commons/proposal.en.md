# QINGHE BLUE-GREEN COMMONS: AI-powered Riverfront Restoration, Waterfront Innovation Living Room & Dual-Use Public Space

**English convenience summary** (full binding narrative is the Chinese proposal.md; this summary may lag the zh text).

以清河滨水段为核心，以「三节点+十场景」将AI生态修复与滨水创新公地嵌入日常公共生活：京张铁路遗址公园—清河交汇的生态文化客厅、滨水科创客厅（AI水环境实验室+开放创新工坊+数据灯塔）、社区亲水平台段（平急两用）。强调平急两用、生态韧性、可逆化设施、净水与数据边界，全部为概念建议，基于 provisional 边界。

## Outline
- Three-Layer Scope: coordinated research (industry & future-city), overall design (~2.3 sq km concept), key-area detailed design (3 nodes).
- Node 1 - Jing-Zhang Railway Heritage Park x Qinghe confluence: eco-cultural living room, rail memory + riverfront ecology.
- Node 2 - Waterfront Innovation Living Room: AI water-environment lab + open innovation workshop + "Qinghe Data Lighthouse".
- Node 3 - Community waterfront section: daily gathering + flood dual-use (平急两用) platforms.
- 10 scenario cards (water-quality patrol, ecological recognition guide, riverside delivery, flood emergency linkage, data art wall, drone alert, science education, test lab, night economy, white-paper co-creation) + 3 test & validation scenarios.
- 16 renewal projects in 3 phases (0-2 / 2-4 / 4-6 years, concept).
- All figures are concept suggestions on a provisional boundary; recompute when official data is published.
