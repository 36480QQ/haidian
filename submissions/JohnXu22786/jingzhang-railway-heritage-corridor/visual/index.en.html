Overview Map

Warp = heritage park slow-traffic and culture spine (Century Timeline 1905-2026-future); weft = street stitching links x5; AI is the new shuttle; the three areas and two wings operate as a collaboration loop (corridor alliance with annual rotation, linking North Latitude Community, Future Science City, Huairou Science City, Beijing E-Town and Beijing-Tianjin-Hebei).

Three-Level Scope

Coordinated research about 43.6 km2 (official text): industry and future-city research; overall design about 11.4 km2: this package's site_boundary concept geometry is about 1,141 ha, basically consistent with the official caliber; key areas total 368.4 ha = Zhongzhiyuan 192.1 + Origin Community 104.3 + Dazhongsi 72.0 (official text, additive check passes).

Key Areas

N1 Daocha Gate (AR time platform + honor wall-H), N2 Whistle Post (services, booking, concert stage under concert rotation), N3 Crossing Garden (assembly point + adoption-points board); all three nodes share the reversible public-space component library and the three-tier signage system (info post-S, guide terminus-T, honor wall-H). Transport and Slow Mobility

The slow spine connects gaps with bridges, underpasses and under-viaduct space; rail interchange with the public stations Qinghe, Shangdi, Zhichun Road and the Jingzhang HSR Qinghe station; the post network integrates toilets, water, infant care, accessibility, AEDs and bike sharing, with non-digital channels (phone and on-site human service).

Blue-Green Network

Heritage park green chain + community garden green knots + Xiaoyuehe green webs; track memory, sleeper paving and the Century Timeline as low-intervention expressions; public spaces remain open and shared, with accessible circulation rings, elderly-friendly rest zones and child-friendly play corners; material palette: steel rail, ballast, brick, cast iron.

Buildings

Stock-first, no new large volumes: historical structures and good-condition buildings are registered and retained (conceptual envelopes: 12 units, about 11 ha, not a survey), with functional replacement and interface upgrading; demolition is argued only case-by-case for dangerous buildings or the very few blocking slow traffic.

Renewal Projects

Pilot packages P1 (demo segment and gateway) and P2 (three slow-mobility posts) in the near phase 1-3 years as pilot areas; P3 (gap connectivity and under-viaducts) and P4 (garden network) mid 3-5 years; P5 (AI governance trial zone and data sandbox) far 5-10 years; P6 (event system) throughout. Every pilot carries lead/collaborator roles (RACI), human-review duties, maintenance arrangements, service/failure procedures, measurable KPIs and expansion/termination conditions.

AI Scenarios

Ten scenario cards (SC-01~SC-10) map onto eight space nodes (scenario_node_count) covering AR touring, accessible accompaniment, crowd sensing, bike posts, event booking, digital archives, garden adoption, under-viaduct activation, run and ride clubs, and a developer sandbox; three industry test scenarios (TS-01~03) go live in tiers after evaluation gates; AI governance: anonymized aggregation only, human review of key decisions, no over-monitoring.
