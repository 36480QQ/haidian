## Outline
- Jingzhang WeaveLine - Century Track Belt (Jingzhang Railway Heritage Park Corridor): a heritage park linear axis organizing culture memory, slow-traffic spine and street stitching - "warp lines tell history, weft lines weave the city, AI threads the seams".
- Node 1 - Daocha Gate: heritage culture gateway (origin of the Century Timeline + AR time-platform).
- Node 2 - Whistle Post Pavilion: slow-traffic vitality post (rest services, event booking, station concert stage).
- Node 3 - Crossing Garden: community stitching node (former crossing imagery linking communities on both sides).
- 5 AI+ scenarios (smart guide & AR time-platform, barrier-free accompaniment, crowd-flow sensing, shared-bike & slow-traffic service posts, event booking); personal data stays local, anonymized and human-reviewed, no individual tracking.
- Low-interference landscape: track memory, sleeper paving, century timeline; phased micro-renewal (1-3 / 3-5 / 5-10 years, concept).
- All figures are concept suggestions on a provisional boundary; recompute when official data is published.
