Slow traffic

The fifteen-minute route is walking- and cycling-first, with a path hierarchy: Level 1 slow-traffic spine (green belt, continuous, priority) then Level 2 neighbourhood links. Reachability is a conceptual standard until network-based recalculation; no road red lines, alignments or engineering conclusions are given.

Blue-green public space

The blue-green skeleton uses the Jing-Zhang Railway Heritage Park green belt and roadside green belts as the spine; micro-stations line the green belt for a continuous "green belt + service point" experience; the Xiaoyue River direction is a blue-green intention only, no water-engineering or blue-line conclusions. Public space component kit (concept): shade, seating, drinking water, lighting, information interface and human service access - all accessibility-oriented.

Buildings

No FAR, floor area, storey or height values are provided; the concept insists on small volume, many points, reversibility and composability, with the three nodes favouring "renovation and sharing" over "new build". The retain-renovate-demolish concept has four steps: verify constraints, retain first, light renovation, relocate or delete; spaces involving heritage, ownership, blue/green lines or rail safety are never concluded.

AI scenarios

Six personas (persona_count=6): older residents, dual-income families, young renters, students, emergency-need neighbours, micro-business owners and community merchants; tourists and international talent, deaf/low-vision users, caregivers and frontline service staff enter every card as cross-cutting views.

12 AI scenario cards (C01-C12, scenario_card_count=12): guide, scheduling, elderly/accessibility assistance, matching, feedback, facility inspection, emergency information; every scenario uses anonymised aggregation + human review + non-AI equivalent channels, with per-card immediate-removal conditions.

3 industry test-and-validate scenarios (industry_test_scenario_count=3): time-shared scheduling, anonymous matching, feedback topic clustering - each with falsifiable hypothesis, data input, model boundary, sandbox process, acceptance indicators, exit threshold and technology-supplier type.

Data lifecycle controls are implemented in cards and operations: collection (minimal), use (anonymised aggregation + review before release), retention (6-12 months per card), sharing (de-identified aggregate interfaces), deletion (within 30 days after trials plus published deletion records), fairness review (pre-launch and annual), complaints and incident response (physical plus online channels).

Developer community and open scenario operation (concept): quarterly hackathons, plug-in co-building; five-step pilot loop "apply - review - pilot - evaluate - keep/modify/remove", with pilot status visible throughout.
