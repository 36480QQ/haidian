# DUAL-USE PEACETIME-EMERGENCY SPACE SYSTEM (Concept)

**English convenience summary** (full binding narrative is the Chinese proposal.md; this summary may lag the zh text).

以「平急融环（DUAL·JZ · 平急两用空间体系）」构建沿京张AI创新带的平急两用空间体系概念：以「两用厅」为平急两用公共空间转换枢纽（平急转换调度与应急物资预置，匿名聚合服务），「转承廊」为平急两用街区转换廊道（街道断面与桥下空间平急转换，慢行与疏散兼容），「安备庭」为平急两用社区应急庭院（社区级应急服务与避灾空间，人工兜底），五类AI+场景（平急转换调度AI、应急资源预置AI、人流疏散推演AI、两用设施体检AI、公众应急反馈AI）仅处理匿名聚合数据、关键决策人工复核、禁止过度监控，交通慢行优先、日常慢行网络与应急疏散通道兼容设计、连续慢行轴贯通三节点，蓝绿公共空间兼作开敞避难与物资集散场地，分期实施+平急转换演练与年度计划。全部为概念建议、参考方案，不给出容积率、建筑高度、具体拆改留或工程实施结论，不编造应急数据，基于 provisional 边界，官方数据发布后复算。

## Outline
- Dual-Use Peacetime-Emergency (DUAL.JZ): a dual-use peacetime-emergency space system concept along the Jingzhang AI innovation belt, using the heritage green belt as the backbone.
- Dual-Use Hall as the peacetime-emergency conversion hub: conversion dispatch and emergency supply prepositioning, anonymized aggregation only (concept-level mechanism suggestion, not statutory emergency management).
- Convertible Gallery as the block-level conversion gallery: street sections and under-viaduct spaces converting between peacetime and emergency modes (concept-level).
- Resilience Court as the community emergency courtyard: community-level emergency services and refuge space, linking slow traffic and blue-green spaces (no engineering figures preset).
- Node 1 - Dual-Use Hall: peacetime-emergency conversion hub. Node 2 - Convertible Gallery: block conversion gallery. Node 3 - Resilience Court: community emergency courtyard.
- 5 AI+ scenarios (conversion dispatch AI, emergency supply prepositioning AI, evacuation flow simulation AI, dual-use facility inspection AI, public emergency feedback AI); anonymized aggregation only, key decisions human-reviewed, no over-surveillance.
- Slow-traffic-first: daily slow-traffic networks and emergency evacuation routes are designed compatibly; a continuous slow-traffic axis along the green belt links the three nodes; blue-green open spaces double as refuge and supply staging grounds; annual events: conversion drill day, resilience community workshop, dual-use space experience season.
- All figures are concept suggestions on a provisional boundary; recompute when official data is published.
