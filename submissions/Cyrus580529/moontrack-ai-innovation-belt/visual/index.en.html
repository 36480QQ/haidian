Core finding: network connectivity

Network-reachable elderly facilities: 0 of 5. Four sit in different components from the candidate depot; the fifth is connected but its final 652 m has no legal right of way. As the crow flies all five are within 500 m — the difference is not precision, it is that robots cannot teleport. Conversely the three shortest gaps measure just 9 m, 12 m and 35 m, 56 m in total; if real and passable, the main network grows from 27,560 m to 57,770 m.

Three-tier scope

The research scope (43.6 sq km) governs ecosystem and positioning; the design scope (11.4 sq km) governs the renewal framework, industrial space and mobility; the key areas (368.4 ha) govern programme, retain-renovate-demolish decisions and public-space connectivity. Each tier's conclusions hold only within the data available at that tier.

Key areas

Zhongzhiyuan originates, the AI Origin Community translates, Dazhongsi serves. All three boundaries are the organizer's provisional polygons, cannot support formal scoring or approval, and are drawn dashed.

Land use

Official regulatory conditions have not been released, so land-use classes, development intensity and building heights cannot properly be drawn and no figures are given. Floor area ratio is recorded as unknown in metrics.json with a stated reason rather than a guess.

Mobility

421 cycleway segments form a 142,150 m network — but not a connected one. It breaks into 62 separate components, the largest only 27,560 m and 14.9% of all nodes. Low-speed robots may travel only within cycleways and not above 15 km/h, so this fragmentation decides whether the flagship scenario is possible at all.

Blue-green public space

The Xiaoyue River runs 10,051 m; five of its eight segments fall inside the design scope and four inside the Zhongzhiyuan key area, overlapping closely with the Jing-Zhang Railway Heritage Park spine. Green and public-space ratios are computable but rest on crowd-sourced data, so they describe relative pattern only.

Buildings

buildings.geojson currently holds only a scaffold footprint. Building scale, massing and retain-renovate-demolish classification cannot be prepared before official controls exist. design_depth_matrix.json marks the true depth of each such item rather than hiding it behind a 'complete' status.

Renewal projects

Eight items. JZ-07, field verification and closure of three key network gaps, comes first: 56 m of connection could grow the usable network from 27,560 m to 57,770 m — but only field verification can confirm it, and the item is struck if the breaks prove to be mapping artefacts.

Service-level solution

The finding that the flagship scenario does not run has been turned into an executable remedy: a minimum connection tree at component level gives four links totalling 596 m; with those built, minimax depot siting puts the worst of the five facilities at 14.0 minutes and the mean at 11.3, and reachable network length rises from 20,341 to 35,733 m. Marginal returns are very uneven — the first 47 m, two links, take facilities on the network from one to three at 186 and 108 metres of network per metre built. The real bottleneck, though, is terminal access: 1,950 m from the five doors to their nearest legal node, 3.3 times the network gaps, with only one facility servable by a stop alone at a 150 m walking threshold. The binding constraint is the last hundred metres of walking environment, not the robots.

AI scenarios

Ten scenario cards; six go deep on Xiaoyue River robotics and elderly care, three are flagged as test-and-validation scenarios. Each card carries the six elements required by scenario.schema.json: users, context, data inputs, public value, risks, human review.

Task coverage

All six agent tasks (agent.1 to agent.6) have narrative sections, layers, metrics and drawing evidence, mapped item by item in compliance_matrix.json. Failing to answer any mandatory task is grounds for mandatory rejection.

Self-check status

Once the four gates (structure, spatial, visual, professional evidence) pass, self_check_submission.py writes self_check.json and updates the manifest's validation_claim. Structural pass is not proof of correct content; the two are reported separately.

Sources

sources.json holds 30 entries: 7 repository-provided official sources and 23 self-collected ones (6 OSM layers including building footprints and existing land use, 2 industry anchors, 3 policy and statistical documents, 1 elderly-care POI set and others), all validated against source.schema.json.

Assumptions

assumptions.json holds 21 entries. A-GEO records geometric attribution; A-NET records the method boundaries of the network analysis (the 8 m snapping tolerance is a choice, small gaps may be mapping artefacts, topological connectivity is not physical passability); A-BLD records the limits of the building data (storeys 9.6 per cent, height under 1 per cent, construction date and ownership at zero); A-LU records what the land-use mapping is (OSM tags are not cadastral records, and untagged land is recorded as reserved rather than inferred).

Recomputable

The package ships two independent verification scripts, Node standard library only with no GIS dependency, runnable from the package root: node visual/assets/verify-network.js recomputes the cycleway connectivity findings, and node visual/assets/verify-geometry.js recomputes the land-use, building and area findings and checks this layer's three commitments — full coverage, zero overlap, and declared areas consistent with the geometry. Neither shares code with the Python implementation that produced the figures, so agreement is a cross-validation rather than a re-run; they exit 1 on any mismatch.

This page is an explanatory layer and loads no remote resources. The authoritative record is the package GeoJSON, metrics.json, the matrix files and the self-check results.
