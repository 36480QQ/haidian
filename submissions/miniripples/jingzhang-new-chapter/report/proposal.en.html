Design Basis and Source List

The Jing-Zhang Railway, the first trunk railway independently planned and built by China, carries more than a century of engineering spirit and innovation. This proposal takes that historical legacy, the former Qinghuayuan Station, the surviving railway traces, and the surrounding green corridor as its principal spatial and cultural basis.

The proposal combines the documented project identity and design narrative in proposal.md with the structured indicators in metrics.json, the requirement responses in compliance_matrix.json, the standards alignment in standard_matrix.json, and the design-depth evidence in design_depth_matrix.json. The base spatial coordinates and geographic data used by the proposal are subject to their applicable open-data licenses.

Coordinated Research Area: Industry and Future City Research

At a time of rapid progress in generative AI and embodied intelligence, “Jing-Zhang: A New Chapter” proposes to transform Qinghuayuan Station and its surrounding green corridor into the world's first open urban AI physical sandbox and innovation community.

The vision combines the century-old spatial legacy—the “rail trace”—with a future digital-intelligence network—the “intelligence vein.” The intended result is a future-city demonstration area with historical and cultural depth, spatial resilience, and the capacity for continuous algorithmic evolution.

The industrial strategy is driven by two mutually reinforcing systems:

  • AI + urban renewal: an open algorithm community supports iterative improvement of physical space and establishes a chain extending from chip and foundation-model validation to embodied-intelligence sandboxes and Urban Agent deployment.
  • Physical-digital integration:

    • - At ground level—history and public life: retain railway traces and historic buildings while creating low-density natural, cultural, public, and innovation-exchange spaces. - Below ground—data and energy: use underground railway-related and layered spaces for edge-computing nodes, modular micro data centers, and low-power sensing networks.

AI Innovation Ecosystem, Personas, and AI+ Scenarios

The proposal defines ten AI scene cards that connect digital systems with specific physical settings.

Card 01: Outdoor Obstacle-Avoidance and Terrain Test Field for Embodied Intelligence

  • Location: the heritage park and complex-terrain area in JZ-03.
  • Operating model: steps, gravel tracks, soil, and other terrain provide a 24/7 test environment for quadruped robots and embodied Agents.
  • AI interaction: embedded high-precision multimodal sensor arrays evaluate gait and path-planning performance.

Card 02: Open-Source Algorithm Café and Physical Agent Node

  • Location: the ground floor of JZ-01 beside the former Qinghuayuan Station.
  • Operating model: developers deploy test code directly to the public sandbox through a high-bandwidth local network.
  • AI interaction: furniture and ambient lighting can be reconfigured by Agents in response to discussion intensity and brainstorming themes.

Card 03: Intelligent Microclimate Ecological Corridor

  • Location: the full linear railway green corridor.
  • Operating model: a lightweight neural-network system supports microclimate regulation.
  • AI interaction: real-time pedestrian distribution and weather forecasts dynamically control misting, shading, and underground water-storage circulation.

Card 04: Underground Modular Edge-Computing Ice Room

  • Location: auxiliary underground railway-related space beneath JZ-02.
  • Operating model: ground-temperature cooling and efficient liquid cooling support low-carbon edge-computing nodes.
  • AI interaction: computing requests from district-wide Agents are served in real time, while waste heat is redirected to heating or greenhouse systems.

Card 05: Autonomous Drone and Geek-Logistics Hub

  • Location: rooftops and automated landing pads in the JZ-04 maker community.
  • Operating model: zero-emission, high-precision delivery provides code-related orders and hardware components within minutes.
  • AI interaction: multi-Agent routing automatically avoids no-fly areas around protected historic buildings.

Card 06: Digital Imprinting of Centennial Memory and AR Walk

  • Location: the Jing-Zhang Railway heritage-trace area.
  • Operating model: Zhan Tianyou's switchback railway engineering data is connected with the history of urban change.
  • AI interaction: AR devices or smart glasses reconstruct historical trains and overlay future spatial scenarios in real time.

Card 07: Flexible Autonomous Maker Workshop

  • Location: shared ground-level space in the JZ-02 laboratory centre.
  • Operating model: robotic arms, 3D printing, and rapid PCB prototyping equipment are made available to users.
  • AI interaction: cloud Agents receive design drawings, schedule production, and coordinate robots to assemble hardware prototypes.

Card 08: Urban Agent Simulation and Decision Sandbox

  • Location: the intelligence-infrastructure data hall in JZ-01.
  • Operating model: pedestrian flow, transport, energy, and environmental data for the Jing-Zhang Innovation Belt are represented through a real-time digital twin.
  • AI interaction: planners and residents interact with “Codex · Jingzhang Urban Agent” in natural language to test the effects of planning adjustments.

Card 09: Adaptive Night-Time Safety and Intelligent Lighting Network

  • Location: public paths and darker park areas throughout the district.
  • Operating model: the system avoids uniformly bright lighting and uses progressive, demand-responsive illumination.
  • AI interaction: movement by runners, pedestrians, and animals triggers low-level following light, balancing personal safety with biodiversity protection.

Card 10: Automated Flood Resilience and Subsurface Hydrological Control

  • Location: sunken plazas and sponge-city water-storage facilities.
  • Operating model: the system responds to extreme rainfall.
  • AI interaction: a hydrological forecasting Agent anticipates waterlogging risk, activates gates and drainage pumps, and directs people away from danger.

Land Use, Building Scale, and Retain-Renovate-Demolish Strategy

The land-use structure combines commercial and research functions, innovation laboratories, an urban park and green corridor, and a mixed-use maker community. The four plots listed in the detailed-design table establish the current control framework for land use, development intensity, height, greening, density, and core functions.

The spatial strategy prioritizes retention of railway traces and historic buildings. Qinghuayuan Station is to be conserved and adaptively reused, while new research, community, and support spaces are inserted around the heritage structure. The current proposal does not identify specific buildings for demolition; any future retain-renovate-demolish decision must follow detailed survey, heritage assessment, and approval procedures.

Transport, Rail, Municipal Infrastructure, and Public Services

The transport strategy is defined as reduced surface traffic, connected underground space, and priority for walking and cycling.

  • A 2.8-kilometre continuous and accessible walking and cycling greenway reconnects Dongsheng Town and the Qinghuayuan area across the former railway divide.
  • A low-speed autonomous shuttle loop runs around JZ-01 to JZ-04 and connects with nearby stations on Metro Lines 13 and 15.
  • The three-level underground system coordinates walking connections, rail-related space, edge data centers, and data-cable routes.
  • Public-facing AI services include heritage interpretation, environmental sensing, safe night-time movement, rapid prototyping, and district-scale simulation.

Blue-Green Network, Public Space, and Urban Character

The railway green corridor is both a heritage landscape and the primary slow-mobility axis. Its public spaces combine railway traces, ecological planting, accessible movement, embodied-intelligence testing, and everyday community use.

  • The green-space ratio is at least 38%.
  • Public open space accounts for 42% of the overall design area.
  • Permeable paving and sponge-city facilities reach a target permeability ratio of at least 85%.
  • New research and commercial buildings target 100% Three-Star Green Building certification and integrate distributed rooftop photovoltaics.
  • Demand-responsive misting, shading, water storage, flood-control facilities, and low-level night lighting support climate resilience and biodiversity.

The intended urban character is low-density and human-scaled at ground level, with fine silver-line-like spatial continuity between heritage structures, green space, contemporary research buildings, and the underground digital network.

Renewal Projects, Implementation Policy, and Phasing

Implementation is organized in three phases:

  • Near term—Year 1: Initiation and Imprinting: conserve and adaptively renovate the former Qinghuayuan Station in JZ-01, install the intelligence-infrastructure data node, and build the core demonstration section of JZ-03.
  • Medium term—Years 2–3: Growth and Community Formation: complete the JZ-02 embodied-intelligence laboratory and JZ-04 developer community, and launch the district-wide Urban Agent simulation system.
  • Long term—Years 4–5: Integration and District-Wide Coordination: complete the continuous slow-mobility greenway and underground computing network, forming an open community that serves the broader Haidian AI Innovation Belt.

Implementation should coordinate heritage conservation, public-space delivery, infrastructure construction, research operations, and community access so that each phase produces a usable place rather than an isolated technology demonstration.
