# JING-ZHANG TRANSLATION GROUNDS

> Translating frontier intelligence into verifiable public value. Every spatial move is a Conceptual Recommendation and reference scheme for professional teams to deepen. Nothing here replaces formal planning or constitutes a government approval.

## Jury One-Page Conclusion: Make Every AI Service a Correctable Urban Proof

Translation Grounds does not add another AI showroom. It introduces the original Six-Gate Civic Proofing method: before an AI service enters public space, it must explain **provenance, consent, access, review, rollback, and stewardship**. The gates are non-compensatory. If one is missing, the service returns for retranslation while ordinary staffed service continues. Three yards validate protocols, translate knowledge, and decide public adoption; six gates turn abstract governance into visible, askable, stoppable urban interfaces [standard:PROJECT-AGENT-OPEN-CALL-TASKBOOK] [depth:overall_spatial_structure] [data:geometry/public_space.geojson#PUBLIC-001].

SCN-05, the Cross-Language City Service Desk, is the minimum reproducible example. A local zero-network test runs one complete path and six single-gate failure paths. All 7/7 synthetic paths reached the expected result. This verifies only reproducible rule logic—not field safety, resident consent, operating performance, or deployment approval [metric:proofing_gate_count] [metric:synthetic_proof_case_count] [metric:synthetic_proof_pass_count].

V3 compresses the protocol into a minimum urban unit that can be inspected and removed. TG-PILOT-01 uses 72 sqm, five spatial zones, and five operating states. Each service creates a non-identifying Civic Proof Receipt showing human ownership, expiry, ordinary service, appeal, and rollback. All 12/12 anonymous synthetic inclusion journeys reached their expected decision; this remains zero-network rule evidence, not a field result [metric:pilot_module_area_sqm] [metric:pilot_operating_state_count] [metric:synthetic_inclusion_journey_pass_count].

V4 does not change this spatial or governance logic. It makes start, accountability, failure handling, acceptance, and exit machine-readable: eight launch gates, seven RACI roles, six incident routes, four degradation modes, eight acceptance metrics, and post-exit public reuse are all located. Current repeatable evidence is 16/16 structural checks, 7/7 Six-Gate logic paths, and 12/12 anonymous synthetic journeys; field status remains `not_started` [metric:pilot_launch_gate_count] [metric:pilot_raci_role_count] [metric:pilot_acceptance_metric_count].

### SCN-05 Six-Gate Proofing Record: a 12-Week Exit-Ready Trial

Weeks 1–2 record the ordinary staffed-service baseline; weeks 3–5 translate synthetic questions offline; weeks 6–8 run parallel service while the staffed route remains primary; weeks 9–11 rehearse public proofing, appeal, and rollback; week 12 allows only adopt, retranslate for a fixed period, or retire. Untraceable provenance, failed notice/withdrawal, interrupted accessible or non-digital routes, failed human takeover, failed rollback, or absent stewardship restores ordinary service [metric:proofing_pilot_duration_weeks] [metric:proofing_stop_condition_count].

The protocol layer retains the original T/R/C/W roles. The site layer expands them into a seven-role RACI: venue custodian, ordinary-service operator, proofing translator, professional reviewer, Community witness, Steward, and AI supplier. These are concept roles with no institutional commitment. Launch gate L3 requires named rosters and signed duties, while the supplier is never accountable for approving its own output. Resource bands remain R0 process/people/paper, R1 removable furniture/signs/offline terminals, and R2 building/utility/system interfaces; no prices are invented [depth:phasing_implementation] [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK].

### TG-PILOT-01: a 72 sqm Removable Proofing Bay

A supplier may submit evidence but never approve its own gates; the ordinary-service operator may restore service without supplier permission. ### MVP Launch, Accountability, Incident, Acceptance, and Exit Loop

TG-PILOT-01 is implementable because it defines what must close before field entry, not because it claims construction readiness. Eight launch gates cover L1 site permission/removability, L2 ordinary-service baseline, L3 named roles/separation of duties, L4 minimum data/rights, L5 applicable professional safety review, L6 synthetic incident/rollback rehearsal, L7 bilingual and accessible notice/receipt, and L8 physical stop/exit reuse. L6 has synthetic contract evidence; the others are design-located but field-untriggered or condition-untriggered. S0 begins only after a venue and operator initiate the real unit in writing [metric:pilot_launch_gate_count] [depth:phasing_implementation].

| Activity | Accountable | Responsible | Consulted / witness | Hard boundary |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Site opening and ordinary baseline | Venue custodian | Ordinary-service operator | Professional reviewer, Community witness | Ordinary service opens before AI |
| Minimum-data intake | Steward | Proofing translator | Professional reviewer | Raw case content is not retained by default |
| Isolated AI draft | Proofing translator | AI supplier | Professional reviewer, Steward | Output does not reach the public directly |
| Six-Gate decision | Professional reviewer | Proofing translator | Community witness, Steward | Supplier cannot self-approve |
| Handoff, appeal, and stop | Ordinary-service operator | Ordinary operator, Steward | Professional reviewer, Community witness | D0 restoration needs no supplier permission |
| Retirement and public reuse | Steward | Venue custodian, ordinary operator | Professional reviewer, Community witness | Delete credentials/buffers; retain civic furniture |

Minimum data is limited to session purpose, the least content needed for service, user-selected language/access channel, and the eight-field anonymous receipt. Name, face, biometric, precise or continuous location, contacts, cross-service identity, and raw audio/video are forbidden by default. AI may translate/reformat cleared content, draft for review, expose provenance/uncertainty, and suggest human routing. People own notice, withdrawal, professional/rights/safety judgment, release, complaint, stop, and retirement. Without AI, the staffed desk, paper/bilingual guidance, step-free/large-print/read-aloud/tactile routes, and scheduled professional review remain available; withdrawal or complaint never reduces ordinary service [metric:pilot_raci_role_count] [metric:proof_receipt_required_field_count].

Six incident routes remain inside the existing gates. Provenance/rights failure isolates output and suspends S3; a materially wrong output is withdrawn with human takeover; an access/language barrier moves to an accessible staffed route; missing the 120-second concept target records failure rather than hiding delay; rollback/stop failure physically isolates and removes the AI layer; a privacy/security event disconnects the workstation and follows the future lawful operator policy. Eight acceptance metrics keep “current evidence” separate from “field status”: ordinary service 1.0, handoff ≤120 seconds, withdrawal acknowledgement ≤1 open shift, receipt 8/8, gates 6/6, inclusion journeys 12/12, stop/restore 1/1, and zero forbidden default fields retained. The first three and real stop rehearsal remain `unmeasured`; synthetic checks are never presented as field performance [metric:pilot_acceptance_metric_count] [metric:pilot_handoff_target_seconds].

At exit, remove the AI workstation, supplier branding, temporary credentials, and temporary case buffers. Keep the staffed desk, quiet appeal zone, accessible guidance, community meeting furniture, and repair/learning tables for public use. Archive only non-personal aggregates, protocol versions, the retirement decision, and maintenance lessons. Ordinary service and civic furniture may not be supplier-locked.

### Civic Proof Receipt: Return Exit Rights to the User

Every public proof creates only eight fields: anonymous token, service purpose, six gate states, human owner role, expiry, ordinary-service route, appeal route, and rollback status. It uses no name, face, continuous location, or “blockchain identity.” A receipt never renews automatically; one failed gate stamps RETURN FOR RETRANSLATION, and the physical stop control restores the space from public proofing to ordinary service [metric:proof_receipt_required_field_count] [metric:proofing_gate_count].

Five states constrain service and space together. S0 runs ordinary service only; S1 proofs public or synthetic questions in isolation; S2 exposes output only to human review; S3 shows a receipted public proof only after all six gates pass; S4 permits only limited adoption, retranslation, or removal of the AI layer and baseline restoration. Two-minute human handoff and withdrawal acknowledgement within one open shift remain unmeasured concept targets for professional calibration [metric:pilot_operating_state_count] [metric:pilot_handoff_target_seconds].
