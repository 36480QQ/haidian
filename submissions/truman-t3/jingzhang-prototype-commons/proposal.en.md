# Jing-Zhang Prototype Commons

**A distributed civic prototyping network for research entrepreneurs, not a linear showcase corridor.** The proposal combines reversible testing, founder services and public understanding across three parallel cores. All spatial actions are conceptual and require later professional, legal and operational confirmation.

## Three cores, nine modules, two task wings

AI Origin is a Founder Commons; Zhongzhiyuan is a Reversible Field Lab; Dazhongsi is a Public Prototype Market. Every core includes trial, founder-support and public-contact modules with different emphasis. Zhongguancun supplies conceptual professional-service connections; Xiaoyuehe organizes public problems, accessibility observation and non-digital service access.

## A 90-day Prototype Season

Teams use a Prototype Recipe Card: public problem, place type, participants, minimum data, human alternative, public output and exit condition. Workstreams are concurrent and may begin at any core. This is an operating concept, not an approval, procurement or deployment claim.

## Deliverables and safeguards

The package includes twelve scenario cards, six personas, four reversible test types, three civic landmarks, bilingual maps and evidence matrices. No FAR, demolition, redline, ownership, budget or engineering conclusion is claimed. Public alternatives, human review, minimum data and exit conditions are integral to every scenario.
