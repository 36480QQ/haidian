## 0. How to read this document

This is a single, self-contained regeneration design specification for a full rebuild of the existing professional design package. It is written so that a later writing-plan agent can decompose it into implementation tasks without inventing architecture: every artifact has a named path, every contract has a stated test, every number is frozen, and every unknown has a declared owner and recalculation trigger.

Three reading rules apply throughout.

1. **Authority order.** Where sources conflict, the live repository validator, schemas, participant skill, and current package bytes win; then the explicit corrections carried into this document; then completed audit and judgment findings; then prior structural drafting. Any statement below that a later reader finds contradicted by the live validator is to be resolved in favour of the validator, and the contradiction recorded as a defect against this specification.
2. **Truthfulness over ambition.** A design target is not a compliance claim. A geometry-derived measure is not a survey. A model output is not an observation. Where the package cannot honestly assert something, it asserts the gap, names the profession or authority that can close it, and names what must be regenerated when it closes.
3. **Reversibility over commitment.** Every **fast-layer intervention** shall be removable and restorable, with a named operator, maintainer, stop condition, removal method, recovery drawing, and restoration liability. Slow-layer support—such as surveyed levels, drainage, planting structure, or a service datum—may be intentionally durable only after tenure, survey, professional review, funding, and statutory authority are evidenced. Durable support must never be described as reversible merely because the components attached to it are removable. Nothing in this package commits public authority, land, statutory approval, or a station interface.

Section numbering below is stable and is the intended decomposition boundary for implementation planning.

## 2. Alternatives considered and decisive recommendation

Four coherent design directions were developed to a comparable depth. Each is stated with its adaptive-systems basis, its spatial consequence, its Beijing specificity, its evidence burden, its distributional effect, and its decisive weakness.

### 2.1 Alternative A — Shearing-Layer Threshold

**Basis.** Support-and-infill separation with differentiated change rates: the slow layer (ground, structure, right-of-way) is committed rarely and publicly; the fast layer (enclosure, fit-out, service, signage) changes on a seasonal cycle without re-opening the slow layer.

**Spatial consequence.** A committed public support — surface, level, drainage, planting, and a service spine — carrying a kit of reversible enclosures whose settings differ by enclosure ratio and servicing rather than by bespoke design.

**Beijing specificity.** Fits an area where the slow layer is not the participant's to commit and the fast layer is exactly what a design package may propose.

**Evidence burden.** Low for the fast layer; the slow layer requires survey and official coordination the package does not have.

**Distributional effect.** Favourable: reversible fit-out is cheap to withdraw, so failure costs fall on the sponsor rather than on displaced occupants.

**Weakness.** It is a construction discipline, not a decision procedure. On its own it says nothing about who decides, when, or on what evidence.

### 2.2 Alternative B — Polycentric Maintenance Commons

**Basis.** The common-pool resource is not space but **maintenance capacity** — the finite supply of staffed attention, cleaning, repair, snow clearance, and dispute handling. Nested, accountable stewardship units with defined membership, provisioning obligations, monitoring, and graduated response.

**Spatial consequence.** A maintenance unit map with a shared seam band between adjacent units, a physical cast marker at each seam, a charter per unit, and reserved seats for the parties who bear the cost.

**Beijing specificity.** Potential Beijing institutional interfaces may include sub-district or township responsibility-planner functions, frontage-maintenance arrangements, and renewal implementation-unit structures. Each must be registered from a cleared public source and its authority confirmed before use. Until then, it is a governance hypothesis and evidence gate, not a claim about the site's current institutional arrangement.

**Evidence burden.** High and specific: actors, access rights, tenure, maintenance obligation, and authority must be evidenced per unit, and most are currently unknown.

**Distributional effect.** Ambiguous. Stewardship without evidenced tenure can entrench whoever already holds access.

**Weakness.** Applied where evidence is missing, it fabricates governance. It must be scoped only to where the actors are known.

### 2.3 Alternative C — Staged Commitment on a Reserved Corridor

**Basis.** Real-options reasoning coupled to an adaptive cycle: reserve the option, run a bounded pilot, measure declared state variables, and let a named human decide to expand, hold, or withdraw at a declared review point.

**Spatial consequence.** A reserved, non-committing corridor relationship; time-boxed pilots at defined nodes; expansion only after a declared evidence threshold is met and a named authority signs.

**Beijing specificity.** Strong: it matches how staged urban renewal is actually authorized, and it refuses to pre-commit an alignment that no participant may commit.

**Evidence burden.** Moderate, and mostly generated by the pilots themselves.

**Distributional effect.** Favourable if stop conditions are real; harmful if a pilot becomes a de facto permanent occupation.

**Weakness.** Without a built payload, staged commitment is an empty schedule.

### 2.4 Alternative D — Counter-Sorting Belt

**Basis.** Segregation-dynamics reasoning: small local preferences aggregate into sorted enclaves, so the design deliberately interleaves uses at a fine grain to resist sorting, in the semilattice rather than tree sense.

**Spatial consequence.** A two-doors rule for each future surveyed and tenure-verified interface test segment, a maximum permitted blank-frontage run, and mixed daily-service adjacency. Until verification, these are non-georeferenced module rules and assert no parcel, ownership boundary, gate, or frontage condition.

**Beijing specificity.** Directly relevant to a campus/community threshold where sorting between an institutional interior and an everyday exterior is the live risk.

**Evidence burden.** Moderate; requires occupancy and frontage observation the package can do at survey stage.

**Distributional effect.** Explicitly redistributive in intent, and the only alternative that treats exclusion as the primary failure.

**Weakness.** As a whole-site organizing idea it over-generalizes a single mechanism.

### 2.5 Decisive recommendation

**Adopt C as the decision spine, with A as the mandatory built payload, and graft the strongest instruments from B and D.**

- **C is the spine** because the package's central honest position is that it may not commit alignment, land, or statutory outcome. Staged commitment is the only alternative whose logic is unchanged by that limitation.
- **A is the payload** because a decision spine with nothing built is unreviewable. Every pilot is delivered as a shearing-layer intervention: a light, removable, restorable fast layer over an untouched slow layer.
- **From B, graft:** the maintenance unit map with a shared seam band of approximately 1.5–2.0 m and a cast marker at each seam boundary; a written charter per unit carrying provisioning obligation and an explicit void clause; reserved seats for cost-bearing parties. Scope this **only** to units where actors, access, tenure, maintenance obligation, and authority are evidenced; elsewhere the unit is an evidence gate, not a governance claim.
- **From D, graft:** the two-doors rule and the maximum-blank-frontage-run rule as **exercise gates** at the campus/community threshold, plus a short observable tripwire table that a non-specialist can complete on site.

This recommendation is the architecture that Sections 3 through 6 elaborate. It is recorded here so that a later reader can see that the rooms-and-seams grammar was neither adopted as a finding nor discarded as worthless, but placed where it belongs: as a hypothesis that the labs test.

## 3. Beijing and Jing-Zhang design thesis, and the CAS operating model

### 3.1 Thesis

The site is a place where a national-scale AI programme, a heritage rail corridor, a university-adjacent campus edge, and ordinary residential daily life are being asked to share one ground plane, on a timetable set by institutions that do not share a single decision-maker. The honest design response is **not** to draw the settled outcome. It is to build a **disagreement atlas** — an explicit, versioned, public record of where the analysis is confident, where it is unstable, and where it is silent — and a **reversible city** — a set of interventions whose withdrawal cost is low enough that being wrong is survivable.

The two canonical titles state exactly this and are preserved verbatim in both languages.

The Jing-Zhang corridor is treated here as a documented heritage reference and a north–south organizing hypothesis. Whether, where, and for whom it functions as a present-day barrier or severance is unverified and requires cleared field, access, ownership, tenure, and movement evidence.

> Every run contains south–north connectivity by construction because the selection rule forces a spanning tree. The ensemble evidence concerns which candidate alignments recur and where the model disagrees under disclosed proxy assumptions; it does not establish empirical demand for a corridor.

This paragraph is the ceiling of what the package may claim from computation. It is reproduced verbatim wherever the ensemble result is summarized, in both languages, and is generated from the bilingual source of truth so that it cannot drift. It does not establish present-day discontinuity, accessibility, an optimal alignment, or a need for a corridor.

### 3.2 The corridor hypothesis: Four Working Rooms, Three Decision Seams

`Four Working Rooms, Three Decision Seams` is retained as an **explicitly provisional, threshold-conditioned spatial grammar**. Every published statement of it carries the following qualifications, generated from the bilingual source of truth:

- It is a **hypothesis about how to organize work and decisions**, not a finding.
- The four rooms are **not** four discovered Beijing neighbourhoods, districts, communities, or planning units. They are working groupings of declared nodes that appear when the selection-frequency threshold is set at the persistence cutoff.
- The three seams are **not** three observed physical, legal, ownership, or access barriers. They are the places where the declared analysis disagrees with itself, which is why decisions are staged there.
- The grammar exists **at one threshold**. At other declared thresholds it does not exist, and the specification publishes that sensitivity in full (Section 5.5).
- Where the word "room" would collide with an existing published spatial element or project name in either language, the published text uses the disambiguated bilingual term from the source of truth. Any Chinese term-hygiene rule applied to gate vocabulary must not corrupt ordinary compounds; a mechanical substitution over the single character 门 is forbidden, because it would damage 部门, 门禁, 门槛, and 入门.

The grammar's status is: **a hypothesis the labs are built to test, and which the labs are permitted to falsify.**

### 3.3 The actual adaptive engine: Three Coupled Urban Learning Labs

The adaptive mechanism in this package is not the ensemble and not the corridor grammar. It is **three coupled urban learning labs**, one distinct lab per required key area:

- **Lab 1 — Zhongzhiyuan:** a controlled validation commons.
- **Lab 2 — Beijing AI Origin Community:** a campus/community threshold.
- **Lab 3 — Dazhongsi:** an arrival, stay, and repair performance brief.

The three labs are **coupled** through exactly two channels and no others:

1. **Comparable evidence.** The labs share one record schema and a small common core—route obstruction, complaints, maintenance labour, service availability, and `G1`–`G7` status. Each lab also has program-specific state variables: takeover and near-miss variables in Zhongzhiyuan; exclusion, affordability, service-equivalence, and night-noise variables in Beijing AI Origin Community; and crowding, detour, snow-route, cycle-obstruction, and wayfinding variables in Dazhongsi. Definitions and protocols are identical only where the measured phenomenon is genuinely the same.
2. **Comparable governance.** Identical gate semantics (`G1`–`G7`), identical stop-condition grammar, identical rollback and restoration obligations, and identical human-authorization requirements.

They are **not** coupled programmatically. Each lab has a materially different brief, a different user population, different failure modes, and different stop conditions. Section 6 specifies each separately, and the specification is not satisfied by a template applied three times.

### 3.4 The CAS operating contract

Every element below is a required, published, testable part of the package. Each is stated once here and referenced by the labs.

**(a) Spatial scope and adaptive-system boundary.** The spatial scope is the provisional site constraint, the three provisional key-area constraints, and the declared conceptual node/edge relationships. The adaptive-system boundary additionally includes the three learning labs, affected user groups, operators, maintainers, accessibility and data stewards, gate authorizers, reversible components, state-variable records, decisions, complaints, stop actions, and recovery states. Official statutory processes, off-site effects, and unresolved professional determinations are external boundary conditions recorded as dependencies, not silently excluded from the system.

**(b) Human and institutional agents.** Every adaptive mechanism names an accountable decision-maker by **role**, never by personal name, and never as "the system", "the algorithm", or "the model". The role registry distinguishes at minimum: the sponsoring/implementing entity; the sub-district or township responsibility planner function; the property or facility operator; the maintainer; the accessibility steward; the data steward; the human authorizer for each gate; and the institution that can lawfully commit the slow layer. Where the accountable role for a mechanism is not yet evidenced, the mechanism is published as an **evidence gate**, not as a governance arrangement.

Every program, project, and role record also carries: `beneficiary_groups`; `worst_affected_groups`; `authority_role`; `authority_basis_source` or `null`; `operator`; `maintainer`; `accessibility_steward`; `data_steward`; `gate_authorizer`; `decision_window_days` or `null`; complaint routes including on-site, paper, and telephone; `escalation_order`; `operating_budget_owner`; `maintenance_budget_owner`; `funding_status`; `conflict_or_recusal_rule`; `stop_authority`; and `restoration_liability`. A missing authority basis, decision window, appeal route, or budget owner is an evidence gate that blocks pilot start or expansion. Every project states effects on residents, cleaners, carers, delivery workers, disabled users, night workers, and small frontage operators; ownership or institutional affiliation may not be the sole basis for representation.

**(c) Local rules.** Each lab publishes a small number of written, human-executable local rules — the two-doors rule, the maximum-blank-run rule, the provisioning obligation, the seam-band responsibility rule, the takeover rule, the appeal-desk rule. Local rules are executable by a non-specialist with a clipboard.

**(d) Reversible variation.** Each lab varies only the fast layer. Every variation is specified with its removal method, its restoration target, and its restoration verification.

Every admitted component has a lifecycle record containing: maintainer role; inspection interval; defect severity classes; response target or `null`; accessibility-defect priority; cleaning, drainage, snow, ice, and waste duties; maintenance-access route; a two-person hand-tool removal test and target time or `null`; standard-parts and spares rule; operating- and maintenance-budget owner; end-of-life destination; recovery-state drawing; restoration verifier; and residual liability. A missing response target, removal method, budget owner, or recovery state blocks deployment.

**(e) Observed state.** Each lab declares its state variables, their measurement method, their measurement cadence, their observation delay, and their measurement uncertainty. A state variable with no declared measurement method may not be used in a threshold.

Every stop or propagation condition resolves to a state-variable record with: stable ID; lab; affected user and space; unit or categorical state; observation method; cadence; observation delay; uncertainty; proposed threshold or `null`; threshold-approval role; decision window; continue, modify, stop, and restore actions; cost bearer; and evidence status. If a threshold is `null`, the pilot is blocked from starting or expanding. A writing-plan or implementation agent may not choose a missing threshold.

**(f) Human selection authority.** Algorithms may compare options only. **Human and legally accountable institutional authority must remain explicit for system boundaries, goals, nodes, candidate edges, weights, thresholds, evidence sufficiency, gates, pilots, stops, recovery, propagation, and final action.** This sentence is published verbatim in both languages, generated from the bilingual source of truth, and is repeated in the governance section of each lab.

**(g) Review cadence across seasons and disruptions.** Every lab and every public-space component has a matrix covering: high heat and shade failure; heavy rain and drainage clearance; snow, ice, and heating-season configuration; wind and dust; standing-water and insect effects; night lighting and noise; cleaning and waste; maintenance access; and equipment, power, or digital-service failure. Each row names the state variable, observation method and cadence, operator, approved threshold or `null`, manual fallback, stop action, and recovery state. Evidence from at least one qualifying summer heat/rain condition and one qualifying winter snow/ice condition may be required only for a year-round-operation claim or propagation review; a 90-day pilot alone cannot establish either. Seasonal qualification never delays ordinary decisions within an already authorized pilot, immediate stop, make-safe action, restoration, or verified closure.

**(h) Public, versioned memory.** Every decision, observation, threshold change, stop, and restoration is recorded in a public, versioned, machine-readable record with a stable identifier, a timestamp, the deciding role, the evidence relied upon, and the dissent recorded. Memory is append-only; corrections are new records that reference the superseded record.

**(i) Stop and restore behaviour.** Every lab publishes stop conditions. A stop condition, when met, halts the pilot without requiring further authorization; **restarting** requires authorization. Every stop is followed by a published restoration verification.

**(j) Cross-scale propagation.** A result may propagate from lab scale to site scale only when: the result satisfied the approved D13 seasonal-qualification criteria; it was reproduced under at least one materially different operating condition; the accountable role for the larger scale is evidenced; and the human authorizer for the larger scale has signed. Propagation is never automatic, and never triggered by a model output.

### 3.5 The computation, named truthfully

The computation is a **seeded minimum-spanning-tree topology/disagreement probe**. It is not biological urban intelligence, not network optimization discovered by an organism, and not a simulation of movement or flow.

**Physarum status.** In public-facing explanatory prose, excluding historical filenames, paths, manifest entries, machine identifiers, source records, and reproducer output, Physarum appears only in two registered blocks: methodological lineage and implementation caution. Whenever a historical identifier is exposed to a reader, the adjacent caution states that the implemented method is a seeded Kruskal-based topology ensemble, not a Physarum flux/conductivity model, not a reinforcement/decay simulation, and not evidence of biological optimality or autonomous urban intelligence. Public paths are preserved; the two data assets remain byte-frozen, while the reproducer receives only the authorized verification amendment defined in Section 11.3.

### 3.7 Why the declared inputs constrain the result

The package must publish, in plain language and in both languages, that the following author decisions determine the outcome:

1. **Nodes are hand-declared.** Ten locations were chosen. No node set was derived from data.
2. **Candidate edges are hand-declared.** Twenty-four plausible links were chosen. An unlisted link cannot be selected, so non-selection never evidences infeasibility.
3. **Weights are hand-declared priors**, sampled per run from declared ranges.
4. **Proxy attributes are hand-declared constructions**, not measurements.
5. **Thresholds are hand-declared**, and the result is knife-edge sensitive to them.
6. **Length enters the score twice.** Length enters directly through the length term, and again indirectly because the maintenance proxy is itself a weighted function of normalized length. The effective length coefficient is therefore the direct length weight plus the length share of the maintenance term. The consequence — that the five-weight space is effectively lower-rank than it appears, and that short edges are systematically favoured beyond the stated length preference — is published as a limitation, not buried in a footnote.
7. **The coordinate frame is strongly anisotropic**, with normalized x and y units corresponding to materially different ground distances (roughly a seven-to-one ratio). Any visual reading of the network diagram as a map of relative distance is misleading. Published edge lengths are frozen **computational input distances derived from conceptual geometry**. They are authoritative only for reproducing this proxy calculation; they are not survey distances, walking distances, route lengths, engineering measurements, or authoritative facts about Beijing. A participant-local verifier checks every graph endpoint against its declared conceptual geometry; until that verification passes, the mapping remains pending.
8. **Jitter is a declared modelling choice**, not an observation. It is registered as its own assumption with its own identifier, and the package publishes a zero-jitter ablation alongside the primary frequencies so that a reader can see how much of the frequency structure is jitter-induced. Both frequency columns are published.
9. **Two summary statistics carry little information by construction** and are published with that caveat: the mean selection frequency across all twenty-four candidates is pinned by construction at `11/24`, and the mean frequency of the persistent set is confined by construction to the interval from the persistence cutoff to 1.0.

### 3.8 Forbidden claims (computation)

The following may not appear anywhere in the package, in any language, in any artifact, including alt text, captions, and figure titles:

- that corridor continuity is absent, missing, severed, or discovered to be lacking;
- that urban seams were verified, observed, or found;
- that legal discontinuity, ownership boundaries, gates, access control, or compound barriers were identified;
- that the four groupings correspond to neighbourhoods, communities, districts, or official planning units;
- that the computation optimized, discovered, learned, or evolved a network;
- that the result is validated, robust, or confirmed by biological analogy;
- that the ensemble supports any specific crossing alignment.

**Legacy wording removal contract.** The migration sweep removes any claim that all discarded candidates occur in the run archive; any claim that the implementation reinforces, prunes, biologically adapts, evolves, or autonomously learns routes; and any “real urban trade-off” wording that treats declared proxies as observations. ## 8. Evidence, data, standards, assumption, metric, and missing-value contracts

### 8.1 Bilingual structured source of truth

A single structured bilingual source of truth generates every repeated string in the package: titles, gate labels, scenario entries, project entries, persona entries, metric labels and values, legends, statuses, captions, and sheet schedules. No artifact hand-authors a string that the source of truth can generate.

**Parity testing.** The parity test asserts **semantic identifier and value parity** — that for every keyed record, the Chinese and English records exist, carry the same identifier, and carry the same numeric values and the same status codes. The parity test does **not** assert equal word counts, equal character counts, or identical line numbers, because those are false proxies for translation quality. Parity is keyed block-set parity: the set of keyed blocks in the Chinese artifact equals the set in the English artifact.

### 8.2 Assumption traceability without a phantom reference syntax

## 10. Drawings, bilingual PDF, font, rights, accessibility, and visual QA contracts

### 10.1 Font strategy

**No ambient build-machine fonts.** The build may not depend on any font installed on the build machine, and may not embed any proprietary face.

**Carrier.** The license-compliant, participant-local Chinese glyph subset is carried only as base64 inside the single closed-path JavaScript file `visual/assets/noto-sans-sc-subset.js`, which remains below the 5 MiB visual-asset limit and the tighter 4 MiB package target. Build code decodes it only to temporary build storage and never commits a font file. No JSON, numbered, split, alternate, or additional carrier is permitted; the implementation plan may not select another carrier form. Contract tests assert its path, uniqueness, byte cap, and decoded hash.

**Subset determinism.** The glyph set is derived **mechanically from the actual strings rendered into the PDFs**, so that the subset is a deterministic function of the content. The build records: the upstream source hash; the subset input hash; the encoded carrier hash; the decoded font hash; the reserved-font-name determination (quoting the shipped licence header and stating which case applies); and the exact glyph coverage list.

**Rights.** The verbatim licence text, the copyright notice, and the attribution are published in `report/copyright_statement.md`, which is the package's rights document, rather than in a forbidden plain-text licence file. Renaming the subset face is retained as good practice — it prevents a partial subset presenting as the complete upstream face — without asserting that the licence compels it unless the shipped header actually does.

### 10.2 PDF contract

Every published PDF must prove all of the following, per file, by test:

| Assertion | Contract |
|---|---|
| Embedded font programs | At least one embedded font-program stream is present |
| No unembedded base faces | No base font name equal to a standard base-14 face or to a non-embedded system CJK face |

#### 14.13.3 Deterministic decimal display contract

The arithmetic in Section 14.3 and in the B03 disclosure is correct, but neither stated a rounding rule, which left every published percentage open to renderer-dependent drift. One rule now governs, everywhere the denominator disclosure is published, in both languages:

1. **Retain full precision throughout. No intermediate rounding.** Every quotient, difference, and percentage-point shift is computed from the exact stored operands `3204005.256`, `1995497.923`, `11412825.386`, and `11400000`. Rounding happens once, at the display boundary, never before.
2. **Rounding mode is decimal `ROUND_HALF_UP`** — decimal, not binary floating-point, and half-up, not banker's rounding.
3. **Human-facing green shares, percentage-point shifts, and the hectare difference render at exactly two decimal places, with trailing zeroes preserved.** `17.50%` is published as `17.50%`, never as `17.5%`.
4. **The nominal denominator-difference percentage renders at exactly four decimal places**, producing `0.1125%`.

The complete closed set of display strings:

|---|---:|---:|
| Land-use-1401 green share, provisional denominator | `28.073725%` | `28.07%` |
| Land-use-1401 green share, nominal denominator | `28.105309%` | `28.11%` |
| Land-use-1401 share shift | `0.031584` pp | `0.03` pp |
| Designed green network share, provisional denominator | `17.484697%` | `17.48%` |

The six-decimal column is a cross-check for the test author, not a publication target. The test computes at full precision and asserts the **exact display strings** in the right-hand column — string equality, not numeric tolerance — so that a dropped trailing zero or a banker's-rounding renderer fails the build.

This rule does not disturb Section 5.1. Section 5.1 governs **areas**, which round to `0.1` ha, and **shares**, which round to `0.01` percentage point; the two-decimal rule above is the same `0.01`-percentage-point precision for shares, and applies two decimals to the nominal *difference* `1.28` ha, which is a difference and not one of the reported areas.
