Mobility & slow traffic

The network renewal adopts the bio-Physarum adaptive-network paradigm: stations act as food sources, yielding a five-level structure (primary vein / branches / slow loop / green corridor / station feeder) with slow-traffic priority and blue-green coupling.

Primary vein

High-grade axial linkage stitching the three zones.

Slow loop

Low-carbon intra-district slow loop linking campus and district.

Blue-green public space

The Qinghe blue-green interface is the ecological backbone; green corridors penetrate blocks along the network, and public open space connects the nodes.
