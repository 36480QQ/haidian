JINGZHANG RISING
A century of self-reliance, the character for people rising
中文
Core judgement: the century-long self-reliance of the Jing-Zhang Railway becomes an AI innovation belt structured as one spine, three terraces, two wings and two stitching systems. Every public-facing AI service must provide both a human fallback lane and a public report card; without either, it may not open. Every graphic here is projected directly from the package geometry layers and every number is recomputed from metrics.json. The page works fully offline.
The site extent and the three key areas use the organiser's provisional rough surrogate boundary. This is a conceptual recommendation only and must not be used as an official redline, an approval basis or a precise-area basis. Recompute in full once official polygons are published.

Public Report Card Simulator · Six-Item Governance & Human Fallback Testing

Citizens and evaluators can verify the six public report card items for any public-facing AI service. Missing any critical item (F1–F4) immediately triggers a circuit-breaker shutdown and reverts to human staff; missing improvement items (F5–F6) triggers a 7-day rectification notice; all items verified lights the green operational status.

▤ Six Report Card Items (Click items to toggle test state)

◎ Real-Time Governance Status & Spatial Action

Green Light: Full Operational Access

Status: All six report card items verified and valid. The AI service is fully licensed, published on kiosk displays, and authorized for interactive public use.

Human Staff on Standby: Even under green status, the on-site human fallback channel (F3) and stainless steel QR nameplate remain physically operable 24/7.
Yellow Light: 7-Day Rectification Notice

Status: Critical items (F1–F4) verified, but improvement items (F5 green compute or F6 public feedback) are pending. Service operates under provisional 7-day observation.

Rectification Action: The operator must complete system integration within 7 working days, or the service will automatically downgrade to Red Light shutdown.
Red Light: Service Suspended · Reverted to Human Staff

Status: Critical item deficiency detected (F1 Filing, F2 Scenario Permit, F3 Human Fallback, or F4 Privacy Protocol). By governance mandate, AI service is immediately shut down!

Physical Fallback Engaged: Terminals automatically close AI reasoning interfaces and switch to staff assistance mode; kiosks display notice and contact details.
