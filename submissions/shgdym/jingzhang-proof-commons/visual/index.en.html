X JINGZHANG

Keep Jing-Zhang public life on the main line. AI may enter only from a closable siding, through a staffed turnout, and must reverse when it fails.

PEOPLE TAKE THE MAIN LINE · AI TAKES A SIDING · A PERSON OWNS EVERY CROSSING

Jing-Zhang is already a public line. The design question is how technology enters without automatic priority.

The corridor already joins parks, neighbourhoods, campuses, research areas, metro stations and commerce. X JINGZHANG adds no separate technology axis. At three lateral interfaces, testing, release and trial use occupy closable sidings: equipment may stop, withdraw and return while the public route stays on its line.

Three key areas, three urban duties that cannot be exchanged

Remove the labels TEST, RELEASE and USE, and the places still address different conditions: research boundaries, public accountability and high-frequency daily life.

01 · TEST

RESEARCH BOUNDARY MEETS OPEN LANDSCAPE

ZHONGZHI

The public path remains continuous at the edge. Machines enter a recessed test pocket; an observation buffer, staffed stop and rear exit guard the only crossing.
02 · RELEASE

TECHNICAL RELEASE MEETS PUBLIC RESPONSIBILITY

AI ORIGIN

The open lab, release table and public steps share one frontage. Responsibility and withdrawal remain visible at the exit; removing a product does not close the collaboration court.
03 · USE

AI SERVICE MEETS EVERYDAY ARRIVAL

DAZHONGSI

AI and staffed service split from the same starting point. The complaint point touches the accessible route; equipment exits at the rear when the trial pocket closes.

SPATIAL DESIGN

Main line, siding, staffed turnout and reverse are flows, closure edges and exit routes, not a process diagram.

LIVED JOURNEY

One product crosses three stations. Public objection is not feedback collection; it makes equipment reverse in physical space.

THE WIDER CITY

The three stations sit within industry, neighbourhoods, blue-green space and east-west links, without turning the heritage park into one long technology show.

DELIVERY AND OPERATIONS

A fourteen-day minimum pilot first confirms routes, edges, staff and withdrawal; after failure the site is restored before retesting is considered.

EVIDENCE BOUNDARY

The overall and three key-area geometries are provisional constraints used only for conceptual relations, public-map slices and package-internal calculation. Formal boundaries, controls, rights, field dimensions, engineering conditions and operators require redesign.

PROFESSIONAL APPENDIX

Tickets, states, failure paths, sources and spatial topology remain reproducible evidence. They are not field validation, approval or delivery authorisation.
