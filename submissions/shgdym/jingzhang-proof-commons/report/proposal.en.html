Three-Level Scope Framework

The proposal follows the three official work scopes: the coordinated research area frames the AI ecosystem and future-city strategy; the overall design area translates that strategy into urban renewal, land use, mobility, blue-green space, and public-service support; the key-area scope tests detailed design moves in Zhongzhiyuan, Beijing AI Origin Community, and Dazhongsi Standard Depth. The working concept is Jing-Zhang Ledger Line: the centennial railway becomes a public AI verification line where every scenario has a spatial anchor, evidence source, human review path, public explanation, exit path, and recalculation trigger when official geometry arrives.
