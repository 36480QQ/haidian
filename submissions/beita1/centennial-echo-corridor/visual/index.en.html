Mobility & walking

Echo Walk

A 9.7-km barrier-free N-S promenade linking three zones, two stations, three landmarks; two conceptual service streets keep the axis calm.

Five E-W stitching corridors

Bridging rail-heritage and fast-road severances: Dazhongsi forecourt, Jimen, Xueyuan Rd, Origin Community, Echo Gate; station-city integration at Dazhongsi and Qinghua East Rd W. All alignments conceptual.

Blue-green public space

One spine, one loop, two waters

Echo Spine green corridor approx. 220.5 ha incl. the northern protective belt; indicative links to the Qinghe and Xiaoyue River (blue lines unpublished). Green ratio 21.2%, public-space ratio 11.8% (design-model values).

AI public-space component library

Eight standard items: explainable wayfinding, edge-computing kiosks, sensing columns, soundscape installations, honour paving, testing markings, accessibility companions, movable stages - public algorithm notes + physical off switch + no-identification defaults.

Buildings & renewal projects

20 concept building clusters

Footprint approx. 10.6 ha across eight types (research, lab, incubation, release, talent housing, community service, culture, mobility); no survey of existing stock, no demolition conclusions.

Renewal projects EB-01 - EB-08 · three phases

P1: Walk continuity + Origin Plaza + conversion street (approx. 5.15 sq km); P2: testing loop + Echo Gate + Dazhongsi integration (approx. 3.69 sq km); P3: mid-belt stitching + component library (approx. 2.57 sq km). All reference schemes.
