The Dao Belt · Twin-Track Quest — Overall Conceptual Design for the Century-Old Jingzhang AI Innovation Belt

Reframing the Jingzhang Belt through the three-stage thesis of 'The Way · Questioning the Way · Emerging the Way': the Jingzhang Railway is the Way, Zhongguancun is Questioning the Way, and the AI era asks here for the path of autonomy that is Emerging the Way. The space carries the Quest narrative through two parallel tracks — the heritage rail track and the light/data track — while governance uses the Provable Sandbox Registry and visible money-and-power as its baseline, deliberately retaining an AI dark-night contrast segment to invert the heritage park from a green backdrop into a city operating system, culminating in a Quest Gate open to the whole city — not a memorial tower, but an open question renewed year after year.

Design Basis and Source Inventory

This proposal takes the International Call for Conceptual Urban Design — Centennial Jingzhang AI Innovation Belt — Prequalification Announcement issued by the Haidian Branch of the Beijing Municipal Commission of Planning and Natural Resources as its primary basis, and uses the provisional coarse boundaries, key areas, enumerations, metrics, and source list registered by maintainers in brief/site-package/ as the machine-readable basis Source Standard. The main name proposed in this proposal is "京张问道带" (English: The Dao Belt · Twin-Track Quest), with the auxiliary line "A road that can self-correct — from twin tracks to questioning the way." All spatial implementation recommendations are conceptual proposals / pending official regulatory planning conditions, and do not replace statutory planning.

A statement of historical accuracy must be established first in this chapter: the Jingzhang Railway (opened 1909) was the first trunk railway built under Chinese-led engineering, not the first operating railway in China — the earlier Wusong railway (1876) in Shanghai predates it; Zhan Tianfu (Jeme Tien Yow) was the chief engineer leading the Jingzhang project, completed jointly with Chinese and foreign engineers and thousands of workers, with the "人字" (herringbone) zig-zag at Qinglongqiao being one of the key engineering decisions. This proposal deliberately does not frame 1909 as an AI precursor, and does not portray Zhan Tianfu as a solo designer; "The Way" points to "the Chinese connecting the road themselves," not to any technological myth Source.

Three-Level Scope Working Framework

The proposal is organized according to the three levels of scope in the announcement: the Coordinating Research Scope addresses the 43.6 km² AI industry ecosystem, strategic positioning, innovation chain, and future urban form; the Overall Design Scope addresses the 11.4 km² urban area within 1–2 km around the Jingzhang Heritage Park, requiring an overall framework for urban renewal, industrial spatial layout, transport and municipal support, and urban landscape control; the Key Area Design Scope addresses the three detailed design areas totaling 368.4 hectares, requiring definitions of functions and business types, building scale, retain/renovate/demolish classification, public space connectivity, and transport organization Source. The three-level scope is mapped clause-by-clause in compliance_matrix.json, ensuring that the mandatory tasks of announcement sections 1.3, 1.4, 1.5 and agent.1–agent.6 all have corresponding chapters, layers, metrics, drawings, and HTML evidence.

The three levels of work are constrained by Depth and Depth, with spatial evidence governed by Spatial data and Spatial data — using the three key areas as the spatial anchors of the Dao Belt. The three levels are not a disjoint set of drawings: the coordinating research determines the "questioning the way" narrative and the industry chain judgment; the overall design translates these judgments into renewal projects, spatial structure, and facility capacity; the key area level validates the implementability of specific parcels.

The overall concept of this proposal is "Twin-Track Quest": to invert the Jingzhang Heritage Park from a "green backdrop" into a "city main board / operating system," carried by two parallel tracks — the heritage rail track (the physical rail heritage carrying the memory of historic locomotives and the public space skeleton) and the light/data track (a "data light corridor" of LED / touch-sensitive floor screens carrying the bus of algorithms, data, and scenario elements) — the two run in parallel without intersecting, each with its own responsibility, and draw no new redlines. The "one track" is this twin-track main axis; the three halls (deliberately using "坊/Hall" to avoid the generic "核/Core") correspond to the three key areas in the taskbook but are renamed; the two wings are the Frame-Quest wing and the Track-Light wing; the one question is the Quest Gate at the south end. The spatial structure is recorded as "One Track · Three Halls · Two Wings · One Question."

The core of the coordinating research scope is to construct a world-class AI innovation ecosystem. This proposal does not adopt the old label "Smart-Connected Belt"; the main name is set as "京张问道带" (English: The Dao Belt), deliberately avoiding homogenized words such as Silk Road and Corridor, taking "Questioning the Way" as the cultural main line and "prova-bility" as the governance baseline Standard. The three-stage core thesis is: The Way — the Jingzhang Railway (1909) was the first trunk railway built under Chinese-led engineering; "the Chinese connecting the road themselves" is "The Way"; Questioning the Way — Zhongguancun is the place that has spent decades repeatedly asking "can we do it ourselves," which is "Questioning the Way"; Emerging the Way — in the AI era, asking here for the next step on the path of autonomy and giving answers that can be re-checked is "Emerging the Way." Space carries the Way and the Questioning via "twin tracks"; the cultural main line is "Questioning the Way"; the governance baseline is "provability."

Overall Design Scope: Urban Renewal and Regulatory-Plan-Depth Urban Design

The overall design scope is required to reach the urban-design depth of a regulatory detailed plan. This proposal proposes the overall spatial structure of "One Track · Three Halls · Two Wings · One Question": the one track is the twin-track main axis of the Jingzhang Heritage Park (heritage track + light/data track in parallel); the three halls divide labor north-central-south; the two wings expand east-west; the one question is the Quest Gate at the south end Depth Spatial data. The reason for this organization is that the heritage belt is the only linear public resource that simultaneously connects the North Fifth Ring Road, Wudaokou, Tsinghua East Road West Station, and Dazhongsi Station; using it as the operating system can most economically stitch the urban fabric fragmented by the railway and the ring roads, allowing innovation resources to agglomerate along the "Questioning the Way" thread rather than treating the park merely as a green backdrop.

The land-use structure is expressed per the territorial-spatial planning classification: park green space and open space along both sides of the heritage park form a green corridor Spatial data; AI R&D innovation land and industrial service commercial land are placed along the heritage track and stations on either side of the light/data track corridor Spatial data, Spatial data; community service and ancillary land are placed along residential parcels to ensure job-housing balance Spatial data. Land-use expression completeness follows Standard, and land-use layout depth is checked by Depth.

The overall urban renewal framework follows the main line "low-efficiency space identification → renewal project list → phased implementation," distinguishing retain, renovate, renew, new-build, and pending-confirmation objects, adopting the retain/renovate/demolish method of "light touch of incremental services, activation of stock renovation, public space first" Depth Spatial data. Content involving floor area ratio, building height, building density, setback, road redline, and facility standards — because official regulatory planning conditions are missing — is uniformly written as "pending official regulatory planning conditions," and agent-speculated values must not masquerade as approved metrics Metric Standard. Development intensity and height-massing control only propose the conceptual direction of "high intensity near the heritage track, low density near the park, height limits on cultural interfaces, roofs and massing echoing the twin-track line," with specific values to be recalculated by a professional team once official conditions are available Depth Depth.

Land Use, Building Scale, and Retain/Renovate/Demolish Plan

The land-use plan is expressed per public standards such as the territorial-spatial survey, planning, and use-control classification, forming a complete, closed, seamless set of land-use zones Standard. The primary evidence for land-use classification and building footprints is Spatial data and Spatial data, and building-scale re-check uses Metric. The building plan distinguishes retain, renovate, renew, new-build, or pending-confirmation objects, and clarifies the recommended hierarchical levels for building footprint, function, scale, style, roof, massing, and height control Depth.

Because current building, ownership, regulatory planning, and engineering conditions are missing, the proposal only presents the method and a pending-calibration list, and does not fabricate retain/renovate/demolish conclusions. The retain/renovate/demolish principle is: for the Jingzhang Railway heritage, cultural relics, and historic buildings, preservation is the premise and only environmental and functional light touches are made; for low-efficiency industrial spaces, renovation and activation predominate; only objects with no preservation value that obstruct public-space connectivity are listed in the pending-confirmation renewal scope, and they must await confirmation of formal ownership, regulatory planning, and heritage-protection conditions. Building height, massing, interface, and style control are governed by Depth, only proposing the conceptual direction of "high intensity near the heritage track, low density near the light/data track, height limits on cultural interfaces, roofs and massing echoing the twin-track line."

Building-scale and intensity metrics must be consistent with metrics.json and layers. When official conditions for total building scale, floor area ratio, building height, building density, green-space ratio, setback, and building control line are missing, status=unknown is used uniformly, with the reason/assumptions fields stating the conditions to be filled, current assumptions, and the recalculation path once formal data arrive; fixed values must not be used to manufacture a false sense of precision Standard.

Differentiation Statement

This proposal deliberately avoids the homogenized pattern of "Belt/Spine/Axis/Ring + three cores + two wings + 12 cards + 5 landmarks," focusing on four differentiations:

1. The Questioning-the-Way narrative replaces the smart-connection slogan — the three-stage thesis of "The Way · Questioning the Way · Emerging the Way" runs throughout, elevating Jingzhang from the engineering fact of "the first trunk railway" to the cultural proposition of "the Chinese hosting the road-opening themselves," elevating Zhongguancun from an "innovation district" to the site of "repeatedly asking whether we can do it ourselves," and asking in the AI era for the next step of autonomy, that is, Emerging the Way; it does not use overused names such as "Smart-Connected / Smart / digital-twin tower." 2. The Provable Sandbox Registry PSR as the governance main line — not writing yet another evaluation base, but writing a registry that lets "claims be re-checked by outsiders"; Jingzhang is defined as the "Capital of AI Provability," where every registered AI scenario carries a model card + reproduction-script hash + clear-rights list + third-party volunteer re-check, turning governance from slogan to an operable urban institution. 3. Money-and-power visibility as an empty competitive slot — compute-bill visibility makes public AI compute energy/data costs public by block and time period like water and electricity, deliberately filling the slot of "where the money comes from and where it goes" that all proposals evade. 4. The AI dark-night / anti-AI-light segment as a contrast point — deliberately retaining a dark-night contrast segment with no active AI, low energy, a starry-night gallery as the witnessing baseline, so that the Dao Belt is not only bright but also dim, allowing the public to perceive the boundary of AI — the greatest contrast with the "all-element smart, 24-hour lit screen" pattern.

At the same time, in naming, "坊/Hall" is used to avoid the generic "核/Core," "Dao Belt" avoids the generic "Smart-Connected Belt," and the "Quest Gate" is a reverse landmark avoiding the generic "digital-twin tower," so that reviewers remember the points of differentiation rather than yet another recitation of a standard template.
