Design Basis and Source Inventory

On a clear early-spring morning, walk a stretch of the old Jing-Zhang line: tender ginkgo leaves pressed against the crushed stone, the steel rail polished bright—over a hundred years ago trains ground over it, now an old man jogs along the heritage rail track, and every time he passes an old milepost he taps it once with his palm, like greeting an old friend. This is the object our proposal takes on: a stretch of railway that has already stopped, and a stretch of city still asking onward. We are not turning it into another park backdrop; we are letting it be passed through again in the age of algorithms.

This proposal takes the International Conceptual Urban Design Call Prequalification Announcement for the Centennial Jing-Zhang AI Innovation Belt, issued by the Haidian Branch of the Beijing Municipal Planning and Natural Resources Commission, as its first authority, and uses the maintainer-registered temporary boundaries, key areas, enumerations, metrics, and source list in brief/site-package/ as its machine-readable basis Source Standard. The principal name is set as 「京张问道带」 (English: The Dao Belt · Twin-Track Quest), with subtitle "A self-correcting road of innovation—from twin tracks to questing the way." All spatial implementation suggestions are "a conceptual proposal / pending official regulatory planning conditions," and do not replace statutory planning.

One line of history, stated plainly first: the Jing-Zhang Railway (opened 1909) was the first trunk railway built under Chinese-led engineering, but not the first operating railway in China—that earlier one was the Shanghai Wusong Railway (1876) Source. Zhan Tian'ai (Jeme Tien Yow) directed and led a collective effort together with Chinese and foreign engineers and thousands of rail workers, and the Qinglongqiao switchback line was one of the key engineering decisions. So this proposal does not frame 1909 as an AI prequel, nor cast Zhan Tian'ai as a lone man hunched over drawings; the "way" points at the deed that "Chinese hands once broke this road open," not at a technology myth.

Three-Level Scope Working Framework

The announcement divides three levels of scope, corresponding to three sizes of "asking": the Coordination Research Scope (about 43.6 km²) answers where the AI industry grows and what the future city looks like; the Overall Design Scope (about 11.4 km² around the heritage park, 1–2 km on either side) holds the renewal framework, industry space, transit-and-utility support, and urban character under control; the Key Area Scope (about 368.4 ha across three detailed-design districts) gets down to the detail of function and tenants, building mass, retain-renovate-demolish, public-space connectivity, and transit organization Source. The three levels line up clause by clause inside compliance_matrix.json, ensuring every mandatory task under announcement 1.3, 1.4, 1.5 and agent.1–agent.6 has its chapter, layer, metric, drawing, and HTML evidence.

Depth is bound by Depth and Depth; spatial evidence is taken from Spatial data and Spatial data—the three key districts are the three pilings of the Dao Belt. The three levels are not three disconnected sets of drawings: coordination research decides the "questing-the-way" story and the industry-chain direction; overall design drops those judgments onto renewal projects and facility support; key areas test whether a given plot can actually land. Think clearly up top, lay it open in the middle, keep your footing steady below.

The overall concept of this proposal is called "Twin-Track Quest": turn the Jing-Zhang heritage park from a "green backdrop" inside-out into a "city main street," carried by two parallel tracks—the heritage rail track (the physical rail遗产, remembering the old stories of the locomotives, serving as backbone for public space) and the data light-track (a "data light-arcade" laid out of LEDs and touch-enabled floor screens, running algorithms, data, and scenarios). The two tracks run in parallel and do not fight, each going its own way, drawing no new red lines. The one rail is this twin-track main axis; the three yards (deliberately using "yard/坊" to dodge the identikit "core/核") correspond to the three key districts in the taskbook but are renamed; the two wings are the Frame-Quest Wing and the Track-Light Wing; the one question is the Quest Gate wall at the south end. The whole reads as "one rail · three yards · two wings · one question."

Coordination Research Scope — Industry and Future-City Research

At two in the afternoon in Wudaokou, bicycle bells jingle into one another like a city heartbeat. Turn into "Convergence Yard" and the mood shifts—as if stepping out of a street market into a courtyard where people still actually live: three sides renewed old red-brick buildings, one side facing the park, the tree shade three degrees cooler. What the Coordination Research Scope has to answer for this stretch is: how to connect the state of "street market and courtyard still here, people still here" with a world-class AI innovation ecosystem, without flattening it.

We no longer use the old name "Smart-Link Belt"; the principal name is set as 「京张问道带」 (English: The Dao Belt), deliberately steering clear of worn-out words like Silk Road or Corridor, taking "questing the way" as the cultural main line and "provability" as the governance floor Standard. The three-part theme, told to the neighbors: the way—the Jing-Zhang Railway (1909) was the first trunk railway built under Chinese-led engineering, "Chinese hands once broke this road open"—that is the way; questioning the way—Zhongguancun is the place that has spent decades repeatedly asking "can we do it ourselves"—that is questioning the way; emerging the way—in the AI era, asking here once more "how does the road walk itself forward next" and laying out answers that can be re-checked—that is emerging the way. Two tracks run the road and run the quest; the cultural line is "questing the way," the governance line is "provability."

Overall Design Scope — Urban Renewal and Regulatory-Planning-Depth Urban Design

The Overall Design Scope must reach the urban-design depth of regulatory detailed planning. The Jing-Zhang heritage belt is the only linear public resource that can simultaneously connect the North 5th Ring Road, Wudaokou, Qinghuadonglu Xikou, and Dazhongsi station. We propose the overall spatial structure of "one rail · three yards · two wings · one question": the one rail is the Jing-Zhang heritage park's twin-track main street (heritage track + light track running side by side, each telling its own story); the three yards divide work north-middle-south; the two wings spread east-west; the one question closes at the south-end Quest Gate wall Depth Spatial data. Treating this belt as "a small street that can answer questions" rather than "a green backdrop" lets it stitch the city grain more economically and lets innovation resources gather along the "questing-the-way" thread.

Land use follows the public classification of territorial-spatial planning: park-green and open space stay along both sides of the heritage park as a green corridor Spatial data; AI R&D-innovation land and industry-service commercial land sit along the heritage track and stations, split on either side of the light-track corridor Spatial data, Spatial data; community-service and ancillary land lines the residential patches to hold job-housing balance Spatial data. Land-use completeness rests on Standard, depth checked by Depth.

Renewal goes by "identify inefficient space—list projects—phase implementation," separating preserve / renovate / renew / newly build / pending-confirmation, methodically "lightly point at incremental service, activate stock renewal, public space first" Depth Spatial data. Floor-area ratio, building height, density, setbacks, road red lines, and facility standards, because official regulatory planning is not in place, are uniformly written as "pending official regulatory planning conditions confirmed"; we do not let AI fabricate a number to snow the reviewers Metric Standard. Development intensity and height give direction only: higher near the heritage track, lower near the park, capped at cultural interfaces, roof massing following the twin-track line—numbers wait for official conditions on site, to be recomputed by a professional team Depth Depth. Set the "rules" first, leave the "numbers" blank.

Land Use, Building Mass, and Retain-Renovate-Demolish Plan

Land use is expressed in the public standards of territorial-spatial survey, planning, and use-control classification, assembled into a complete, gap-free land-use zoning Standard. Land-use classification and building-footprint evidence are Spatial data and Spatial data; mass re-check uses Metric. The building plan separates preserve / renovate / renew / newly build / pending-confirmation, writing out the recommendation level of footprint, function, mass, character, roof, volume, and height control Depth.

Because current-building, ownership, regulatory-plan, and engineering conditions are all missing, the plan gives only method and a to-be-calibrated list, and does not fabricate retain-renovate-demolish conclusions. Principle: Jing-Zhang relics, cultural relics, and historic buildings are unconditionally preserved as a premise, only a "light tap" on environment and function; inefficient industrial space is activated mainly by renovation; only objects with no preservation value that also block public-space connectivity go on the "pending-confirmation" list, and they may only move after formal ownership, regulatory plan, and cultural-protection conditions land. Height, volume, interface, and character are governed by Depth, giving only the conceptual direction of "higher near the heritage track, lower near the light track, capped at cultural interfaces, roof massing following the twin-track line."

Building mass and intensity metrics must line up with metrics.json and the layers. Total building mass, FAR, height, density, green ratio, setback, and building control line—wherever official conditions are missing, uniformly status=unknown, and in reason/assumptions write out what is still missing, current assumptions, and how to recompute once formal data arrives; never use a fixed number to manufacture precision Standard. Better to say "we don't know, waiting on data" than to slip a number past.

The meaning of key metrics in plain words: overall-scope area constrains how space is divided and whether the blue-green can carry it Metric Spatial data; green and public-space ratios hold up daily contact, innovation chance-encounter, and outdoor testing, recomputation via Metric, Metric Spatial data; building footprint area reflects the order of renewal mass Metric; key-area count ensures the three mandatory districts are all present Metric. Control metrics like FAR, with no official regulatory plan, sit at status unknown, recomputed once formal data arrives Metric.

At formal deepening, each metric splits into three classes: first, spatial metrics that submitted geometry can directly recompute (boundary area, green ratio, public-space ratio, building footprint area, phasing area Spatial data); second, control metrics that need official regulatory plan or taskbook annex support (FAR, building height, density, setback, road red line, facility standard); third, performance metrics that need operational or industry data for continuous calibration (provability-registration count, pickup-stand entry count, Quest Gate adopted-question count, Dao Week participation, dark-segment witness hours). The three classes respectively enter metrics.json, assumptions.json, compliance_matrix.json. Provability governance and privacy boundary are the proposal's core risk control: the provability table registers only model card + reproduction-script hash + rights-clearance list, not hosting raw data; the neighborhood watch-sheet exchanges only status-summary vectors, threshold hits reviewed by a human dispatcher; the oral-history pickup stand archives only after three-academy vetting and oral accounts and portraits require self/family authorization; the Quest Gate wall auto-de-identifies before display and retains a withdraw button. Every AI-native mechanism in this proposal carries human review + operating entity + a withdraw mechanism; there is no "fully automatic, no re-check" single point.

Differentiation Statement

This proposal deliberately avoids the recycled template of "belt / vein / spine / axis / ring + three cores two wings + 12 cards + 5 landmarks," and pushes four forks: the questing-the-way narrative replacing the smart-link slogan (using the "the way · questioning the way · emerging the way" three-part to lift Jing-Zhang and Zhongguancun one cultural proposition up); the provability table as the governance main line (a small table where the "claim" can be reconciled); compute-bill visibility filling the empty seat of "where the money comes from and where it goes"; the AI-dark segment as the contrast point (a stretch of dark setting off everyone's light). Using "yard" to escape the identikit "core," using "Dao Belt" to escape the identikit "Smart-Link Belt," using the "Quest Gate wall" as an inverse landmark to escape the identikit "digital-twin tower"—so the reviewers remember the forks instead of memorizing the same template once more.

And finally, a confession on this city's behalf: The city does not answer for you. It hangs your questions on a wall, then turns and keeps building the road.
