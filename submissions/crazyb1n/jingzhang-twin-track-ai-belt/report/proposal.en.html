Three-Level Scope Work Framework

The proposal organizes its work according to the three levels defined in the announcement: the overall study scope covers the 43.6 km² AI industry ecosystem, strategic positioning, innovation chain, and future urban form; the overall design scope covers the 1–2 km urban and industrial areas around the 11.4 km² Jingzhang Heritage Park, and requires an overall urban renewal framework, industrial spatial layout, transport and municipal support, and urban character control; the key area scope covers the three detailed design areas of 368.4 hectares, and requires clarification of functional programs, building scale, retain-renovate-demolish classification, public space connectivity, and traffic organization Source. The three levels of scope are mapped item by item in compliance_matrix.json, ensuring that Sections 1.3, 1.4, and 1.5 of the announcement and the mandatory tasks of agent.1–agent.6 all have chapter, layer, metric, drawing, and HTML evidence.

The depth items of the three-level work framework are constrained by Depth and Depth, spatial evidence is based on Spatial data and Spatial data, and task basis is based on Standard.

The three levels of work are not a set of mutually isolated drawings: the overall study determines the judgments on the industry chain and urban form; the overall design translates those judgments into renewal projects, spatial structure, and facility capacity; and the key area detailed design verifies the implementability of specific plots, buildings, transport, public spaces, and AI application scenarios. The overall concept suggested by this proposal is "Jingzhang Twin-Track · Smartly Connected Belt": with the Jingzhang Heritage Park as the public space main axis (heritage origin track) and the AI digital services corridor as a parallel new track (digital intelligence track), the three cores and two wings are linked by the "slow-mobility smart meridians ring"; the "belt" is not an extra red line drawn on top, but a translation of the three-level scope of the announcement into a working method Source.

Industry and Future City Research in the Overall Study Scope

The core task of the overall study scope is to build a world-class AI innovation ecosystem. This proposal suggests the primary name "京张双轨·智联一带" (Jingzhang Twin-Track · Smartly Connected Belt), abbreviated as "智联一带" (JZ·Link Belt), with the English name Jingzhang Twin-Track AI Belt; the Logo direction is suggested as "dual parallel tracks + digital nodes," making "JZ" isomorphic with "∞," symbolizing infinite connection between history and the future. All naming, abbreviations, English names, and Logo directions are conceptual proposals, to be developed further by professional teams only, and do not constitute an established brand or official identity Source Standard.

The core metaphor is the "twin track": the Centennial Jingzhang Railway is the heritage origin track, carrying the memory of the historical locomotives and public space; AI innovation is the parallel digital intelligence track, carrying agents, data, and scenarios. The two tracks run parallel, intersect, and coexist—historical locomotives and agents meet "on the same platform." The spatial translation is: one belt (the Jingzhang Heritage Park vitality belt, cultural main axis / public space main axis), three cores (Zhongzhiyuan North Core · full-stack autonomy and governance, Origin Community Central Core · open source and talent, Dazhongsi South Core · intelligent economy and international exchange), two wings (Zhongguancun Technology-Services Wing, Xiaoyue River Scenario-Empowerment Wing), a twin-track interchange platform (JZ/Hub concept station), and the slow-mobility smart meridians ring.

Urban Renewal and Regulatory-Plan-Depth Urban Design in the Overall Design Scope

The overall design scope is required to reach the urban design depth of a regulatory detailed plan. The proposal puts forward the overall spatial structure of "one belt, three cores, two wings, one ring, and multiple platforms": the belt is the Jingzhang Heritage Park vitality belt, serving as the north–south through-running public space and cultural main axis; the three cores correspond to the three key districts; the two wings extend in the east–west direction; the ring is the "slow-mobility smart meridians ring," linking the heritage park, the Qinghe waterfront, the Xiaoyue River Wing, and the three cores; and the multiple platforms are the twin-track interchange platform (JZ/Hub) concept stations set along Metro Line 13 and the Jingzhang corridor Depth Spatial data. The reason for this organization is that the heritage belt is the only linear public resource that can simultaneously connect the North 5th Ring Road, Wudaokou, the Qinghua East Road West Entrance, and the Dazhongsi station; using it as the axis most economically stitches together the urban fabric fragmented by the railway and ring roads, so that innovation resources agglomerate along the cultural thread.

The land use structure is expressed according to the national territorial spatial planning classification: park green space and open space are arranged on both sides of the heritage park to form a green corridor; AI R&D and innovation land and industrial-service commercial land are arranged near the tracks and stations to form innovation cores; community service and supporting land are arranged along residential districts to ensure job–housing balance and daily life Spatial data Spatial data Spatial data Spatial data. The completeness of the land use expression is based on Standard, and the depth of the land use layout is verified by Depth.

The overall urban renewal framework takes "identification of inefficient space—renewal project list—phased implementation" as its main line, distinguishing retain, renovate, renew, new-build, and to-be-confirmed objects, and adopts the retain-renovate-demolish method of "light-touch incremental services, activation of existing stock, and public space first" Depth Spatial data. Content involving floor area ratio, building height, building density, setback lines, road red lines, and facility standards is written uniformly as "pending official regulatory control conditions," and agent-inferred values must not be passed off as approved metrics Metric Standard. Development intensity and height and massing control only propose the conceptual recommendation direction of "high intensity near tracks, low density near parks, and height limits on cultural frontages"; specific values are to be recalculated by professional teams after official conditions are available Depth Depth.

Land Use, Building Scale, and Retain-Renovate-Demolish Scheme

The land use scheme is expressed in accordance with public standards such as national territorial spatial survey, planning, and use-control classification, forming complete, closed, seamless land use zones Standard. The primary evidence for land use classification and building footprints is Spatial data and Spatial data, and building scale verification uses Metric. The building scheme distinguishes retain, renovate, renew, new-build, or to-be-confirmed objects, and clarifies the recommended hierarchy for building footprint, function, scale, character, roof, massing, and height control Depth.

Because the existing buildings, property rights, regulatory plans, and engineering conditions are missing, the proposal only puts forward methods and a to-be-calibrated checklist and does not fabricate retain-renovate-demolish conclusions. The retain-renovate-demolish judgment principles are: the Jingzhang railway heritage, cultural relics, and historic buildings are always to be retained as a precondition, with only light-touch environmental and functional intervention; inefficient industrial space is mainly activated through renovation; and only objects with no preservation value that hinder public space connectivity are listed in the to-be-confirmed renewal scope, which must await confirmation of formal property rights, regulatory plan, and heritage protection conditions. Building height, massing, frontage, and character control are managed by Depth, which only proposes the conceptual recommendation direction of "high intensity near tracks, low density near parks, height limits on cultural frontages, and roof and massing echoing the twin-track alignment."

Building scale and intensity metrics must be consistent with metrics.json and the layers. Where official conditions are missing for total building scale, floor area ratio, building height, building density, green ratio, setback lines, and building control lines, status=unknown is used uniformly, and the reason/assumptions fields state the to-be-supplemented conditions, current assumptions, and the recalculation path after official data arrive; fixed values must not be used to create a false sense of precision Standard.

Transport, Rail, Municipal Works, and Public Service Facilities

The transport scheme responds to the announcement's requirements on rail station integration, road microcirculation, slow-mobility gaps, external transport, parking, non-motorized vehicle parking, and green transport systems, with emphasis on the North 5th Ring Road, the Jingzhang Heritage Park road-crossing node, Wudaokou, the Qinghua East Road West Entrance, the Dazhongsi station, and transport connections around key enterprises Source. The professional depth of transport and municipal works is constrained respectively by Depth and Depth, and the layer evidence references Spatial data.

"Twin-track · Smart Connection" has a specific meaning at the transport layer: the rail track is the physical track belt formed by Metro Line 13 and the Jingzhang railway corridor, and the smart track is the AI digital services corridor laid along the corridor (including slow-mobility and innovation services corridors). The proposal sets up "twin-track interchange platform" (JZ/Hub) concept stations at Wudaokou (Origin Community gateway), the Qinghua East Road West Entrance (near-campus transfer), the Dazhongsi station (South Core station integration), and the road-crossing node over the North 5th Ring Road (stitching the heritage belt), so that physical tracks and digital services meet at the same station Spatial data. Around rail stations, transfer and non-motorized vehicle parking are organized on the principle of "rail first, slow mobility first"; road microcirculation stitches together blocks fragmented by the railway and ring roads; and bus, cycling, walking, and parking supply are jointly verified; road red lines, alignment, and bridge and tunnel works are all listed as pending official transport and engineering conditions.

Municipal works and public service facilities cover AI industrial service facilities, innovation service platforms, talent life service facilities, new infrastructure, distributed energy, on-device compute, and the integration of traditional municipal works. The proposal treats distributed energy and on-device compute as the municipal implementation of the "digital intelligence track," explaining facility standards, spatial layout, service radius, operating models, and phased implementation logic, but when engineering data such as pipelines, energy, drainage, flood control, and fire protection are missing, they are all listed as preconditions for formal deepening Depth. Blue-Green Space, Public Space, and Urban Character

The blue-green space scheme takes the Jingzhang Heritage Park vitality belt as its skeleton, coordinates the travel needs of the Qinghe, Xiaoyue River, surrounding universities, enterprises, and communities, and proposes a walking, cycling, and green space system that runs through north–south and connects east–west, forming the "slow-mobility smart meridians ring" Depth Spatial data. The slow-mobility smart meridians ring links the three cores and two wings: the northern segment runs along the Qinghe waterfront to connect Zhongzhiyuan, the central segment traverses the Jingzhang Heritage Park to connect the Origin Community, the southern segment runs along the Xiaoyue River Wing to connect Dazhongsi, and the east–west direction stitches gaps with roads and public space. The green space and public space ratio supports daily interaction, serendipitous innovation encounters, and outdoor testing Metric Metric Spatial data. The proposal identifies slow-mobility gaps, road-crossing nodes, and landscape nodes at the north and south ends of the park, and proposes composite utilization strategies for parking, sports, innovation exchange, technology testing, application display, and public services.

The AI public space positioning of the Jingzhang Heritage Park is "the same platform for the heritage origin track and the digital intelligence track": retaining railway elements such as rails, platforms, and milestones as historical memory, overlaying explainable AI wayfinding, data art installations, and scenario experiences, and forming a spatial strategy of east–west stitching (slow mobility and functional connectivity across the east and west sides of the railway) and north–south through-running (a continuous axis from the North 5th Ring Road to Xizhimen Outer Street) Standard. On this basis, three AI pilgrimage landmarks (conceptual proposals) are proposed: first, the Qinghuayuan Railway Station "Time-Space Platform," juxtaposing the origin of the centennial railway with the starting point of AI innovation as the origin of the public space; second, the Jingzhang Rails "Milestone" installation belt, using the milestones along the line as a narrative thread to convert the construction mileage of 1909 into today's innovation nodes; third, the "Smart Link Tower" digital twin landmark, placed at the Dazhongsi or Origin Community node, presenting the belt's vitality through real-time, explainable innovation-heat visualization. All three landmarks must satisfy heritage protection, green space, blue-line, and traffic safety constraints, involve no bridge/tunnel or underground engineering conclusions, and must not arbitrarily alter enterprise buildings or property spaces Source.

The urban character integrates the Jingzhang railway history and culture, Zhongguancun innovation culture, and AI innovation culture, and draws on cultural resources such as the Qinghuayuan Railway Station and Beijing Film Academy, proposing guidance on the urban tone, architectural character, roof form, massing, frontage, and public art Depth.
