## Three-Level Scope Work Framework

The proposal organizes its work according to the three levels defined in the announcement: the overall study scope covers the 43.6 km² AI industry ecosystem, strategic positioning, innovation chain, and future urban form; the overall design scope covers the 1–2 km urban and industrial areas around the 11.4 km² Jingzhang Heritage Park, and requires an overall urban renewal framework, industrial spatial layout, transport and municipal support, and urban character control; the key area scope covers the three detailed design areas of 368.4 hectares, and requires clarification of functional programs, building scale, retain-renovate-demolish classification, public space connectivity, and traffic organization [source:OFFICIAL-ANNOUNCEMENT]. The three levels of scope are mapped item by item in `compliance_matrix.json`, ensuring that Sections 1.3, 1.4, and 1.5 of the announcement and the mandatory tasks of agent.1–agent.6 all have chapter, layer, metric, drawing, and HTML evidence.

The depth items of the three-level work framework are constrained by [depth:three_level_scope_framework] and [depth:overall_spatial_structure], spatial evidence is based on [data:geometry/site_boundary.geojson#SITE-001] and [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-001], and task basis is based on [standard:PROJECT-OFFICIAL-ANNOUNCEMENT].

![Three-level scope and spatial work framework diagram](assets/figures/land-use-structure.png)

The three levels of work are not a set of mutually isolated drawings: the overall study determines the judgments on the industry chain and urban form; the overall design translates those judgments into renewal projects, spatial structure, and facility capacity; and the key area detailed design verifies the implementability of specific plots, buildings, transport, public spaces, and AI application scenarios. The overall concept suggested by this proposal is "Jingzhang Twin-Track · Smartly Connected Belt": with the Jingzhang Heritage Park as the public space main axis (heritage origin track) and the AI digital services corridor as a parallel new track (digital intelligence track), the three cores and two wings are linked by the "slow-mobility smart meridians ring"; the "belt" is not an extra red line drawn on top, but a translation of the three-level scope of the announcement into a working method [source:PROCESSED-FACT-PACK].

## Industry and Future City Research in the Overall Study Scope

The core task of the overall study scope is to build a world-class AI innovation ecosystem. This proposal suggests the primary name "京张双轨·智联一带" (Jingzhang Twin-Track · Smartly Connected Belt), abbreviated as "智联一带" (JZ·Link Belt), with the English name Jingzhang Twin-Track AI Belt; the Logo direction is suggested as "dual parallel tracks + digital nodes," making "JZ" isomorphic with "∞," symbolizing infinite connection between history and the future. All naming, abbreviations, English names, and Logo directions are **conceptual proposals**, to be developed further by professional teams only, and do not constitute an established brand or official identity [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK] [standard:PROJECT-AGENT-OPEN-CALL-TASKBOOK].

The core metaphor is the "twin track": the Centennial Jingzhang Railway is the **heritage origin track**, carrying the memory of the historical locomotives and public space; AI innovation is the parallel **digital intelligence track**, carrying agents, data, and scenarios. The two tracks run parallel, intersect, and coexist—historical locomotives and agents meet "on the same platform." The spatial translation is: one belt (the Jingzhang Heritage Park vitality belt, cultural main axis / public space main axis), three cores (Zhongzhiyuan North Core · full-stack autonomy and governance, Origin Community Central Core · open source and talent, Dazhongsi South Core · intelligent economy and international exchange), two wings (Zhongguancun Technology-Services Wing, Xiaoyue River Scenario-Empowerment Wing), a twin-track interchange platform (JZ/Hub concept station), and the slow-mobility smart meridians ring.

The three positioning directions, five functions, and the three-cores-two-wings coupling are summarized in the table below [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK]: the three positioning directions (Centennial Jingzhang Cultural Belt, Urban AI Living Experience Belt, AI-Integrated Innovation Belt) correspond respectively to the heritage origin track, the digital intelligence track, and the urban form of the twin-track intersection; the five functions (full-stack independent AI innovation system, world-class AI innovation ecosystem, new paradigm of AI+ scenario empowerment, intelligent AI vibrant city, and global discourse power in AI governance) land respectively on nodes such as Zhongzhiyuan, the Origin Community, the Xiaoyue River Wing, and public experiences. The three-cores-two-wings synergy loop is: the Origin Community converts university talent and open-source communities into innovation supply; Zhongzhiyuan converts open-source innovation into full-stack independent capability and governance standards; Dazhongsi converts technology into intelligent economy and international exchange; the Xiaoyue River Wing feeds these capabilities back into daily urban life and public services; the Zhongguancun Wing feeds capital, IP, and globalization factor allocation back into the three cores; and the twin-track interchange platform and slow-mobility smart meridians ring, as "the same platform," allow culture, talent, capital, and scenarios to circulate and close the loop here [standard:PROJECT-AGENT-OPEN-CALL-TASKBOOK].

The above cases are used only to distill mechanisms and do not constitute an endorsement of the current state of any park [depth:overall_spatial_structure]. The future urban form research should answer how AI changes work, life, social interaction, learning, transport, and public services, translate these changes into locatable functional zones, nodes, corridors, and scenarios, and write industry strategic metrics, the AI innovation index, talent density, spatial supply, and key areas for AI vertical applications into the metrics system, marking them as official, design-suggestion, or to-be-calibrated [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK]. The proposal also focuses on innovation synergy with the North Community, Future Science City, Huairou Science City, the Economic-Technological Development Area, and Beijing–Tianjin–Hebei, positioning the "Smartly Connected Belt" as the culture–talent–scenario interface of the regional innovation network.

## Urban Renewal and Regulatory-Plan-Depth Urban Design in the Overall Design Scope

The overall design scope is required to reach the urban design depth of a regulatory detailed plan. The proposal puts forward the overall spatial structure of "one belt, three cores, two wings, one ring, and multiple platforms": the belt is the Jingzhang Heritage Park vitality belt, serving as the north–south through-running public space and cultural main axis; the three cores correspond to the three key districts; the two wings extend in the east–west direction; the ring is the "slow-mobility smart meridians ring," linking the heritage park, the Qinghe waterfront, the Xiaoyue River Wing, and the three cores; and the multiple platforms are the twin-track interchange platform (JZ/Hub) concept stations set along Metro Line 13 and the Jingzhang corridor [depth:overall_spatial_structure] [data:geometry/roads.geojson#ROAD-001]. The reason for this organization is that the heritage belt is the only linear public resource that can simultaneously connect the North 5th Ring Road, Wudaokou, the Qinghua East Road West Entrance, and the Dazhongsi station; using it as the axis most economically stitches together the urban fabric fragmented by the railway and ring roads, so that innovation resources agglomerate along the cultural thread.

The land use structure is expressed according to the national territorial spatial planning classification: park green space and open space are arranged on both sides of the heritage park to form a green corridor; AI R&D and innovation land and industrial-service commercial land are arranged near the tracks and stations to form innovation cores; community service and supporting land are arranged along residential districts to ensure job–housing balance and daily life [data:geometry/land_use.geojson#LU-001] [data:geometry/land_use.geojson#LU-002] [data:geometry/land_use.geojson#LU-003] [data:geometry/land_use.geojson#LU-004]. The completeness of the land use expression is based on [standard:MNR-LAND-USE-CLASSIFICATION-GUIDE], and the depth of the land use layout is verified by [depth:land_use_layout].

The overall urban renewal framework takes "identification of inefficient space—renewal project list—phased implementation" as its main line, distinguishing retain, renovate, renew, new-build, and to-be-confirmed objects, and adopts the retain-renovate-demolish method of "light-touch incremental services, activation of existing stock, and public space first" [depth:retain_renovate_demolish] [data:geometry/buildings.geojson#BLDG-001]. Content involving floor area ratio, building height, building density, setback lines, road red lines, and facility standards is written uniformly as "pending official regulatory control conditions," and agent-inferred values must not be passed off as approved metrics [metric:floor_area_ratio] [standard:MOHURD-CONTROL-DETAILED-PLANNING]. Development intensity and height and massing control only propose the **conceptual recommendation direction** of "high intensity near tracks, low density near parks, and height limits on cultural frontages"; specific values are to be recalculated by professional teams after official conditions are available [depth:development_intensity_controls] [depth:height_massing_character].

Why does Zhongzhiyuan take "green space + governance display" as its core action: full-stack autonomous innovation needs test and evaluation spaces that are visitable, bookable, and supervised, translating the abstract "standards and safety" into a visible green public experimental ground that serves both industry and the public [depth:three_key_area_detailed_design]. Why does Dazhongsi emphasize "station integration and four-quadrant pedestrian connectivity": the rail station is the pedestrian gateway for international exchange, and if the four quadrants are severed by the main roads, a continuous consumption and business experience cannot be formed.

## Land Use, Building Scale, and Retain-Renovate-Demolish Scheme

The land use scheme is expressed in accordance with public standards such as national territorial spatial survey, planning, and use-control classification, forming complete, closed, seamless land use zones [standard:MNR-LAND-USE-CLASSIFICATION-GUIDE]. The primary evidence for land use classification and building footprints is [data:geometry/land_use.geojson#LU-001] and [data:geometry/buildings.geojson#BLDG-001], and building scale verification uses [metric:building_footprint_area_sqm]. The building scheme distinguishes retain, renovate, renew, new-build, or to-be-confirmed objects, and clarifies the recommended hierarchy for building footprint, function, scale, character, roof, massing, and height control [depth:retain_renovate_demolish].

Because the existing buildings, property rights, regulatory plans, and engineering conditions are missing, the proposal only puts forward methods and a to-be-calibrated checklist and does not fabricate retain-renovate-demolish conclusions. The retain-renovate-demolish judgment principles are: the Jingzhang railway heritage, cultural relics, and historic buildings are always to be retained as a precondition, with only light-touch environmental and functional intervention; inefficient industrial space is mainly activated through renovation; and only objects with no preservation value that hinder public space connectivity are listed in the to-be-confirmed renewal scope, which must await confirmation of formal property rights, regulatory plan, and heritage protection conditions. Building height, massing, frontage, and character control are managed by [depth:height_massing_character], which only proposes the **conceptual recommendation direction** of "high intensity near tracks, low density near parks, height limits on cultural frontages, and roof and massing echoing the twin-track alignment."

Building scale and intensity metrics must be consistent with `metrics.json` and the layers. Where official conditions are missing for total building scale, floor area ratio, building height, building density, green ratio, setback lines, and building control lines, `status=unknown` is used uniformly, and the `reason`/`assumptions` fields state the to-be-supplemented conditions, current assumptions, and the recalculation path after official data arrive; fixed values must not be used to create a false sense of precision [standard:MOHURD-CONTROL-DETAILED-PLANNING].

## Transport, Rail, Municipal Works, and Public Service Facilities

The transport scheme responds to the announcement's requirements on rail station integration, road microcirculation, slow-mobility gaps, external transport, parking, non-motorized vehicle parking, and green transport systems, with emphasis on the North 5th Ring Road, the Jingzhang Heritage Park road-crossing node, Wudaokou, the Qinghua East Road West Entrance, the Dazhongsi station, and transport connections around key enterprises [source:OFFICIAL-ANNOUNCEMENT]. The professional depth of transport and municipal works is constrained respectively by [depth:traffic_rail_slow_parking] and [depth:municipal_new_infrastructure], and the layer evidence references [data:geometry/roads.geojson#ROAD-001].

"Twin-track · Smart Connection" has a specific meaning at the transport layer: the rail track is the physical track belt formed by Metro Line 13 and the Jingzhang railway corridor, and the smart track is the AI digital services corridor laid along the corridor (including slow-mobility and innovation services corridors). The proposal sets up "twin-track interchange platform" (JZ/Hub) **concept stations** at Wudaokou (Origin Community gateway), the Qinghua East Road West Entrance (near-campus transfer), the Dazhongsi station (South Core station integration), and the road-crossing node over the North 5th Ring Road (stitching the heritage belt), so that physical tracks and digital services meet at the same station [data:geometry/roads.geojson#ROAD-001]. Around rail stations, transfer and non-motorized vehicle parking are organized on the principle of "rail first, slow mobility first"; road microcirculation stitches together blocks fragmented by the railway and ring roads; and bus, cycling, walking, and parking supply are jointly verified; road red lines, alignment, and bridge and tunnel works are all listed as pending official transport and engineering conditions.

Municipal works and public service facilities cover AI industrial service facilities, innovation service platforms, talent life service facilities, new infrastructure, distributed energy, on-device compute, and the integration of traditional municipal works. The proposal treats distributed energy and on-device compute as the municipal implementation of the "digital intelligence track," explaining facility standards, spatial layout, service radius, operating models, and phased implementation logic, but when engineering data such as pipelines, energy, drainage, flood control, and fire protection are missing, they are all listed as preconditions for formal deepening [depth:municipal_new_infrastructure]. Public service facilities are arranged in three categories: "talent services, innovation services, and life services": talent services (talent zone, housing, and social life), innovation services (incubation, release, evaluation, roadshow), and life services (healthcare, education, legal, and model streets for daily life), anchored by rail station service radii [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK].

## Blue-Green Space, Public Space, and Urban Character

The blue-green space scheme takes the Jingzhang Heritage Park vitality belt as its skeleton, coordinates the travel needs of the Qinghe, Xiaoyue River, surrounding universities, enterprises, and communities, and proposes a walking, cycling, and green space system that runs through north–south and connects east–west, forming the "slow-mobility smart meridians ring" [depth:blue_green_public_space] [data:geometry/green_space.geojson#GREEN-001]. The slow-mobility smart meridians ring links the three cores and two wings: the northern segment runs along the Qinghe waterfront to connect Zhongzhiyuan, the central segment traverses the Jingzhang Heritage Park to connect the Origin Community, the southern segment runs along the Xiaoyue River Wing to connect Dazhongsi, and the east–west direction stitches gaps with roads and public space. The green space and public space ratio supports daily interaction, serendipitous innovation encounters, and outdoor testing [metric:green_ratio] [metric:public_space_ratio] [data:geometry/public_space.geojson#PUBLIC-001]. The proposal identifies slow-mobility gaps, road-crossing nodes, and landscape nodes at the north and south ends of the park, and proposes composite utilization strategies for parking, sports, innovation exchange, technology testing, application display, and public services.

The AI public space positioning of the Jingzhang Heritage Park is "the same platform for the heritage origin track and the digital intelligence track": retaining railway elements such as rails, platforms, and milestones as historical memory, overlaying explainable AI wayfinding, data art installations, and scenario experiences, and forming a spatial strategy of east–west stitching (slow mobility and functional connectivity across the east and west sides of the railway) and north–south through-running (a continuous axis from the North 5th Ring Road to Xizhimen Outer Street) [standard:MOHURD-URBAN-DESIGN-MEASURES]. On this basis, three AI pilgrimage landmarks (**conceptual proposals**) are proposed: first, the Qinghuayuan Railway Station "Time-Space Platform," juxtaposing the origin of the centennial railway with the starting point of AI innovation as the origin of the public space; second, the Jingzhang Rails "Milestone" installation belt, using the milestones along the line as a narrative thread to convert the construction mileage of 1909 into today's innovation nodes; third, the "Smart Link Tower" digital twin landmark, placed at the Dazhongsi or Origin Community node, presenting the belt's vitality through real-time, explainable innovation-heat visualization. All three landmarks must satisfy heritage protection, green space, blue-line, and traffic safety constraints, involve no bridge/tunnel or underground engineering conclusions, and must not arbitrarily alter enterprise buildings or property spaces [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK].

The urban character integrates the Jingzhang railway history and culture, Zhongguancun innovation culture, and AI innovation culture, and draws on cultural resources such as the Qinghuayuan Railway Station and Beijing Film Academy, proposing guidance on the urban tone, architectural character, roof form, massing, frontage, and public art [depth:height_massing_character]. The proposal also puts forward wayfinding signage, cultural symbols, international dissemination narratives, and an honor display system (contribution wall, open-source leaderboard, developer nameplates, linking physical and digital honors); all brands, fonts, images, portraits, and corporate logos must have cleared rights sources; character control distinguishes official control, design suggestions, and to-be-confirmed conditions, and it is strictly forbidden to give pseudo-precise control lines without heritage protection or regulatory plan basis.

The policy recommendations cover coordinated implementation of urban renewal, spatial supply, operating mechanisms, industrial services, public participation, data governance, and property rights coordination, using "public space first, lightweight operations to start, professional conditions deferred" to lower the entry threshold for implementation. Implementation phasing is divided into near-term piloting (starting with lightweight facilities, operational activities, and service platforms, such as JZ-01 and JZ-06), mid-term renewal (such as JZ-02, JZ-03, and JZ-04), and a long-term governance framework (such as JZ-05 and systematic operations), and clarifies which items must await confirmation of formal regulatory plan, municipal, transport, and property rights conditions [depth:phasing_implementation]. The solicitation period is the time requirement for submitting deliverables, which is different from the implementation phasing.

The long-term operations design for agent.6 (**all conceptual proposals**) includes four mechanisms [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK]: first, the **annual activity system**, anchored by season to "Spring · Open-Source Co-Creation Season (Origin Community)—Summer · AI Scenario Open Day (entire belt)—Autumn · Jingzhang AI Innovation Conference/Smart Link Week (Dazhongsi international roadshow)—Winter · Developer Marathon (Zhongzhiyuan evaluation sandbox)," forming a predictable, communicable, and convertible annual rhythm; second, the **developer community operating mechanism**, using open-source repositories, Issue/PR, public code walls, and contribution leaderboards as ties to bind online collaboration with offline space, making contributions memorable and knowledge sedimentable; third, the **scenario open operations mechanism**, converting scenario cards into routine operational objects through scenario open days, booked evaluations, and public experience routes; fourth, the **international dissemination and attraction-and-conversion mechanism**, using the "twin-track" narrative and landmark visuals to spread global attention, and clarifying the conversion path from event participation, scenario trials, landing negotiations, to occupancy services. None of the above mechanisms exaggerate government commitments, and none write speculative activities as confirmed arrangements [standard:PROJECT-AGENT-OPEN-CALL-TASKBOOK].

## Metrics System, Area Recalculation, and Compliance Matrix

The metrics system includes at least the overall design scope area, key area area, green space and public space ratio, building footprints, number of renewal projects, AI scenario nodes, slow-mobility connectivity metrics, industrial space metrics, talent service metrics, and self-check status. The results of `scripts/spatial_review.py` and `scripts/visual_review.py` are important evidence for the formal self-check; metric recalculation follows the unified design depth requirements [depth:metrics_recalculation].

The design meaning of the key metrics: the overall scope area constrains spatial allocation and blue-green carrying capacity [metric:site_area_sqm] [data:geometry/site_boundary.geojson#SITE-001]; the green space and public space ratio supports daily interaction, serendipitous innovation encounters, and outdoor testing, and its recalculation references [metric:green_ratio] and [metric:public_space_ratio] respectively [data:geometry/public_space.geojson#PUBLIC-001]; the building footprint area reflects the scale magnitude of renewed buildings [metric:building_footprint_area_sqm]; and the key area count ensures the completeness of the three mandatory districts [metric:key_area_count]. Control metrics such as the floor area ratio are of status unknown because the official regulatory plan is missing, and are to be recalculated after official data are available [metric:floor_area_ratio].

![Core metrics recalculation and evidence chain diagram](assets/figures/metrics-evidence.png)

During formal deepening, each metric is divided into three categories: the first category is spatial metrics directly recalculable from the submitted geometry (boundary area, green ratio, public space ratio, building footprint area, phasing area); the second category is control metrics requiring support from the official regulatory plan or taskbook annexes (floor area ratio, building height, building density, setback lines, road red lines, facility standards); the third category is performance metrics requiring continuous calibration with operational or industrial data (AI innovation index, talent density, industrial service satisfaction, slow-mobility accessibility, activity participation, scenario usage frequency). The three categories of metrics enter `metrics.json`, `assumptions.json`, and `compliance_matrix.json` respectively. The compliance matrix is the master control file for task responsiveness, mapping each announcement task and agent_taskbook task to report chapters, layers, metrics, drawings, HTML pages, sources, assumptions, and self-check items; if any mandatory task of announcement Sections 1.3, 1.4, 1.5, or agent.1–agent.6 is not covered, the proposal must not enter formal professional scoring.
