# The Dao Belt · Twin-Track Quest — Overall Conceptual Design for the Century-Old Jingzhang AI Innovation Belt

## Design Basis and Source Inventory

This proposal takes the *International Call for Conceptual Urban Design — Centennial Jingzhang AI Innovation Belt — Prequalification Announcement* issued by the Haidian Branch of the Beijing Municipal Commission of Planning and Natural Resources as its primary basis, and uses the provisional coarse boundaries, key areas, enumerations, metrics, and source list registered by maintainers in `brief/site-package/` as the machine-readable basis [source:OFFICIAL-ANNOUNCEMENT] [standard:PROJECT-OFFICIAL-ANNOUNCEMENT]. The main name proposed in this proposal is "京张问道带" (English: The Dao Belt · Twin-Track Quest), with the auxiliary line "A road that can self-correct — from twin tracks to questioning the way." All spatial implementation recommendations are *conceptual proposals / pending official regulatory planning conditions*, and do not replace statutory planning.

A statement of historical accuracy must be established first in this chapter: the Jingzhang Railway (opened 1909) was the first trunk railway built under Chinese-led engineering, not the first operating railway in China — the earlier Wusong railway (1876) in Shanghai predates it; Zhan Tianfu (Jeme Tien Yow) was the chief engineer leading the Jingzhang project, completed jointly with Chinese and foreign engineers and thousands of workers, with the "人字" (herringbone) zig-zag at Qinglongqiao being one of the key engineering decisions. This proposal deliberately does not frame 1909 as an AI precursor, and does not portray Zhan Tianfu as a solo designer; "The Way" points to "the Chinese connecting the road themselves," not to any technological myth [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK].

The source registry table uses boundaries as follows: 7 formal usable sources, 1 background source, 1 provisional-only source; the agent may not elevate `background_only` or `provisional_only` sources to the status of official boundary, statutory regulatory planning, formal scoring basis, or government implementation commitment [source:SOURCE-REGISTRY]. `agent_fact_pack.md` is a reading-navigation layer, not an authoritative source; factual judgments must still return to the original materials [source:PROCESSED-FACT-PACK]. When the official `SITE_BOUNDARY` and the three `KEY_AREA` are not yet available, the scaffold uses `provisional_boundaries.geojson` to generate a provisional formal package; in the submission package all geometry is tagged with `provisional_constraint` and `official_boundary=false`, for use only in proposal generation, self-check, and visualization, and may not serve as official redlines, approval basis, or precise area conclusions [depth:existing_conditions_diagnosis].

## Three-Level Scope Working Framework

The proposal is organized according to the three levels of scope in the announcement: the *Coordinating Research Scope* addresses the 43.6 km² AI industry ecosystem, strategic positioning, innovation chain, and future urban form; the *Overall Design Scope* addresses the 11.4 km² urban area within 1–2 km around the Jingzhang Heritage Park, requiring an overall framework for urban renewal, industrial spatial layout, transport and municipal support, and urban landscape control; the *Key Area Design Scope* addresses the three detailed design areas totaling 368.4 hectares, requiring definitions of functions and business types, building scale, retain/renovate/demolish classification, public space connectivity, and transport organization [source:OFFICIAL-ANNOUNCEMENT]. The three-level scope is mapped clause-by-clause in `compliance_matrix.json`, ensuring that the mandatory tasks of announcement sections 1.3, 1.4, 1.5 and agent.1–agent.6 all have corresponding chapters, layers, metrics, drawings, and HTML evidence.

The three levels of work are constrained by [depth:three_level_scope_framework] and [depth:overall_spatial_structure], with spatial evidence governed by [data:geometry/site_boundary.geojson#SITE-001] and [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-001] — using the three key areas as the spatial anchors of the Dao Belt. The three levels are not a disjoint set of drawings: the coordinating research determines the "questioning the way" narrative and the industry chain judgment; the overall design translates these judgments into renewal projects, spatial structure, and facility capacity; the key area level validates the implementability of specific parcels.

![Three-Level Scope and "One Track · Three Halls · Two Wings" Spatial Framework Diagram](assets/figures/land-use-structure.png)

The overall concept of this proposal is "Twin-Track Quest": to invert the Jingzhang Heritage Park from a "green backdrop" into a "city main board / operating system," carried by two parallel tracks — the **heritage rail track** (the physical rail heritage carrying the memory of historic locomotives and the public space skeleton) and the **light/data track** (a "data light corridor" of LED / touch-sensitive floor screens carrying the bus of algorithms, data, and scenario elements) — the two run in parallel without intersecting, each with its own responsibility, and draw no new redlines. The "one track" is this twin-track main axis; the *three halls* (deliberately using "坊/Hall" to avoid the generic "核/Core") correspond to the three key areas in the taskbook but are renamed; the *two wings* are the Frame-Quest wing and the Track-Light wing; the *one question* is the Quest Gate at the south end. The spatial structure is recorded as "One Track · Three Halls · Two Wings · One Question."

## Coordinating Research Scope: Industry and Future City Research

The core of the coordinating research scope is to construct a world-class AI innovation ecosystem. This proposal does not adopt the old label "Smart-Connected Belt"; the main name is set as "京张问道带" (English: The Dao Belt), deliberately avoiding homogenized words such as Silk Road and Corridor, taking "Questioning the Way" as the cultural main line and "prova-bility" as the governance baseline [standard:PROJECT-AGENT-OPEN-CALL-TASKBOOK]. The three-stage core thesis is: **The Way** — the Jingzhang Railway (1909) was the first trunk railway built under Chinese-led engineering; "the Chinese connecting the road themselves" is "The Way"; **Questioning the Way** — Zhongguancun is the place that has spent decades repeatedly asking "can we do it ourselves," which is "Questioning the Way"; **Emerging the Way** — in the AI era, asking here for the next step on the path of autonomy and giving answers that can be re-checked is "Emerging the Way." Space carries the Way and the Questioning via "twin tracks"; the cultural main line is "Questioning the Way"; the governance baseline is "provability."

The three major positionings, five major functions, and three halls plus two wings are coupled according to the table below [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK]: the Centennial Jingzhang Cultural Belt falls along the heritage rail track and the cultural narrative N1–N4; the Urban AI Living Experience Belt falls along the light/data track and the Track-Light wing's public compute/scenario experience; the AI Convergence Innovation Belt falls along the innovation chain of the three halls. The five major functions map respectively onto Origin Hall (full-stack autonomous AI innovation system + Provable Sandbox Registry PSR), Convergence Yard (world-class AI innovation ecosystem + Cultural Vector Memory CVM), Frontier Gate (intelligent-native new business forms + international communication), the Frame-Quest wing (AI+ scenario empowerment + Civic Aggregation Crowdsourcing CACS), the Track-Light wing (intelligent AI vitality city + Compute-Data-Scenario tripartite arbitration CDSA), and the three-chamber governance system (global discourse on AI governance).

## Overall Design Scope: Urban Renewal and Regulatory-Plan-Depth Urban Design

The overall design scope is required to reach the urban-design depth of a regulatory detailed plan. This proposal proposes the overall spatial structure of "One Track · Three Halls · Two Wings · One Question": the one track is the twin-track main axis of the Jingzhang Heritage Park (heritage track + light/data track in parallel); the three halls divide labor north-central-south; the two wings expand east-west; the one question is the Quest Gate at the south end [depth:overall_spatial_structure] [data:geometry/roads.geojson#ROAD-001]. The reason for this organization is that the heritage belt is the only linear public resource that simultaneously connects the North Fifth Ring Road, Wudaokou, Tsinghua East Road West Station, and Dazhongsi Station; using it as the operating system can most economically stitch the urban fabric fragmented by the railway and the ring roads, allowing innovation resources to agglomerate along the "Questioning the Way" thread rather than treating the park merely as a green backdrop.

The land-use structure is expressed per the territorial-spatial planning classification: park green space and open space along both sides of the heritage park form a green corridor [data:geometry/land_use.geojson#LU-002]; AI R&D innovation land and industrial service commercial land are placed along the heritage track and stations on either side of the light/data track corridor [data:geometry/land_use.geojson#LU-001], [data:geometry/land_use.geojson#LU-003]; community service and ancillary land are placed along residential parcels to ensure job-housing balance [data:geometry/land_use.geojson#LU-004]. Land-use expression completeness follows [standard:MNR-LAND-USE-CLASSIFICATION-GUIDE], and land-use layout depth is checked by [depth:land_use_layout].

The overall urban renewal framework follows the main line "low-efficiency space identification → renewal project list → phased implementation," distinguishing retain, renovate, renew, new-build, and pending-confirmation objects, adopting the retain/renovate/demolish method of "light touch of incremental services, activation of stock renovation, public space first" [depth:retain_renovate_demolish] [data:geometry/buildings.geojson#BLDG-001]. Content involving floor area ratio, building height, building density, setback, road redline, and facility standards — because official regulatory planning conditions are missing — is uniformly written as "pending official regulatory planning conditions," and agent-speculated values must not masquerade as approved metrics [metric:floor_area_ratio] [standard:MOHURD-CONTROL-DETAILED-PLANNING]. Development intensity and height-massing control only propose the conceptual direction of "high intensity near the heritage track, low density near the park, height limits on cultural interfaces, roofs and massing echoing the twin-track line," with specific values to be recalculated by a professional team once official conditions are available [depth:development_intensity_controls] [depth:height_massing_character].

![Index Map of the Three Key Areas (Origin Hall · Convergence Yard · Frontier Gate)](assets/figures/key-areas.png)

Why Origin Hall places the "Provable Sandbox Registry" as its core action: what is scarcest in full-stack autonomous innovation is not compute, but that "claims can be re-checked by outsiders." PSR requires every registered AI scenario/model to register a model card + reproduction-script hash + clear-rights list, re-checkable by third-party volunteers; Jingzhang is thereby defined as the "Capital of AI Provability" rather than yet another "evaluation base" [depth:three_key_area_detailed_design]. Why Convergence Yard emphasizes "campus-park-street stitching + oral-history vector library": university talent is the largest source of AI talent; opening up both sides of the wall while using CVM to index the authorized weekly oral histories submitted by developers, vetted by the three chambers for inclusion, makes the collective memory of "Questioning the Way" retrievable and inheritable rather than scattered in PR comments. Why Frontier Gate emphasizes "station-integrated four-quadrant pedestrian flow + Quest Gate": rail stations are the entry points for international visitors, and if the four quadrants are cut apart by arterials, a continuous commercial and business experience cannot form; the Quest Gate does not erect a memorial tower but stands up an annual question open to the whole city — the public asks one question each year, and the developer community responds with open-source prototypes, making it both the reverse version of a pilgrimage landmark and the core of Dao Week.

## Land Use, Building Scale, and Retain/Renovate/Demolish Plan

The land-use plan is expressed per public standards such as the territorial-spatial survey, planning, and use-control classification, forming a complete, closed, seamless set of land-use zones [standard:MNR-LAND-USE-CLASSIFICATION-GUIDE]. The primary evidence for land-use classification and building footprints is [data:geometry/land_use.geojson#LU-005] and [data:geometry/buildings.geojson#BLDG-001], and building-scale re-check uses [metric:building_footprint_area_sqm]. The building plan distinguishes retain, renovate, renew, new-build, or pending-confirmation objects, and clarifies the recommended hierarchical levels for building footprint, function, scale, style, roof, massing, and height control [depth:retain_renovate_demolish].

Because current building, ownership, regulatory planning, and engineering conditions are missing, the proposal only presents the method and a pending-calibration list, and does not fabricate retain/renovate/demolish conclusions. The retain/renovate/demolish principle is: for the Jingzhang Railway heritage, cultural relics, and historic buildings, preservation is the premise and only environmental and functional light touches are made; for low-efficiency industrial spaces, renovation and activation predominate; only objects with no preservation value that obstruct public-space connectivity are listed in the pending-confirmation renewal scope, and they must await confirmation of formal ownership, regulatory planning, and heritage-protection conditions. Building height, massing, interface, and style control are governed by [depth:height_massing_character], only proposing the conceptual direction of "high intensity near the heritage track, low density near the light/data track, height limits on cultural interfaces, roofs and massing echoing the twin-track line."

Building-scale and intensity metrics must be consistent with `metrics.json` and layers. When official conditions for total building scale, floor area ratio, building height, building density, green-space ratio, setback, and building control line are missing, `status=unknown` is used uniformly, with the `reason`/`assumptions` fields stating the conditions to be filled, current assumptions, and the recalculation path once formal data arrive; fixed values must not be used to manufacture a false sense of precision [standard:MOHURD-CONTROL-DETAILED-PLANNING].

## Transport, Rail, Municipal, and Public Service Facilities

The transport plan responds to the announcement's requirements on rail-station integration, road micro-circulation, slow-mobility breakpoints, external transport, parking, non-motor-vehicle parking, and green transport systems, with focus on the North Fifth Ring Road, the Jingzhang Heritage Park cross-ring nodes, Wudaokou, Tsinghua East Road West Station, Dazhongsi Station, and transport connections around key enterprises [source:OFFICIAL-ANNOUNCEMENT]. Transport and municipal professional depth is governed by [depth:traffic_rail_slow_parking] and [depth:municipal_new_infrastructure], respectively, with layer evidence citing [data:geometry/roads.geojson#ROAD-001].

"Twin-Track Quest" has concrete referents at the transport layer: the heritage rail track is the physical rail corridor (including the Metro Line 13 corridor); the light/data track is the data light corridor laid along the corridor (including slow-mobility and innovation services). The proposal sets "twin-track parallel nodes" at Wudaokou (Convergence Yard gateway), Tsinghua East Road West Station (near-campus transfer), Dazhongsi Station (Frontier Gate station integration), and the cross-North-Fifth-Ring node (stitching the heritage belt), so that the heritage physical track and the data-service light track run side by side at the same station without interflow [data:geometry/roads.geojson#ROAD-001]. Around rail stations, transfers and non-motor-vehicle parking are organized on the principle of "rail first, slow-mobility first"; road micro-circulation stitches blocks fragmented by the railway and ring roads; supply of bus, cycling, walking, and parking is uniformly checked; road redlines, alignments, and bridge-tunnel engineering are all listed as pending formal transport and engineering conditions.

## Blue-Green Space, Public Space, and Urban Landscape

The blue-green space plan takes the Dao Belt's twin-track main axis as its skeleton, coordinating the demands of Qinghe, Xiaoyuehui, surrounding universities, enterprises, and community travel, proposing a north-south贯通 and east-west-connected system of walkways, cycle paths, and green spaces forming the "Dao Quest slow-mobility loop" [depth:blue_green_public_space] [data:geometry/green_space.geojson#GREEN-001]. The slow-mobility loop links the three halls and two wings: the north section follows the Changchun/Qinghe interface to Origin Hall; the middle section crosses the Dao Belt to Convergence Yard; the south section follows Xiaoyuehu to Frontier Gate; east-west connections stitch breakpoints via roads and public space. Green-space and public-space ratios support daily interaction, serendipitous innovation encounters, and outdoor testing [metric:green_ratio] [metric:public_space_ratio] [data:geometry/public_space.geojson#PUBLIC-001].

The Dao Belt's AI public-space positioning is "the same main board of the heritage track and the light/data track": preserving rails, platforms, and milestones as historical memory while overlaying explainable AI signage, data-art installations, and scenario experiences to form east-west stitching and north-south connection strategies [standard:MOHURD-URBAN-DESIGN-MEASURES]. On this basis, three differentiated public-space contrasts are proposed, deliberately creating contrast: first, the N2 twin-track parallel segment — 200 m historic rail + 200 m light-track floor screen in parallel without intersecting; the light track answers only on-site history and events, refusing out-of-scope inference; second, the **AI lights-off / dark-night contrast segment** — deliberately low-energy, no active AI, a starry-night gallery as a witnessing baseline (an empty competitive slot, a contrast point), so the Dao Belt is not only bright but also dim, allowing the public to perceive the boundary of AI; third, the south-end Quest Gate — the public asks, AI auto-de-identifies and displays, with a revoke button retained, and deliberately no closing ceremony.

The three AI pilgrimage landmarks are all conceptual proposals: first, the N1 "Light Car" at Tsinghuayuan Station — a nightly light-track-projected "light car" as an art device stating it is not a replica, with a nameplate listing verifiable engineer names and trades + source cutoff year; second, the N3 Zhongguancun "Imagination Core's" "living-archive wall" — developers submit authorized weekly oral histories vetted by the three chambers, with BFA building "technology and image jointly shaping China's modern imagination" as a living archive; third, the **south-end "Quest Gate"** — a deliberately reverse landmark, not a memorial tower, but a question open to the whole city, renewed annually by the public and answered by the developer community with open-source prototypes — this is the core of Dao Week and the greatest contrast with homogenized patterns such as "Smart-Connected Tower / digital-twin tower." All three landmarks must satisfy heritage-protection, green-space, blue-line, and traffic-safety constraints, contain no bridge-tunnel or underground-engineering conclusions, and do not unauthorizedly modify enterprise buildings or ownership spaces [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK].

Naming/IP directions (all conceptual proposals): the main name "京张问道带" (English: The Dao Belt); the three halls Origin Hall / Convergence Yard / Frontier Gate; the two wings Frame-Quest / Track-Light; the IP symbol "Quest-Rail" (问号轨) — a straight rail smoothly bent into a question mark, abstracted from the Qinglongqiao herringbone-zig geometry, serving triple use as public paving pattern + signage header + event badge. Signage is bilingual Chinese-English + a historical layer with traditional-script phonetic annotations (station name plates use 1909 Chinese characters + Zhuyin phonetic symbols), open-source typeface (Source Han), NFC + machine-readable GeoJSON points + authorization list, Braille tactile graphics and offline voice tours [standard:MOHURD-URBAN-DESIGN-MEASURES]. The urban landscape integrates Jingzhang railway historical culture, Zhongguancun innovation culture, and AI innovation culture, leveraging resources such as the Tsinghuayuan Railway Station and BFA to propose guidance on the urban tone, architectural style, roof form, massing, interfaces, and public art [depth:height_massing_character].

The cultural-narrative three-stage four-nodes (north→south) are recorded into the cultural main line: **N1 Origin · 1909** @ Tsinghuayuan Station heritage segment — a nightly light-track-projected light car as an art device stating it is not a replica, with a nameplate listing verifiable engineer names and trades + source cutoff year, with traditional-script phonetic signage; **N2 Relay · Twin-Track Parallel** @ Wudaokou segment — 200 m historic rail + 200 m light track in parallel without intersecting; the light-track floor screen is a constrained-retrieval Q&A, answering only on-site history and events, refusing out-of-scope inference; **N3 Convergence · Zhongguancun Imagination Core** @ Zhongguancun-BFA segment — a living-archive wall, developers submit authorized weekly oral histories vetted by the three chambers, BFA builds "technology and image jointly shaping China's modern imagination"; **N4 Emerging · South-End Open Core** — an open-air theater + Quest Gate, the public asks, AI auto-de-identifies and displays + a revoke button, with deliberately no closing ceremony [standard:PROJECT-AGENT-OPEN-CALL-TASKBOOK].

## Differentiation Statement

This proposal deliberately avoids the homogenized pattern of "Belt/Spine/Axis/Ring + three cores + two wings + 12 cards + 5 landmarks," focusing on four differentiations:

1. **The Questioning-the-Way narrative replaces the smart-connection slogan** — the three-stage thesis of "The Way · Questioning the Way · Emerging the Way" runs throughout, elevating Jingzhang from the engineering fact of "the first trunk railway" to the cultural proposition of "the Chinese hosting the road-opening themselves," elevating Zhongguancun from an "innovation district" to the site of "repeatedly asking whether we can do it ourselves," and asking in the AI era for the next step of autonomy, that is, Emerging the Way; it does not use overused names such as "Smart-Connected / Smart / digital-twin tower."
2. **The Provable Sandbox Registry PSR as the governance main line** — not writing yet another evaluation base, but writing a registry that lets "claims be re-checked by outsiders"; Jingzhang is defined as the "Capital of AI Provability," where every registered AI scenario carries a model card + reproduction-script hash + clear-rights list + third-party volunteer re-check, turning governance from slogan to an operable urban institution.
3. **Money-and-power visibility as an empty competitive slot** — compute-bill visibility makes public AI compute energy/data costs public by block and time period like water and electricity, deliberately filling the slot of "where the money comes from and where it goes" that all proposals evade.
4. **The AI dark-night / anti-AI-light segment as a contrast point** — deliberately retaining a dark-night contrast segment with no active AI, low energy, a starry-night gallery as the witnessing baseline, so that the Dao Belt is not only bright but also dim, allowing the public to perceive the boundary of AI — the greatest contrast with the "all-element smart, 24-hour lit screen" pattern.

At the same time, in naming, "坊/Hall" is used to avoid the generic "核/Core," "Dao Belt" avoids the generic "Smart-Connected Belt," and the "Quest Gate" is a reverse landmark avoiding the generic "digital-twin tower," so that reviewers remember the points of differentiation rather than yet another recitation of a standard template.
