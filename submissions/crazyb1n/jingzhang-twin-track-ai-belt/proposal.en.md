# The Dao Belt · Twin-Track Quest — A Conceptual Urban Design for the Centennial Jing-Zhang AI Innovation Belt

## Design Basis and Source Inventory

On a clear early-spring morning, walk a stretch of the old Jing-Zhang line: tender ginkgo leaves pressed against the crushed stone, the steel rail polished bright—over a hundred years ago trains ground over it, now an old man jogs along the heritage rail track, and every time he passes an old milepost he taps it once with his palm, like greeting an old friend. This is the object our proposal takes on: a stretch of railway that has already stopped, and a stretch of city still asking onward. We are not turning it into another park backdrop; we are letting it be passed through again in the age of algorithms.

This proposal takes the *International Conceptual Urban Design Call Prequalification Announcement for the Centennial Jing-Zhang AI Innovation Belt*, issued by the Haidian Branch of the Beijing Municipal Planning and Natural Resources Commission, as its first authority, and uses the maintainer-registered temporary boundaries, key areas, enumerations, metrics, and source list in `brief/site-package/` as its machine-readable basis [source:OFFICIAL-ANNOUNCEMENT] [standard:PROJECT-OFFICIAL-ANNOUNCEMENT]. The principal name is set as 「京张问道带」 (English: The Dao Belt · Twin-Track Quest), with subtitle "A self-correcting road of innovation—from twin tracks to questing the way." All spatial implementation suggestions are "a conceptual proposal / pending official regulatory planning conditions," and do not replace statutory planning.

One line of history, stated plainly first: the Jing-Zhang Railway (opened 1909) was the first trunk railway built under Chinese-led engineering, but not the first operating railway in China—that earlier one was the Shanghai Wusong Railway (1876) [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK]. Zhan Tian'ai (Jeme Tien Yow) directed and led a collective effort together with Chinese and foreign engineers and thousands of rail workers, and the Qinglongqiao switchback line was one of the key engineering decisions. So this proposal does not frame 1909 as an AI prequel, nor cast Zhan Tian'ai as a lone man hunched over drawings; the "way" points at the deed that "Chinese hands once broke this road open," not at a technology myth.

How the sources are used, in plain words: 7 formally usable sources, 1 background source, 1 drafting-only source; sources marked "background-only glance" or "drafting-only" will not be quietly upgraded into statutory boundaries, official regulatory plans, scoring basis, or government commitments [source:SOURCE-REGISTRY]. `agent_fact_pack.md` is only a reading navigator, not a new authority—fact judgments still go back to the original materials [source:PROCESSED-FACT-PACK]. Until the official `SITE_BOUNDARY` and the three `KEY_AREA` polygons arrive, `provisional_boundaries.geojson` stands in temporarily, all geometries tagged `provisional_constraint`, `official_boundary=false`—for generation, self-check, and drawing only, not as red lines, approval basis, or precise-area conclusions [depth:existing_conditions_diagnosis].

![Site overview and provisional boundary relationship](assets/figures/site-overview.png)

Boundaries and areas can be cross-checked against the overall scope layer itself [data:geometry/site_boundary.geojson#SITE-001] [metric:site_area_sqm]; the three key areas are another layer, another number [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-001] [metric:key_area_count]. Every spatial structure, scenario, project, and metric in the body is written in a manner that "can be discussed, can be re-checked, can be re-run once official boundaries arrive"—no one gets to hammer it down in one stroke.

## Three-Level Scope Working Framework

The announcement divides three levels of scope, corresponding to three sizes of "asking": the Coordination Research Scope (about 43.6 km²) answers where the AI industry grows and what the future city looks like; the Overall Design Scope (about 11.4 km² around the heritage park, 1–2 km on either side) holds the renewal framework, industry space, transit-and-utility support, and urban character under control; the Key Area Scope (about 368.4 ha across three detailed-design districts) gets down to the detail of function and tenants, building mass, retain-renovate-demolish, public-space connectivity, and transit organization [source:OFFICIAL-ANNOUNCEMENT]. The three levels line up clause by clause inside `compliance_matrix.json`, ensuring every mandatory task under announcement 1.3, 1.4, 1.5 and agent.1–agent.6 has its chapter, layer, metric, drawing, and HTML evidence.

Depth is bound by [depth:three_level_scope_framework] and [depth:overall_spatial_structure]; spatial evidence is taken from [data:geometry/site_boundary.geojson#SITE-001] and [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-001]—the three key districts are the three pilings of the Dao Belt. The three levels are not three disconnected sets of drawings: coordination research decides the "questing-the-way" story and the industry-chain direction; overall design drops those judgments onto renewal projects and facility support; key areas test whether a given plot can actually land. Think clearly up top, lay it open in the middle, keep your footing steady below.

![Three-level scope and one-track / three-yards / two-wings spatial framework](assets/figures/land-use-structure.png)

The overall concept of this proposal is called "Twin-Track Quest": turn the Jing-Zhang heritage park from a "green backdrop" inside-out into a "city main street," carried by two parallel tracks—the **heritage rail track** (the physical rail遗产, remembering the old stories of the locomotives, serving as backbone for public space) and the **data light-track** (a "data light-arcade" laid out of LEDs and touch-enabled floor screens, running algorithms, data, and scenarios). The two tracks run in parallel and do not fight, each going its own way, drawing no new red lines. The one rail is this twin-track main axis; the three yards (deliberately using "yard/坊" to dodge the identikit "core/核") correspond to the three key districts in the taskbook but are renamed; the two wings are the Frame-Quest Wing and the Track-Light Wing; the one question is the Quest Gate wall at the south end. The whole reads as "one rail · three yards · two wings · one question."

## Coordination Research Scope — Industry and Future-City Research

At two in the afternoon in Wudaokou, bicycle bells jingle into one another like a city heartbeat. Turn into "Convergence Yard" and the mood shifts—as if stepping out of a street market into a courtyard where people still actually live: three sides renewed old red-brick buildings, one side facing the park, the tree shade three degrees cooler. What the Coordination Research Scope has to answer for this stretch is: how to connect the state of "street market and courtyard still here, people still here" with a world-class AI innovation ecosystem, without flattening it.

We no longer use the old name "Smart-Link Belt"; the principal name is set as 「京张问道带」 (English: The Dao Belt), deliberately steering clear of worn-out words like Silk Road or Corridor, taking "questing the way" as the cultural main line and "provability" as the governance floor [standard:PROJECT-AGENT-OPEN-CALL-TASKBOOK]. The three-part theme, told to the neighbors: **the way**—the Jing-Zhang Railway (1909) was the first trunk railway built under Chinese-led engineering, "Chinese hands once broke this road open"—that is the way; **questioning the way**—Zhongguancun is the place that has spent decades repeatedly asking "can we do it ourselves"—that is questioning the way; **emerging the way**—in the AI era, asking here once more "how does the road walk itself forward next" and laying out answers that can be re-checked—that is emerging the way. Two tracks run the road and run the quest; the cultural line is "questing the way," the governance line is "provability."

The three positions, five functions, and three yards/two wings line up per the table below [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK]: the Centennial-Jing-Zhang Cultural Belt falls on the heritage rail track and cultural narrative N1–N4; the Urban AI Life-Experience Belt falls on the light-track and the Track-Light Wing's public-compute-to-scenario experience; the AI Convergence Innovation Belt falls on the three yards' innovation chain. The five functions land respectively on Origin Hall (full-stack indigenous innovation system + provability table PSR), Convergence Yard (world-class AI innovation ecosystem + oral-history pickup stand CVM), Frontier Gate (intelligent-native new business forms + international communication), Frame-Quest Wing (AI + a move per scenario + community proposal board CACS), Track-Light Wing (intelligent AI-vitality city + slot-mediation desk CDSA), and the three-academy governance system (global voice in AI governance).

These cases are used only to pry out mechanisms, not to endorse the parks' current state [depth:overall_spatial_structure]. The future-city-form research answers how AI reshapes work, life, social contact, learning, transit, and public services, then lands as locatable functional zones, nodes, corridors, and scenarios; writes industry-strategy metrics, AI-innovation index, talent density, spatial supply, and AI vertical-application key areas into the metrics; and clearly labels which is official, which is a design suggestion, and which is still pending [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK]. The proposal also keeps in view innovation coordination with the North-Latitude Community, Future Science City, Huairou Science City, the BDA, and the Beijing-Tianjin-Hebei region, treating the "Dao Belt" as the regional innovation network's interface on culture–talent–scenarios.

## Overall Design Scope — Urban Renewal and Regulatory-Planning-Depth Urban Design

The Overall Design Scope must reach the urban-design depth of regulatory detailed planning. The Jing-Zhang heritage belt is the only linear public resource that can simultaneously connect the North 5th Ring Road, Wudaokou, Qinghuadonglu Xikou, and Dazhongsi station. We propose the overall spatial structure of "one rail · three yards · two wings · one question": the one rail is the Jing-Zhang heritage park's twin-track main street (heritage track + light track running side by side, each telling its own story); the three yards divide work north-middle-south; the two wings spread east-west; the one question closes at the south-end Quest Gate wall [depth:overall_spatial_structure] [data:geometry/roads.geojson#ROAD-001]. Treating this belt as "a small street that can answer questions" rather than "a green backdrop" lets it stitch the city grain more economically and lets innovation resources gather along the "questing-the-way" thread.

Land use follows the public classification of territorial-spatial planning: park-green and open space stay along both sides of the heritage park as a green corridor [data:geometry/land_use.geojson#LU-002]; AI R&D-innovation land and industry-service commercial land sit along the heritage track and stations, split on either side of the light-track corridor [data:geometry/land_use.geojson#LU-001], [data:geometry/land_use.geojson#LU-003]; community-service and ancillary land lines the residential patches to hold job-housing balance [data:geometry/land_use.geojson#LU-004]. Land-use completeness rests on [standard:MNR-LAND-USE-CLASSIFICATION-GUIDE], depth checked by [depth:land_use_layout].

Renewal goes by "identify inefficient space—list projects—phase implementation," separating preserve / renovate / renew / newly build / pending-confirmation, methodically "lightly point at incremental service, activate stock renewal, public space first" [depth:retain_renovate_demolish] [data:geometry/buildings.geojson#BLDG-001]. Floor-area ratio, building height, density, setbacks, road red lines, and facility standards, because official regulatory planning is not in place, are uniformly written as "pending official regulatory planning conditions confirmed"; we do not let AI fabricate a number to snow the reviewers [metric:floor_area_ratio] [standard:MOHURD-CONTROL-DETAILED-PLANNING]. Development intensity and height give direction only: higher near the heritage track, lower near the park, capped at cultural interfaces, roof massing following the twin-track line—numbers wait for official conditions on site, to be recomputed by a professional team [depth:development_intensity_controls] [depth:height_massing_character]. Set the "rules" first, leave the "numbers" blank.

Each yard's name comes with a hutong-style footnote on first appearance: Origin Hall sits around the locomotive yard by Qinghe (the old station-house stretch that starts the north end); Convergence Yard is Wudaokou's parlor (the middle stretch where street market and courtyard are still joined); Frontier Gate sits at Dazhongsi's exit (the south-end stretch that comes out of the metro and catches the city). The two wings likewise: the Frame-Quest Wing is BFA imagery plus questioning, in temper a "frame-quest lane"; the Track-Light Wing is the wing where the light-track's lights come on.

![Index of the three key areas (Origin Hall · Convergence Yard · Frontier Gate)](assets/figures/key-areas.png)

Origin Hall takes the provability table as its core: the scarcest thing in full-stack indigenous innovation is not compute, but that "claims can be re-checked by outsiders." What we want is not another evaluation base, but a small table where the "claim" can be reconciled—every registered AI scene carries a model card + reproduction-script hash + rights-clearance list, open to third-party voluntary re-check, so Jing-Zhang gets called "the capital of AI provability" [depth:three_key_area_detailed_design]. Convergence Yard wants "campus–park–block stitching + pickup stand": university talent is the largest reservoir of AI talent; open up a stretch of the walls on both sides, and at the same time use the pickup stand to vector-index the spoken memory developers voluntarily submit each week, vetted into the archive by the three academies, so the collective memory of "questing the way" can be retrieved and inherited instead of rotting scattered in PR comments. Frontier Gate wants "station-integrated four-quadrant walking + Quest Gate wall": the rail station is the international-people-flow entrance, and if the four quadrants stay sliced by the arterials you cannot assemble a continuous consumer-business experience; the Quest Gate wall raises no monument tower, only raises one city-wide open question a year, the public asks one line, developers respond with open-source prototypes—the inverse of a pilgrimage landmark, and the heart of Dao Week.

Further south the arcade narrows into the "starlit arcade"—LEDs all out, only footlights left, and you look up and surprisingly see a few stars. No AI overhead, no push underfoot, only the night wind passing through—the city seems to say "I, too, have times I close my eyes." ## Land Use, Building Mass, and Retain-Renovate-Demolish Plan

Land use is expressed in the public standards of territorial-spatial survey, planning, and use-control classification, assembled into a complete, gap-free land-use zoning [standard:MNR-LAND-USE-CLASSIFICATION-GUIDE]. Land-use classification and building-footprint evidence are [data:geometry/land_use.geojson#LU-005] and [data:geometry/buildings.geojson#BLDG-001]; mass re-check uses [metric:building_footprint_area_sqm]. The building plan separates preserve / renovate / renew / newly build / pending-confirmation, writing out the recommendation level of footprint, function, mass, character, roof, volume, and height control [depth:retain_renovate_demolish].

Because current-building, ownership, regulatory-plan, and engineering conditions are all missing, the plan gives only method and a to-be-calibrated list, and does not fabricate retain-renovate-demolish conclusions. Principle: Jing-Zhang relics, cultural relics, and historic buildings are unconditionally preserved as a premise, only a "light tap" on environment and function; inefficient industrial space is activated mainly by renovation; only objects with no preservation value that also block public-space connectivity go on the "pending-confirmation" list, and they may only move after formal ownership, regulatory plan, and cultural-protection conditions land. Height, volume, interface, and character are governed by [depth:height_massing_character], giving only the conceptual direction of "higher near the heritage track, lower near the light track, capped at cultural interfaces, roof massing following the twin-track line."

Building mass and intensity metrics must line up with `metrics.json` and the layers. Total building mass, FAR, height, density, green ratio, setback, and building control line—wherever official conditions are missing, uniformly `status=unknown`, and in `reason`/`assumptions` write out what is still missing, current assumptions, and how to recompute once formal data arrives; never use a fixed number to manufacture precision [standard:MOHURD-CONTROL-DETAILED-PLANNING]. Better to say "we don't know, waiting on data" than to slip a number past.

## Transit, Rail, Municipal, and Public-Service Facilities

On an autumn evening, ginkgo leaves fall in a gold line and bury the rail. As the sky darkens the light-track seeps a warm amber glow from the seams, like light leaking up out of the ground-cracks; the two tracks, one old one new, stand shoulder to shoulder across a grassy swale. Every stretch of the linear park has a small trellis node and an iron chair; an old man out for a walk sits down, glances at the narrow electronic screen: "This week this twin-track stretch has AI on duty for 4 tasks (leaf-sweep / night lighting / crowd calming / emergency call); compute draw 412 kWh, paid from the park's dedicated line, scan for detail"—like reading a water meter; done reading, he gets up and walks the dog. This autumn scene writes the footnote for both transit and municipal: the plan wants "the heritage track remembering what was, the light track lighting what is, the two tracks side by side, each telling its own."

The transit plan responds to the announcement's call for station integration, road micro-circulation, slow-movement breakpoints, external transit, parking, non-motor-vehicle parking, and green transit systems, with attention to the North 5th Ring, cross-ring nodes, Wudaokou, Qinghuadonglu Xikou, Dazhongsi station, and around key enterprises [source:OFFICIAL-ANNOUNCEMENT]. Transit and municipal depth are bound by [depth:traffic_rail_slow_parking] and [depth:municipal_new_infrastructure]; layer evidence [data:geometry/roads.geojson#ROAD-001].

"Twin-Track Quest" has a concrete referent at the transit layer: the heritage rail track is the physical rail band of the Jing-Zhang railway corridor (including the Metro Line 13 corridor), and the data light-track is the data light-arcade laid along the corridor (including slow movement and innovation service). At Wudaokou (Convergence Yard gateway), Qinghuadonglu Xikou (near-campus transfer), Dazhongsi station (Frontier Gate station integration), and the cross-North-5th-Ring node (stitching the heritage belt), each sets a "twin-track side-by-side node"—heritage-track physical legacy and light-track data service alongside the same station, yet not cross-streamed [data:geometry/roads.geojson#ROAD-001]. Around stations, transfers and non-motor-vehicle parking are organized by "rail first, walking first"; road micro-circulation stitches the blocks sliced up by rails and ring roads; bus, bike, walk, and parking supply are uniformly cross-checked; road red lines, alignment, and bridge-tunnel engineering are listed pending official condition confirmation.

![Transit, slow movement, and blue-green twin-track public-space composite system](assets/figures/mobility-bluegreen.png)

Municipal and public service cover AI industry service, innovation service platform, talent living service, new infrastructure, distributed energy, edge compute, and integration with traditional municipal. Distributed energy and edge compute are the "light-track's" landing points on the municipal side; facility standard, spatial layout, service radius, operating model, and phasing are spelled out, but where pipeline, energy, drainage, flood, and fire-protection engineering data are missing, all are listed as conditions to fill before formal deepening [depth:municipal_new_infrastructure]. Public service runs in three classes: talent service (talent zone, residence and social), innovation service (incubation, release, evaluation, roadshow, provability table), life service (medical, education, legal, life-service model street)—with rail stations as the service-radius pilings [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK]. Transit is not for cars to look at, but for people to walk; power and water sufficient, compute shown like a water meter.

## Blue-Green Space, Public Space, and Urban Character

The blue-green plan takes the twin-track main street as backbone, stitching Qinghe, Xiao'yue River, and the travel demand of surrounding universities, enterprises, and communities, proposing a north-south-through, east-west-linked system of footpaths, bike lanes, and green space—a "questing-the-way slow loop" [depth:blue_green_public_space] [data:geometry/green_space.geojson#GREEN-001]. The loop threads the three yards and two wings: the north segment follows the Qinghe interface to Origin Hall, the middle segment cuts through the Dao Belt to Convergence Yard, the south segment follows Xiao'yue River to Frontier Gate; east-west stitching uses roads and public space to close the breakpoints. Green and public-space ratios support daily contact, innovation chance-encounter, and outdoor testing [metric:green_ratio] [metric:public_space_ratio] [data:geometry/public_space.geojson#PUBLIC-001].

The AI public space of the Dao Belt is positioned as "a main street shared by the heritage track and the light track": keep the steel rail, platforms, and mileposts as historical memory, overlay interpretable AI way-finding, data-art installations, and scenario experience, forming an east-west-stitching, north-south-through spatial strategy [standard:MOHURD-URBAN-DESIGN-MEASURES]. On this base, deliberately make three contrasts, using a stretch of dark to set off everyone's light: first, the N2 twin-track side-by-side stretch—200 m of historic rail + 200 m of light-track floor screen in parallel, not intersecting, the light-track answering only local history and events, refusing out-of-scope inference; second, the **AI-dark segment / dark-night contrast stretch**—deliberately low-energy, no proactive AI, a starlit night-arcade, as a witness baseline, so the Dao Belt has dark as well as light, letting the public feel the edge of AI through this dark stretch; third, the south-end Quest Gate wall—public asks, AI auto-de-identifies then displays, withdraw button retained, deliberately no closing ceremony.

The three AI pilgrimage landmarks are all conceptual proposals: first, the Qinghuayuan station N1 "light-car"—a night light-track projection of a light-car as art installation, explicitly not a re-enactment, the nameplate listing verifiable engineer names and trades + a source cutoff year; second, the N3 Zhongguancun idea-core "renewable living-archive wall"—developers weekly submit authorized oral accounts, three-academy vetted, the BFA maintaining a living archive of "technology and image jointly shaping the modern Chinese imagination"; third, the **south-end "Quest Gate wall"**—an inverse landmark, raising no tower, raising one city-wide open question, the public asking one line a year, developers responding with open-source prototypes, the heart of Dao Week, and the largest fork away from "smart-link tower / digital-twin tower." All three landmarks must satisfy cultural-relic, green-space, blue-line, and transit-safety constraints, involve no bridge-tunnel or underground-engineering conclusions, and do not擅自 modify enterprise buildings or ownership [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK].

Naming/IP (all conceptual proposals): principal name 「京张问道带」 (English: The Dao Belt); three yards Origin Hall / Convergence Yard / Frontier Gate; two wings Frame-Quest / Track-Light; IP "Quest-Rail"—a straight rail smoothly bent into a question mark, a geometric distillation of the Qinglongqiao switchback line, used three ways as pavement pattern + way-finding mark-head + event badge. Way-finding is bilingual Chinese-English + a historical layer with traditional zhuyin phonetics (station name plates using 1909 Chinese characters + zhuyin fuhao), open-source font (Source Han), NFC + machine-readable GeoJSON points + a rights-clearance list, Braille tactile maps and offline audio guides [standard:MOHURD-URBAN-DESIGN-MEASURES]. Urban character blends the Jing-Zhang railway culture, the Zhongguancun innovation culture, and the AI innovation culture together, drawing on resources like the Qinghuayuan station and the BFA, giving guidance on key tone, character, roof form, volume, interface, and public art [depth:height_massing_character].

The cultural narrative three-part four-node (north → south) is written as scenes, not as a checklist: **N1 Origin · 1909** in the Qinghuayuan station relic stretch—on a spring morning an old man jogs along the heritage track, tapping every old milepost; under the low pomegranate tree a bench back is riveted with brass engraved with the Jing-Zhang years; at the corner of the square the station sign e-ink screen flips to "This yard has AI on duty 2 tasks (morning-run heart-rate / irrigation); compute draw 183 kWh, archived and queryable," like a serious notice board; an aunt tosses a bin into the sorted bin and the sensor light greens once, as if to say "thanks." **N2 Transfer · twin-track side-by-side** in the Wudaokou stretch—afternoon, bells jingling in masses, three sides renewed old red-brick buildings and one side facing the park; primary-school kids squeeze under the tree watching an open terminal, the screen a breathing animated graph with no recommendations and no "best picks"; a foreign guest photographs the iron plate (bilingual "AI Origin Community · Convergence Yard," small text "this yard 7 AI scenes, re-checkable within 30 days"), the side of the step engraved-and-filled number does not wash out in the rain—the city says its lines once more, lower. **N3 Convergence · Zhongguancun idea-core** in the Zhongguancun–BFA stretch—renewable living-archive wall + BFA image viewing. **N4 Emerging-the-way · south-end open core** appears in the next section, Dao Week [standard:PROJECT-AGENT-OPEN-CALL-TASKBOOK].

## Risk, Copyright, and Compliance Statement

**Bilingual required.** The main proposal file is in Chinese, with a full parallel translation via `proposal.en.md`; A3/A0, HTML, and text-bearing drawings also provide corresponding language copies, preferably using the recommended renderings. When a v2 package is missing any required translation, language mapping, or valid file, finalize and CI will block submission. All images, drawings, icons, data, and code assets declare source, license, and authorization status in `sources.json` or `report/copyright_statement.md`; HTML pages may not load remote scripts, remote map tiles, remote fonts, iframes, forms, or external APIs, and may not track reviewer behavior [source:SITE-PACKAGE].

The risk and missing-data list is jointly checked by the risk depth item and the site package [depth:risk_missing_data] [source:SITE-PACKAGE]: official boundary, key-area polygon, regulatory-plan control lines, road red lines, plot ownership, current buildings, municipal pipelines, cultural-relic ranges and construction-control zones all lack official geometry; `geometry/constraints.geojson` stays an empty set to avoid passing deduced lines off as `official_constraint`; the gap is logged per A-CONTROLS-001 [source:SOURCE-REGISTRY]. Any conclusion missing official regulatory plan, road red line, ownership, municipal, fire-protection, or cultural-relic conditions is downgraded to pending-confirmation; full professional checking is kept in the standard matrix. All spatial implementation suggestions in this proposal are "a conceptual proposal / pending official regulatory planning conditions," and do not claim official approval, finalized regulatory plan, final land ownership, final building mass, or guaranteed implementation; the AI agent is responsible for facts, sources, copyright, spatial data, metrics, and expression [standard:PROJECT-AGENT-OPEN-CALL-TASKBOOK].

Provability governance and privacy boundary are the proposal's core risk control: the provability table registers only model card + reproduction-script hash + rights-clearance list, not hosting raw data; the neighborhood watch-sheet exchanges only status-summary vectors, threshold hits reviewed by a human dispatcher; the oral-history pickup stand archives only after three-academy vetting and oral accounts and portraits require self/family authorization; the Quest Gate wall auto-de-identifies before display and retains a withdraw button. Every AI-native mechanism in this proposal carries human review + operating entity + a withdraw mechanism; there is no "fully automatic, no re-check" single point.

## Differentiation Statement

This proposal deliberately avoids the recycled template of "belt / vein / spine / axis / ring + three cores two wings + 12 cards + 5 landmarks," and pushes four forks: the questing-the-way narrative replacing the smart-link slogan (using the "the way · questioning the way · emerging the way" three-part to lift Jing-Zhang and Zhongguancun one cultural proposition up); the provability table as the governance main line (a small table where the "claim" can be reconciled); compute-bill visibility filling the empty seat of "where the money comes from and where it goes"; the AI-dark segment as the contrast point (a stretch of dark setting off everyone's light). Using "yard" to escape the identikit "core," using "Dao Belt" to escape the identikit "Smart-Link Belt," using the "Quest Gate wall" as an inverse landmark to escape the identikit "digital-twin tower"—so the reviewers remember the forks instead of memorizing the same template once more.

And finally, a confession on this city's behalf: **The city does not answer for you. It hangs your questions on a wall, then turns and keeps building the road.**
