T1 FIELD EVIDENCE MINIMUM PACKAGE NOT AUTHORIZED · NOT RUN

Minimum field evidence package

Paired walks begin only after lawful authorization and a safety plan. This is neither a public-opinion survey nor an engineering survey.

01

Paired-walk unit

Same origin, destination, period, weather and works state: accompanied representative-user walk + independent professional repeat.

02

Users and consent

Wheelchair users, older people, carers and pram users participate voluntarily, may withdraw, and receive travel and access support.

03

Time coverage

Pair weekday morning/evening peaks, off-peak and weekend daytime; do not force exposure to extreme conditions.

04

Minimum observations

Detour time, crossing wait, barrier breaks, construction and service state; every current value remains null.

05

QA and independent review

Calibrate forms, retain anomalies and missingness, and use a reviewer not involved in initial collection for sample repeats.

06

Privacy minimization

No identifiable faces, plates, names or precise personal traces; keep necessary aggregate records and a deletion date only.

07

Field stop conditions

Stop for unsafe conditions, withdrawal, failed support, unauthorized capture, or weather/crowding that invalidates the protocol.

08

Required outputs

Anonymized walks, barrier register, time comparison, QA record, bilingual summary, and an explicit proceed or no-decision record.

IMMEDIATE PAUSE

Any trigger means stop, then investigate

  • Serious safety event or warning
  • Break in the complete accessible chain
  • Failure of equivalent non-digital access
  • Unauthorized data collection
  • Failure of responsibility or maintenance capacity

The model cannot resume automatically. Record and repair the cause, then require review by the accountable entity and affected users.
