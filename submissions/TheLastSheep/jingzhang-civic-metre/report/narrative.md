# 京张一米 / THE CIVIC METRE

百年京张曾以轨道、站台与时刻表把北京连接到远方。算法时代的连接问题更小，也更容易被忽略：一个人能不能跨过站口、路缘、门口和柜台之间的最后一米？本方案用十二个可审计节点、一条连续服务主脊、三处共测原型和一套人工接管协议，把 AI 从展厅主题变为公共责任。

The century-old Jingzhang Railway connected Beijing outward through tracks, platforms and timetables. In the algorithmic city, a smaller connection is easily missed: can everyone cross the last metre between station exit, curb, door and counter? Twelve auditable nodes, a continuous service spine, three co-test prototypes and a human-takeover protocol turn AI from exhibition content into civic responsibility.
