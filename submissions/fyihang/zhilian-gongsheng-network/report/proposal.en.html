Zhilian Symbiosis Network

The proposal links knowledge production, industrial conversion, community services and public governance through one civic chain, three hubs, two wings and multiple reversible nodes. It prioritizes adaptive reuse, permeable surfaces, rain gardens, shade, accessibility and maintainable components. All spatial actions are conceptual; provisional geometry is only for display and intake validation. 来源 深度

Enhanced resubmission: implementable collaborative-renewal evidence

Regional network, cases and ecosystem

The framework connects daily services, research collaboration, application transfer and Jing-Jin-Ji scenario recognition as a conceptual cooperation network, not an administrative claim. Six cases translate only low-impact, reversible and public-interest methods: Barcelona superblocks, Helsinki urban experiments, Seoul regeneration, Copenhagen blue-green public space, Kyoto university-industry collaboration and Amsterdam circular districts.

Three test protocols and public governance

T-01 tests shaded, step-free walking and paper wayfinding through observation and immediate human stop rules. T-02 records rainwater performance, replacement cycles and maintenance labour without personal data. T-03 tests public-service scenarios with de-identified facility data, human review, appeal, pause and exit. Older people, children, disabled people, night users, non-smartphone users and low-income residents retain offline services, paper information and staffed counters.

Projects, operation and acceptance

JZ-01—JZ-06 combine short pilots, medium adaptive renewal and long network governance. A district operator leads, owners and specialist agencies collaborate, and public authorities supervise. Cost bands are low, medium and high pending engineering confirmation. Entry requires clear ownership, fire safety, accessibility and maintenance responsibility; exit follows safety, maintenance or public-feedback failure. KPIs cover removed access gaps, shade and rainwater-facility condition, offline-service availability, closed feedback loops and maintenance response time.

Landmarks, identity and annual programme

Three landmarks—Chain Gateway, Co-learning Steps and Public Adoption Court—use a component identity system of deep teal, rainwater blue, vegetation green and warm gold. Annual programming includes an open-scenarios week, public-maintenance day, joint review day and outcome-return season; long-term assets are an open protocol library, maintenance log, offline-service guide and public-feedback archive.

Boundary and metrics

Area and ratio values are estimated from provisional_only geometry for illustration and comparison only. They are not official boundaries, precise areas or scoring inputs; official data triggers recalculation of boundary, key areas, metrics, figures and boards.
