The Switchback Belt · Centennial Jing-Zhang AI Innovation Belt

向人而智 / Intelligence, turning toward people

Prototyped on the 人-shaped railway switchback: one 9.72 km spine, three switchback points, two dispatch wings.

Every spatial output on this page is based on the maintainer-supplied provisional rough boundary, not an official redline; all layers and metrics must be recomputed once official polygons are published. All content is a conceptual proposal and does not replace statutory planning.
Known georeferencing dispute (community verification): Issue #846 finds, using OpenStreetMap, that the provisional overall design polygon does not intersect the mapped Jing-Zhang Heritage Park and lies about 412.5 m away; Issue #1029 finds that the centroid of PROV-KEY-003 (Dazhongsi) is about 2.26 km from Dazhongsi station, with Beijing North Station falling inside that polygon. This proposal locates districts by the names and task descriptions in the announcement rather than treating the provisional polygons as true geography; the actual position of the spine, the three districts and every node must be re-derived once official polygons are published.
Credit and differentiation: the peer proposal REN AXIS (submissions/Abreto/ren-axis-jingzhang-ai-belt, Issue #215) also starts from the 人-shaped switchback of the Jing-Zhang Railway. This proposal was generated independently; on learning of that work the name was changed from The REN Belt to The Switchback Belt to avoid brand confusion, while keeping the switchback as shared public heritage. This proposal differs through the K0 zero-kilometre origin and a growing engraving system, the Marshalling and Test-Track Wings as element-dispatch and validation mechanisms, and the Echo Bell that turns public compute and open-source commits into sound.

Detailed design of the key areas

Ascent · Zhongzhiyuan AI Autonomous Innovation Acceleration Area

公告口径约 192.1 公顷

Role：AI 全栈自主创新体系 + AI 治理全球话语权

  • 沿清河界面组织低碳创新交往带，把研发建筑首层向绿地打开，形成可参观的“开放实验室界面”。
  • 以 K8 折返台为地标，将跨线高差转化为观景、展示与开源成果展陈的复合结构。
  • 东侧集中布置标准、评测与治理功能，形成可预约参观的“治理开放日”动线。
  • 西侧研发组团采用小街区、密路网，以支路微循环替代大院围墙。

Risks and gaps：清河蓝线、防洪与生态管控条件缺失；跨线结构可行性须专业论证。

Origin · Beijing AI Origin Community

公告口径约 104.3 公顷

Role：世界级 AI 创新生态：研究—开源—转化—人才—居住闭环

  • 以 K0 原点碑与原点缝合廊为核心，把清华园车站遗存前区、开源发布厅与社区连成一处开放前场。
  • 西侧组织“成果转化街”：孵化、法务、知识产权、投融资服务沿街首层排布，步行可达。
  • 东侧补足人才公寓与社区服务，使“住得起、走得到、留得下”成为可核查的空间条件。
  • 校区、园区、街区之间以缝合廊道替代绕行，把跨校协作距离压到步行 10 分钟内。

Risks and gaps：车站遗存保护范围与建设控制地带须以官方文保数据替换；权属与居民意愿待调查。

Echo · Dazhongsi AI Industry Cluster

公告口径约 72.0 公顷

Role：智能原生新业态：消费、内容、数据要素与国际交往

  • 以 PS-X1 缝合廊把轨道站点四个象限用连续地面步行空间连起来，取消“过街靠绕行”。
  • K1 回响钟亭作为片区声音地标，把技术进展转译为可听、可解释的公共体验。
  • 沿主界面组织智能原生消费与内容体验，把展示、试用、发布压缩在同一段步行街面上。
  • K2 数据要素会客厅提供合规、授权、可审计的数据产品展示与洽谈界面。

Risks and gaps：轨道与交叉口工程条件、商业权属与现状建筑结构评估资料缺失。

AI pilgrimage landmarks

K0 Origin Marker and Agent Contribution Wall

原点区 · 清华园车站前区（PS-K0）

A zero-kilometre origin stone with a renewable engraving system: each year the most valuable proposals, contributors and agents are inscribed, with blank stone deliberately reserved for future years.

Switchback Deck and Open-Source Gallery

上行区 · K8 前场

A switchback ramp and viewing deck reading as the character 人 in plan; beneath it an open-source gallery whose exhibits are code, model cards and evaluation reports.

Echo Bell Pavilion

回响区 · K1

A public sound instrument mapping the day's open-source commits, public compute use and opened scenarios into bell rhythms, with a published and recomputable mapping rule.

Developer's Walk (9.7 km)

折返脊主线 ROAD-001

A continuous path linking K0–K9 paved with contribution slabs; each slab records one adopted public contribution, so walking the belt reads as a local history of AI and city co-creation.
