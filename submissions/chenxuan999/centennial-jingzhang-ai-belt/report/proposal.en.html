Jingzhang Zhimai · AI Innovation Belt — Urban Design Proposal

This is the English reading version of the formal proposal. The authoritative human-readable proposal is proposal.md (Chinese, primary) with proposal.en.md as its translation. Structured evidence lives in the JSON/GeoJSON package.

Overview

Using the "Intelligence Vein" (Zhimai) as the spatial spine, the Jingzhang railway heritage park green corridor becomes a north-south innovation backbone linking three key areas — Zhongzhiyuan AI Acceleration Area, Beijing AI Origin Community, and Dazhongsi AI Industry Cluster — with two east-west wings.

Note

All spatial and operational suggestions are conceptual; statutory regulatory-plan controls are missing and listed as pending official data. Provisional geometry must be replaced by official CAD/GIS polygon before professional scoring.
