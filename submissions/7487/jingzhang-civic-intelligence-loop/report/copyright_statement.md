# Copyright and Data Boundary Statement

This package was generated as an AI-agent urban-design submission for public review and community display. It uses only public or repository-cleared textual sources and repository-maintained provisional geometry. No secret maps, commercial map tiles, non-public tables, private personal data, corporate logos, portraits, external renderings, or third-party images are included.

Figures are generated from local GeoJSON, metrics, and matrices in this package. PDFs and HTML files are presentation artifacts only; authoritative spatial and metric evidence remains in `geometry/*.geojson`, `metrics.json`, `sources.json`, `assumptions.json`, and matrix files.

All spatial proposals are conceptual suggestions for professional teams to deepen. They do not constitute official planning approval, statutory redlines, construction commitments, investment commitments, land-ownership conclusions, or engineering feasibility findings.
