The Century Answer

百年应答 · BAINIAN YINGDA

In 1909 the Jingzhang Railway answered a century question: could China build its own railway? Today, beside the 9-kilometre park the same railway left behind, more than two thousand AI companies are answering a new one: can China build its own AI? This page renders the full submission package — all nine GeoJSON layers, machine-checked metrics, the task-coverage matrix and self-check evidence — as one readable engineering blueprint in thirteen sheets: overview map, three-tier scope, key areas, land use, mobility, blue-green and public space, structures and renewal, AI scenarios, core metrics, task coverage, self-check status, sources and assumptions.

Three mobility mechanisms (concept suggestions)

  • Break-point parlours · connect first, then stay: answer each break point listed in the official traffic-optimisation scheme with a concept-level connection strategy, then layer staying scenes (benches, canopies, info screens, water points).
  • Robot ramp = accessible ramp: one retrofit, two uses at the 46 entrances (subject to as-built count); wheelchairs share the ramps with low-speed delivery robots. "Wheelchair priority, robots must yield and stop" is a hard pass condition; accessibility metrics carry veto power.

No concept line represents a road redline, cross-section or engineering conclusion; gradients, widths, loads, fire evacuation and operating hours await professional design under accessibility codes and Beijing's low-speed unmanned-delivery-vehicle regulations (A-ROBOT-001).

Assumptions

假设assumptions.json · 7 entries

A-CONTROLS-001 pending_professional_confirmation

Detailed regulatory controls, road redlines, ownership, municipal utilities and engineering constraints must be confirmed from official attachments before any statutory use; the package is reviewable only on the provided boundaries and submitted design layers.

A-BOUNDARY-001 pending_official_redlines

All spatial layers are drawn from the provisional boundary and public information; the three key-area figures are checked only for ±3% consistency and are not survey or ownership conclusions. Any boundary change propagates to the spine alignment, node sitings and area metrics.

A-RECALC-001 standing_trigger

Recomputation trigger: once officials release road redlines, land redlines, heritage-protection zones, regulatory plans, or rail and utility control lines, the affected layers, metrics and drawings must be recomputed item by item; until reverified, no old values or drawings may be cited.

A-ROBOT-001 concept_only

The dual-use robot/accessible ramp and the phase-I test siting are concept illustrations with no gradient, width, load, fire or operating-hours conclusions; they must be developed under accessibility codes and Beijing's low-speed unmanned-delivery-vehicle regulations.

A-GATE-001 pending_as_built

"46 entrances" is the phase-II planning figure, subject to the as-built count; Answer-Gate adoption points adjust with the actual number, which changes point counts but not the mechanism itself.

A-HERITAGE-001 pending_heritage_consultation

GIS data for the Tsinghuayuan Station protection zone is missing: the Kilometre-Zero Post, Answer Book and similar devices are all labelled concept sitings pending heritage consultation. The Answer Bell is a newly cast bell beside the Ancient Bell Museum — a cultural echo only, never striking or using heritage objects.

A-DATA-001 time_sensitive

Registered-LLM counts are time-point figures, not live values (122 in the main text / 128 as footnote / 209 citywide). Each new registration batch or statistical release triggers a full recheck of spike counts, footnotes and display wording.
