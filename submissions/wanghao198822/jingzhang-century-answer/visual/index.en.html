CH-01

Origin of the Idea

灵感由来1909 → 2026 · one question answered twice

In 1909 the Jingzhang Railway could not climb the Guangou grade. Zhan Tianyou drew the character 人: a train runs into a switchback, reverses direction, and two gentle gradients replace one impossible climb. Surveyed, designed and built by Chinese engineers, the line opened that year and answered the century question — can Chinese people build their own railway?

Tsinghuayuan Station was completed in 1910, its name board written by Zhan Tianyou. On 25 March 1949 the Party Central Committee arrived in Beiping "to sit the examination", and this small station was its first stop in the Beijing area; the restored station house reopened on 25 March 2023. The railway itself also handed in an answer: the Jingzhang high-speed line opened in 2019, the old tracks were vacated, and the rails gave way to a park — phase one, 2.4 km and 16.8 ha, opened in June 2023; the park runs about 9 km in total, with 46 entrances planned along phase two, which broke ground in December 2024.

On both sides of the park the candidates have already filled the hall: Haidian hosts more than 2,000 AI companies with core AI industry scale approaching CNY 360 billion; 122 registered and released large models (district government figure as of February 2026); citywide 209 registered models cumulatively, close to a third of the national total, recorded in the statistical communiqué.

Put those two sets of facts side by side and the core image appears by itself: 1909 and today are the same question answered twice. This 9-kilometre park should be built as an answer sheet unrolled across the ground — rail spikes record each answer, the bell announces each milestone, the station preserves the original question. Citizens do not come to read display boards: they crouch down and count the spikes — how far has China's own innovation got?

Question · the century-old Jingzhang heritage belt

The 1909 question is the prompt. The 人-shaped switchback, Tsinghuayuan Station, the old station house and the surviving track form the narrative base — never a theme-park replica.

Answer · the AI innovation belt

Companies and models answer. Every large model independently registered in Haidian earns one bronze rail spike at the path edge; each registration or IPO milestone rings the newly cast Answer Bell.

Reading · the urban AI life belt

Citizens mark the paper. Counting spikes, hearing the bell, passing through Answer Gates and climbing the 人 bridge along 9 km — statistical figures translated into public-space evidence you can crouch down and touch.

CH-02

Scheme Design

方案设计one spine · three segments · two wings · three key areas

The scheme tells one story and tells it at three scales. Spatially it becomes a ~9 km answer-scroll spine in three segments: South Question at Dazhongsi ("Gate of the Bell"), Middle Answer beside Tsinghuayuan Station ("Answer Origin"), North Leap at Zhongzhiyuan ("Leap of 人"); east-west, 46 Answer Gates seep into nearly 70 neighbourhoods (subject to what is actually built). The whole structure sits inside the approved statutory regulatory plan's "one belt, one axis, two centres, multiple nodes" framework — the green spine is the belt, and the Gate of the Bell and the Answer Origin fall on the two centres. This proposal is a concept elaboration above the statutory plan: it does not stand beside it, does not replace it, and does not cite its unpublished control indicators.

2.3

Five Mechanisms Running the Whole Belt

贯穿全带的五个机制mechanism before form

Rail-Spike Band · one model, one bronze spike

A continuous line of bronze spikes along the path edge: one for every large model independently registered and released in Haidian, each engraved with its number and year. Whether or not a break-point is bridged yet, the paving language runs continuously — the answer sheet is visually written through first.

The count is a point-in-time figure (A-DATA-001), updated on the statistical release cycle, never presented as real time.

Answer Bell · one strike per milestone

A newly cast bell beside Dazhongsi, standing in Bell Plaza (concept). A company in the belt that completes registration or an IPO comes to strike it once; the plaza also stitches the four pedestrian quadrants of the metro station.

The new bell is only a cultural echo of the 1733 Ancient Bell Museum collection; it never involves striking or using heritage objects (A-HERITAGE-001).

Answer Gate · one page of the exam paper

Built on the 46 entrances planned for phase two, each gate becomes a page turned open: the century question carved on the outer face, today's answer on the inner face, with nearly 70 neighbourhoods adopting the gates along the belt.

46 is a planning figure, subject to what is actually built (A-GATE-001); a different count does not break the mechanism.

Break-point Living Room · connect first, then stay

For each break-point listed in the official "transport system optimisation" task, a concept-level connectivity response comes first, then a reason to linger: every break-point space is furnished with a combined set of seating, shelter, information screen and drinking point — turning "waiting to pass" into "willing to stay".

Factor-service stations · manufacture a reason to cross

Policy advice, registration coaching and transfer-matching stations placed in low-efficiency space on both sides of the park (never on-the-spot disbursement); the closer to the park, the more shared the facility. Function creates the reason to cross east-west — stitching only counts when people actually walk across.

Three mobility mechanisms (concept suggestions)

  • Break-point Living Room · connect first, then stay: a concept-level connectivity response for each break-point named in the official transport-optimisation task, then a stay-scenario overlay (seating, shelter, information screen, drinking point).
  • Robot ramp = accessible ramp: one retrofit serving two purposes at the 46 entrances (subject to completion); low-speed delivery robots share the ramp with wheelchairs. "Wheelchair priority, robot must yield and stop" is a hard pass condition — accessibility metrics carry a veto.
  • Test first: robot testing is proposed on the opened 2.4 km / 16.8 ha phase-one section with physical separation, a safety attendant and an always-available emergency stop; extension only after staged approval.

No concept line represents a road redline, cross-section or engineering conclusion; gradient, clear width, loading, fire safety and operating hours await professional refinement under accessibility design standards and Beijing's rules for low-speed unmanned delivery vehicles (A-ROBOT-001).
