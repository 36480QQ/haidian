The Century Answer (百年应答)

In 1909, Zhan Tianyou used the Jing-Zhang Railway to answer a question of the century: could the Chinese people build a railway on their own? Today, beside the roughly 9-kilometre heritage park released by that same railway, more than 2,000 AI enterprises in Haidian are answering a new question: can China build its own AI? This proposal turns the Jing-Zhang Railway Heritage Park into a century-spanning answer sheet laid open on the ground — for every large model independently registered in Haidian, a bronze rail spike is set into the edge of the walkway; when an enterprise inside the belt completes registration or an IPO, it rings the newly cast Answer Bell beside Dazhongsi; and every year on 25 March, Tsinghuayuan Railway Station hosts nothing but a public-interest commemorative open day. Citizens do not come to look at display boards — they crouch down and count the spikes: how far has China's independent innovation been nailed in?

All spatial and development content in this proposal is a conceptual recommendation and reference scheme offered for further study by professional teams; it does not constitute a government-approved conclusion. This proposal makes no conclusive judgement on floor area ratio, building height, road right-of-way lines, or demolish–renovate–retain classification.

Three-Level Scope Framework

The proposal organises its work by the announcement's three levels: the Coordinated Research Area (about 43.6 square kilometres) answers the AI industry ecosystem, strategic positioning, and future urban form; the Overall Design Area (about 11.4 square kilometres, the urban area within 1–2 kilometres of the Jing-Zhang Railway Heritage Park) answers the urban renewal framework, industrial spatial layout, transport and municipal support, and urban character control; the Key-Area Detailed Design Area (three detailed-design districts totalling about 368.4 hectares) answers detailed schemes for functions and formats, public-space connectivity, and traffic organisation. The task mapping of the three levels is linked item by item in the compliance matrix Standard, and the spatial evidence rests on the submitted boundary and the three key-area layers Spatial data Metric.

The three levels are tightened by one narrative main line — the "Question–Answer–Reading" mapping of the three belts: the Centennial Jing-Zhang Culture Belt is the question sheet (the question of 1909), the AI Fusion Innovation Belt is the answer sheet (enterprises and models answering), and the Urban AI Life Experience Belt is the marking hall (citizens counting spikes, hearing the bell, and watching showcases along the 9-kilometre walkway). Spatially, this main line lands as an overall structure (conceptual recommendation) of "one 9-kilometre Answer Scroll main axis, three segments — Question in the South, Answer in the Middle, Leap in the North — and 46 Answer Gates permeating east and west into nearly 70 communities": the southern segment is Dazhongsi's Gate of the Ringing Bell, the middle segment is the Answer Origin beside Tsinghuayuan Railway Station, and the northern segment is Zhongzhiyuan's Ren-Shaped Leap Depth Depth.

Coordinated Research Area: Industry and Future City Research

Naming system and logo direction (Task 1). The primary name is "The Century Answer (百年应答)": it gathers the three grand positionings into a single story that can be told, walked, and counted — Question (the Centennial Jing-Zhang Culture Belt), Answer (the AI Fusion Innovation Belt), Reading (the Urban AI Life Experience Belt). A family of names derives from the primary case: the Answer Scroll (the 9-kilometre main axis), the Answer Bell (the newly cast bell at Dazhongsi), the Answer Origin (the Tsinghuayuan Station segment), the Answer Gates (the 46 entrances, subject to actual completion), the Rail-Spike Band (the registered-large-model commemorative band), and the Ren-Shaped Leap (the conceptual node across the Fifth Ring Road). The primary logo motif is "spike cross-section × answering sound rings" — the circle of a bronze rail spike seen from above plus outward-spreading sound-wave rings, the rings echoing both the bell chime and a dialogue bubble; the alternative is an abstracted "人" (ren)-shaped skeleton. Collision screening shows 265 hits for the "人" symbol across the full library, with an existing competing entry using "人" as its primary form, so the primary case must avoid the "人" primary form and the "spike × cursor" composition; "Century Answer / Answer Bell / Rail-Spike Band mechanism" scores only 0–1 hits across the full library, so differentiation rests on the mechanism layer rather than on symbol exclusivity Source. The logo carries a version number (starting from v1.0), iterating annually and being archived, turning "able to learn, able to think, able to evolve" from a slogan into a verifiable loop.

Innovation ecosystem map (Task 2). Haidian's family assets are the confidence behind this answer sheet: 122 registered and launched large models (the district-government official calibre as of February 2026, close to sixty percent of the city total) and about 5,000 PCT patent applications per year Source; the citywide cumulative total of 209, close to one third of the national total, has entered the statistical communiqué Source; an AI core industry scale of nearly RMB 360 billion and a cluster of more than 2,000 AI enterprises (district-level public reporting calibre Source); figures such as 12,300 AI scholars and 26 unicorn companies appear in district-level public promotional material, are not listed among this package's load-bearing sources, and serve as background description only. The ecosystem map is configured (as a conceptual recommendation) as eight factors — land, space, capital, talent, computing power, data, scenarios, policy — across the "Three Zones and Two Wings": among these, scenarios and data are the scarce supplies unique to the Jing-Zhang belt (9 kilometres of continuous public space plus the real demand of nearly 70 communities), while land, capital, and computing power follow an "access and linkage" approach rather than starting new ventures — the focus is to wire the existing industrial assets to the park belt's public-space supply, not to build another industrial park.

Six international benchmark cases (Task 2). Each case is explicitly marked comparable or not comparable: King's Cross (London) defines a district through flagship institutions — comparable as railway-land regeneration, not comparable in its single-developer ownership structure; Kendall Square (Cambridge) lowers start-up thresholds with shared laboratories — comparable in its walkable top-university radius, not comparable in its asset-heavy biomedical format; Singapore's one-north directly inspires this proposal's robot-testing path with its graded testing regime of "start small, open one level after validating the previous" — its city-state unitary ownership is not comparable; Shenzhen's Hetao proves that a small space can host high-level institutional experiments, but its cross-border authorisation logic is not comparable and no authorisation statement may be extrapolated from it; Seoul's DMC shows a "negative-asset reversal + anchor institutions first" path — its government-built-new-district model is not comparable; Sidewalk Toronto is the cautionary counter-example of data-governance failure — large-scale sensor collection lost public trust and the project withdrew, warning this proposal that every digital touchpoint defaults to minimal collection, anonymisation, and published inventories. The six cases together say one thing: districts survive on mechanisms, not on concepts. What can be borrowed are four mechanism prototypes (anchors defining identity, shared facilities lowering thresholds, graded opening of testing, small spaces concentrating institutional trials) plus one red line (data governance precedes technology deployment), not the spatial form of any single case Standard.

The future-urban-form research answers accordingly: what AI changes is not buildings, but what people are willing to do in the city. This proposal designs "counting spikes, hearing the bell, passing through Answer Gates, climbing the Ren-shaped bridge" as everyday urban gestures of the AI era, translating the industry figures in the statistical communiqué into public-space evidence one can crouch down and touch Depth.

Overall Design Area: Urban Renewal and Regulatory-Plan-Level Urban Design

This chapter operates as a conceptual deepening on top of the approved regulatory plan's “one belt, one axis, two centers, multiple nodes” structure: the green axis is the “belt”, and the ten flanking concept parcels are organized within the statutory “two centers and multiple nodes” framework, changing or replacing no statutory control content Source.

The renewal framework of the Overall Design Area is "one axis, two wings, ten blocks" (conceptual recommendation): the 9-kilometre park green axis is the main axis Spatial data, and the two wings arrange ten conceptual block zones — culture, commerce, research, residence, education, business, and flexible reserved land — across the Question-South, Answer-Middle, and Leap-North segments, with the northern Leap segment specifically holding flexible reserved land Spatial data, keeping room for the "questions of the future" that today's answers must not fill up. The block zoning passed topology checks in EPSG:4548 projected space (coverage gap below 1 square metre, overlap below 1 square metre), all land-use codes are taken from the site-package enumerations, and the required depth follows regulatory-detailed-planning-level urban design Standard Depth.

The core of the renewal strategy is to answer the urban question of "east–west stitching" — this railway once separated the two sides of the city for a century. The proposal offers three stitching moves. First, Breakpoint Living Rooms (connect first, then linger): for each breakpoint listed in the official "transport system optimisation solution", the proposal first responds with a conceptual connectivity-optimisation strategy, then overlays scenarios — each breakpoint connection space is furnished with a combined set of seating, rain shelter, information screen, and drinking point, turning "waiting to cross" into "willing to stay". Second, 46 Answer Gates × 70 community adoption: relying on the 46 entrances planned in Phase II (subject to actual completion Source), each gate is made into "a page of an opened examination paper" — the outer face carries the question of the century, the inner face carries today's answer — adopted by the nearly 70 communities along the line. Third, Factor Service Stations: stations for policy consultation, registration coaching, and results matchmaking (no on-the-spot granting) are placed in low-efficiency spaces along both the east and west sides of the park, with sharing intensity rising closer to the park — using functions to create reasons for east–west crossing; only when people are willing to walk through does it count as stitching Spatial data.

The Rail-Spike Band is the thinnest yet longest through-running element: a continuous bronze-spike band along the walkway edge across the full line, whose paving vocabulary runs continuous regardless of whether each breakpoint is already connected, letting the "answer sheet" read as visually complete first. The comprehensive carrying capacity, municipal support, and urban-character control of urban renewal are coordinated under the overall urban design method Standard; wherever building height, development intensity, road right-of-way lines, setbacks, or facility standards are involved, this proposal gives no conclusive values, uniformly stating "pending confirmation of formal regulatory planning conditions", and the corresponding indicators honestly remain in unknown status in the metrics table Depth.

Detailed Design of Key Areas

The three key areas share the official area calibres: Zhongzhiyuan 192.1 hectares, the Beijing AI Origin Community 104.3 hectares, and Dazhongsi 72.0 hectares; the submitted layers continue to use provisional rough extents Spatial data and are not official redlines or precise area bases. On the 9-kilometre scroll, the three areas each carry one narrative segment: Question in the South, Answer in the Middle, Leap in the North Depth.

Dazhongsi AI Industry Cluster = Gate of the Ringing Bell (southern Question segment). The conceptual core is "the Answer Bell and the Bell Chime Plaza": an open circular plaza whose ground paving spreads outward in concentric sound-wave rings (the logo motif enlarged onto the ground); at the centre stands a steel bell frame in the Jing-Zhang truss vocabulary — I-beams, riveted joints, spike-shaped finials — holding a newly cast contemporary bronze Answer Bell; in the distance, the silhouette of the Dazhongsi historical complex founded in 1733 and the new bell frame face each other, one ancient and one present Source. Iron rule: the Answer Bell stands in cultural correspondence with the Ancient Bell Museum and its collection only — no heritage bell is ever struck, moved, or used commercially. Enterprises inside the belt that complete large-model registration or an IPO may book one ring of the bell; ringing eligibility is bound to publicly verifiable events, recording facts without endorsing enterprises. Four "sound-wave paths" radiate from the plaza into the four quadrants around Dazhongsi Station on Subway Line 13, stitching the pedestrian flows severed by roads (the flow organisation is a conceptual recommendation; the traffic scheme awaits professional deepening) Spatial data. Commercial showcases, bell-ringing ceremonies, and spike-installation ceremonies are all concentrated in this segment and carried over into the Zhongzhiyuan segment.

Where does the land for the plaza come from? That is the most practical question in this segment. Field checking shows that within roughly 0.9 by 0.85 kilometres around Dazhongsi there are more than fifty buildings with a combined footprint of about fifteen hectares: a fully built-up, high-density district with no ready vacant land — the plaza can only come out of urban renewal, not out of a drawing. That route has already been demonstrated and published in this very area: within the Dazhongsi pilot area, a low-efficiency commercial site (a home-furnishing mall) has entered urban renewal under a “government coordination + private capital participation + listed land transfer” model, shifting from retail to a mix of technology offices, innovation exchange, and public services, with explicit requirements to improve landscaping and complete the pedestrian system, positioned as a key node of the Jing-Zhang Relics Park innovation-exchange belt and folded into the approved regulatory plan system Source. What this proposal offers is therefore a mechanism recommendation, not a site designation: in future renewal of low-efficiency land in the Question-South segment, reserve no less than a quarter of the parcel as one consolidated public open space of about one hectare (roughly 100 metres square) to host the Bell Plaza. Which parcel, and how, is for rights holders, competent authorities, and professional teams to settle through statutory procedures; this proposal designates no parcel and disposes of no party’s property rights or development arrangements. The scale also stands up to arithmetic: a one-hectare plaza is about a quarter of a three-to-four-hectare renewal parcel, moving in the same direction as such projects’ own goals of adding green space and completing pedestrian links.

Beijing AI Origin Community = the Answer Origin (middle Answer segment). Not one brick or tile of the old Tsinghuayuan Railway Station building (built in 1910, station name inscribed by Zhan Tianyou) is touched; the heritage-protection GIS extent is missing, so every placement is a conceptual siting pending consultation with heritage authorities Source. In front of the station a "plain plaza" is kept: low colour, low commerce, low volume, holding only two objects — an "Answer Book" (turnable metal pages, each engraving one year's summary of the city's answer sheet) and the "Kilometre-Zero Post of the Rail-Spike Band" (marker K0+000, with spike No. 0 embedded at its base, engraved with the event itself: "1909 — the Jing-Zhang Railway completed and opened") Spatial data. The campus interface follows low-disturbance stitching: walls and ownership boundaries stay untouched, permeability is achieved through Answer Gates and improved walking interfaces. Every year on 25 March, only a public-interest commemorative open day is held — no corporate naming, no commercial showcase, no on-site spike installation.

Zhongzhiyuan AI Independent Innovation Acceleration Area = the Ren-Shaped Leap (northern Leap segment). The "Ren-Character Footbridge" across the Fifth Ring Road is a conceptual node responding directly to the official wording of "a landmark node spanning the ring road", and contains no engineering conclusions on alignment, spans, or structure: seen from above the pedestrian bridge reads as the character "人" (ren, "human"), its two strokes rising gently from green space on both sides of the ring road and closing above it, its underside linking the Qinghe blue-green space so that the main-axis narrative and the Qinghe ecology form a cross here Spatial data. The parapet display screens show only a few core figures updated on the statistical publication cycle (never real-time). The "人" pays tribute to the herringbone railway at Qinglongqiao — in 1909 Zhan Tianyou used the ren-shaped switchback to climb the Guangou pass, and today the ren-shaped bridge crosses the ring-road barrier: from "climbing the mountain" to "crossing the ring", the same character, the same engineering wisdom of answering a hard question with leverage. Zhongzhiyuan also serves as the "home ground of testing" for robot testing and model-demo integration testing (see the scenarios chapter).

Land Use, Building Scale, and Retain-Renovate-Demolish Strategy

The land-use plan is expressed under the public national territorial land-use classification standard Standard: ten conceptual block zones fully cover the submitted boundary with seamless topology (EPSG:4548 recalculation shows a coverage gap of 0.398 square metres and a maximum overlap of 0.087 square metres); the park green axis (1401) runs through north–south, and the two wings hold culture (0803), commerce (0901), research (0802), residence (0701), education (0804), business (0902), and reserved (16) land, with every code drawn from the site-package enumerations Spatial data. The zoning is a conceptual recommendation and does not replace statutory regulatory detailed planning.

The building scheme is deliberately lightweight: only three small conceptual footprints are proposed — the Answer Bell pavilion (inside the Bell Chime Plaza), the Urban Evolution Archive installation (beside the Rail-Spike Band), and a demonstration Factor Service Station (in low-efficiency space on the east wing) — totalling about 3,125 square metres of building footprint Spatial data Metric, none carrying height conclusions. The proposal's spatial claim never rested on added building volume, but on activating existing public space with minimal physical intervention (spikes, gates, posts, the bell, furniture sets) — this is both a design judgement and an honest response to the absence of heritage, ownership, and regulatory-planning conditions Depth.

The retain–renovate–demolish strategy gives method only, not conclusions Depth: absent data on existing buildings, ownership, regulatory planning, and engineering conditions, this proposal classifies no specific building as demolish, renovate, or retain, and instead offers three classification principles for professional deepening — heritage and historical buildings (such as the old Tsinghuayuan station building) are absolutely retained with the fabric untouched, and any sited activity awaits consultation with heritage authorities; renewable industrial space along the line prioritises "renovate" and functional substitution, linking to district-level stock-renewal policy research; and micro-renewal is studied only for low-efficiency leftover spaces whose ownership has been clarified (the direction for Factor Service Station siting). Total building scale, floor area ratio, building coverage ratio, and similar indicators lack official conditions, uniformly remain status=unknown with their recalculation preconditions stated Metric, and no fixed values are used to manufacture false precision.

Transport, Rail, Municipal Infrastructure, and Public Services

The walking and cycling network is the main body of this proposal's transport answer Depth: the 9-kilometre Answer Scroll walking main line is a continuous conceptual line across the full length (about 9.7 kilometres) Spatial data, and three east–west Answer Gate stitching demonstration lines serve the Gate of the Ringing Bell, the Answer Origin, and the Ren-Shaped Leap segments respectively Spatial data; all are tagged conceptual and represent no road right-of-way lines. Rail integration focuses on stitching the four quadrants of Dazhongsi Station: pedestrian flows are reorganised around the Bell Chime Plaza as the centre, with four sound-wave paths radiating into the four quadrants, letting transfer flows, community flows, and campus flows converge on the plaza before dispersing — the reason for stitching is not "a road was built" but "the plaza is worth the detour" (conceptual recommendation; traffic organisation awaits professional deepening).

Breakpoint connection follows "connect first, then linger": for each breakpoint listed in the official transport-system optimisation solution, a conceptual connectivity-optimisation strategy is given first, then the Breakpoint Living Room scenario is overlaid; the Ren-Character Footbridge answers the official wording as a conceptual node spanning the ring road, with engineering feasibility left to a dedicated study. The low-speed delivery-robot testing corridor is a conceptual line Spatial data; the proposal suggests first delimiting a test loop and time windows within the opened Phase I 16.8-hectare segment (physically separated, safety officers accompanying throughout, instant-stop and withdrawal at any time), and only after validation studying extension toward the two wings' communities level by level with regulatory filing, following the graded rhythm of one-north. Robot ramp = accessibility ramp: one retrofit at the 46 entrances serves two uses (subject to actual completion) — low-speed delivery robots and wheelchairs share the ramps, turning compliance cost into an innovation selling point; "wheelchair first, robots must stop and yield" is written into the hard conditions for passing tests, and accessibility indicators carry a one-vote veto.

The full line shares one standardised kit of ten components: the standard bronze spike, the Answer Gate set, the dual-use ramp, the Breakpoint Living Room furniture set, the Factor Service Station module, the sound-ring bench, the evolution archive column, the youth curation frame, the digital scoreboard screen (updated on the statistical publication cycle, no real-time, no advertising), and the guide answering post — sharing the spike-bronze colour, the sound-wave-ring geometry, and the examination-paper layout as one vocabulary, so that the 9 kilometres read "recognisable at a glance, never the same twice".

The honours display system follows "spikes instead of steles": it inherits the core of China's stele-inscription tradition (engrave verifiable facts for later generations to check) but flips the form — no tall stele, no celebrity wall; honour is made into "the smallest possible monument" laid flat on the ground, not for looking up at, but for crouching down to count. Five tiers of carriers: the model-tier bronze spike (engraving registration event + date), the milestone-tier one ring of the Answer Bell (sound archived), the annual-tier one page of the Answer Book (summary of the three auditable lists), the community-tier gate-adoption plaque, and the youth-tier signed curation plaque. Three iron rules run through: engrave facts only, never rankings; record events only, never endorse entities; decouple from the entity's continued existence — if an enterprise exits, its spike is not pulled, for the spike records the historical fact that "in a given month of a given year a given model completed registration" — and that is the exit logic.

The cultural narrative braids the centennial Jing-Zhang culture, Zhongguancun culture, and the new AI culture into one timeline: the 1909 opening is the question sheet and the first answer; the 1910 station building is the answer sheet's physical receipt Source; the 2019 opening of the Jing-Zhang high-speed railway and the retirement of the old line is the old paper handed in and a new sheet spread open; the 2023 opening of Phase I is the first page of the answer sheet unfolded Source; the 2024 start of Phase II is the sheet unfolding further Source; today the Rail-Spike Band sets down the first line of the new answer. Three narrative claims: Jing-Zhang culture is not decorative background for AI, nor is AI a modern footnote to Jing-Zhang — they are two answers to the same question; Zhongguancun's "dare to be first, tolerate failure" is the methodological bridge from question to answer, and the Urban Evolution Archive publicly archives the proposal's iteration history and public feedback, making tolerance of failure a visitable loop; honour is granted only to verifiable facts — the deepest respect for the engineering culture of Zhan Tianyou. For urban character, the proposal suggests a "Jing-Zhang tricolour" conceptual palette (spike bronze, bronze-patina green, station-brick grey) and a mileage-vocabulary wayfinding system (K0+000-style markers), bilingual throughout and oriented toward open-licence typefaces; character coordination follows the urban design administrative measures Standard, and the character around heritage sites defers to heritage-authority determination.

Risk, Copyright, and Compliance

Boundary of use for the revolutionary narrative (a dedicated iron rule of this proposal). The historical fact of the 25 March 1949 "march to the examination" arrival in Beijing is presented only at Tsinghuayuan Railway Station in a public-interest commemorative manner Source: 25 March each year is a public-interest commemorative open day with no corporate naming, no commercial showcase, and no on-site spike installation; commercial showcases, bell-ringing ceremonies, and spike-installation ceremonies all move to the Dazhongsi and Zhongzhiyuan segments and are never phrased in parallel with the revolutionary narrative; "Examination Season" as an event-calendar term points only to the public meaning of "accepting examination, handing in an answer sheet", and its activities never enter Tsinghuayuan Station; related interpretive content goes live only after review by cultural-history professionals, and only the approved version is played during open days.

Data-calibre discipline. The body text uniformly uses the district-government official calibre of 122 registered large models as of February 2026 Source; the Beijing Daily calibre of 128 reported in mid-February 2026 is a statistical-timing difference, footnoted honestly in parallel Source; a later, higher calibre also circulates but has not been verified verbatim in this package's registered sources, so this proposal does not cite it. Pre-emptive disclosure of data dynamism: the registration count grows month by month, and any "spike No. N" statement follows the government calibre at the time of installation.

Honest disclosure of symbol collisions. Full-library screening results: 29 hits for the rail-spike image, 265 for the "人" symbol, 26 for "march to the examination", 26 for "bell ringing" — these symbols are public heritage vocabulary and this proposal claims no exclusivity; its differentiation claim is limited to the mechanism layer (the Rail-Spike Band's KPI-bound living mechanism, the Examination Day, the Answer Bell, the Answer Gates, the Evolution Archive, the curation rights — each scoring only 0–1 hits in the full library). This proposal uses no scarcity figure that full-library screening does not support.

Heritage and engineering risk. The heritage-protection GIS extent of Tsinghuayuan Station is missing, so every forecourt placement is a conceptual siting pending consultation with heritage authorities; the Answer Bell is a newly cast contemporary bell, strictly separated from the Dazhongsi heritage bells Source; the Ren-Character Footbridge contains no engineering conclusions; robot testing is conditional on administrative permits. Data-governance risk absorbs the Sidewalk Toronto lesson: every digital touchpoint defaults to minimal collection, anonymisation, and published inventories, adds no surveillance-type collection, and data rules are suggested to be government-led, published, and open to feedback Depth.

Boundary and recalculation risk. The submitted boundary and the three key areas are provisional constraints Spatial data (the constraints layer is deliberately kept an empty collection with a declared data gap — better empty than fabricated); missing items on official redlines, regulatory planning, ownership, municipal systems, and heritage conditions have entered the assumptions register and the missing-data checklist, and all conclusions are constrained by them. Bilingual and copyright. This proposal fulfils the bilingual contract (proposal.en.md as the complete counterpart translation); the licence status of all images, drawings, and data assets is stated in the source registry and the copyright statement; the HTML loads no remote resources and tracks no reviewers.
