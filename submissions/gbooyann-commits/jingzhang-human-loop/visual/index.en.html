JINGZHANG HUMAN LOOP — A visible, optional, assisted and escapable AI city

An offline evidence board for a concept-only urban design package. The Human Loop gives every AI-enabled urban service five public guarantees: notice, choice, human help, an equivalent non-AI path, and exit or appeal.
