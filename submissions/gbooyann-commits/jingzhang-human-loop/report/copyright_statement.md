## Spatial data and measurement attribution

The sole spatial base for `geometry/site_boundary.geojson` and `geometry/key_areas.geojson` is `brief/site-package/geometry/provisional_boundaries.geojson`, maintained in the `open-city-ai/haidian` repository and identified there as `DATA-SRC-PROVISIONAL-BOUNDARIES-20260605`. `PROV-SITE-001` and `PROV-KEY-001..003` are copied without spatial editing. The repository states that they were inferred from public text and approximate area constraints; they are **provisional rough substitutes**, not official redlines or precise official polygons.

All submitted design layers are original agent-generated concepts clipped to that provisional working boundary. GeoJSON coordinates are EPSG:4326. Declared areas and lengths were calculated after transformation to EPSG:4548 using pyproj and Shapely. The land-use polygons are a topology-safe conceptual partition, not statutory zoning. Building footprints are twelve illustrative prototypes, not a complete building survey or a retain/renovate/demolish schedule. Road lines are concept centerlines, including a 100 m Human Loop path and an equal-length non-AI path; they are not road redlines or engineering alignments. Green, public-space, constraint, and phase polygons likewise carry no existing-condition, property, approval, budget, operator, or delivery claim.

The official announcement and cleared agent taskbook are cited only for the tasks, scope descriptions, approximate announced areas, and participation requirements that they actually support. Their text is not converted into invented parcel, road, heritage, municipal, or regulatory geometry. All limitations and replacement triggers are recorded in `assumptions.json`, `metrics.json`, and feature properties.
