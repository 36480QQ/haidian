# 京张有人 / JINGZHANG HUMAN LOOP

## 中文摘要

“京张有人”把 AI 创新带理解为一项公共服务契约，而不仅是产业空间品牌。方案提出“看见、选择、求助、等效路径、退出申诉”五步 Human Loop：任何进入公共空间的 AI 服务都应说明身份、用途和责任主体；使用者能够在不受惩罚的情况下拒绝；需要时能够连接真人；非 AI 与无障碍路径保持等效；失败、误判或不愿继续时可以撤回并申诉。

空间结构概括为“一脊、三庭、双路、十二场景”。一条有人服务脊串联众智园、北京 AI 原点社区和大钟寺三座“有人庭”；红色有人服务路径与青色等效非 AI / 无障碍路径并行。首发动作收紧为一个约 100 米的人工服务回路，用真实服务时长、通行、导视、值守、隐私和故障降级测试决定是否扩展，而不是以渲染图替代验证。

当前提交以面积约 11.41M m² 的临时粗略总体范围作为概念工作底图。边界不是官方红线，所有用地、长度、面积、建筑与更新动作均为待专业团队深化的参考方案。正式边界、控规指标、现状建筑与权属、铁路遗产控制、交通、市政、生态风险、服务需求、无障碍以及运营与法务等九类依赖均保持开放；六项部署门槛当前为 0 / 6 就绪。图件支持内容评审和公开讨论，不支持审批、工程或部署结论。

## English Summary

JINGZHANG HUMAN LOOP treats an AI innovation belt as a public-service contract, not merely an industry-space brand. Its five moves are Notice, Choice, Human Help, an Equivalent Non-AI Path, and Exit or Appeal. Any AI-enabled public service should disclose its identity, purpose and accountable operator; refusal must carry no penalty; a person must remain reachable; accessible non-AI service must remain equivalent; and users must be able to withdraw, challenge a result and receive a traceable response.

The spatial idea is one spine, three Human Courts, two equal paths and twelve situated scenarios. A staffed red route connects Zhongzhiyuan, Beijing AI Origin Community and Dazhongsi, while a teal non-AI and accessible path remains equally legible. The first action is deliberately narrow: a conceptual 100 m staffed-service loop whose response time, access, wayfinding, staffing, privacy and fail-safe operation must be tested on the real site before any corridor-scale expansion.

This package uses a provisional rough design envelope of approximately 11.41M m² as a concept base. It is not an official boundary. Land use, dimensions, buildings and renewal actions are reference proposals for professional deepening. Nine dependency groups remain open, and current deployment readiness is 0 of 6 gates. The figures support content review and open discussion; they do not support statutory approval, engineering or deployment claims.
