Overview · The Five-Speed Master Plan

The five layers

L1 River Clock · Site & hydrology · 500+ years

The Qinghe, the Xiaoyue River, waterfront buffers. Instrument: the blue-green protection line. Procedure: not amendable.

L2 Line Clock · Alignments & streets · 100 years

The Jing-Zhang alignment and trunk rights of way; 3+1 reserved corridors. Instrument: the alignment-protection plan. Procedure: the 25-year Repave Assembly.

L3 Block Clock · Block grain · 50 years

Block and tenure grain, easements. Instrument: the grain guideline + easement clause. Procedure: public hearing.

L4 Section Clock · Sections & supports · 15–30 years

Elastic sections, the 0.8–1.5 m device band, conduit headroom, SI separation. Instrument: the section atlas. Procedure: planning permit.

L5 Device Clock · Devices & AI · 0.5–5 years

All AI devices and interfaces. Instrument: the five-element lease + ledger plates + deposit pool. Procedure: filing; default exit on expiry.

Key Areas

Land Use (Conceptual Mosaic)

Mobility and Reserved Corridors

"The robots need it" is never a reason to modify the street network.

Blue-Green and Public Space

The L1 River Clock is not amendable: the Qinghe and Xiaoyue River are the hydrological constant; corridor + blue-green interface = 21.6% green; the continuous walk + Five Clocks Plaza + device bands = 4.0% public space.

Buildings (Demonstrative Conceptual Footprints)

Nine conceptual footprints, ~53,000 m²: the Section Tower, the Lease Service Station, three youth apartments, the component workshop, two test-field buildings, the trade hall. Stage props of the institution, not a build-out; new buildings follow SI support/infill separation.

Renewal Projects R-01…R-10

Phase 1 (0–1 y) is all zero-construction: plans, census, lease system, curation, baseline archive; Phase 2 the demo street and paving; Phase 3 corridor and grain pilots as opportunity allows; Phase 4 the Assembly cycle. Every project carries an exit condition; chains ≤ 2.
