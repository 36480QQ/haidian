# Formal Narrative

`京张接单 / JINGZHANG TAKES THE CALL` treats the Centennial Jing-Zhang AI Innovation Belt as a public intake and delivery system rather than another city-brain platform. The shared scene order turns a need into a qualified task with a problem owner, assigned owner, first response, non-AI baseline, human gate, acceptance test, exit condition and reasoned closure.

The provisional spatial structure uses one public intake spine, six east-west service seams, three stations and two wings. AI Origin frames problems, Zhongzhiyuan performs bounded validation, and Dazhongsi records adoption, referral, rejection or retirement. Xiaoyue River supplies lived problems; Zhongguancun supplies legal, IP, standards, finance and procurement support.

All spatial geometry remains conceptual and low-confidence. The package does not claim an official redline, parcel, control plan, building survey, road alignment, approval, budget or operating result. Twelve scenes are marked `not_run`, operational KPIs remain `unknown`, and official geometry must trigger a complete regeneration, recalculation, render and review cycle.

The structured evidence lives in the nine GeoJSON layers, `metrics.json`, `assumptions.json`, `sources.json`, three review matrices, bilingual HTML, bilingual figures and four bilingual drawing PDFs. The Chinese proposal remains the primary narrative; the English file is its complete companion.
