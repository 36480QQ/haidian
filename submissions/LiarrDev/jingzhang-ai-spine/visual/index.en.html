Spatial Structure

The proposal establishes a spatial structure of "one spine, three vertebrae, two wings, and a blue-green slow-mobility composite ring," binding industry judgment, urban renewal, and key-district design to layers and metrics.

Core Structure Concepts

One Spine
The Jingzhang Heritage Park serves as the north-south public space axis and cultural spine
Three Vertebrae
Zhongzhiyuan (Source Vertebra), AI Origin Community (Pulse Vertebra), and Dazhongsi (Convergence Vertebra) distributed north to south along the spine
Two Wings
Zhongguancun tech-service wing (west) + Xiaoyue River scenario-enablement wing (east)
Blue-Green Slow-Mobility Composite Ring
With the Heritage Park as the north-south axis and the Qing River (north) and Xiaoyue River (east) as lateral corridors, forming a ring-shaped slow-mobility system

Land Use Zoning

Research Land
Core areas of Zhongzhiyuan and AI Origin Community, carrying full-stack AI indigenous innovation and achievement translation
Commercial & Service Land
Dazhongsi district and around rail stations, carrying industry convergence and international exchange
Residential Land
Primarily renewal of existing communities, supplemented with youth talent apartments and international community service facilities
Green & Plaza Land
Heritage Park and blue-green corridors, forming an "I-shaped" blue-green skeleton

Land-use layout follows the National Territorial Space Land-Use Classification Guide [standard:MNR-LAND-USE-CLASSIFICATION-GUIDE], covering road and transport facility land (including rail station integration) [data:geometry/land_use.geojson#LU-001].
