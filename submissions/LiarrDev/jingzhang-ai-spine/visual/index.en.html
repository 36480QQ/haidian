Spatial Structure

The proposal presents a spatial structure of "One Spine, Three Vertebrae, Two Wings Unfolded, Blue-Green Slow-Mobility Loop," binding industry analysis, urban renewal, and key area design to layers and metrics.

"One Spine, Three Vertebrae" Brand Mark

The vertical spine connects three innovation nodes: Source Vertebra (Zhongzhiyuan), Pulse Vertebra (AI Origin Community), Convergence Vertebra (Dazhongsi)

Core Structure Concept

One Spine
Jingzhang Railway Heritage Park as the north-south public space axis and cultural spine
Three Vertebrae
Zhongzhiyuan (Source Vertebra), AI Origin Community (Pulse Vertebra), Dazhongsi (Convergence Vertebra) distributed north to south along the spine
Two Wings
Zhongguancun Tech Service Wing (West) + Xiaoyuehe Scenario Empowerment Wing (East)
Blue-Green Slow-Mobility Loop
Heritage Park as the north-south main axis, Qinghe River (North) and Xiaoyuehe River (East) as lateral corridors, forming a loop slow-mobility system
