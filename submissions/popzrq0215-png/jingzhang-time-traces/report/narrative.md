# 正式叙述 / Formal Narrative

## 京张时轨：空间弹性接口驱动的四维弹性城市走廊

1909 年，詹天佑以"人"字形折返线破局关沟陡坡——那是中国铁路史上第一个"物理空间弹性接口"。1949 年，这根铁轨承载"进京赶考"的历史转折。2026 年，高铁全面入地，9 公里地表轨痕演变为纵贯众智园、北京 AI 原点社区与大钟寺三大核心集群的创新轴线。

本方案将视野拉伸至 1909—2100 的两百年时空坐标系，以"空间弹性接口"为核心技术哲学：三维分层（空中蜂窝航道 / 地表数字绿洲 / 地下算力管廊）+ 一轴三核梯度演进 + 4D 流体立体交通 + AOD（人工智能导向开发）模式，并以 30% 绝对非智能区作为对抗科技异化的伦理红线，把曾经割裂城市的物理疤痕转化为"超级城市智脊"与未来"AI 数字绿洲"。

叙述性内容以 `proposal.md`（中文权威版）与 `proposal.en.md`（英文全译）承载；空间主张以 9 套 GeoJSON 图层落图；数量主张以 `metrics.json` 复算链承载；远期主张（+300% 效率、≥65% 绿化、智慧矩阵、磁悬浮物流等）全部登记为 `assumptions.json` 中的情景目标假设，不作为当前量化承诺。图册（`drawings/a3-booklet*.pdf`）、展板（`drawings/a0-boards*.pdf`）与离线电子展示（`visual/index*.html`）互为印证，全部内容可由 `self_check.json` 与三张矩阵交叉校验。

## English summary

In 1909, Zhan Tianyou's switchback at Qinggualing was the first "physical spatial elastic interface" in Chinese railway history; in 2026 the high-speed rail moves underground and the 9 km surface trace becomes an innovation axis linking three core clusters. This scheme stretches the horizon across the 1909–2100 two-century coordinate system: three vertical layers, one axis with three cores evolving in gradients, 4D fluid multi-level transport, and the AOD (AI-Oriented Development) model, with a 30% absolutely non-smart zone as the ethical red line — transforming a physical scar into a super urban smart spine and the future AI Digital Oasis.

Narrative claims live in `proposal.md` / `proposal.en.md`; spatial claims in nine GeoJSON layers; quantitative claims in the `metrics.json` recalculation chain; all long-range claims are registered as scenario-target assumptions in `assumptions.json`, never as current commitments. The A3 booklet, A0 boards, and the offline visual exhibit cross-verify each other, and everything is checkable via `self_check.json` and the three matrices.
