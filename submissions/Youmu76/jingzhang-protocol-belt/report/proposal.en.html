JINGZHANG PROTOCOL BELT

Write AI urban design as an auditable public protocol. In 1909, Zhan Tianyou solved the Qinglongqiao grade problem with a chevron (人-shaped) alignment — going sideways for a stretch in order to climb higher overall. That was not a detour; it was topology — an engineer's protocol-like response to a geographic constraint. Today this corridor is to become an AI innovation belt. This proposal's judgment: the scarcest resource is not compute, not land quota, not industrial policy — it is a protocol between AI and the city: a public rulebook that makes every AI deployment auditable, rollback-able, and accountable. The Jing-Zhang Railway was China's first trunk line built to indigenous standards; the Jing-Zhang Protocol Belt should become the first city-scale AI public protocol laboratory.

All spatial, activity, policy and phasing content in this proposal is open co-creation concept material — reference schemes for professional teams to deepen, not a substitute for statutory planning, not an approved government conclusion, and not any parcel-level demolition/renovation, road redline, rail alignment or engineering decision 来源. 3.2 Core proposition: how the belt constrains AI

Mainstream proposals ask "what can AI bring to this belt". This proposal inverts the question — "how does this belt constrain AI" 深度.

The Jing-Zhang Railway's historical significance is not merely "building a line"; it is "establishing an indigenous standard" — China's first trunk line with its own gauge, its own charter, its own engineering method. Read into the AI era, the belt's real legacy should not be "another park" but a city-scale AI public protocol — auditable, rollback-able, accountable public rules for every AI deployment.

The Jing-Zhang Protocol — five core clauses (the institutional twin of the spatial design) 来源:

1. Data minimization: collect only what the task requires; nothing more is retained; 2. Rollback: any AI scenario can be paused, reverted, dismantled under public oversight; 3. Human review: AI outputs affecting public decisions must pass human review nodes; 4. Benefit sharing: public value created by AI scenarios flows back to public space and communities; 5. Full traceability: every datum, decision and change is auditable.
