# JINGZHANG PROTOCOL BELT

> **Write AI urban design as an auditable public protocol.**
> In 1909, Zhan Tianyou solved the Qinglongqiao grade problem with a chevron (人-shaped) alignment — going sideways for a stretch in order to climb higher overall. That was not a detour; it was topology — an engineer's *protocol-like* response to a geographic constraint. Today this corridor is to become an AI innovation belt. This proposal's judgment: **the scarcest resource is not compute, not land quota, not industrial policy — it is a protocol between AI and the city**: a public rulebook that makes every AI deployment auditable, rollback-able, and accountable. The Jing-Zhang Railway was China's first trunk line built to indigenous standards; the Jing-Zhang Protocol Belt should become the first city-scale AI public protocol laboratory.

All spatial, activity, policy and phasing content in this proposal is **open co-creation concept material — reference schemes for professional teams to deepen**, not a substitute for statutory planning, not an approved government conclusion, and not any parcel-level demolition/renovation, road redline, rail alignment or engineering decision [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK]. All geometry is generated on the repository's provisional rough boundary with `official_boundary=false`; the whole package must be recalculated when official polygons are released [source:BOUNDARY-SOURCE].

## 3. Coordinated Research Area: Overall Concept and Naming

### 3.1 Overall concept: Jing-Zhang Protocol Belt

**Chinese name**: 京张协议带 (Xieyi Dai, "Protocol Belt")
**English name**: JingZhang Protocol Belt (JPB)
**Logo direction**: Zhan Tianyou's chevron (人) as meta-symbol — two switchback strokes forming an abstract "人", ringed by a protocol loop (circular arrow) meaning "switchback = rollback-able" and "protocol = auditable". Palette: "Zhan Tianyou Bronze" (#B87333) for railway memory, "Protocol Blue" (#1A5276) for trusted governance, "Jing-Zhang Green" (#27AE60) for blue-green public space [depth:overall_concept_and_naming].

**Naming system**: three beacons named by protocol tier — Zhongzhiyuan = TRUST BEACON, Origin = OPEN BEACON, Dazhongsi = COEXIST BEACON; wings = Zhongguancun Technology-Service Wing / Xiaoyuehe Scenario-Empowerment Wing; twelve scenario nodes numbered P-01…P-12 [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK].

### 3.2 Core proposition: how the belt constrains AI

Mainstream proposals ask "what can AI bring to this belt". This proposal inverts the question — **"how does this belt constrain AI"** [depth:overall_concept_and_naming].

The Jing-Zhang Railway's historical significance is not merely "building a line"; it is "establishing an indigenous standard" — China's first trunk line with its own gauge, its own charter, its own engineering method. Read into the AI era, the belt's real legacy should not be "another park" but **a city-scale AI public protocol** — auditable, rollback-able, accountable public rules for every AI deployment.

**The Jing-Zhang Protocol — five core clauses** (the institutional twin of the spatial design) [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK]:

1. **Data minimization**: collect only what the task requires; nothing more is retained;
2. **Rollback**: any AI scenario can be paused, reverted, dismantled under public oversight;
3. **Human review**: AI outputs affecting public decisions must pass human review nodes;
4. **Benefit sharing**: public value created by AI scenarios flows back to public space and communities;
5. **Full traceability**: every datum, decision and change is auditable.

### 3.3 Three positionings and five functions

**Three positionings** [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK]: Centennial Jing-Zhang Culture Belt (green protocol ridge with chevron nodes, stitch crossings and protocol steles) [data:geometry/constraints.geojson#CONST-RAIL-HERITAGE]; Urban AI Life Experience Belt (twelve protocol scenario nodes) [data:geometry/public_space.geojson]; AI Convergence Innovation Belt (three beacons + two wings loop) [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson].

**Five functions loop**: AI full-stack indigenous innovation → Zhongzhiyuan (switchback gates control pace); world-class AI ecosystem → Origin; AI+ scenario empowerment → Xiaoyuehe wing; intelligent vibrant city → Dazhongsi; global AI governance voice → three beacons jointly (protocol publication, ethics review, global developer dialogue).

### 3.4 Global AI ecosystem cases (Agent.2)

## 4. Overall Design Area: Urban Renewal and Regulatory-Depth Concept

### 4.1 The chevron as spatial and institutional language

The essence of the 人-shape is not "turning" but **"switchback"** — trading time for feasibility through deliberate lateral movement [depth:overall_concept_and_naming]. Spatially: five east-west **stitch crossings** are five switchback arms reconnecting the east and west halves that the railway split for a century [data:geometry/roads.geojson#RD-STITCH-01]. Institutionally: **switchback governance** — the stronger AI becomes, the more the city needs rollback-able deceleration strips; every deployment passes a switchback checkpoint: data compliance → public notice → rollback plan → benefit-sharing arrangement.
