# Formal Narrative / 正式叙事摘要

The Jing-Zhang Civic Loop treats the heritage park as an existing civic spine and assigns three distinct, reversible trial contracts: T1 AI Origin Community for accessible civic service, T2 Zhongzhiyuan for isolated edge-model and recovery tests, and T3 Dazhongsi for permission, withdrawal and audit display. Each contract begins with an ordinary non-AI path and adds AI only behind an entry gate, staffed takeover and explicit stop/exit conditions.

Version 0.1.4 develops the Dazhongsi Reversible Civic Interface as the flagship depth prototype. Four nominal review scales connect city structure, key-area routes, an eleven-component reversible operating cell and a typical section. OPEN, PILOT, PAUSE and RESTORE states keep the ordinary public route available while separating bounded AI, human stop authority, appeal, withdrawal, removal and restoration. The scales are concept review devices, not official geometry or engineering drawings.

京张共生回路把遗址公园视为既有公共脊，并为三处重点区配置不同且可撤回的试点契约：T1 AI 原点社区验证无障碍公共服务，T2 众智园验证隔离式边缘模型与恢复，T3 大钟寺展示权限、撤回与审计。每项均先运行普通非 AI 路径，只有通过准入门后才增加 AI，并配置人工接管和明确的停止/退出条件。

v0.1.4 将“大钟寺可逆公共接口”推进为旗舰深度样板：四个名义审阅尺度贯通城市结构、重点区流线、11 组件可逆运行单元与典型断面；OPEN、PILOT、PAUSE、RESTORE 四态均保持普通公共路线开放，并分离受控 AI、人工停止、申诉、撤回、撤展与恢复。比例尺只服务概念审阅，不代表官方几何或工程图。

Official and registered facts, provisional geometry, design responses and pending statutory data remain separate. The contextual OpenStreetMap extract is attributed under ODbL 1.0 and is used only for relative roads, water and place context; it does not replace official boundaries, parcels, control lines or approvals. Geometry, metrics, compliance matrices, bilingual proposals, drawings and offline visual pages remain cross-checked deliverables. The heritage and regulatory constraint GeoJSON intentionally remains empty until official georeferenced polygons are supplied; this is a safety decision, not an omission to be filled by inference.

Responsibility and delivery are now explicit: five RACI workstreams, seven pending permit dependencies, three comparison modes with a declared denominator, eight risk dimensions, regional evidence-exchange limits and four handoff packages are recorded in machine-readable files. The proposed sample threshold is not an achieved result or official standard, and no existing organisation is presented as an assigned owner or confirmed partner.
