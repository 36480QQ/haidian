# Formal Narrative and Bilingual QA Record / 正式叙事与双语质检记录

## Current convergence / 当前收敛

Version 0.1.7 keeps the Jing-Zhang heritage corridor as the coordinated urban-design frame while selecting one small-site prototype on evidence, not visual preference. AI Origin Community, a Jing-Zhang Railway Heritage Park entrance and Qinghe Station were compared against ordinary-task clarity, independent non-AI completion, surface-underground evidence, unknowns and reversibility. Qinghe's public interface outside fare/security control is preferred because registered public records can support one bounded task: a person without a smartphone obtains human guidance for a step-free Line 13 transfer. This is a P1/P2 review package, not an implementation siting decision.

v0.1.7 保留京张遗址廊道作为统筹城市设计框架，并以证据而非图面偏好选择一个小场地原型。北京 AI 原点社区、京张铁路遗址公园入口与清河站按普通任务清晰度、完整非 AI 路径、地上—地下证据、未知项和可逆性比较。首选清河站安检/闸机外公共接口，是因为登记公开资料可支持一个受限任务：不用智能手机的人取得 13 号线无台阶换乘的人工说明。这是 P1/P2 审阅包，不是实施选址结论。

The prototype begins with a fixed non-electronic overview, paper route card and human handoff. Optional AI may translate, read aloud or prompt only; it may not confirm lift status, invent an unknown route or make an account, scan or phone mandatory. Evidence supports functional relationships among surface walking, underpasses, B1 services and transfers, but the exact entrance, segment route, dimensions, level change, operating state, public boundary and staffed point remain unknown. Every Qinghe drawing is NTS, non-survey and non-implementation.

原型先以固定非电子总览、纸质路径卡和人工接力闭合普通服务。可选 AI 只可翻译、朗读或提示，不得确认电梯状态、补写未知路线，或强制账户、扫码和手机。证据只支持地面慢行、地下通道、B1 服务与换乘联系等功能关系；准确入口、逐段路线、尺寸、高差、开放状态、公共边界和人工点位仍未知。所有清河图件均为 NTS、非测绘、非实施。

## Review-response systems / 审查补强系统

The belt-wide library now separates four identities and operating contracts: B01 civic-service baseline, B02 cultural wayfinding, B03 activity interface and B04 honours display. Each records entry conditions, rights clearance, an ordinary-service baseline, optional AI increment, future maintenance role and removal/retirement condition. The belt mark, cultural wayfinding, activity brand and honours display cannot borrow one another's authority. The bilingual geometric identity sample is original to this proposal; it is not a registered trademark, approved sign or existing railway mark.

全带组件库明确区分 B01 公共服务基线、B02 文化导视、B03 活动接口和 B04 荣誉展示，并逐项写明进入条件、权利清理、普通服务基线、可选 AI 增量、待指定维护责任与撤除/退役条件。全带 Logo、文化导视、活动品牌和荣誉展示不得相互借用权威。双语几何识别样张为本方案原创概念，不是注册商标、获批导视或既有铁路标识。

Developer issue clinics, open scenario calls, bilingual international explanation, talent/company follow-up and regional evidence exchange are specified as pending interfaces with explicit inputs, outputs, rights boundaries, confirmation gates and feedback loops. They are not claims of an existing community, event, partnership, recruitment result or enterprise conversion. Without an assigned receiver, ordinary participation path, rights boundary and review cycle, they remain P0 documents.

开发者问题诊所、场景开放征集、双语国际传播、人才/企业后续接力和区域证据交换被定义为待确认接口，均给出输入、输出、权利边界、确认门与反馈闭环。它们不构成现有社区、活动、合作、人才招募或企业转化业绩。接收角色、普通参与路径、权利边界和复核周期未确认时，只停留在 P0 文档层。

The full-stack ecosystem is now mapped from research/open source, compute/recovery, data/rights, talent/community service, capital/professional service and everyday/international scenarios to conceptual carriers, ordinary baselines and public returns. The Xiaoyue River wing is explicitly a translation-and-return interface for all ten scenario cards, not their single physical location. Reusable R1/G1/M1/L1 templates keep responsibility, entry evidence, post-authority measurement and long-term resources blank until lawful confirmation; they contain no amount, staffing, cost or continuity promise.

全栈生态现已把研究/开源、算力/恢复、数据/权利、人才/社区服务、资本/专业服务和日常/国际场景逐项映射到概念载体、普通基线与公共回报。小月河翼被明确为十张场景卡的翻译与返还接口，而非统一物理落位。R1/G1/M1/L1 可复用模板将责任、准入证据、获授权后测量和长期资源保持为空白待确认，不填造金额、人员、成本或持续运营承诺。
