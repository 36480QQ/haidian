Three People-First Tests

Public-life observation is a gate, not a slogan
Jacobs / diversity

Mixed uses, different time periods, and active ground floors must pass first; no single-enterprise public-space trial without a use/time baseline.

Gehl / public life

Observe walking, staying, sitting, talking, and service use before choosing AI components; proxies are not field observations.

Newman / low-car access

Stations, walking, cycling, and public transport precede a smart-mobility interface; no continuous spine claim without a verified network.

Active edges and public lifeEVIDENCE NEEDED

active_frontage_ratio · public_life_observation_count

Accessible-route continuityEVIDENCE NEEDED

accessible_route_continuity_ratio

Station and frequent-transit accessEVIDENCE NEEDED

station_800m_walk_coverage_ratio · frequent_transit_access_ratio
