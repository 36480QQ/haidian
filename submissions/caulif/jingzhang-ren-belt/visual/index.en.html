07 — Inclusion and public accountability

No service requires a smartphone, payment or continuous location tracking. Paper, phone, audio and staffed access run in parallel.

inclusion-framework.json

Disabled users

Continuous accessible paths, tactile/audio wayfinding, rest points and 48h complaint closure.

Low-income users

Free public space, transparent prices, offline booking and at least 20% public-interest quota.

Care + family

Visible care points, stroller/wheelchair parking, toilets, water and family-friendly hours.

Night workers

Safe lighting, open toilets, quiet routes, night staff and response within 15 minutes.

Digital exclusion

Paper maps, telephone, human desks and appeal paths remain available without QR codes.

Annual fairness audit

Publish access, public-interest quota, complaint response, safety, prices and successful exits by user group.

Participation loop

Quarterly resident forum → open day → responsible owner and deadline → public response → annual audit.
