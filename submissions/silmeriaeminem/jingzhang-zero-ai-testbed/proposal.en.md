# Jingzhang New Era · Creative AI Time-Track — Official Proposal for the Centennial Jing-Zhang AI Innovation Belt Urban Design Open Call

> Nature: a conceptual urban design proposal. All spatial suggestions are open, co-created reference schemes and do not constitute an approved planning conclusion, government approval, implementation commitment, or engineering feasibility conclusion. This proposal was written by an AI agent based on public and cleared materials; formal citation must be verified against the source list and registry.

> **Exclusivity claim (the first judgment of this proposal).** The Jingzhang New Era · Creative AI Time-Track is China's first city-scale testbed that runs autonomous vehicles and robots on the retained original rails along a real railway-heritage park; more fundamentally, it treats **time as the primary planning variable** — a 'Time-Track' framework (timeline × tracks) that lets industry, population, economy, and experience evolve over time instead of freezing into a static blueprint. This is the methodological separation from most static spatial masterplans [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK] [depth:overall_spatial_structure].

- **Jingzhang — the anchor of history and space.** From Zhan Tianyou's Jing-Zhang railway (China's first trunk railway independently surveyed, designed, and built) to the intelligent Jing-Zhang high-speed rail serving the Winter Olympics, "Jingzhang" is not just a geographic concept but a symbol of China's self-reliance and innovation [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK].
- **New Era — a paradigm shift.** It signals breaking convention, positioning the project as the starting point and bellwether of next-generation intelligent development.
- **Creative — the core catalyst.** It stands for human creativity, open association, and a "directorial" macro vision — the subjective force driving the project forward.
- **AI — the core rendering engine.** In today's context, AI acts as a "super-renderer" and agent orchestrator, breaking silos and automating coordination to fuse scattered elements.
- **Time-Track — the structural backbone.** A condensation of "Timeline × Track", borrowing the architecture of professional audiovisual editing to metaphorize multi-dimensional composite development.

**Brand positioning: the Intelligent Director.** The proposal signals that Jingzhang New Era is using AI to direct time and space, weaving a century of historical depth with future technological height into an era-defining masterpiece.

The logo direction upgrades from "herringbone line × code fork" to **"Timeline × Track"**: a multi-track editing timeline as the motif — a horizontal time axis (1909 / 2026 / 2049) crossed by parallel tracks (city collaboration / cultural ecology / AI technology), coupled by the flowing nodes of the AI rendering engine. The palette stays heritage rust-red, AI tech-blue, and Jing-Zhang green.

### The Time-Track framework: timeline × tracks

The proposal borrows the timeline × track architecture of video editing and elevates it into a development blueprint, replacing the conventional "static spatial masterplan" method [depth:overall_spatial_structure].

**Horizontal = Time — the timeline's forward progress (narrative order).** The timeline threads three spatio-temporal stages from a century of railway history, through present AI empowerment, to boundless future possibility: **historical accumulation → present momentum → future imagination**. It gives the project direction, continuous vitality, and unstoppable forward momentum.

**Vertical = Track — the tracks' vertical stacking (space and dimensions).** In editing, video, audio, effects, and subtitles run in parallel on different tracks; here "track" metaphorizes the project's multi-dimensional industry layout — three tracks laid in parallel, non-interfering, yet overlapping and compounding vertically into a high-density, three-dimensional creative ecosystem:

| Track | Content | Corresponding space/mechanism |
|---|---|---|
| Track 3: AI technology / infrastructure base | co-track test line, shared compute, digital twin, permit sandbox | Zhongzhiyuan acceleration area, test-line spine |
| Track 2: cultural heritage / green-ecological industry | century narrative, blue-green ecology, cultural tourism | Jing-Zhang heritage park belt, three landmarks |
| Track 1: city collaboration / developer ecosystem | developer community, employment transition, AI-inclusive services (investment in people) | Origin Community, corridor neighborhoods |

**The final convergence — panoramic intelligent rendering (output).** The horizontal time flow and the vertical track flow, under AI (agent ecosystem / large-model engine), are no longer isolated lines but undergo high-frequency real-time computation and fusion; like the final "mix and pixel render" of audio-video tracks, AI fuses the cross-era, cross-domain "time" and "track" into a single high-fidelity, high-synchronization "Jingzhang epic cut" delivered to the world.

### Four-dimensional dynamics: time as the primary planning variable

![Time-sharing: day and night](assets/figures/04_time_sharing_day_night.jpg)

![Zhongzhiyuan AI accelerator + shared lab](assets/figures/11_zhongzhiyuan_shared_lab.jpg)

The source of separation is a methodological switch: most proposals draw space; this proposal composes space-time — industry, population, economy, and experience all evolve over time rather than freezing into one page.

- **Industry × time = the innovation diffusion clock.** Industry is not a static allocation ("21% research land") but grows along an S-curve: validation (0–2 years, the test line attracts testing demand) → agglomeration (2–5 years, hidden champions set up R&D) → anchoring (5–10 years, anchor headquarters + ecosystem closure). Land-use share is a function of time L(x, t).
- **Population × time = tidal rhythm.** Vitality is not a constant but a time function with amplitude: weekday developer tides, morning/evening commute tides, weekend visitor tides. Tidal amplitude becomes a new human-centric vitality indicator.
- **Economy × time = time-mismatch value.** Compute billed by the minute, testing billed per run (unlike conventional annual rents); renewal revenue recycles into public space via time-mismatched financing. The same space has different value at different times.
- **Experience × time = the time-sharing script.** One day of the co-track test line carries different functions: pre-dawn data training / daytime semi-open testing + commuting / night closed testing / weekend public opening. The same rail, time switches the function.

These four dynamics unify into the operating mechanism of the Time-Track, detailed in the Metrics section (a new time-indicator group: time-sharing ratio, growth rate, tidal amplitude, innovation diffusion stage).

### Methodological moat: AI-augmented six-layer mapping

**Making traditional mapping data-driven and machine-verifiable.** Mature international urban-renewal projects do not produce "one masterplan" but a multi-method mapping system — from Lynch's mental maps to Gehl's public-life surveys, from Space Syntax to cultural mapping. This proposal upgrades it into an "AI-augmented six-layer mapping method": traditional mapping relies on human experience and subjective judgment, whereas this proposal data-fies and machine-verifies all six layers, forming a recomputable, self-checkable, iterable research method — with no precedent in Chinese urban-design competitions [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK].

**Convergence of five frontier ideas.** 2024–2026 frontier ideas converge on "people-first, culture as soul, digital as wing, stitching as method"; five best-fit ideas are selected: **city stitching** (Jingzhang's essence is repairing the urban fabric divided by the railway, from "city edge line" to "development axis"), **culture-led regeneration** (old stations/rails/factories into cultural facilities, AI×culture dual IP), **15-minute innovation circle** (mixed R&D + apartments + commerce around stations), **meta-life city** (all-modality spatio-temporal model + agent "city as a service", linking the OPC community and data flywheel), and **computational design** (MOHURD 2025 official discourse, AI-assisted heritage conservation — a compliance basis) [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK].

### Global case benchmarking and survey summary

**(1) No global precedent.** Survey of 50 urban renewal cases (30 domestic + 20 international) across four quadrants (linear heritage activation / innovation cluster / rail TOD / city-center renewal): New York High Line (2.4 km pure public space), Shanghai Yangpu Riverside (15.5 km waterfront), and Paris Rive Gauche (130 ha planar) have no AI-testbed property; Cambridge Kendall, Barcelona 22@, and Shanghai Zhangjiang are planar districts without a linear heritage spine; King's Cross and Hudson Yards are point-station developments; Toronto Quayside is the only AI-city concept but failed and tiny. The Jingzhang combination "linear railway-heritage activation × central-city stock renewal × AI innovation cluster" has no direct precedent domestically or internationally [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK].

**(2) Six key mechanisms (selected benchmark).**

| Case | Core mechanism | Transferable mechanism |
|---|---|---|
| Stanford Research Park (US) | University-enterprise-capital closed loop | "10-minute innovation circle"; open campus boundary |
| King's Cross (UK) | Old railway district renewed as knowledge quarter | Heritage park as interaction spine; renewal revenue feeds public space |
| Punggol Digital District (SG) | Smart buildings + digital twin platform | "One platform, multiple scenarios" test infrastructure |
| Pangyo Techno Valley (KR) | Government-led park + incubation chain | One-stop services embedded in buildings |
| High Tech Campus Eindhoven (NL) | Open innovation model | Shared pilot and compute facilities; developer community |
| Hangzhou Future Sci-Tech City (CN) | Talent policy + scenario opening + VC | Compute subsidy, scenario opening, financing |

**(3) Five transferable lessons.** First, anchor institutions first (Kendall, Mission Bay, King's Cross). Second, public-space ignition (High Line, Yangpu, Rive Gauche). Third, institutional authorization (Barcelona 22@ legislation, Copenhagen Ørestad land-value financing, Hudson Yards air rights). Fourth, cultural narrative (Beijing Longfusi, Shanghai Panlong). Fifth, long-term operation (Tokyo Marunouchi, Yokohama MM21) [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK].

**(4) Cautionary tale: Toronto Quayside.** Sidewalk Labs' AI-city project was terminated in 2020 over data-governance disputes, public-trust deficit, and unclear scale/power boundaries — technology does not equal trust. This confirms the institutional track (machine-verifiable permit sandbox + data sovereignty + public-participation veto) is a precondition, not an optional accessory [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK].

**(5) Existing-condition POI diagnosis.** The spatial baseline comes from 8 Amap POI layers with 1,127 features [data:geometry/land_use.geojson]. Five diagnoses: (i) AI-enterprise POIs number only 4 and internet firms 3 — the AI industry is physically sparse, so the co-track test line is the pre-recruitment infrastructure; (ii) education/research POIs number 173 (75 research institutes + 70 universities + 28 academies) — intellectual density is Jingzhang's hardest asset, underpinning AI4S and the academia-industry loop [data:geometry/education.geojson]; (iii) only 27 parks — the data root of the "green vs parking" dispute [data:geometry/green_space.geojson]; (iv) 50 parking lots + 39 charging stations — strong parking and charging demand directly supporting the smart parking hub (S11) and ecological parking (U6); (v) only 7 heritage sites — heritage scarcity must be compensated by the "century relay" narrative and new AI landmarks [data:geometry/cultural_heritage.geojson].

**(6) Multi-party feedback problem list (design from problems).** The survey covers residents, visitors, enterprises, and government [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK]: the biggest resident tension is the "green-to-parking" dispute (Tianheng Xueyuan-li east side), demanding ecology-first, transparent disclosure, and compromise design, plus mosquito, sandpit-drainage, and shade micro-pain-points; the visitor "check-in economy" has formed (Wudaokou flower sea, 1909 train restaurant) but parking, facilities, and queues are three hard problems; enterprises say "computing is expensive, financing is hard" and "cross-city paperwork" needs to be pushed down to the building; government is open — all 5 public comments adopted, a "super AI testbed", Origin Community as "core of cores". The proposal's spatial and scenario designs (ecological parking, environmental sensing S9, affordable compute U5, policy accompaniment, smart parking S11) respond one-to-one, forming a "survey finds problems → proposal responds" loop.

Three positionings — the Centennial Jing-Zhang Cultural Belt, the Urban AI Life Experience Belt, and the AI Convergence Innovation Belt — with five functions and a "three cores + two wings" structure [source:OFFICIAL-ANNOUNCEMENT]. The synergy loop is "source – validate – apply": cores originate, the test line and governance sandbox validate, and corridor communities apply and feed back.

### Future city form and continuous green space

The proposal proposes "rail as skeleton, green as vein, compute as network" [depth:blue_green_public_space].

### Investment in people (the heart)

The core value judgment: AI and industry must ultimately serve human civilization. The Jingzhang Time-Track Testbed is therefore not "a testbed for AI" but "a testbed for human-machine civilization". The graded test sections carry a dual meaning — technical validation plus social-trust grading: closed sections = machine-only testing; semi-open sections = building human-machine trust; open sections = social-acceptance testing [depth:three_key_area_detailed_design]. Four mechanisms realize this: AI-inclusive services (education, healthcare, eldercare), developer night schools and resident AI-literacy courses, employment transition (traditional roles → AI-collaborative roles), and resident participation rights in the permit sandbox. The human-centric indicator group is recorded in `metrics.json`.

### Spatial structure

On the base of the "one belt, one axis, two centers, multiple points" structure [source:OFFICIAL-ANNOUNCEMENT], the proposal proposes "one rail, one axis, three cores, multiple corridors": the retained rail as the test line, the Chengfu–Zhichun–Xueyuan corridor as the development axis, three key areas as cores, and perpendicular green/slow-mobility corridors [depth:overall_spatial_structure].

### Industry goals and functional layout

Based on existing POI data, the proposal lays out the vertical "base–technology–application" industry chain and the horizontal "data, compute, scenario, capital" service chain [data:geometry/land_use.geojson#LU-001]. The design land-use partition fully covers the boundary without gaps or overlaps; research land (0802), residential land (0701), and park green (1401) form the three main bodies [metric:land_use_area_by_code_sqm].

**First principle: do "intelligence × scenario × policy", not "compute hardware".** Industry-location research shows Jingzhang's comparative advantage is not chip-making or big-factory construction (capital-heavy, locked in by Yizhuang and Lingang) but the combination of intellectual density (30+ universities and research institutes; 135 AI2000 scholars, 34% of China) × scenario density (9 km of real rail + park + business + campus) × policy tools (compute subsidy, token vouchers, scenario opening) — ideal soil for the model, agent, data, embodied, and AI4S layers, forming "R&D in Haidian, mass production in Yizhuang" synergy rather than homogeneous competition [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK].

**Three local industry closed loops (already started, not drawn from scratch)** [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK]: (i) **embodied-intelligence "real-scenario data flywheel"** — real-scenario deployment collects embodied data (Lightwheel Intelligence, "the world's first embodied-data unicorn", is already on the belt) → annotation/synthesis → training → pilot → back to real scenarios; the 9 km linear real scenario is a natural scaled data-collection field and the global differentiator; (ii) **agent economy "OPC one-person-company cluster"** — the OPC order platform (78 ordering enterprises, 1,230 registered users) dispatches orders → one-person companies develop (nearly 180, mostly aged 25–30) → shared workstations + elastic compute → delivery and monetization; no "one-person-company city" form exists nationally, making it a differentiator; (iii) **AI4S "research–transfer–accelerate–industry"** — university basic research → Qingzhiyuan transfer (150 projects, 60 landed) → Zhongzhiyuan AI4S acceleration → AI+medicine/materials/life-science landing.

### Urban renewal framework

Four renewal object types: rail heritage and corridor public space (retain and activate), existing industry buildings (re-functionalize), old residential surroundings (livelihood improvement), and inefficient land (rectification) [depth:retain_renovate_demolish].

### Public space, transport, and municipal support

The park belt is the spine for public space; transport is organized around rail TOD anchors; municipal and new infrastructure are layered [depth:traffic_rail_slow_parking].

### Character control and pending items

Segmented character — low and permeable along the heritage belt, compact and modern in industry areas, coordinated and livable in residential areas [standard:MOHURD-URBAN-DESIGN-MEASURES]; height and density controls await official regulatory conditions [depth:development_intensity_controls].

**6 cooperation models** (who contributes, who gains, what mechanism makes it work): ① **test validation** — firms contribute robots/unmanned vehicles to real 9 km scenarios in exchange for the scarcest real physical-world data; the belt gains "the world's first real-scenario AI data infrastructure" brand premium; ② **compute & data supply** — Moore Threads, Cambricon, Kunlunxin supply domestic compute and Lightwheel supplies the simulation-data engine in exchange for scaled real orders; ③ **scenario opening & orders** — ByteDance's Dazhongsi HQ, the OPC order platform, and Haidian's "ten application demonstration projects" release billable benchmark orders; ④ **industry-academia-research** — Tsinghua/Peking/CAS joint labs feed results directly into co-track validation; ⑤ **investment & incubation** — Zhongguancun Science City Company led investments in Zhipu, Shengshu, ModelBest, LinkerBot, and Lightwheel in 2024–2026, binding "investment + scenario" [source:CHAIN-ENTERPRISES]; ⑥ **data-factor circulation** — Lightwheel's data factory plus the National Blockchain Center's "data stays in domain, usable but not visible".

**Three most persuasive models**: first, "test validation + data-factor circulation" (scenario for data, data for industry) hits the industry's two hardest pain points — scarce real data and hard road permits; second, "investment & incubation" — the state-fund list is already drawn, only "investment" needs binding to "co-track scenario landing"; third, "scenario opening & orders" — ByteDance Dazhongsi, OPC, and the ten application projects are ready-to-run levers that upgrade the 9 km space from a "test field" into a "cash-flow-generating operating field" [source:CHAIN-ENTERPRISES].

## Risk, Copyright, and Compliance

**Source legality.** Only official public documents, public reports, and cleared local data are used [source:OFFICIAL-ANNOUNCEMENT].
