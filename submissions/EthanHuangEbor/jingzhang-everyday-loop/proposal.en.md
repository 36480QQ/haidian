# Jing-Zhang Symbiotic Everyday Loop: An AI Innovation Belt Everyone Can Use, Question and Leave

## Design Basis and Source List

This proposal treats urban design as public infrastructure that people can use every day, not as a form that exists only on review boards. The Jing-Zhang heritage spine can connect history, neighbourhood life, research and transit. The added value of an AI innovation belt should therefore be measured by whether people reach services more easily, understand why an algorithm is present, find human help when it fails, and choose not to use it. This produces a conceptual structure of one spine, three everyday loops and nine service stations. It does not alter statutory boundaries or assume demolition and development intensity; reversible facilities, active ground floors, walking links and public operations create an observable learning system first.

The evidence chain has six layers: sources, assumptions, geometry, metrics, figures and self-checks. Sources are limited to registered public or rights-cleared materials. Assumptions expose missing precise boundaries, ownership, regulatory controls, utilities and operators. Geometry distinguishes provisional_constraint from design_proposal. Metrics state formula, unit, source file and confidence. Figures explain only registered data. Four independent gates cover deterministic validation, spatial review, visual packaging and professional evidence. A reader can move from a design judgement in the narrative to the structured evidence without trusting an unlabelled rendering.

Spatial actions follow public-interest, minimum-intervention and opt-out principles. Every AI service station displays purpose, data category, proposed accountable operator, human channel, stop condition and appeal route. Facial recognition is not a condition of access to public space; individual movement traces are not exchanged for basic navigation; model output is not an administrative or professional decision. Functions involving children, older people, disabled users, night-time activity or cultural narratives begin as small, staffed and reversible tests, and continue only against understandable service measures.
