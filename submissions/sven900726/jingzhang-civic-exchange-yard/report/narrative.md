# 京张交换场叙事摘要

京张交换场把铁路编组场的“识别—编组—转轨—到达”转译为 AI 城区的公共协议。三个交换场分别承担全栈验证、研究转译和城市应用；四类支线连接高校、企业、社区与国际协作；十二个触点用公共交换凭证记录输入、公共回报、责任人、数据边界、开放日历和停止条件。

The Civic Exchange Yard turns railway marshalling—identify, assemble, switch, arrive—into a public protocol for an AI district. Three yards validate stacks, translate research, and test urban applications. Four spur types connect universities, enterprises, neighbourhoods, and global collaborators. Twelve touchpoints use a Public Exchange Ticket to register inputs, public returns, ownership, data limits, opening times, and stop conditions.
