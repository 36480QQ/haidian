# Formal Narrative

Jing-Zhang First Mile reframes the Centennial Jing-Zhang AI Innovation Belt as a governed passage from prototype to adoption. Zhongzhiyuan proves product readiness, Beijing AI Origin Community proves first real use, and Dazhongsi proves operations and reuse. The two wings connect professional services with named real-world needs.

The proposal is controlled by five gates: real need, product ready, first use, first adoption and first reuse. Twelve scenario cards, three industry test settings, three civic landmarks and eight reversible renewal projects make the concept operational. No gate may be replaced by publicity, visitor volume or an unverified claim of procurement.

This narrative is derived from the structured AI package. GeoJSON, metrics, compliance and professional-depth matrices, bilingual drawings, and `visual/index.html` remain the cross-checked authoritative deliverables. Current spatial quantities use a repository-supplied provisional boundary and must be recalculated after official polygons and professional controls are released.
