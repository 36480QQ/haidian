# 成果叙事 / Formal Narrative

“京张开源验真场”把城市 AI 从一次性的展示对象改写为有来路、有边界、有维护者、可暂停、可召回、可人工接管、可公开复盘的城市服务制品。仅在官方几何和公共可达性核验支持时，京张铁路遗址公园才优先承载生命周期公共廊道；若官方资料确认二者不相交，廊道迁入总体设计范围内经核验的公共慢行网络，遗址公园只保留为外部联系与文化叙事。众智园承担可信发布，北京 AI 原点社区承担公共追溯，大钟寺承担召回与服务交换；中关村科技服务翼和小月河场景赋能翼分别提供专业支持与低风险日常共测。

The Jing-Zhang Open Integrity Yard treats urban AI not as a one-off showcase but as a service artifact with provenance, limits, maintainers, suspension and recall paths, human takeover, and safe public learning. The Jing-Zhang Railway Heritage Park is preferred as the public lifecycle corridor only if official geometry and public-access review support that relationship. If official material confirms non-intersection, the corridor moves to a verified public walking network inside the Overall Design Area and the park remains only an external link and cultural narrative. Zhongzhiyuan hosts trusted release, Beijing AI Origin Community hosts public traceability, Dazhongsi hosts recall and service exchange, and the two wings support professional services and low-risk daily-use trials.

空间表达采用“一廊三站两翼一库”，运营流程采用“来源登记—隔离验证—人工评审—合成签名发布—运行观察—协调披露—召回/修复/退役”。十二张服务设计场景卡中四张是产业测试验证场景，其余覆盖公众查询、内容来源教育、人工兜底、小月河生活服务和事故学习；它们按共用能力归并为六个合成演练场景节点和十二项审计任务，三种计数互不替代。所有安全事件、制品、依赖和召回均为无真实目标、无利用细节的合成演练；没有认证、生产部署或真实事故主张。

The spatial structure is “one corridor, three stations, two wings, and one continuity depot.” Its operating sequence is provenance intake, isolated validation, human review, synthetic signed release, operational observation, coordinated disclosure, and recall, repair, or retirement. Four of twelve service-design cards are industry validation scenarios; the remaining eight cover public trace search, content-provenance education, human fallback, Xiaoyue River daily services, and incident learning. They are grouped by shared capability into six synthetic exercise scenario nodes and twelve auditable tasks; the three counts are not interchangeable. Every artifact, dependency, vulnerability, and recall event is fictional and non-exploitable. No certification, production deployment, or real incident is claimed.

The ODA and all three key-area polygons remain provisional and support concept location and temporary recalculation only. Community cross-check Issue #846 reports 0% intersection coverage and a 412.5 m separation between `PROV-SITE-001` and the OSM-mapped heritage park; this submission has not independently reproduced the measurement, and neither OSM nor the provisional polygon is official, so only official geometry can decide the relationship [source:BOUNDARY-CROSSCHECK-ISSUE-846]. Official geometry, controls, roads, buildings, ownership, utilities, fire, heritage, and operating conditions require complete recomputation and professional development.
