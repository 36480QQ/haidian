High-impact scenario acceptance

SCN-02 · Pedestrian safety

Closed tests record routes, stop commands, and abnormal contact. Failure to stop or unintended contact is L3: stop all operation and transfer to the safety officer.

SCN-05 · Accessible routes

User representatives and staff walk the route together, with a printed fallback. Physical/digital mismatch removes the advice and activates staff guidance.

SCN-06 · Medical AI

Use synthetic or lawfully de-identified cases only. Unreviewed diagnostic implication, out-of-scope advice, or sensitive leakage stops the service.

SCN-09 · Commute recovery

Public service state drives broadcast, print, and crowd-transfer drills. When current state is uncertain, personalized advice stops and staff communicate one route set.
