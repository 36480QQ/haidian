Jing-Zhang Co-Creation Loop

Design Basis and Source List

This proposal translates the connective spirit of the Jing-Zhang Railway into public AI innovation infrastructure. It uses the maintained site package, source registry, taskbook, standards, submitted GeoJSON, metrics, and assumptions. The site and key areas are provisional constraints, not official redlines or statutory controls.

Three-Level Scope Framework

The coordinated research area, overall design area, and three key areas are handled as one evidence chain. The main loop, three anchors, two wings, blue-green links, and public scenarios are a conceptual structure for discussion and professional refinement.

Coordinated Research Area: Industry and Future City Research

The research framework connects universities, open-source collaboration, enterprises, public experience, and international communication. Claims about industry, policy, investment, or implementation remain subject to source and professional confirmation.

Overall Design Area: Urban Renewal and Regulatory-Plan-Level Urban Design

Land use, building footprints, roads, green space, public space, constraints, and phasing are machine-readable design layers. Missing official controls are recorded as pending rather than invented.

Detailed Design of Key Areas

Zhongzhiyuan is the innovation accelerator; Beijing AI Origin Community is the daily-life interface; Dazhongsi is the industry showcase and city gateway. Each key area receives spatial actions, AI scenarios, mobility, public-space, and implementation dependencies.

AI Innovation Ecosystem, Personas, and AI+ Scenarios

Ten scenario cards cover open-source publishing, safety governance, edge computing, accessible mobility, international demonstration, low-carbon innovation, university transfer, data governance, daily services, and a global AI activity route. Three industry tests cover API and data integration, Agent evaluation and observability, and robot delivery with public-space coordination. Five user groups are developers, startups, enterprise visitors, residents, and university communities.

Land Use, Building Scale, and Retain-Renovate-Demolish Strategy

The design layers express a conceptual spatial partition. Building scale, FAR, height, ownership, and demolition decisions remain unknown or pending until official controls and professional confirmation are available.

Transport, Rail, Municipal Infrastructure, and Public Services

The proposal prioritizes walking, cycling, rail connection, accessibility, public services, and low-intrusion pilots. Road redlines, utilities, fire safety, drainage, and engineering conditions require later confirmation.

Blue-Green Network, Public Space, and Urban Character

The historic railway, park, river interfaces, campuses, enterprises, and communities form a public-space narrative. Visual assets use the same submitted data layers and disclose provisional geometry.

Renewal Projects, Implementation Policy, and Phasing

Near-term pilots focus on guidance, open publishing, mobility diagnosis, and public-service prototypes. Medium-term work coordinates the three key areas. Long-term operation supports developer communities, open scenarios, international communication, and professional deepening. These are concept recommendations, not government commitments.

Metrics, Area Recalculation, and Compliance Matrix

Known metrics are recalculated from the submitted geometry. Regulatory controls and operational performance indicators are marked unknown or pending where official evidence is absent. The compliance matrix covers announcement tasks 1.3–1.5 and agent.1–agent.6.

Risk, Copyright, and Compliance

The package does not claim official approval, final land rights, statutory planning controls, construction scale, or guaranteed implementation. All assets must remain licensed or cleared. Offline HTML must not call remote scripts, tiles, fonts, iframes, forms, or APIs.
