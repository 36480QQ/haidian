Jingzhang Silver-Age Accessibility Belt

The Jingzhang Railway Heritage Park is re-imagined as a "silver-age service spine" for elderly communities along the corridor: an AI-assisted public-service belt for elderly medical visits, civic errands and barrier-free mobility. One spine, three areas, multi-nodes; 10 AI elderly-service scenario cards (incl. 3 test/validation scenarios), 7 silver service nodes, ~17.3 km barrier-free slow-mobility network.
