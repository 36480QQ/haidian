International Design Solicitation · Centennial Jing-Zhang AI Innovation Corridor · Concept Proposal

Jingzhang TrifoldRail · Academy · Intelligence Meridian

One corridor bears the imprint of three national basic-capability leaps: the Rail Layer (1909 — the first trunk railway designed and built by Chinese engineers traversed this corridor, a network for the movement of people), the Academy Layer (1952, the “Eight Colleges” of Xueyuan Road and Zhongguancun — a knowledge-production network), and the Intelligence Meridian Layer (today’s AI innovation cluster — an intelligent-service network, with interfaces reserved for the next general-purpose technologies). Structured as One Spine, Two Corridors, Three Harbors, Nine Segments Ten Stations — and held together by the Trifold Protocol — the concept folds the three leaps into one walkable, readable, ever-growing urban innovation belt.

Concept: The Trifold

For developers this is an innovation corridor; for ordinary people it is the park on the commute, a laboratory for children, a memory hall for parents, and the lamp that stays on late at night. AI stays behind the scenes; people stand in front.

Rail Layer1909

The first Chinese-built trunk railway traversed this corridor; Zhan Tianyou's herringbone switchback conquered the Qinglongqiao grade. A logistics base built by railway workers — carried spatially by the spine greenway and the heritage alignment.

Academy Layer1952

The 1952 reorganization raised the “Eight Colleges” along Xueyuan Road and the CAS institutes of Zhongguancun — the knowledge base of a republic. A layer of scholars and readers, carried by the Academy Corridor.

Intelligence Meridian Layer2020s

Dazhongsi–Zhichunlu–Wudaokou has become one of the most AI-intensive areas in China (background figure, promotional caliber). The layer of dreamers — with spatial mechanisms (reserved land ≈4.4%) kept open for whatever general-purpose technology comes after AI.

The Trifold Protocol: turning “fold” from a slogan into a design rule

The three layers are not three separate zones: every flagship node must contain three things at once — one readable piece of historical evidence, one public learning activity, and one explainable and exitable AI service. The South Harbor also carries the belt-wide AI status display; the Middle Harbor preserves a readable railway-heritage layer; the North Harbor includes public education and builders' memory. Only when all three layers appear at every key node does the Trifold hold.

Mobility

A slow-mobility-first skeleton (dual spines, multiple cross links)

  • The spine greenway runs ~9.6 km north–south, linking all K0–K9 station markers;
  • The Xiaoyue River waterfront cycleway runs along the site's east edge, forming the second north–south slow line — a “dual spines” skeleton together with the main spine;
  • Twenty-one “stitching streets” cross the corridor east–west at ~500 m spacing as the “multiple cross links”, healing a century of railway severance (seven double as secondary vehicular streets; the rest are pedestrian/cycling only);
  • Total road centerline length ≈ 79.80 km (computed on concept alignments).

Rail connections, parking and micro-circulation

  • Each harbor gets one conceptual rail-connection corridor towards Dazhongsi, Wudaokou–Qinghua East Rd West, and Qinghe stations;
  • Vehicle arrival and parking are organized at the harbors' edges; within 200 m of the spine, slow mobility takes priority;
  • Existing local streets are optimized as paired one-way and time-shared schemes — directional suggestions only, no engineering conclusions.

Blue-Green Public Space

Blue-green skeleton (dual spines, multiple cross links) and a three-level public space system

The ~120 m-wide spine park and the Xiaoyue River blue-green corridor on the east edge run side by side as the “dual spines”, while the 21 stitching streets form the east–west “multiple cross links”; ten K-node pocket parks and twenty-one station-park plazas complete an “avenue–park–plaza” hierarchy. Standalone green-layer coverage ≈ 29.5% (≈ 3.37 million m²), ≈29.7% under the land-balance caliber; 34 standalone plazas and public open spaces total ≈ 238,875 m², a public-space ratio of ≈ 2.1% — with the concept target of one stayable public place within a 500 m walk, pending formal network analysis.

AI pilgrimage landmarks: one primary landmark + three public memory-and-contribution nodes

  • Primary landmark — “New Bell” light-and-sound installation (South Harbor): Yongle Bell voiceprint × AI soundscape in real-time dialogue, the core of the corridor's sonic identity;
  • K0 Origin Plaza (Middle Harbor): the belt's narrative kilometre zero (a concept label, not the historic mileage origin of the Jing-Zhang Railway), paving engraved to the 1435 mm standard gauge;
  • Herringbone Bridge & Qinghuayuan Old Station Memory Hall (near K2): accessible ramps and a “problem–experiment–failure–correction” exhibition make the switchback an experienceable mechanism; sleepers and spikes re-designed as street furniture;
  • K9 Gate of the Future (north end): a humble threshold structure to walk through and linger at, with a low-brightness light pulse at night pointing to “the next corridor”.

The “Trifold walk of fame”: railway builders on the Rail Layer, scholars on the Academy Layer, and an annual open-source contributor plaque on the Intelligence Meridian.

Flagship scenarios in depth (S4 / S5 / T3 full mechanisms: user journey · data minimization · human review · exit)

S4Flagship 1 · Night-walk guardian lighting

  • User journey: A late-returner enters the spine — the lighting brightens as they walk — anomalies are confirmed manually before any response.
  • Data minimization: Inputs are only anonymous crowd density and illuminance; no facial recognition, no individual trajectory recording; on-device processing with 24-hour rolling deletion.
  • Human review: The park operator's duty customer-service staff reviews and confirms before response; a sensor false-alarm rate above the published threshold or a lighting failure counts as failure.
  • Degradation & exit: Degrades to scheduled full-on mode; exit means shutting the system down while retaining basic lighting, with sensor modules physically removable. Near-term pilot: the southern 1 km of the spine.

S5Flagship 2 · Memory collection van

  • User journey: Community appointment — on-site oral-history collection — the narrator listens and confirms — authorized archiving — optional public display.
  • Data minimization: Inputs are audio and photos voluntarily provided by interviewees; every archive requires written consent and can be withdrawn at any time; AI only transcribes and pre-screens.
  • Human review: The community archive programme editor is the reviewer; every finished piece is checked and signed by a human compiler; authorization flaws or factual disputes count as failure.
  • Degradation & exit: Degrades to offline archiving only, without publication; exit means taking the archive offline and deleting identifiable information. Near-term pilot: 2–3 communities in the South Harbor.

T3Flagship 3 · Robot staging unit

  • User journey: Enterprise application — safety assessment — staging-area public notice and testing — public feedback — evaluation for formalization or exit.
  • Data minimization: Inputs are robot operation logs and public feedback, collected per the minimization principle; during the notice period anyone may review the test rules and raise objections.
  • Human review: The safety assessment committee is the reviewer; a safety accident or a failed evaluation counts as failure.
  • Degradation & exit: Degrades to shorter test windows and areas; exit means terminating the test, publishing the reasons and restoring the site to public space. Near-term pilot: modules M1–M3 of the North Harbor test field.
