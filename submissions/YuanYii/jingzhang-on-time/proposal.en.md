> **Proposition: AI public services in the city should run like trains on a published timetable — punctuality is verifiable, changes require public notice, suspensions are announced in advance, delays come with compensation, and cross-service transfers are connected by schedule.**

## 10 Operating Diagram Governance Protocol and Open Operation

**Urban Operating Diagram (v1.0).** Every AI public service registers its spatio-temporal schedule (service ID / interval / window / punctuality definition / delay thresholds / change notice / suspension notice / transfer / AI-off path / stop condition). Publicly queryable, machine-readable (timetable.schema.json), machine-verifiable (check_timetable.js; 10 fixtures: 3 accepted, 7 rejected).

**Three-signal display.** Green = punctual; amber = delayed with human takeover; red = suspended, reverting to the no-AI baseline. Signal posts make governance state spatially legible.

**Punctuality KPIs.** Punctual = completed on schedule without human takeover. Six KPIs (punctuality / delay / suspension fulfilment / window execution / transfer connection / compensation fulfilment) are sourced from the diagram log, published monthly, archived annually.

**Weekly window.** Every Tuesday 06:00–12:00: AI off line-wide for maintenance (equipment / model / data / interface), public space returns to no-AI state — periodic AI exit becomes civic rhythm, not emergency. Execution enters KPI-4.

**Transfer protocol.** Cross-district handovers follow transfer times; receipts require human sign-off; delays never silently skip a transfer; no receipt, no operation.

**Gates G0–G4.** Registration → baseline verification → controlled trial → punctuality assessment → release/suspend. Each gate has inputs, judgement and stop actions; years and hype never substitute for evidence gates. Suspension is a qualified outcome (falsify-first).

**Paired testing (AI on / AI off).** Same task, both states; equivalence gap (completion / time / accessibility / appeal) is mandatory evidence for registration; denominators include failures.

**Operating community (five parties).** District authority (lead + authorization), park operator (execution), university alliance (resources), community representatives (veto), independent audit (review). No single party decides everything. Legal basis: Urban Renewal Regulation + Generative AI Interim Measures Art. 14/15 + Zhongguancun sandbox framework. Five-fund firewall (public base / research commission / public-interest commission / time-limited sponsorship / operating revenue); three ledgers (capex / opex / exit reserve) required before any G3 pilot. Civic Value Protocol: 10–20% of corridor commercial computing-node and scenario-test revenue studied for injection into the community public-value fund (voluntary CSR charter, ratio and rules to be determined by authorities and community representatives).
