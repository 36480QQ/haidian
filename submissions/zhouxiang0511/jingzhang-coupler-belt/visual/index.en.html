04

Key-Area Detailed Design

Each of the three key areas is deepened on "positioning + spatial structure + building renewal + mobility + public space + AI scenarios + implementation risk". All three boundaries are provisional; the conclusions are directional concept design.

key_areas.geojson · PROV-KEY-001/002/003

Zhongzhi Park · Full-Stack Assembly Station ≈192.1 ha

  • Positioning: garden-style AI self-innovation district carrying full-stack innovation and the "global AI governance voice".
  • Structure: compute core + pilot-test ring + garden belt; links Qinghe riverside green belt to the north.
  • Buildings: retrofit low-efficiency industrial and park buildings; preserve valuable historical factory fabric.
  • AI scenarios: public compute services, model evaluation and full-stack assembly line, urban-agent emergency drill ground.
  • Risk: complex ownership and large retrofit scope; phased co-development by platform firms and park owners.

Beijing AI Origin Community · Talent Departure Station ≈104.3 ha

  • Positioning: near-campus AI innovation district serving university staff, students and startups.
  • Structure: campus-city interface + talent corridor + entrepreneurship courtyards.
  • Buildings: functional conversion of street-front commerce and ageing buildings; new talent apartments and incubation space.
  • AI scenarios: talent service hall (departure platform), AI governance experience centre (interlocking tower), AI+ education lab.
  • Risk: sensitive campus boundaries and peak-hour traffic; joint study with universities and traffic authorities.

Dazhongsi · Outcomes Market Station ≈72.0 ha

  • Positioning: urban AI innovation district — an "outcomes market" turning AI capabilities into experienceable, consumable, transferable applications.
  • Structure: market core + experience ring + station-city gateway.
  • Buildings: convert existing commercial space into AI application experience stores and performance venues.
  • AI scenarios: AI application experience district, low-speed robot delivery demo, AI cultural guide.
  • Risk: operating entities and transit pressures; phased, zoned, reversible pilots recommended.

06

Mobility, Slow Traffic & Blue-Green Public Space

Transfer is organised as "rail + slow traffic + low-speed feeder"; blue-green space follows "one ridge, two waters, many nodes". All capacities and alignments are conceptual directions for professional deepening with official data.

roads.geojson · green_space.geojson · public_space.geojson

Mobility & slow traffic

  • Roads: retain and optimise the north–south secondary arterial; add 8 east–west stitching connectors; micro-circulation and park-and-ride around the three stations.
  • Slow traffic: continuous mainline greenway north–south; continuous footpaths and cycleways on side streets; safe-crossing islands and level ramps at stitching turnouts.
  • Rail transfer: transfer loops anchored on existing stations (incl. Dazhongsi); shared-mobility and logistics stops within an 800 m radius.
  • New infrastructure: distributed compute nodes, energy micro-grids and a sensor public base integrated with existing utilities (conceptual direction).

Blue-green network (one ridge · two waters · many nodes)

  • One ridge: the Jingzhang Heritage Park vitality mainline green belt.
  • Two waters: Qinghe riverside belt (north) + Xiaoyue River ecological corridor (east).
  • Many nodes: community parks and corner greens.
  • Readings: green and open space ≈31.4%, public space ≈5.0% (provisional-boundary recomputation).

Three pilgrimage landmarks (3 · concept proposals requiring professional deepening and clearance)

Coupler Plaza

On the north mainline (toward the former Qinghuayuan station): a physical "coupler + rail" installation honouring the interoperability wisdom of China's first self-built trunk railway, and a long-term showcase for open-interface and developer outcomes.

Interlocking Tower

At the Origin Community station plaza: a legible "signal-light" interface (concept) showing the real-time review status of agents accessing public scenarios — an urban living room for transparent AI governance.

Origin Platform No. 0

At the Beijing North Station gateway plaza: a timeline installation "1909 departed from here → 2026 AI departs from here", telling the story from the "railway of self-respect" to the "belt of self-reliance in intelligence".

Companion elements: an agent contribution honour wall (along the mainline, linked to the project's permanent commemoration system) and a public-space component library (coupler-shaped seating, signal-light masts, open-source galleries and other standardised reusable components).

renewal_project_list · phasing.geojson

Retrofits · 7 items

Park renewals · 6 items

Zhongzhi Park low-efficiency factory retrofits, Origin Community incubation courtyards, Dazhongsi commercial-space renewal, etc.

New nodes · 5 items

Three station plazas, the interlocking tower, Coupler Plaza, Beijing North Station gateway, open-data workshop.

Phasing plan

  • Near term (0–2 yrs): Origin Community + mainline green belt + Coupler Plaza pilots — low-cost, reversible, lightweight projects first.
  • Mid term (2–5 yrs): Zhongzhi Park and the Zhongguancun wing; Dazhongsi market-core renewal.
  • Long term (5+ yrs): full stitching of the two wings and activation of reserved land.

Implementation policies (conceptual directions): scenario opening loop of "apply — review — test — exit", public-data opening and open-source licensing, developer-community co-building, public participation and human review. All policies and funding arrangements are directions for deepening, not confirmed government arrangements.
