## Executive Summary

The Jing-Zhang AI Commons proposes a novel governance model: **Open-Shaft Protocol**, borrowing from the Badaling Tunnel's vertical shaft excavation method to transform closed AI R&D into an open, verifiable innovation process with multiple parallel work faces.

**Core Structure: One Spine · Three Cores · Two Wings · Multiple Chains**

| Element | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **One Spine** | Jing-Zhang Railway Heritage Park — 9km north-south public vitality corridor |
| **Three Cores** | North: Zhongzhiyuan (R&D & Trust Verification Core) · Center: AI Origin Community (Transformation & Talent Core) · South: Dazhongsi (Application & International Reception Core) |
| **Two Wings** | West: Zhongguancun Technology Services Wing · East: Xiaoyuehe Ecological Wing |
| **Multiple Chains** | East-west cross-links connecting universities, communities, parks, and transit stations |

**Shaft Collaboration Protocol**: Four verification shafts (Open Evaluation / Low-Speed Testing / Data Sandbox / Community Deliberation) operate in parallel with L1 Disclosure / L2 Controlled Testing / L3 Bounded Operations tiers. Annual cycle: Spring (Opening) → Summer (Verification) → Autumn (Commons Week) → Winter (Backfill Review).

## Overall Design — Urban Renewal & Regulatory-Planning Depth

The overall design centers on the "One Spine · Three Cores · Two Wings · Multiple Chains" structure. The 9km Jing-Zhang Railway Heritage Park serves as the north-south public spine, with three differentiated core zones and two specialized service wings.

## Key Area Detailed Design

### Zhongzhiyuan — AI Innovation Accelerator (North)
R&D & Trust Verification Core. Houses the Open Engineering Lab with garden testing courtyard, open model evaluation, low-speed robot testing, shared pilot interfaces, and ground-floor exhibition corridor. Physical separation between closed testing zones and public recreation areas.

### AI Origin Community — Transformation Hub (Center)
Transformation & Talent Incubation Core. Serves as the "first kilometer" from campus innovation to urban service. Provides open-source release, achievement incubation, IP legal clinic, and youth collaboration spaces.

### Dazhongsi — AI Industry Hub (South)
Application & International Reception Core. Features four-quadrant station portal integration, AI agents/terminals/digital content industries, a "city living room" where non-consumption stays are welcome, and international collaboration services.

## JZ-VP (Jing-Zhang Verification Protocol): From Mechanism to Protocol

The protocol formalizes the shaft collaboration model: entry conditions, verification cycles, evidence reuse, exit triggers. Three tiers of AI governance: L1 Disclosure (public red-line publication), L2 Controlled Testing (limited-speed verification with physical safety backup), L3 Bounded Operations (public service delivery with expiration and exit conditions).

## Urban Verification Economics: Computable Innovation Competitiveness Indicators

VCI (Verified Competitiveness Index), VDR (Verified Deployment Rate), TFC (Trust Failure Cost), and error-correction cost curves provide measurable innovation governance metrics. All numerical examples are SYNTHETIC TEST VECTORS, not actual performance data.

## Shaft Spatial Grammar: Translating Verification Protocol into Urban Form

Seven shaft grammar units translate protocol into spatial form: shaft mouth (public entry), shaft tunnel (verification corridor), shaft barrel (data/evidence channel), shaft platform (review station), shaft eye (observation point), shaft marker (public registry), shaft skirt (community interface).

## Protocol Governance and Anti-Capture: Verifying the Verifier

JZ-GPoV (Governance Proof of Verification), distributed council, three-tier dispute mechanism, capture stress testing, equivalent mutual recognition, and non-exclusive exit collectively constrain rules, budgets, registries, and emergency powers. Formal correctness does not substitute legality, professional judgment, or external remedy.

## AI Nativity and Verifiability Mechanisms

AI participation in public decisions has boundaries: health, education, legal, transportation, and public resource judgments require responsible persons, human review, explanation, appeal, and exit channels. Physical autonomous systems are limited to verified operating envelopes with safety operator override.
