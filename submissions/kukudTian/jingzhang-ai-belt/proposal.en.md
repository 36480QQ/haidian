# JingZhang Code OPEN-JZ

This is the English equivalent translation of the Chinese proposal (proposal.md). The Chinese version is the primary human-readable proposal. All structured data (GeoJSON, metrics, matrices, self-check) is shared.

## Concept

**Open-Source City Operating System.** The Jing-Zhang Railway is the origin of Chinese independent innovation; Zhongguancun is the origin of Chinese open source. OPEN-JZ merges the two: the city is a continuously evolving codebase. Urban renewal = commit, review, merge, revert. Citizens are reviewers, developers are contributors, government is maintainer.

## Spatial Structure

- **MAIN line**: Jing-Zhang Heritage Park green corridor, 9 km, slow-traffic first.
- **TRAIN-REPO (Zhongzhiyuan, 192.1 ha)**: computing clusters, model training, standards, security governance.
- **COMMUNITY-REPO (Origin Community, 104.3 ha)**: open-source square, incubation, talent zone, Qinghuayuan station heritage.
- **APP-REPO (Dazhongsi, 72.0 ha)**: smart terminals, content consumption, data elements.
- **PROTOCOL layers**: three east-west stitching lines for data flow, scenario testing, ethics governance.

## Deliverables

- 10 AI scenario cards; 3 industrial test scenarios; 5 user profiles; 3 AI pilgrimage landmarks (Origin Station, Zhongzhi Computing Tower, Dazhongsi AI Bell Tower).
- Naming & visual identity: terminal aesthetics (dark navy, fluorescent green, code white); signal-light language for AI city status.
- Phasing: 2026-2028 first commit (Origin Community); 2028-2030 main-branch merge (green corridor + Zhongzhiyuan); 2030-2035 continuous delivery (Dazhongsi + city-wide).

## Boundary Statement

All spatial proposals are conceptual and based on the provisional boundary registered by the organizers; official redline requires full recalculation. This proposal does not replace statutory planning or government-approved conclusions. Official planning control indicators are missing and treated as data gaps.
