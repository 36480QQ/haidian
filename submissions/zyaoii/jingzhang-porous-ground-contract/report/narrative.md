# 京张会呼吸 / THE POROUS GROUND CONTRACT — Executive Narrative

## 中文

让地面打开，不让噪声直送到人。方案不建设另一套环境看板，而把每个首层孔隙写成“源—路径—接收者”空间合同：北部众智园处理源与公众边界，中部AI原点同时保障穿行与停留，南部大钟寺只做未锚定的换乘—静侧类型研究。被动断面、错位开口、阴影与静侧是基础；AI只比较B1人工规则以外的可解释候选，不能稳定胜出就删除。当前没有真实断面、授权、E3/E4或效果；所有试点均为NO-GO。90日协议把基线、消融、可逆样段、负向结果、停止与RESTORED签收放进同一工程闭环。

## English

Open the ground, not a direct noise path. This is not another environmental dashboard; every ground-floor opening becomes a source–path–receiver spatial contract. Northern Zhongzhiyuan addresses the source–public boundary, central AI Origin holds passage and dwelling together, and southern Dazhongsi remains an unanchored arrival-to-quiet-side type study. Passive sections, offset openings, shade and a quiet side are foundational. AI only compares explainable alternatives against manual B1 and is deleted when it cannot win stably. There is no real transect, authority, E3/E4 evidence or measured outcome; every pilot is NO-GO. The 90-day protocol joins baseline, ablation, reversible mock-up, adverse results, stopping and signed RESTORED evidence in one engineering loop.
