# JING-ZHANG CIVIC STUDIO

The proposal organizes the Centennial Jing-Zhang AI Innovation Belt as an accountable urban content-production system: One Story Rail, Three Civic Studios, Two Support Wings, and Twelve Frames. Zhongzhiyuan tests trusted media tools, AI Origin supports co-creation, and Dazhongsi hosts civic review and creator services.

This narrative is a convenience summary only. `proposal.md` is the authoritative human-readable proposal; GeoJSON, metrics, matrices, assumptions, sources, and self-check files are the authoritative structured evidence. All spatial geometry is concept-only and remains constrained by repository-provided provisional envelopes.
