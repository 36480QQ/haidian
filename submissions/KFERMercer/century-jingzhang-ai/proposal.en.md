# Centennial Jing-Zhang AI Innovation Belt · Smart-Rail Integrated Urban Design Proposal

## Design Basis and Source Inventory

This formal proposal takes the official announcement issued by the Haidian Branch of Beijing Municipal Commission of Natural Resources and Planning as its primary basis, and uses the provisional boundaries, key areas, enumerations, metrics, and source inventory registered by maintainers in `brief/site-package/` as machine-readable evidence. The proposal must be organized from the announcement, the agent-facing taskbook, and site materials; only the most critical basis is placed adjacent to judgments [source:OFFICIAL-ANNOUNCEMENT] [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK] [depth:existing_conditions_diagnosis].

## Three-Level Scope Framework

The proposal is organized according to three levels defined in the announcement: the coordinated research area focuses on AI industry ecology and future city form across 43.6 km²; the overall design area addresses 11.4 km² of urban districts and industrial zones around the Jing-Zhang Heritage Park; the key detailed design area covers 368.4 ha across three zones requiring regulatory-plan urban design depth.

The overall concept proposed is "Jing-Zhang Smart-Vein Symbiosis Belt": taking the Jing-Zhang Heritage Park as the historical and public space spine, with Zhongzhiyuan, Beijing AI Origin Community, and Dazhongsi as innovation anchors, forming "one belt, three cores, multi-point scenarios, blue-green slow-traffic composite ring" spatial organization.

## Coordinated Research Area: Industry and Future City Research

The core task is to build a world-class AI innovation ecosystem. The proposal organizes Haidian's university research institutes, leading enterprises, computing power/algorithms/data elements, incubation platforms, listed companies, unicorns, and technology service resources to propose a spatial coordination framework for AI innovation chains, industrial chains, talent chains, and urban service chains.

## Overall Design Area: Urban Renewal and Regulatory-Plan Depth Urban Design

The overall design area requires regulatory-plan urban design depth. The proposal establishes an overall spatial structure for urban renewal, identifies inefficient spaces, creates a renewal project inventory, proposes implementation policies, and defines industrial functional proportions and spatial organization patterns.

## Key Area Detailed Design

### Zhongzhiyuan AI Independent Innovation Acceleration Area
Positioned as a garden-style full-stack independent innovation district, strengthening the Qinghe interface, industrial display, low-carbon innovation exchange, and external transportation organization.

### Beijing AI Origin Community
Positioned as a near-campus achievement transformation and talent community, organizing campus-district-neighborhood slow-traffic stitching and supplementing achievement release, talent services, and living support.

### Dazhongsi AI Industry Cluster
Positioned as an urban-style smart economy and international exchange district, centered on Dazhongsi Station integration and four-quadrant pedestrian connectivity.

## Transportation, Rail, Municipal, and Public Service Facilities
