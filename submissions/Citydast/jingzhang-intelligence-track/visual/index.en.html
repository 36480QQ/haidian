JING-ZHANG INTELLIGENCE TRACK · 京张智轨From a Century-Old Rail to an Intelligence Track

Translating the century-old Jing-Zhang Railway into the city's "artery": anchored on a 9 km innovation corridor, with Zhongzhiyuan / Beijing AI Origin Community / Dazhongsi as three key-area anchors and the three zones and two wings as the framework, the proposal forms one belt, three cores, multiple scenario nodes and a blue-green smart loop. Adopting the premise that "the city is an organic living organism", it reads the blue-green smart loop as the city's ecological pulse and intelligent neural network.
