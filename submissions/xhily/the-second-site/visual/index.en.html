Zhongzhiyuan AI acceleration area

Garden-style autonomous-innovation district. Emphasises national platforms, industry showcase, Qinghe culture, external transport and low-carbon interaction space.

Beijing AI Origin community

Near-campus achievement-transfer district. Emphasises university-origin innovation, open-source community, talent services, retain-renovate-demolish and station integration.

Dazhongsi AI industry cluster

Urban intelligent-economy district. Emphasises leading enterprises, agent-native new formats, commercial services, composite green-space use and four-quadrant intersection connectivity.
