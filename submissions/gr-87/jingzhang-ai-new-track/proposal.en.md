# JingZhang NewTrack: Concept Urban Design for the Centennial Jing-Zhang AI Innovation Belt

## 0. Review First Screen

**Judgment**: JingZhang NewTrack organizes the heritage, transit, and data tracks into one reversible public service chain; AI, like a Jing-Zhang switchback locomotive, returns to the human track on any obstacle — ordinary service, human stop, appeal, exit-restore are never absent.

### Naming System: Three Tracks, One Pulse

Master name: **JingZhang NewTrack**. "Track" recalls the Jing-Zhang Railway, China's first independently designed trunk line; "new track" points to Rail Line 13 sharing the corridor [data:geometry/constraints.geojson#CON-002] and the AI "data track." Sub-names: Zhongzhiyuan=Zhongzhi Platform, Origin=Zero Point Station, Dazhongsi=Bell Station [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK] [depth:overall_spatial_structure].

The Jing-Zhang Railway (1905-1909, Jeme Tien Yow chief engineer) and the Qinglongqiao switchback, where two locomotives reversed to climb; the Qinghuayuan Station site lies inside this belt [data:geometry/constraints.geojson#CON-005] — common public history, a naming and narrative anchor only, not an accredited conclusion [source:SITE-PACKAGE]. The switchback becomes the AI fallback prototype.

**Logo**: three rail lines with a data pulse center, reading "N"; gray-blue, tech blue, pulse green. Design proposal, no trademark claims [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK].

## Original Mechanism: Switchback Fallback (JZ-SWITCHBACK-001)

"Three Tracks, One Pulse" drills down to primary mechanism **JZ-SWITCHBACK-001**: three tracks as evidence constraints, one public service chain as acceptance spine, Switchback Fallback as state machine, three Switchback Test Sections as anchors; all concept-level states [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK] [depth:mechanism_design].

The Jing-Zhang Railway (1905-1909, Jeme Tien Yow chief engineer), the Qinglongqiao switchback reverse-climb, and the Qinghuayuan Station site in this belt [data:geometry/constraints.geojson#CON-005] — common public history, naming and narrative anchor only, not an accredited conclusion [source:SITE-PACKAGE]. A generic fallback state machine carries no site memory; Switchback Fallback translates "reverse on obstacle" from railway engineering into an urban AI governance protocol that only the Jing-Zhang corridor can fully invoke [depth:mechanism_design].

**Three-track evidence constraints**:
- **Heritage Track**: sources, cultural narrative, heritage boundaries [source:SITE-PACKAGE].
- **Transit Track**: spatial relations, ordinary passage, engineering/ownership preconditions [standard:MOHURD-CONTROL-DETAILED-PLANNING].
- **Data Track**: data minimization, human review, appeal, exit; stop on breach [standard:DATA-SRC-GENERATIVE-AI-INTERIM-MEASURES].

**Public service chain**: `choose → use → stop → appeal → return to human → exit/restore`. Every AI scenario must answer each step [depth:public_service_chain].

**Switchback Fallback protocol**: `ordinary service → optional assistance → switchback (stop/human takeover) → restored ordinary service`. Restoration does not raise maturity or create deployment authorization; stops and restorations must be recorded [depth:fallback_states].

**Switchback Test Sections**: JZ-SW1 Zhongzhiyuan (SC-02), JZ-SW2 Origin Community (SC-04/09/11 AI–human equivalence), JZ-SW3 Dazhongsi (SC-12), all `concept_only` [depth:validation_windows]. Full fields in compliance_matrix.json `mechanism_register` [source:PROCESSED-FACT-PACK].

**Evidence discipline**: complete fields prove design coverage only; offline replay proves rule reproducibility; real operations, permits, performance stay `unknown`, never guessed [depth:evidence_discipline].

## Risk, Copyright, and Compliance

| Risk | Trigger | Immediate action | Responsibility role | Recovery |

| Accessibility route interrupted | Signage conflict or discontinuity | Stop AI signage, return to human | Local coordination | Obstacle verified, route restored |
