Coordinated Research Area: Industry and Future City Research

Overall concept: the Jing-Zhang Archive Belt. The Jing-Zhang Railway is the birthplace of modern Chinese engineering archives and operational discipline—drawings, running logs, and bridge ledgers turned a self-built railway into a traceable century of memory. Zhongguancun is the birthplace of Chinese innovation records and entrepreneurship archives—from Electronics Street to the National Innovation Demonstration Zone, every milestone has a verifiable record. In the AI era, this belt is best positioned to answer the question: when agents and humans co-create the city, how do contributions become visible, remembered, and passed on? Source

The Archive Belt treats the archive as a public institution and spatial language: contributions enter the archive, services keep archives, corrections amend archives, and honors are archives. This is inherently aligned with the official requirements of "evidence chain, recomputability, and memorable contributions." The three positionings translate as follows: the Centennial Jing-Zhang Culture Belt becomes the century-long archive narrative on the Memory Spine; the Urban AI Living Experience Belt makes every public AI service carry a readable "service archive card"; and the AI Integration Innovation Belt records, names, and inherits the contributions of human-agent co-creation Source.

The five functions translate as follows: the Full-Stack Independent AI Innovation System is archived at the Source Archive Station (compute, models, standards, testing); the World-Class AI Innovation Ecosystem at the Transform Archive Station (outcomes, talent, enterprises); the AI+ Scenario Enablement paradigm at the Scenario Archive Station and the Xiaoyue River wing (operations and corrections); the Smart AI Vitality City through archive facilities and tiered-searchable urban memory in public space; and the Global Voice in AI Governance through the Archive Charter itself—an authorized-public, correctable, inheritable governance interface Source.

Naming system and visual identity direction (agent.1): the main name follows the official "Centennial Jing-Zhang AI Innovation Belt (JZ-AI Belt)"; the spatial naming system is "Memory Spine — Source / Transform / Scenario Archive Station — Record Wings"; the Logo direction uses an "archive card × rail sleeper" motif—archive cards arranged along the rails, each bearing an "archiving line," with space above the line always awaiting new contributions; the identity language uses the three archive-status colors (in archive / pending review / corrected) across wayfinding, scenario cards, and public interfaces, homologous with railway signal culture. All logos, fonts, and graphics must be rights-cleared before use Source.

Conceptual differentiation from adjacent peer themes (originality argument)

The archive axis is unoccupied among merged peer proposals, but adjacent families exist; this proposal therefore states its distinctions explicitly (comparisons are based on public catalog summaries, not evaluations):

  • Archive ≠ provenance: some proposals turn "provenance" into full-element attribution tags (every object answers who proposed it, on what evidence, reviewed by whom); the Archive Belt instead builds "contribution archives"—searchable, correctable, inheritable records and honors for people who join open co-creation. Provenance answers "where did this come from"; the archive answers "who did what, and who remembers."
  • Archive ≠ memory chains: some proposals attach a memory link to every AI action (a governance ledger); the Archive Belt makes the archive a public institution and spatial ritual—three archive stations, the Archive Charter, the Honor Yearbook Node, and Open-Source Commit Steles—serving agent.4 recognition, agent.5 cultural narrative, and agent.6 long-term brand assets.
  • Archive ≠ milestone inscriptions: some proposals build a walkable milestone memorial system; the Archive Belt elevates recognition into an institution (open archiving / searchability / corrections / human-final honors) and aligns it with the official "evidence chain, reproducibility, memorable contributions" culture—the archive is the evidence, with a longer time horizon and a more complete governance interface.
  • Archive ≠ ledger receipts: some proposals give every service a readable receipt (a service voucher); the Archive Belt emphasizes long-term accumulation and corrective re-archiving: corrected records retain version relationships and minimum-necessary audit information, while public display follows withdrawal, deletion/anonymization and retention rules—a brand asset for decade-scale operations, not a single-transaction voucher.
  • Archive ≠ memory boundary: we do not make a forgetting institution our main concept. "Record contributions, not trajectories" is the guardrail of an inheritance institution. Honor display is public only after explicit voluntary authorization and retains withdrawal, deletion or anonymization routes; our mainstay is tiered archiving, human-final honors, and the Honor Yearbook Node.
  • Archive ≠ evidence audit: we do not turn evidence into an engineering-audit verb chain (verify→live→convert→govern). We instead build the archive lifecycle (archiving→search→correction→human-final review→inheritance). "The archive is the evidence" is a shared starting point, not the destination.
  • Archive ≠ service windows: we do not build window-style industrial service infrastructure in the AI Origin Community. The Transform Archive Station is an "institution + honor space"—the Origin Reception Hall, the Open-Source Commit Stele, and the Honor Yearbook Node. Same area, different track.
  • Archive ≠ acceptance-test failure archive: we record "who did what and who remembers," not an acceptance receipt for "whether a service can launch"; timely stopping is also recorded as a contribution, but it is not the subject of the archive. Acceptance verifies services; archives inherit people.

Overall Design Area: Urban Renewal and Regulatory-Plan-Level Urban Design

The spatial structure of the Overall Design Area is "one belt, three stations, two wings, multiple points, two networks" Depth:

  • Memory Spine: an archive promenade along the Jing-Zhang heritage park; "record ticks" are embedded in the sleepers, with a decade archive every hundred steps—the promenade is itself a readable archive belt Spatial data;
  • Three archive stations: Source (Zhongzhiyuan), Transform (AI Origin Community), Scenario (Dazhongsi), carried by the three key areas Spatial data;
  • Two record wings: the Zhongguancun Technology Services Wing archives factors (IP, capital, services); the Xiaoyue River Scenario Enablement Wing archives use and corrections;
  • Multiple points: name walls, Honor Yearbook Nodes, Open-Source Commit Steles, and Service Archive Panels as public archive facilities Spatial data;
  • Two networks: a walking/blue-green network carrying the spine and stitch corridors, and an archive retrieval network of public facilities that explicitly collects no personal trajectories.

Same-topic benchmark (background reference, no endorsement implied): public reports on the 2020 Jing-Zhang Railway Heritage Park international open call show its expert-review dimensions of culture, openness, intensiveness, ecology, and implementability, together with publicly reported delivery figures (20 dead-end roads connected, 9.3+ km of walking/cycling paths, 79 ha of green gardens) and the Wudaokou starter-zone demonstration Source. The Memory Spine responds on the same terms: a continuous spine of about 9.7 km, three east–west stitch bands and eight conceptual permeability corridors, with archive nodes and service facilities configured at both the 15-minute and 5–10-minute life-circle levels (conceptual values pending official road and facility data). An "Archive Starter Zone" is proposed (analogous to the Wudaokou starter zone, located on the AI Origin Community segment) to demonstrate Contribution Intake Desks, Service Archive Panels, and memory-installation prototypes within 1–2 years Depth.

The urban renewal framework adopts archive-based renewal units (conceptual recommendation): the first layer is the renewal units anchored on the three archive stations; the second layer is micro-renewal along the spine—pocket archive nodes, connection points, and ground-floor function swaps—with low disturbance, phasing, and reversibility Depth. Archive Charter (seven principles)

1. Tiered archiving: service states and co-creation outputs are first classified as public, restricted or internal; personal information and unauthorized material are not public by default; 2. Tiered search: the public catalogue is searchable, while restricted records are available only to authorized processing roles; 3. Corrections and withdrawal: residents may request review; honor display and attribution can be withdrawn, deleted or anonymized where appropriate; 4. Privacy boundary: only minimum-necessary contribution information, never personal trajectories; classification and access logging apply; 5. Honor rules: names and honors receive human and professional final review and are displayed only after explicit voluntary authorization Source; 6. Archive as evidence: metrics, sources, assumptions, and self-check records enter the archive; full recalculation is archived when official boundaries are released. 7. Non-AI equivalent paths: every AI service entering public space must retain a non-AI equivalent path (paper, human window, phone, or on-site service); on device failure or staff shortage, services degrade to human fallback—digital access is not the only door Standard.

Appendix A: Scenario Archive Cards (12 · complete set)

Nature: conceptual recommendations only; they do not constitute approved operations or confirmed government arrangements. Each card follows 8 fields: positioning / user journey / input data / AI capability / infrastructure / operating entity (conceptual) / failure mode / privacy and human review. Testing and validation scenarios (marked TEST) specify operating-entity ideas, risk control, human-review points, and data boundaries. Every card also applies three spatial-quality check items: PPS place qualities (access, comfort, uses, sociability); the four accessibility principles (safe and convenient, practical and easy to use, broadly beneficial, combined with age-friendliness); and two-tier life-circle reachability (15-minute / 5–10-minute).

S01 Contribution Intake Desk (Memory Spine)

  • Positioning: open co-creation participants (developers, residents, agents) submit contributions along the spine, generating searchable archive entries with name recognition
  • User journey: arrive at the desk → choose contribution type → submit → entry generated (queryable ID) → name wall display
  • Input data: contribution category, de-identified summary and verification state; tiered as “public catalogue / restricted archive / internal audit,” not by source-material grade
  • AI capability: summary generation, duplicate-similarity suggestions (human final review)
  • Infrastructure: desk terminal + entry printout + spine power/network (conceptual)
  • Operating entity: district science commission with open-source community (conceptual)
  • Failure mode: terminal failure → paper archive card fallback; misjudgment → human review
  • Privacy and human review: no personal trajectories; anonymous submission; honors decided by humans

S02 Service Archive Panel (public space)

  • Positioning: residents read, query, and correct public-space AI service archives without any device
  • User journey: pass by → read service status → register correction on-site/QR → reviewed result archived
  • Input data: service status, correction requests (de-identified)
  • AI capability: status aggregation and summary, correction classification suggestions (human final review)
  • Infrastructure: panel + archive terminal + edge node (conceptual)
  • Operating entity: neighborhood service station with subdistrict (conceptual)
  • Failure mode: terminal failure → paper notice board fallback; misjudgment → human review
  • Privacy and human review: no identity trajectories; anonymous corrections; review entry within walking distance

S03 Memory Era Installations (heritage park)

  • Positioning: three chapters of archives (Jing-Zhang / Zhongguancun / AI) as a walkable urban memory reader
  • User journey: walk the spine → touch era nodes → read the three chapters → join message archiving
  • Input data: public historical materials, archive entries (no personal data)
  • AI capability: multilingual guide content generation (history verified against sources, human gatekeeping)
  • Infrastructure: era installations + walkway + bilingual signage (conceptual)
  • Operating entity: park operator with cultural institutions (conceptual)
  • Failure mode: content disputes → human correction; device failure → static signage
  • Privacy and human review: no personal data collection; message archiving requires content review

S04 Honor Yearbook Node (AI Origin Community)

  • Positioning: annual recording, display, and conferral space for contributor honors
  • User journey: annual nomination → human review → archiving → yearbook release → name recognition
  • Input data: nomination and review records (de-identified, consent-based)
  • AI capability: annual contribution aggregation, layout assistance (review and conferral by humans)
  • Infrastructure: yearbook node + archive repository + conferral space (conceptual)
  • Operating entity: joint operating committee (conceptual)
  • Failure mode: nomination disputes → human final ruling; data breach → withdrawal + notification
  • Privacy and human review: contributions only, no trajectories; human-final honors; appealable

S05 Open-Source Commit Stele (AI Origin Community)

  • Positioning: names of code/scheme/dataset contributors settle into public memory
  • User journey: contribution passes review → choose real name, pseudonym or no display → stele entry generated; displayed attribution may later be withdrawn, deleted or anonymized on request
  • Input data: open-source submission metadata (no source content, no personal privacy)
  • AI capability: contribution classification and archive generation (human review)
  • Infrastructure: stele + digital archive terminal (conceptual)
  • Operating entity: open-source community with district platform (conceptual)
  • Failure mode: entry errors → correction archiving; accidental deletion → restore only after renewed confirmation by the person, while normal deletion retains only a minimum non-public audit record
  • Privacy and human review: contributor consent governs; no scraping of personal information

S06 Scenario Test Block TEST (Zhongzhiyuan–Dazhongsi)

  • Positioning: public testing and validation venue for AI city services before launch (industry testing scenario)
  • User journey: apply for road access/filing → test → data return → safety assessment → results archived
  • Input data: vehicle/terminal status, road conditions, incident records (desensitized, tiered access)
  • AI capability: test generation, safety monitoring, anomaly grading (disposition by humans)
  • Infrastructure: test block + fencing + dispatch platform (conceptual)
  • Operating entity: district testing office with third-party inspection (conceptual)
  • Failure mode: incident → stop testing + human investigation; data leak → sandbox isolation
  • Privacy and human review: fully traceable; human-involved scenarios require prior public consent; tests are not written as approved operations

S07 Model Evaluation & Standards Hall TEST (Zhongzhiyuan)

  • Positioning: open evaluation, discussion, and archiving of AI models and standards (industry testing scenario)
  • User journey: submit evaluation application → public benchmark → results archived → standards discussion
  • Input data: benchmark sets, evaluation results (public, reproducible)
  • AI capability: evaluation execution, result recomputation (transparent rules, versioned)
  • Infrastructure: evaluation hall + compute pool + discussion space (conceptual)
  • Operating entity: evaluation institution with standards organization (conceptual)
  • Failure mode: benchmark disputes → version rollback; compute shortage → queue with public notice
  • Privacy and human review: reproducible results; no personal data; human confirms publication

S08 AI Cultural Guide (Memory Spine / Xiaoyue)

  • Positioning: multilingual, dual-mode Jing-Zhang AI cultural guide (international visitors and residents)
  • User journey: book/scan → voice or AR guide → feedback → correction archived
  • Input data: public historical materials, exhibition archives (no personal data)
  • AI capability: multilingual generation and Q&A (history verified, human gatekeeping; generative-service compliance follows clause-level Interim Measures boundaries)
  • Infrastructure: guide terminal/app + signage (conceptual)
  • Operating entity: park/cultural institution with technology partner (conceptual)
  • Failure mode: content errors → human correction and archiving; device failure → human guide fallback
  • Privacy and human review: no trajectories; complaint channels respond promptly without invented statutory deadlines

S09 Smart Civic Service Hub (neighborhood nodes)

  • Positioning: intelligent service plus human fallback for high-frequency civic/health/elderly matters (low-digital-literacy users)
  • User journey: arrive → self-service or human → service completed → service archive queryable
  • Input data: service items, appointments (minimized; sensitive data tiered and encrypted)
  • AI capability: matter navigation, document pre-review (health matters refer, never diagnose)
  • Infrastructure: hub + human window + self-service terminal (conceptual)
  • Operating entity: community health center / government service outlet (conceptual)
  • Failure mode: AI misjudgment → doctor/human review; network loss → purely human channel
  • Privacy and human review: on-site guidance and human handling retained (accessibility-law scenarios); opt-out and appeal possible

S10 AI-Native Consumption Block (Dazhongsi)

  • Positioning: AI-native consumption and business experience with station-city pedestrian connection
  • User journey: metro arrival → four-quadrant walking → smart-terminal experience → consumption feedback archived
  • Input data: crowd aggregation (no individual recognition), consumption-scenario data (consent-based)
  • AI capability: smart-terminal experience, crowd-routing suggestions (aggregated, human review)
  • Infrastructure: pedestrian connection + experience street + interchange facilities (conceptual)
  • Operating entity: block operator with commercial tenants (conceptual)
  • Failure mode: crowding risk → human diversion; device failure → conventional service
  • Privacy and human review: no personal profiling; anonymous feedback; station-renovation engineering pending special review

S11 Xiaoyue Public Experience Path (Xiaoyue wing)

  • Positioning: public experience of AI+ health/education/life scenarios and scenario-archive collection
  • User journey: enter path → experience scenarios → feedback → scenario archive updated
  • Input data: aggregated usage data (no individual identification)
  • AI capability: scenario recommendations and health tips (tips only, never diagnosis)
  • Infrastructure: experience path + scenario nodes + slow-traffic facilities (conceptual)
  • Operating entity: landscape authority + subdistrict + service provider (conceptual)
  • Failure mode: misleading tips → human review; device failure → conventional slow traffic
  • Privacy and human review: data minimization; human windows retained; opt-out possible

S12 Archive Open Season Venue (belt public space) TEST

  • Positioning: annual "Archive Open Season" global developer conference, hackathon, and honor-conferral venue (industry testing scenario: event-system stress test)
  • User journey: register → attend/compete → submit → review → honor archived
  • Input data: event registration (consent-based), on-site crowd aggregation
  • AI capability: event matching, simultaneous interpretation, content pre-check (human final review)
  • Infrastructure: main hall/outdoor venue + network + streaming (conceptual)
  • Operating entity: joint operating committee (conceptual)
  • Failure mode: safety event → pause + contingency plan; crowd overflow → flow control
  • Privacy and human review: streaming consent; human-final review; the event is a conceptual arrangement, not a confirmed commitment
