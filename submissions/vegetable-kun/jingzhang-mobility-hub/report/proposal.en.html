Three-Level Scope Framework

  • Strategic Scope: ~43.6 km², constructing the "Jing-Zhang AI Symbiosis Belt"
  • General Design Scope: ~11.4 km², reshaping Jing-Zhang Heritage Park area
  • Key Areas Scope: ~368.4 ha, three detailed design districts

Strategic Scope: Industry & Future City Research

Responding to Announcement 1.5 (1). References: [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK]. Case studies: Silicon Valley, Cambridge, Munich, Tel Aviv, Shenzhen, Singapore, Helsinki, Toronto.

General Design Scope: Urban Renewal & Regulatory Planning Depth

Responding to Announcement 1.5 (2), reaching control planning urban design depth.

Key Areas Detailed Design

Zhongzhiyuan AI Autonomous Innovation District (PROV-KEY-001)

Garden-style full-stack autonomous innovation block, strengthening Qinghe interface, industrial exhibition, low-carbon innovation exchange.

Beijing AI Origin Community (PROV-KEY-002)

University-adjacent talent and translation community, stitching campus-district-neighborhood walkability.

Dazhongsi AI Industry Cluster (PROV-KEY-003)

Urban intelligent economy and international exchange block, four-mode zero-transfer hub.

AI Innovation Ecology & AI+ Scenarios

10 AI scenario cards, including 3 industry test/validation scenarios. Covering AI+software, medical, education, legal, lifestyle, transportation, public space.
