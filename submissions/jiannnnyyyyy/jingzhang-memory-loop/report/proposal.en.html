Coordinated Research Area: Industry and Future City Research

The concept is one belt, three innovation anchors, multiple scenarios, and a blue-green slow-mobility loop. It translates the three call positions into AI full-stack innovation, world-class ecosystem, AI+ scenarios, intelligent public life, and AI governance Standard. Seven benchmark cases are used only as methods: MaRS Toronto Source, Station F Source, the Alan Turing Institute Source, Pittsburgh Robotics Network Source, Punggol Digital District Source, Shibuya QWS Source, and Brainport Eindhoven Source. Their lessons become a checklist for space, compute, data, scenarios, capital, talent, and governance; no local company, investment, or policy promise is invented.

The seven-element ecosystem loop is “researchers frame questions; Zhongzhiyuan tests safety and standards; AI Origin Community supports near-campus transfer; Dazhongsi demonstrates products and content; the Zhongguancun service wing connects IP, capital, and international exchange; the Xiaoyuehe scenario wing collects public experience feedback.” These are conceptual interfaces only: public actors set rules and review, enterprises and universities opt in, developers use open APIs and open-source licenses, and residents can respond through non-digital channels. No vendor, investor, or procurement commitment is assumed.

Regional collaboration is proposed as interfaces rather than administrative commitments: Beiliwei Community for community services and young talent exchange, Future Science City for frontier research, Huairou Science City for long-horizon science facilities, E-Town for intelligent manufacturing and scale-up, and the Beijing-Tianjin-Hebei region for transfer and talent mobility. Each interface is tested through public annual questions, reciprocal visits, reusable data standards, and outcome reviews Source.

The identity “JZ CIVIC SPINE / Jing-Zhang Civic Spine” uses a rail spine, branching links, and three civic nodes. Deep teal, copper, and signal green form a proposed palette; the mark is a continuous axis with three nodes, to be redrawn with cleared fonts and assets Depth.

The minimum visual guide uses a 2:1 horizontal spine-and-three-node mark and a circular seal for nodes; stretching and unauthorized corporate marks are prohibited. Deep teal #0F2433, copper #B88A3B, signal green #2D8A55, and river cyan #4D9EB0 are the four functional colors. “JZ CIVIC SPINE” is the mark, “Jing-Zhang Civic Spine” is the name, and “Open Test / Civic Life / Heritage Rail” are replaceable sublabels. Activity subbrands remain text locks beside the master mark. Fonts, graphics, printing, and accessibility require clearance before use.

AI Innovation Ecosystem, Personas, and AI+ Scenarios

Five personas are a near-campus researcher, open-source developer, industry service worker, community family, and international visitor. The ten scenario cards below are complete, reviewable concept service packages. Each card binds a provisional area, node, or space type to a user, workflow, data status, minimization rule, human review, exit/rollback, operating responsibility, stop condition, and evidence record. Locations remain provisional design relationships rather than site permissions; data is public, synthetic, or explicitly authorized and minimal Standard.

Ten AI scenario cards (concept suggestions; numeric thresholds pending professional confirmation)

SC-01 Open-source Launch Hall\nUsers: developers and researchers. Space: the adaptable ground-floor public interface of AI Origin Community (PROV-KEY-002). Flow: submit public code/docs -> service desk checks license and sensitive content -> human confirmation -> publish to contribution wall and catalog. Data: repository URL, version, and license supplied voluntarily from a public or authorized repository; no identity profiling. Human review: two-person desk check for rights, malicious code, and reversible attribution. Exit/rollback: author withdrawal or paper consultation when review fails. Operator: community service desk and data-governance lead. Stop: unclear license, personal data, or unreviewable material. Evidence: publication record, license checklist, human sign-off, withdrawal log.

SC-02 Civic-Agent Sandbox\nUsers: scenario developers and operators. Space: Zhongzhiyuan open test garden and standards workshop (PROV-KEY-001). Flow: register test goal -> run synthetic data and public geometry -> safety review -> time-bounded demo -> retrospective. Data: synthetic dialogue, public GeoJSON, and test logs; no production accounts or real trajectories. Human review: safety panel checks privilege escalation, bias, and explainability. Exit/rollback: close the model interface and return to manual scripts and paper logs on anomaly. Operator: test operator and safety panel. Stop: escalation, unexplainable output, missing logs, or rising risk. Evidence: red-team report, stop reason, version hash, retrospective.

SC-03 Walkability Diagnosis\nUsers: older adults, people with disabilities, and visitors. Space: heritage-park memory loop, station entrances, and crossings near PROV-KEY-001. Flow: paper or human report -> staff field check -> alternative route -> public accessibility issue list. Data: public road/public-space geometry and human observation; no face, phone ID, or continuous trajectory. Human review: accessibility advisor and community representative confirm route usability. Exit/rollback: uncertain navigation directs the user to a human desk, paper map, and phone. Operator: public-space maintenance team and community desk. Stop: route cannot be confirmed, construction conflict, or safety incident. Evidence: issue list, alternative-route map, accessibility checklist, paper feedback.

SC-04 Talent Life Concierge\nUsers: researchers, students, and community families. Space: AI Origin Community human service desk (PROV-KEY-002). Flow: spoken or paper request -> search public service directory -> human handoff -> user confirms resolution. Data: public directory and one-time voluntary input; no persistent profile or unnecessary fields. Human review: desk worker confirms scope and completes handoff. Exit/rollback: voluntary withdrawal; paper directory, phone, and human service remain available. Operator: community desk with governance lead supervising retention. Stop: sensitive data required, unexplained advice, or failed handoff. Evidence: anonymous handoff log, withdrawal record, directory version, complaint form.

SC-05 AI Safety Corridor\nUsers: developers, educators, and governance staff. Space: Zhongzhiyuan Safety Dome and movable display corridor (PROV-KEY-001). Flow: choose cleared test sample -> safety panel runs red-team script -> publish pass/stop reason -> archive improvement item. Data: synthetic samples, public standards, and authorized model notes; no unlicensed IP. Human review: signed test card with independently recorded disputes. Exit/rollback: remove failed display and return to manual explanation and offline samples. Operator: safety panel and exhibition operator. Stop: escalation, personal-data leak, unclear rights, or irreproducible test. Evidence: signed test card, risk list, version record, removal record.

SC-06 University-Enterprise Salon\nUsers: university teams, small firms, and researchers. Space: AI Origin Community shared ground-floor salon (PROV-KEY-002). Flow: publish public question -> voluntary booking -> match by project metadata -> human-hosted exchange -> parties confirm next step. Data: public project summary, authorized contact, and topic tags; no unlicensed patent, source code, or trade secret. Human review: host checks permissions, conflicts, and minutes. Exit/rollback: either party withdraws; failed match retains only the public question. Operator: community operator and university-enterprise coordinator. Stop: unclear rights, forced promotion, or undisclosed conflict. Evidence: question card, authorization, minutes, match review.

SC-07 Data Commons Theatre\nUsers: residents, firms, and public institutions. Space: Dazhongsi Open Terminal Walk (PROV-KEY-003). Flow: show licensed aggregate data -> explain source and limits -> paper/oral feedback -> governance review before update. Data: licensed aggregate statistics and public source cards; no person-level records. Human review: governance lead checks license, anonymization, and context. Exit/rollback: unclear license, privacy, or source removes the dataset and switches to a blank template. Operator: Dazhongsi exhibition operator and governance lead. Stop: license expiry, re-identification risk, or uncorrectable public misunderstanding. Evidence: source card, license record, display version, feedback summary, removal record.

SC-08 Low-carbon Compute Stop\nUsers: maintenance staff, residents, and visitors. Space: a movable equipment box at a heritage-park or public-service node (concept location). Flow: show aggregate energy/device state -> maintenance inspection -> publish maintenance result -> power down or degrade on anomaly. Data: aggregate equipment telemetry and inspection only; no identity, voice, or location trajectory. Human review: maintenance team checks temperature, noise, power, and permissions. Exit/rollback: overheating, fault, or unauthorized collection powers down and returns to a static sign. Operator: facilities team and public-space manager. Stop: safety threshold breach, data overreach, or unmaintainable condition. Evidence: maintenance log, power-down record, energy summary, permission checklist.

SC-09 Jing-Zhang Memory Route\nUsers: families, schools, and visitors. Space: the railway spine, heritage park, and three key-area nodes (PROV-KEY-001--003). Flow: milepost/codeplate guide presents three acts -> cultural guide answers questions -> paper feedback -> periodic source check. Data: cleared historical material and public source cards; no face, name, or continuous trajectory. Human review: cultural guide and editor verify facts, rights, and bilingual equivalence. Exit/rollback: uncertain facts or expired rights remove the panel and show a “pending verification” note. Operator: cultural-content team, guides, and community desk. Stop: unverifiable history, expired permission, or unsafe wayfinding conflict. Evidence: source card, route review, rights record, public feedback.

SC-10 Global AI Week Route\nUsers: developers, international visitors, and residents. Space: the three key areas plus Zhongguancun service-wing and Xiaoyuehe scenario-wing interfaces. Flow: public question call -> optional registration and accessibility needs -> time-slotted route -> human retrospective with aggregate results. Data: minimal registration fields, optional consent, and aggregate participation; no default location, portrait, or sensitive data. Human review: event coordinator checks capacity, accessibility, rights, and safety. Exit/rollback: crowding, rights, or accessibility risk pauses the route and switches to online/paper activity. Operator: event coordinator, venue operators, and public-review team. Stop: safety incident, inadequate authorization, accessibility failure, or data incident. Evidence: event checklist, consent record, aggregate participation, incident log, retrospective.

All ten cards use the Boolean acceptance gate “a record exists, a human can review it, and an exit path exists.” Numeric values for attendance, response time, energy, or accessibility must be jointly confirmed by professionals, operators, and participants before any pilot. Three reference validation scenes are model red-team testing, accessible walk navigation, and smart-device/content integration; they improve services but do not replace regulation or engineering acceptance.

Three AI pilgrimage landmarks are the Jing-Zhang Civic Spine Hall, the Zhongzhiyuan Safety Dome, and the Dazhongsi Open Terminal Walk. They are low-intensity, reversible concept nodes and do not require altering heritage fabric or private buildings. The honor system uses verifiable contribution IDs, optional attribution, and withdrawal. The public-space component kit includes movable seats, accessible wayfinding, low-glare night markers, switchable sensor boxes, and paper feedback boxes Depth.

The cultural narrative has three acts: railway engineering and public infrastructure; Zhongguancun open knowledge and near-campus innovation; and accountable agents, public contribution, and human governance. A bilingual “milepost + codeplate” guide separates historical facts from contemporary concepts. The international line is “From railway memory to accountable intelligence / 从铁路记忆到可问责的智能”.

Renewal Projects, Implementation Policy, and Phasing

Eight reference projects are JZ-01 heritage-park walkability repair, JZ-02 Qinghe innovation edge, JZ-03 Origin Community conversion street, JZ-04 Dazhongsi four-quadrant walking link, JZ-05 public AI service and edge-compute nodes, JZ-06 global AI week route, JZ-07 three pilgrimage landmarks and contribution wall, and JZ-08 cross-region developer exchange and data-ethics workshops Spatial data Depth. The sequence is operate first, renew second, coordinate later: pilot wayfinding and open days, then retrofit interfaces and service nodes, then deepen construction after controls, ownership, utilities, and finance are confirmed Depth.

The reference annual calendar is memory/open-source season in spring, scenario open week in summer, global responsible-AI innovation week in autumn, and public review season in winter. The developer community maintains public issues monthly, publishes scenario packs quarterly, and selects reversible contribution cases annually. The conversion path is public question, short visit, compliant pilot, professional review, and voluntary collaboration. A conceptual RACI assigns public-space and safety review to local/professional teams, scenario design to participants and operators, data and rights to a governance lead, and public feedback to community desks. Any unreviewable, unauthorized, or unsafe output pauses and rolls back to paper/manual service.
