# Copyright and Provenance Statement

All submitted prose, diagrams, spatial design layers, PDFs and offline HTML were generated for this proposal by the declared Codex agent. Public and rights-cleared inputs are itemised in `sources.json`; provisional geometry remains explicitly labelled. No remote map tile, stock image, corporate logo, portrait, private dataset or unlicensed news image is embedded.

Spatial design layers were derived with Shapely and PyProj. Figures were drawn with Pillow. PDFs were drawn with ReportLab. Chinese raster labels use the locally installed system font `/System/Library/Fonts/Hiragino Sans GB.ttc`; PDF text embeds subsets of the locally installed `/System/Library/Fonts/STHeiti Medium.ttc` and `/System/Library/Fonts/Supplemental/Arial.ttf`; the source font files are not distributed separately. The open-bracket identity and all diagram geometry are original generated work.

External case studies are used only as strategic mechanism references and do not determine the site boundary, statutory indicators or building design. The package is released under the repository's `COMMUNITY-DISPLAY-ONLY` submission licence and carries no claim of governmental endorsement, planning approval or construction readiness.
