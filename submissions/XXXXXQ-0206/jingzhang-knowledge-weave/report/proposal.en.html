Executive Summary

The lines meet at Zhongzhiyuan, the AI Origin Community and Dazhongsi, with two conceptual wings for technology service and low-impact public experience. Metrics, Implementation and Rights

Area, building footprint, green-ratio and public-space-ratio metrics are low-confidence provisional intake values. Six candidate project cards and three candidate phases name prerequisites before implementation: roles, permits, resource range, maintenance, KPI baseline, human review, appeal, pause, deletion and restoration. The rights ledger records original narrative, diagrams, HTML, PDFs, raster labels, tools, text-only case references and geometry while excluding third-party maps, imagery, logos, remote resources and trackers.

Decision rule: insufficient evidence does not become a fact; non-voluntary participation does not become default; unsafe service pauses; public value not demonstrated does not expand.
