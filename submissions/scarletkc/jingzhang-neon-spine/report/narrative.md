# 方案摘要 / Proposal Summary

京张霓光脊把百年轨道遗产转化为一条可见、可质询、可停用的公民 AI 公共协议。方案以一脊、三引擎、两翼、十二端口、双账本组织空间、产业、场景与治理；核心图件均由 provisional GeoJSON 和可复算指标生成，不把赛博朋克封面当作空间证据。

Jingzhang Neon Spine turns railway heritage into a visible, contestable and reversible civic AI protocol. One Spine, Three Engines, Two Wings, Twelve Ports and Two Ledgers join space, industry, scenarios and governance. Core figures derive from provisional GeoJSON and reproducible metrics; the cyberpunk cover is illustrative only.
