# 京张相遇公地｜一页故事

## 一句话

不用本地账号、不交私人关系数据，也能沿京张找到一项已授权的公共任务、和别人一起完成，并带着看得见的成果安全离开。

## 一个人

以第一次到访的国际新人“小林”为叙事线索：小林不需要下载应用、注册账号或公开个人关系，只需沿无障碍路径找到人类主持人，领取一张纸质相遇票，即可选择参与、旁观或离开。小林是合成人物，用来检查体验是否完整，不代表真实访谈或现场调研。

## 三段体验

- FLOW-A：在 AI 原点（`PROV-KEY-002`），人与主持人共同把模糊想法写成可执行的小任务。
- FLOW-B：在众智园（`PROV-KEY-001`），任务进入可逆、可停机的空间测试，AI 只处理获准字段。
- FLOW-C：在大钟寺（`PROV-KEY-003`），测试结果以公开展览和安静反馈的方式回到社区，不做关系匹配，也不以手机为入口。

`FLOW-A/B/C` 只表示故事顺序；固定空间编号仍是众智园 `PROV-KEY-001`、AI 原点 `PROV-KEY-002`、大钟寺 `PROV-KEY-003`，并且不复用 RACI 或费用级字母。三段是同一项任务可分日完成的“共创—验证—交换”流程；90 分钟只指 AI 原点的一场共桌，不是三地之间的一次行程。

## 一组实施准备证据

离线仿真包含 13 项任务、18 项通过断言、64 种空间布尔组合和 9 个预期拒绝。它只说明数字契约会按预期“放行或拒绝”，不是现场绩效。正式实施仍需权属、保护范围、消防、无障碍、运维和四方联合踏勘完成后才能放行。

# Encounter Commons｜One-page story

## One sentence

Without a local account or disclosure of private relationships, a newcomer can find an authorised public task along the Jingzhang corridor, complete it with others, and leave safely with a visible result.

## One person

Lin, a first-time international newcomer, is the narrative lens. They need no app, account or relationship disclosure. An accessible route leads them to a human host and a paper Encounter Ticket; they may join, observe or leave. Lin is a synthetic persona used to test the completeness of the journey, not evidence of interviews or field research.

## Three experiences

- FLOW-A: At AI Origin (`PROV-KEY-002`), Lin and a host turn a vague idea into a small, actionable task.
- FLOW-B: At Zhongzhiyuan (`PROV-KEY-001`), the task enters a reversible, stoppable spatial test; AI may process only authorised fields.
- FLOW-C: At Dazhongsi (`PROV-KEY-003`), results return to the community through a public display and quiet feedback table, without relationship matching or phone-only access.

`FLOW-A/B/C` indicate story order only; the fixed spatial IDs remain Zhongzhiyuan `PROV-KEY-001`, AI Origin `PROV-KEY-002`, and Dazhongsi `PROV-KEY-003`, and the flow labels are not reused for RACI or cost classes. The three experiences form a co-create—verify—exchange task process that may span different days. Ninety minutes refers only to one AI Origin table session, not a single journey between all three places.

## One implementation-readiness evidence set

The offline simulation contains 13 tasks, 18 passing assertions, all 64 spatial Boolean combinations and 9 expected rejections. It shows that the digital contract releases or rejects cases as designed; it is not field performance. Ownership, protection limits, fire safety, accessibility, operations and a four-party joint walk must still be resolved before any real-site release.
