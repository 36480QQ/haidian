Three key areas, three urban prototypes

Zhongzhiyuan | Open R&D Garden

Shared test courtyards, a Qinghe waterfront commons, and a walkable R&D loop replace an inward-facing campus model. Ground floors prioritize test showcases, standards consultation, shared meeting space, and low-carbon infrastructure demonstrations.

Public-realm stitching comes before technology display; river, fire-safety, property, and energy conditions require professional confirmation.
Beijing AI Origin Community | Campus-to-City Translation Seam

Use 5–10 minute walking continuity to connect universities, parks, workplaces, and neighborhoods. Open-source release, IP/legal services, youth talent space, and everyday services occupy accessible ground floors.

Success is walkability, active frontage, and shared resident value rather than device count.
Dazhongsi | Four-Quadrant Station Exchange Hall

Link station access, industry, commerce, and public space through four pedestrian quadrants, with roadshows, smart-device exhibits, and civic services activating the station-city edge.

PROV-KEY-003 is a rough placeholder and does not prove that the real station lies inside the polygon.

10 AI scenario cards

01 Origin CommunityOpen-Source Release Hall

Reusable launch, exhibition, and peer-review space for faculty, students, and developers.

Fallback: no identity-trajectory tracking; human facilitation remains available.
02 ZhongzhiyuanAI Safety Governance Sandbox

Controlled red-teaming, benchmark testing, and safety demonstrations producing auditable records rather than automatic certification.

Fallback: high-risk tests require human approval and immediate shutdown/isolation.
03 Belt-wideEdge Compute Pods

Small edge nodes support wayfinding, sensing, and low-latency demos while coordinating energy and cooling constraints.

Fallback: basic public facilities keep working offline.
04 Heritage ParkAccessible Slow-Mobility Guidance

Reports slopes, temporary obstacles, crowding, and accessible alternatives for older people, wheelchair users, blind/low-vision users, and visitors.

Fallback: physical signs, staffed help, and non-digital accessible routes remain.
05 DazhongsiInternational Roadshow Lounge

Short-cycle launch and exchange space for agent, smart-device, and content companies integrated with pedestrian flows.

Fallback: rights clearance is required; no government endorsement is implied.
06 Qinghe WaterfrontLow-Carbon Innovation Corridor

Stormwater, shade, walking, cycling, outdoor collaboration, and low-power sensing in one continuous public space.

Fallback: ecology and access performance take priority over digital display.
07 Origin CommunityNear-Campus Tech-Transfer Street

Incubation, IP, legal, investment, and shared meeting services line a walkable ground-floor seam.

Fallback: AI organizes information only; professional judgments require human sign-off.
08 DazhongsiData Compliance Commons

Public-facing explanation of authorization, minimization, audit, and withdrawal without unapproved data exchange.

Fallback: no explicit authorization means no processing; staffed consultation remains.
09 Neighborhood NodesAI Everyday-Service Street

Education, medical triage information, legal information, community services, and daily assistance embedded in ordinary street corners.

Fallback: high-impact decisions escalate to humans; no credit scoring or commercial profiling.
10 Belt-wide RouteGlobal AI Event Week Route

A walkable sequence links railway heritage, open-source community, industry demonstrations, and station-city exchange spaces while remaining ordinary public realm outside events.

Fallback: operations concept only; venue, safety, and copyright permissions are separate.

Three validation scenarios

T1 Accessible-navigation fault injection

Test positioning drift, network loss, sensor outage, and false crowd alerts; non-AI signage must still provide basic access.

T2 Sandbox safe shutdown

Test abnormal output, unauthorized data requests, and overload; require human stop control, traceable logs, and isolation.

T3 Human review for civic services

Test low-confidence and conflicting education/medical/legal answers; high-impact matters must escalate to humans.
