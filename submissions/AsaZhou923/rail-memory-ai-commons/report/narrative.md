# 轨忆智链 / Rail Memory AI Commons

轨忆智链把百年京张 AI 创新带定义为公共 AI 记忆基础设施：每一个 AI 场景、空间改变、文化解释、数据集、模型、维护动作和公共决策，都生成可复核的 Memory Link，记录来源、权利、版本、人工 steward、有效期、失败退出和复算触发。

每条 Memory Link 还采用“登记 - 认领 - 处理中 - 待结案复核 - 重开或升级 - 归档”的概念生命周期；结案争议沿原记录重开并保留版本和责任历史，具体复核主体、隐私规则、时限与服务等级待专业及公众参与设计。

每条活跃记录还需建立运维契约，登记责任所有方、日常运营方、资金状态、检查频率、最低服务、更换或退役触发与交接路径；状态感知仅面向资产和环境，不追踪或排名一线人员。

每条记录还需同时公开影响账：基线、预期收益、受益群体、资源/工作/隐私/环境代价、代价承担群体、责任角色、纠偏触发与退出条件；记录完整不等于已经证明公平或有效。

The proposal organizes the corridor as One Rail, Three Repositories, Two Wings, and Twelve Memory Points. Zhongzhiyuan Stack Memory Forge validates models, data, and robotics; Beijing AI Origin Open Memory Studio translates open source, university-community needs, talent services, and rights clinics; Dazhongsi Public Adoption Memory Exchange previews enterprise copilots, terminals, and content products through public feedback.

Each Memory Link also follows a conceptual lifecycle of registered, assigned, in action, awaiting closure review, reopened or escalated, and archived. A disputed closure reopens the existing record and preserves version and responsibility history; eligible reviewers, privacy rules, timing, and service levels still require professional and public-participation design.

Each active record also needs a maintenance covenant covering the accountable owner, day-to-day operator, funding status, inspection interval, minimum service, replacement or retirement trigger, and handover route. Status sensing is limited to assets and environmental conditions, never frontline-worker tracking or ranking.

Each record also needs a public impact ledger covering its baseline, intended benefit, beneficiary groups, resource/workload/privacy/environmental costs, cost-bearing groups, responsible role, correction trigger, and exit condition. Complete documentation does not itself prove fairness or effectiveness.

当前空间动作均为概念建议或参考方案，当前几何为临时约束范围，不是官方红线或精确边界；内容评审就绪不等于正式专业评分、规划批准或实施承诺。

All spatial and operational actions require professional deepening after official boundaries, RDP controls, transport, utilities, ownership, heritage, and rights data are completed.
