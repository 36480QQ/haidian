# Jingzhang AI Innovation Belt with a Civic Calibration Layer

The full Jingzhang AI Innovation Belt is the complete urban proposition. Research, open-source production, prototyping, incubation interfaces, first use, public service, culture and continuous operation are organized across one railway-heritage spine, three urban rhythms, three key areas and two supporting wings. Civic Calibration is the distinctive operating layer: it defines how innovation is allowed to enter, remain in or leave public life rather than defining the entire belt.

The north links Zhongzhiyuan, Qinghe and the Fifth Ring as a low-disturbance research and controlled-validation interface. The central corridor links university knowledge, open-source reproduction, engineering heritage and developer contribution. The south retains the reality-checked Dazhongsi Release-Retirement Demonstration District as the place for limited release, public verification, appeal, rollback and retirement. Zhongguancun is shown only as a potential standards, intellectual-property, professional-service and capital interface; Xiaoyuehe is an everyday-life and public-feedback wing. Both require partnership and are not claimed as confirmed programs.

Eight global precedents are used to extract transferable principles, not to copy brands, institutions or partnership models. Ten AI scenarios are arranged in tiers: three flagships and seven supporting applications. A separate No-AI Continuity System Card defines the non-digital baseline and is not counted as an AI scenario. Three proposed evaluation protocols cover safety and accessibility, low-speed embodied service, and public-information accuracy and provenance. No measured performance, signed operator, dataset permission or deployment approval is claimed.

The cultural route connects an Engineering Problem Archive, an Open-source Contribution Ground and the existing Release-Retirement Commons. Its public value is ordinary walking, shade, seating, reading and accessible movement; its developer value is to make problem definition, reproducibility, adoption, correction and version history visible. A five-stage annual operating loop returns evidence from public use and retirement to the next year's problem intake.

The public OpenStreetMap extract remains a traceable contextual background only. Repository site and key-area geometries remain provisional. The coordinated 43.6 km2 research context and the approximately 11.4 km2 provisional computation geometry are different planning layers. The reality-checked Dazhongsi study area, intervention polygons, pedestrian routes, lifecycle nodes and sections remain geometrically fixed unless a factual blocker is discovered.

The package reaches competition-level conceptual urban-design depth. Zhongzhiyuan and the AI Origin interfaces are explicitly indicative; they are not survey-grade detailed plans. Official boundaries, road redlines, station engineering, ownership, building surveys, heritage controls, utilities, traffic counts, cost data, operating agreements and dataset permissions remain required before statutory planning, implementation or deployment.
