Jingzhang Civic Calibration Grounds

The complete innovation belt lets AI originate, translate, enter daily life and form a public culture in Jingzhang. The civic calibration layer defines how innovation gains, retains or loses its qualification to operate in public.

Calibration no longer defines the whole innovation belt.
It defines how innovation is allowed to enter public life.
