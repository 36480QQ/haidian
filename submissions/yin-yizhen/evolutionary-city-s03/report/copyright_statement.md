# Copyright and Data Provenance Statement

All submitted text, code, deterministic diagrams, PDFs and offline HTML were produced within the declared human–AI collaboration or derive from organizer-provided and public sources recorded in `sources.json` and `data/evidence-manifest.json`.

The public story uses a frozen OpenStreetMap XML snapshot acquired on 2026-08-20 for the declared S03 analytical window. The map data is © OpenStreetMap contributors and licensed under ODbL 1.0. `visual/assets/reproduce-story.js` embeds the gzip-compressed XML snapshot and deterministic build/test sources; request metadata, bbox, access time, byte length, licence link and original snapshot SHA-256 remain visible in the evidence files. Running the script reconstructs and tests the story data in a temporary directory without making a remote request. The analytical window is not an official redline, cadastral boundary, ownership record or engineering survey.

The old Tsinghuayuan Station address, published opening rules and residential-quiet context come from the 2023 official Haidian public notice recorded in the evidence manifest. The competition purpose, three-level textual scope and design tasks come from the official competition announcement. Neither source supplies a verified statutory polygon for S03.

The twelve D0 analytical cells are generated deterministically from the frozen OSM road network in EPSG:4548 and are labelled `derived_analysis`. The public simulator derives 2,744 walking nodes and 3,237 edges from the same snapshot, then routes 100 seeded synthetic users through three parent plans, four scenarios and three recombination rounds. These routes, relative crowding values, dwell events and selection records are labelled `simulated_design`; they are not observed movement, measured performance, public consensus, implementation commitments or real resident feedback. D1–D40 and the 1,440 urban individuals remain an expert-layer method study under the same simulation label. Missing climate, ownership, entrance, slope, fire, traffic-flow and public-feedback evidence remains `null / unknown`.

Earlier inaccurate city-model plates and the former atmospheric underlay have been removed from the runtime and final package. The current website loads only clearly labelled generated design-intent images, and no remote map tiles, CDN, external fonts, iframe, tracking or API. All evidence maps and digital-user paths are rendered directly from the frozen GeoJSON/OSM data and explicit simulated design changes in code. `assets/media/cover.webp` is a deterministic Chinese editorial composition made from the approved `design-intent-s03.webp`: it adds only typography, a dark tonal overlay, flat lineage traces and already-declared simulation counts, and introduces no separate factual geographic claim.

`assets/media/design-intent-s03.webp` is a new Image 2 conceptual physical-model visualization. It is explicitly labelled as design intent, not an existing photograph, survey base, measured geometry or implementation promise. The first generated draft was rejected because it introduced an elevated megastructure and invented circular tower. The retained revision removes those elements and presents only an at-grade, lightweight public-space intention. Its Chinese method and limitation record is bundled as `assets/media/design-intent-s03.md`; all factual geometry remains governed by GeoJSON and public-data diagrams.

`assets/media/node-old-station.webp`, `node-wudaokou.webp` and `evolution-tableau.webp` are additional conceptual Image 2 outputs used only in the redesigned exhibition boards and interactive story. The node images do not claim accurate facades, vehicles, vegetation or implemented facilities. The first evolution-tableau draft was rejected because translucent genes appeared elevated; the retained revision flattens every gene layer to the model ground plane. Prompts, rejection reasons, reference hash and final hashes are recorded in `assets/media/node-intent-images.md`.

`assets/media/experience.mp4` is a silent 90-second sequence assembled only from local screenshots of the same offline story. It contains no third-party music, voice, identifiable people or additional geographic imagery. The WebVTT captions and Markdown transcript/rights note are bundled locally, and the video never autoplays.

`visual/assets/fonts.css` contains proposal-specific glyph subsets of Noto Sans SC regular and bold, rebuilt on 2026-08-26 through the official Google Fonts subset service and embedded as local data fonts. Noto is redistributed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1; the copyright notice and complete licence text are preserved in the CSS header. The four Chinese/English HTML deliverables embed the same local subset and make no runtime font request. The subset covers every non-ASCII character used by the proposal, taskbook index, interactive story and evidence labels at the time of submission.

The six named ecosystem cases in `visual/assets/taskbook-index.json` use only cited public pages from the City of Cambridge, JTC Singapore, Barcelona City Council, City of Helsinki, STATION F and MaRS Discovery District. No case image, drawing, logo, company list, investment amount or performance figure is copied into the proposal. The case table records only a paraphrased mechanism, the condition for considering it in Jingzhang and an explicit non-transfer boundary.
