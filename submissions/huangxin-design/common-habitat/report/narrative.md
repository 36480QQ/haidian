# 共益生境：公开说明

## COMMON HABITAT · PUBLIC NARRATIVE

共益生境以完整的一天作为京张创新带的公共验收单位。不同年龄、身体与感官能力、语言、照护负担和数字经验的人，都应能独立完成抵达、识路、休息、参与、求助和安全离开。判断结果按能力与情境的交叉条件公布；缺少代表性参与者的组别标记为 unknown，不用总体平均补齐证据。

Common Habitat uses one complete day as the public test for the Jingzhang corridor. Across age, physical and sensory ability, language, care duties and digital confidence, people should be able to arrive, orient, rest, participate, get help and return safely. Results are reported by intersecting capability and context conditions. An unrepresented group remains unknown; an overall average cannot fill that evidence gap.

慢行、无障碍、遮阴、饮水、雨水、生境、人工服务和普通导视保持独立可用。N0 与低技术方案达到公共结果和硬性护栏时，AI 不进入公共服务层。仍有缺口的场景须提前登记可感知的改善、三年全成本和停止条件，并由独立复测确认最弱交叉条件没有退步。权利或安全事件、儿童数据越界、人工接管失效、维护责任或预算中断，均触发停机并恢复 N0。

Walking, universal access, shade, drinking water, rainwater, habitat, human service and ordinary signage remain independently usable. When N0 or a low-tech measure meets the public outcome and all hard safeguards, AI does not enter the service layer. Any remaining use case must pre-register a perceptible improvement, three-year whole-life cost and stop conditions, then pass an independent retest with no regression for the least-served intersection. Rights or safety incidents, child-data boundary breaches, failed human takeover, or a lapse in maintenance responsibility or funding trigger shutdown and return to N0.

## 多格式线下手册【PLAN · NOT YET DELIVERED】

## MULTI-FORMAT OFFLINE GUIDE【PLAN · NOT YET DELIVERED】

六种版本仍处于待共创状态，没有成品，也不声称已经合规或交付。各版本分别设计和验收：

1. 盲文 + 触觉线路图：专业盲文转写，两名合格校对人员双检，触觉线路图另行专业复核，并与盲人使用者共测；盲文文字版式参考现行 GB/T 44725—2024。[source:CN-BRAILLE-LAYOUT-44725-2024]
2. 低视力大字版：大字、高对比、非颜色唯一编码。
3. 儿童易读版：易读图文与明确求助步骤；照护者提示独立成页。
4. 老人易读版：大字、短句、一步一页，标清时段、费用、厕所、休息点和人工电话。
5. 音频版：与纸本使用同一版本号，支持暂停、重复和音量控制。
6. 电话 + 现场人工讲解：公开可接通时段、等待方式和责任单位；二维码只作附加入口。[source:CN-ACCESSIBILITY-LAW]

The six formats remain a co-design plan. No finished guide has been delivered, and no compliance claim is made. Each format has its own design and acceptance route: BRAILLE + TACTILE MAP; LARGE PRINT + HIGH CONTRAST; CHILD EASY-READ; OLDER-ADULT EASY-READ; AUDIO GUIDE; and PHONE + HUMAN GUIDE. Braille text will be professionally transcribed and checked by two qualified proofreaders; the tactile route map requires separate specialist review and co-testing with blind users.

经场地责任主体确认后，六种版本计划投放或提供于站点、社区服务台、学校和公园等任务发生处。共测记录找到信息、理解、独立完成、错误、求助和尊严感；尊严感由使用者本人说明是否被迫公开障碍或隐私、是否被幼儿化对待、是否只能依赖陪同者，设计团队不得代为评分。任一版本缺少对应使用者参与、专业复核、现场测试、版本日期或更新责任，只能保持 PLAN。

Subject to confirmation by each site owner, the six formats are planned for stations, community service desks, schools and parks where the tasks occur. Co-testing records whether people can find and understand the information, complete the task independently, recover from errors, ask for help and retain dignity. A format without relevant user participation, specialist review, field testing, a version date and an update owner remains PLAN.

## 公共证据合同

## PUBLIC EVIDENCE CONTRACT

场景结果同时记录 N0、低技术和可选 AI 路径。24 人可用性试点只用于发现失败；正式放行使用另一批参与者和预登记规则。儿童路径默认不采集身份、生物特征、精确位置、原始声音、可识别作品或长期画像。常设 AI 在 G1 前须提交运行用电、网络与存储、设备更换、维修工时、备件和退役去向组成的三年资源账。每个服务还须公开公共结果、现场运营、维护、数据与 AI、独立复核和受影响使用者六类责任。

Each use case records N0, low-tech and optional-AI routes side by side. A 24-person usability round finds failures; formal admission uses a separate cohort and pre-registered rules. Child-facing routes collect no identity, biometric data, precise location, raw voice, identifiable work or persistent profile by default. Before G1, any permanent AI layer must disclose a three-year resource account covering operating electricity, network and storage, device replacement, maintenance labour, spare parts and end-of-life handling. Each service also names six responsibilities: public outcome, site operations, maintenance, data and AI, independent review and the affected-user group.

空间上，一条京张共生脊连接铁路记忆、公共生活和三处重点区；公平抵达网修补日常通行断点，水土生境网处理热舒适、雨洪、土壤、生境、暗夜和维护。国际框架只用于把价值转译成可测试任务：UN SDG 11、CRPD、WHO 老龄友好城市、SPACES 儿童公共空间、UNESCO AI 伦理和 W3C WCAG 2.2 不构成认证，也不替代中国标准、北京规范、场地实测和专业责任。[source:INT-UN-SDG11] [source:INT-UN-CRPD9] [source:INT-WHO-AGE-FRIENDLY] [source:INT-CHILD-SPACES] [source:INT-UNESCO-AI-ETHICS] [source:INT-W3C-WCAG22]

Spatially, the Jingzhang Common Spine connects railway memory, public life and three focus areas. An equitable-access network repairs everyday travel gaps; a water-soil-habitat network addresses thermal comfort, stormwater, soil, habitat, darkness and maintenance. International frameworks translate values into testable tasks. UN SDG 11, the CRPD, WHO age-friendly cities, the SPACES child public-space guide, UNESCO AI ethics and WCAG 2.2 provide no certification and do not replace Chinese standards, Beijing requirements, field evidence or professional accountability.

本成果为独立概念建议。当前场地边界与三处重点区 polygon 均为 provisional；容积率、高度、道路红线、工程、市政、权属、资金和运营授权保持 unknown。多格式线下手册、现场测试、生态季节证据和 AI 资源账均处于 PLAN。所有数字承诺属于未来试点的准入目标，不代表已实现绩效。

This is an independent conceptual proposal. The site boundary and three focus-area polygons remain provisional. Floor-area ratio, height, road redlines, engineering, municipal systems, ownership, finance and operating authority remain unknown. The multi-format offline guide, field tests, seasonal ecological evidence and AI resource accounts remain PLAN. Every numerical commitment is an admission target for a future pilot, not achieved performance.
