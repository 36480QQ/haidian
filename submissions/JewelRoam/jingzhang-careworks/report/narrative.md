# 京张道班：正式叙事摘要

京张道班把百年铁路的养护伦理转化为 AI 创新带的城市维修与照护基础设施。空间结构是“一线三厂十班次”：一条人工优先的养城线，众智园可靠性测试、AI 原点社区照护、大钟寺循环维修三处道班工场，以及公厕、排水、热浪、无障碍、夜班、绿地、修理图书馆、低速机器人、备件与公开故障账十个班次。

方案的 AI 边界非常窄：只做去重、预警和建议；现场人员确认、批准、执行和申诉。三个 90 天试点都有责任角色、基线、观测窗口、停止阈值与人工回退。正式 GIS/CAD 边界、法定控制、现场调查、预算和运营承诺缺失项保持 provisional、unknown 或 not_run。

区域协同采用五对象矩阵：北纬社区、未来科学城、怀柔科学城、北京经开区（亦庄）与京津冀区域。每项只定义概念性问题分工、交换要素、合作接口、进入/退出条件和证据边界，不虚构签约、政策、资金或绩效。agent.4 进一步提出三类荣誉/失败展示与可替换公共空间组件，并把大钟寺消费/商务转化拆成空间合规、运营责任、收益公平和退出复盘四道前提门。

# Jingzhang Careworks: formal narrative summary

Jingzhang Careworks translates the maintenance ethic of the century-old railway into public infrastructure for urban repair and care. Its spatial system is one Care Line, three yards and ten shifts: reliability testing in Zhongzhiyuan, community care in the AI Origin Community, circular repair in Dazhongsi, and ten everyday services spanning toilets, stormwater, heat, accessibility, night work, landscape care, repair knowledge, low-speed inspection, civic spares and a public reliability ledger.

AI has a narrow role: deduplication, alerts and suggestions. Accountable people verify, approve, act and handle appeals. Each 90-day pilot carries an owner role, baseline, measurement window, stop rule and manual fallback. Missing official GIS/CAD controls, field evidence, budgets and operating commitments remain provisional, unknown or not_run.

Regional coordination is expressed through a five-object matrix: North Latitude Community, Future Science City, Huairou Science City, Beijing E-Town and the Beijing-Tianjin-Hebei region. Each row defines only a conceptual problem division, exchange elements, cooperation interface, entry/exit conditions and evidence boundary; it invents no contract, policy, funding or performance. agent.4 adds three honour/failure displays and replaceable public-space components, and splits Dazhongsi conversion into four prerequisite gates for space, operations, fair revenue and exit review.
